Part Five found that Netanyahu's warnings about Iran began as warnings about terrorism, with Iran named as one state sponsor among several in 1986, and that his first dated estimate of an Iranian bomb is reported from 1992. [cite:ir5_nyrb_1986] [cite:hz11b_week_1992] Every estimate he gave with a date and no condition, in 1992, 1995 and 2009, passed without a bomb, though each was framed as capability rather than a weapon, and US intelligence had judged by 2007 that Iran's weapons work had stopped in 2003; the IAEA found none after 2009. [cite:hz11b_intercept_wolf] [cite:hz11_wh_nie2007] [cite:ir5_iaea_gov2015] His later warnings, at the UN in 2012 and on the first day of the 2025 war, were conditional, and the conditions changed before they could be tested; on Iran's growing stockpile the record bore him out, while on Iran's intent the US Director of National Intelligence and the IAEA's director general found no decision to build a weapon.
