Who Lit the Fires

The record separates actors that the phrase "the Inquisition" runs together. Popes built the medieval procedure and authorised torture. Kings asked for the Spanish and Portuguese tribunals, chose their judges and used them for royal ends. Rome's own tribunal judged Bruno, Galileo and Menocchio. Secular and territorial courts, Catholic and Protestant, did most of the killing in the witch trials. [cite:vt28_wiki_adextirpanda] [cite:vt34_wiki_spaninq] [cite:vt34_wiki_portinq] [cite:vt34_va_cdf_profile] [cite:vt30_wiki_witchtrials]

Three findings stand out. The first is that the tribunals were built to search, not to wait. From the bishops' sworn visits of 1184 to the papal judges who opened cases without an accuser, the novelty was an office that went looking for wrong belief. [cite:vt28_wiki_adabolendam] [cite:vt28_herodote_1233] The second is that the gravest offence was refusal. The obstinate and the relapsed were burned; those who repented were imprisoned or given penance. Gui burned about 45 of 636; Menocchio was burned the second time, not the first; Bruno was burned because he would not retract. [cite:vt28_mednet_gui] [cite:iq_wiki_menocchio] [cite:vt29_wiki_bruno]

The third finding concerns the targets. In Spain and Portugal the tribunals' first and largest category was people who had been made Christians by decree or by force, baptised Jews and Muslims and their descendants, and in Goa, Hindu converts. The crowns created the converts and then the courts to judge whether their conversion was real. [cite:vt34_wiki_alhambra] [cite:vt34_wiki_manuel] [cite:vt34_wiki_goainq] That finding is about institutions, crowns and tribunals, and the people they persecuted; it names the persecutors by office and the persecuted by what was done to them. The counter-finding belongs beside it: the same machinery, in Rome and in Spain, was among the first in Europe to demand evidence in witch trials and stop the burning. [cite:vt30_wiki_witchtrials] [cite:vt30_wiki_basque] The institution that claimed the right to define the truth was capable of caution; it was not capable of giving up the claim.
