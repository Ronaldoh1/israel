## Draft parts
1. **The Phrase and Its Meanings.** Eretz Yisrael HaShlema; the biblical borders (Genesis 15) vs political programmes; the three versions (maximal, Revisionist, post-1967 territorial).
2. **Founders' Maps, 1896–1937.** Herzl's and the Zionist Organization's 1919 map submitted at Paris; Jabotinsky and "both banks of the Jordan"; the Peel debate; Ben-Gurion's statements (quoted accurately, with context).
3. **1948–1967.** Borders of 1949; the 1956 Sinai campaign and Ben-Gurion's "Third Kingdom of Israel" speech; withdrawal under US pressure.
4. **1967 and the Land of Israel Movement.** The conquests; the Movement for Greater Israel (1967 manifesto); Allon Plan; Gush Emunim.
5. **Likud and the Ideology in Government, 1977–2026.** Begin's "Judea and Samaria"; the 1977 Likud platform ("between the Sea and the Jordan"); the 2018 Nation-State Law ("the development of Jewish settlement as a national value"); the 2022–2026 coalition guidelines; Smotrich's map and statements; Ben-Gvir.
6. **The Maps at the UN.** Netanyahu's 2023 "New Middle East" map with no Palestine; 2024 maps; the pattern after.
7. **The Neighbours, Front by Front.** Golan (1981 law; US recognition 2019); south Lebanon zones (2024–26); Syria buffer zone and Sufa 53 (2024–26); Gaza buffer zones and annexation statements; Sinai and Egypt; Jordan and "Jordan is Palestine"; Iraq/Iran claims tested.
8. **The Documents People Cite.** The Yinon article (1982), A Clean Break (1996): what they say, who wrote them, and what they did and did not become.
9. **The Religious Engine.** Religious Zionism, Third Temple movements, Christian Zionism and the US evangelical constituency (link Book 3 material, traced).
10. **The Counter-Record.** Israeli withdrawals (Sinai 1982, Lebanon 2000, Gaza 2005), peace treaties, Israeli opposition to expansionism, official denials.
11. **Settled, Contested and Open.**

## Discipline
- Ideology attributed to named movements, parties, documents and officials, never to Jews or Israelis collectively.
- Every map and quote sourced to its original; disputed attributions (e.g. "from the Nile to the Euphrates" claims, Knesset-inscription myths) checked and labelled.
- The counter-record given equal care.
