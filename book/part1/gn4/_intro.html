Part Four reads the Gnostic writings of devotion rather than myth. It begins with the Gospel of Thomas, its 114 sayings and its kingdom "inside of you", sets that saying beside the disputed translation of the same idea in Luke, and follows how church writers told the faithful not to read it. It then turns to the sacraments and the bridal chamber of the Gospel of Philip, the Gospel of Truth, the poem Thunder, Perfect Mind, and the Hermetic and Platonic texts that were bound into the same library.
