Part Six turns from the money to the members. A small group in Congress takes no pro-Israel money at all, and in 2025 a few members who had taken it began to refuse it. Some of those who refused and criticized the lobby faced its largest campaigns or formal rebuke; one, Marjorie Taylor Greene, left Congress after being attacked by its leading organizations and abandoned by the president. This part sets out who refuses the money, what happened to them, and the full sequence of Greene's break and resignation, with her own reasons and her critics' accusations side by side.
