Aid After Every War

Who began each war, and whether each was justified, is examined in the volumes that tell those wars: The Cold War Architecture for 1973, The Hezbollah Architecture for Lebanon, Gaza, Documented for the wars in Gaza. This part asks a narrower question: what happened to American aid afterwards.

The pattern in the table is consistent: each of these wars was followed by additional money, much of it for the missile defences that protect Israeli civilians from rockets and missiles, and the 2021 replenishment passed the House by 420 votes to 9. [cite:ow8_ap_irondome2021] [cite:dv4_crs_aid_2025] The single restriction of the most recent war, the bomb shipment paused in May 2024, lasted until January 2025. [cite:dv4_national_bombs_2025]

Supporters read the pattern as an ally being kept able to defend itself after each attack on it. Critics read it as aid that is never conditioned on how a war is fought. Both readings describe the same record.
