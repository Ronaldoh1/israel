Part Eleven established that, measured in constant dollars, Israel's output per person rose from 59.8 per cent of the American level in 1980 to 66.1 per cent in 2022 and fell back to 62.7 per cent in 2025 after two years of war, while its total output grew about six-fold against America's 3.3-fold because its population grew more than twice as fast. [cite:us11_wb_gdppc_const] [cite:us11_wb_pop] Israel's turn came on 1 July 1985, when a plan of budget cuts, devaluation, a wage pact with the Histadrut, a price freeze, a ban on printing money for the deficit and a $1.5 billion American grant ended inflation that had averaged 373 per cent in 1984; America's came through Volcker's Federal Reserve from October 1979 and the cut in the top tax rate from 70 to 28 per cent by 1988. [cite:us11_wb_cpi] [cite:us11_brookings_peres] [cite:us11_frh_volcker] [cite:us11_tpc_toprate] Israel taxes a larger share of its economy, leaning more on VAT and with about half of potential income-tax payers below the threshold, while in the United States the top 1 per cent's share of federal taxes and of income both doubled from 1979 to 2022. [cite:us11_oecd_rev_il] [cite:us11_globes_taxburden] [cite:us11_cbo_dist2022] Their debts moved in opposite directions: Israel's from about 163 per cent of GDP in the mid-1980s to 61.9 per cent in 2023 before the war pushed it to 68.5 per cent, America's from 26 per cent in 1980 to 99 per cent in 2025. [cite:us11_jvl_melnick] [cite:us11_imf_debt] [cite:gi16_boi_2025] [cite:us11_omb_debt] [cite:us11_cbo_outlook2026] In September 2026 the Bank of Israel cut its rate to 3.25 per cent and the Federal Reserve raised its range to 3.75–4 per cent. [cite:us11_globes_boi_sep2026] [cite:us11_fed_sep2026] Israel broke up its business pyramids by statute in 2013, while the United States argues over how much its industries have concentrated and acts case by case, and trade under their 1985 agreement was $54.2 billion in 2025 despite the tariffs of that year.
