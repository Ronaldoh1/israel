The Swiss Bank Contract

One of the five paying clients can be traced document by document. It is not a family. It is a company: the Swiss private bank Edmond de Rothschild, run by Ariane de Rothschild. It is a separate business from the French bank Rothschild & Co, and the record ties neither that bank nor any other branch of the family to this contract.

The problem the bank had

In 2015 the bank was negotiating with the US Department of Justice under the Swiss Bank Program, which let Swiss banks settle over undeclared American accounts. On 18 December 2015 two of its Swiss units, Edmond de Rothschild (Suisse) and Edmond de Rothschild (Lugano), signed a non-prosecution agreement and paid a penalty of $45.245 million. [cite:ep7_doj_edr2015]

What Epstein was hired to do

Ten weeks earlier, on 5 October 2015, Ariane de Rothschild signed a contract with Southern Trust Company, the Virgin Islands firm of which Epstein was president. The counterparty named in the contract was Edmond de Rothschild Holding. [cite:ep7_ibt_rothschild2026] On paper the service was "risk analysis and application of certain algorithms." The bank later described it as strategic consulting that included help with its dispute with the Justice Department. [cite:ep7_pbm_edr2026]

The fee depended on the size of the penalty. If the penalty came in under $75 million, Epstein would receive $25 million. If it fell between $75 million and $150 million, he would receive $10 million. The money was due within three days of the settlement. [cite:ep7_pbm_edr2026] The penalty came in at $45.245 million, inside the band that paid the larger fee. [cite:ep7_doj_edr2015] CBS News reported two transfers to Epstein, and reporting citing the files puts them on 17 and 21 December 2015, either side of the settlement announcement. [cite:ep7_cbs_ruemmler2026] [cite:ep7_pbm_edr2026]

What the record shows, and what it does not

Three things are documented. A bank in a federal tax case hired a convicted sex offender's company. The fee was tied to how lenient the government's penalty would be. And the lawyer who negotiated that penalty for the bank had been introduced to the bank's chief by Epstein himself. That is a fee for an outcome in a government negotiation, routed through a man whose own federal case had ended in a non-prosecution agreement seven years earlier.

What the record does not show matters as much. Nothing released shows that Epstein contacted anyone at the Justice Department about the bank's case, or that the penalty was set because of him. For comparison, Pictet paid more than $120 million and Julius Baer $547 million in similar American cases. [cite:ep7_pbm_edr2026] Reuters, reviewing the correspondence, found no apparent evidence of criminal wrongdoing by Ariane de Rothschild. [cite:ep7_reuters_ariane2026] The bank says she knew nothing of his crimes until they became public. [cite:ep7_pbm_edr2026] A contingent fee is not a bribe, and an introduction is not a fix. The open question is what $25 million bought, and only the bank's internal records and Southern Trust's books can answer it.

Le Monde reported further details that this book has not been able to confirm against the documents: that the introduction ran through Terje Rød-Larsen and the bank's then secretary general, Olivier Colom; that Epstein arranged meetings between Ariane de Rothschild and Ehud Barak; that an offer from Leon Black's Apollo for the bank's asset-management arm was discussed internally in 2016; and that Epstein told Peter Thiel in February 2016, "I represent the Rothschilds." [cite:ep7_lemonde_ariane2026] The last is a record of what Epstein said, not proof that he held any such role. Each stays a lead until the underlying emails are read.

Other Rothschild names in the record

The same test applies to every other person with the surname, and to Maxwell. The released record contains no document showing Ghislaine Maxwell in contact with Ariane de Rothschild or her bank. The one other Rothschild who appears repeatedly is Lynn Forester de Rothschild, a businesswoman married into the British branch, and what the record shows about her is introductions, all of them other people's accounts. Alan Dershowitz has said she introduced him to Epstein. Maxwell reportedly told an interviewer in 2025 that she introduced Prince Andrew to him. Emails reported in February 2026 place Peter Mandelson's introduction to Epstein at a house the de Rothschilds owned on Martha's Vineyard. Epstein claimed he had helped her financially during her divorce in the 1990s; her spokesperson called that "one hundred percent false". [cite:ep7_wiki_lfdr] No document shows her paying Epstein, doing business with him, or knowing of his crimes, and no response from her to the introduction accounts has been found.

One name is often counted as a Rothschild link and is not one. In 2010 Epstein wrote that he would have Maxwell contact the British socialite Annabelle Neilson, who then wrote that she was putting "a little group of girls together" for him; the ages of the women are not stated in what has been reported. [cite:ep7_aol_neilson2026] Neilson died in 2018. Whatever she did, she did as herself, and no document connects it to any Rothschild. No document in the released record shows the French bank Rothschild & Co, or any other Rothschild-family bank, doing business with Epstein. The surname appears; the connections that hold up on paper are to one Swiss bank and to the social circle of one woman.
