The Patron

From 1949 the patron was the United States. No country has received more American aid since the Second World War: about $298 billion from 1946 to 2024, in 2024 dollars, by the Congressional Research Service's figures. [cite:dv4_crs_aid_2025] The current memorandum pledges $3.8 billion a year from 2019 to 2028, and from October 2023 to September 2025 American military aid to Israel came to $21.7 billion. [cite:fdd_mou_phaseout] [cite:aa_hartung_2025]

The vote follows the money. The United States had vetoed Security Council resolutions critical of Israel 45 times by December 2023. [cite:ccga_vetoes] At home, AIPAC and its super PAC, formed in January 2022, topped $100 million in election spending for a second cycle running in 2026; in the races studied, its advertising rarely mentioned Israel. [cite:pm_wiki_aipac] [cite:pm_sludge_100m_2026] [cite:punchbowl_udp_no_israel] Since 2019, executive orders have placed the IHRA definition of antisemitism, with its examples about Israel, inside American civil-rights enforcement. [cite:ow11_eo13899]

The Abraham Accords of 2020 belong here too: their documented drivers were fear of Iran and American rewards, among them recognition of Morocco's claim to Western Sahara, Sudan's removal from the terrorism list and an arms package for the Emirates. [cite:brit_abraham] [cite:w_abraham] Aid and Arms, The One-Way Mirror, Follow the Money and Washington tell this in full.
