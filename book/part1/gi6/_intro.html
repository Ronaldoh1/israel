Part Six follows the years, 2019 to 2026, in which formal annexation of West Bank land moved from a principle in party documents to a pledge with a date, a map and a bill. It begins with Netanyahu's election promise of September 2019 to annex the Jordan Valley, the Trump plan of January 2020 that endorsed Israeli sovereignty over about 30 per cent of the West Bank, and the suspension of that step in August 2020 as the price of normalization with the United Arab Emirates. It then traces how the coalition formed in 2022 moved West Bank civil government into the hands of Bezalel Smotrich, how he set a "year of sovereignty" and drew a map to annex 82 per cent of the territory, and how the Knesset voted on the principle and on two bills in 2025 while Washington and Abu Dhabi said annexation would not happen. It ends with the measures of 2026, land registration and the E1 tender, that changed the administration of the land without declaring sovereignty over it, and with where each stood a week before the election of 27 October 2026. The settlement record beneath these decisions is documented in The West Bank, Documented, to which this part links rather than repeats.
