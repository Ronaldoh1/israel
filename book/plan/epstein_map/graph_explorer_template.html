The Epstein Network 🕸️ Interactive Graph

Every documented tie is an edge, each with its source. Trust structures, art-finance arrangements, selected flight-log evidence and correspondence networks are mapped from DOJ trust agreements, entity schedules, partnership agreements, inventories, transaction emails, FBI interview records, court-filed JPMorgan email-review exhibits and DOJ Datasets 9–11 email and financial records. Email-derived links distinguish direct correspondence, scheduling, proposed arrangements and participant-reported financial assistance. Proposed arrangements and payment instructions are explicitly distinguished from completed transactions; roles do not imply criminal conduct. Expand outward from any node, walk inbound edges, find loops, trace a path, or remove a node to see what still connects.

Dataset 11 additions include financial correspondence, proposed unit purchases, a conditional helicopter deposit agreement, aircraft-service proposals and investment/contribution requests. “Reported”, “planned” and “to verify” are deliberately distinct from completed payments.
