- A book on the structural connections between US agencies and Israel, part by part: CIA, FBI, NSA (and Unit 8200), Homeland Security, the military branches, Palantir, Epstein, and others.
- How each works, how it benefits Israel, and the laws, bills, administrations and votes that put it in place over decades, including the role of pro-Israel candidates.

1. The Legal Spine: every law, MOU and executive action, with the administration, sponsors and vote.

9. Palantir and the Data Contractors.
10. Israeli Spyware and Security Firms in America: NSO, Paragon, Cellebrite, Elbit, Cognyte, Black Cube.

12. Epstein: What Is Documented.
13. The One-Way Mirror: Israeli operations against the US; what US oversight cannot see; why it is rarely reported.
14. Settled, Contested and Open.

## Discipline
- Every connection is a documented relationship (law, contract, MOU, funding, appointment), never inferred from identity. No collective attribution to Jews or any people.
- Each part gives the official purpose stated by both governments beside the critical finding.
- Benefits to the US (intelligence, technology, missile defense) are stated where documented, so the "one-way" claim is tested, not assumed.
- Epstein: only court records and named-outlet reporting; contested intelligence claims labelled as such.
