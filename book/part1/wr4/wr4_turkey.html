Turkey: Partner to Rival

Herzl sought a charter for Palestine from the Ottoman Sultan from 1896, offering help with the empire's debts; his diary records the Sultan's reply that he could not sell even a foot of the land, because it belonged to his people. [cite:w_herzl] [cite:w_abdulhamid] The Ottoman government restricted Jewish immigration and land purchase from the 1880s, unevenly. [cite:w_ottoman_land_code] The Republic of Turkey, founded in 1923, gave up all claims to the Arab provinces in the Treaty of Lausanne. [cite:w_lausanne]

Partners

Turkey recognised Israel in March 1949, the first Muslim-majority state to do so. In 1958 it joined a secret intelligence pact with Israel and Iran, and in 1996 the two signed military and defence-industry agreements: Israeli pilots trained in Turkish airspace and Israeli firms upgraded Turkish aircraft and tanks. [cite:w_isr_tur]

The break

After the Gaza war of 2008–09, Prime Minister Erdoğan confronted Shimon Peres at Davos and walked off the stage. On 31 May 2010 Israeli commandos boarded the Mavi Marmara, leading a flotilla trying to break the Gaza blockade; nine activists were killed, one of them a Turkish-American, and a tenth died later. A UN panel found the blockade legal but the force "excessive and unreasonable", and criticised the organisers. [cite:w_flotilla] Israel apologised in 2013 and paid $20 million in 2016; ambassadors returned, were expelled again in 2018, and returned in 2022. [cite:w_isr_tur] [cite:w_torunlar]

Turkey has never listed Hamas as a terrorist group, and Hamas leaders have operated from its territory. [cite:w_tur_hamas] It recalled its ambassador in October 2023, suspended trade in 2024, joined South Africa's genocide case at the ICJ and later closed its ports and airspace to Israel. [cite:w_torunlar] [cite:khaleej_warrants] [cite:almon_warrants] Azerbaijani oil bound for Israel kept flowing through Turkey, and allegations surfaced of trade by businesses tied to Erdoğan's family. [cite:wilson_tur_isr] On 7 November 2025 Istanbul's chief prosecutor announced arrest warrants for 37 Israelis, including Netanyahu; Israel rejected them and noted that the same office had detained Erdoğan's main rival, Istanbul's mayor. [cite:almon_warrants] [cite:tribune_warrants]

Rivals in Syria

Turkey backs the government that replaced Assad; Israel holds a buffer zone in southern Syria and opposes a Turkish military presence there. Pro-Israel analysts describe Turkey as building a "ring of fire" around Israel. [cite:mosaic_ring_fire] [cite:national_sufa53] In June 2026 Israel's government moved to recognise the Armenian genocide, a long-standing grievance in Ankara, and Netanyahu urged Washington not to sell F-35s to Turkey. [cite:wr_majalla_tur_2026] Status as of 26 November 2026: relations exist on paper, without ambassadors, trade or open airspace, and the two states are rivals in Syria; no restoration has been reported.
