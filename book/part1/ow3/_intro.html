Part Three follows the Central Intelligence Agency's relationship with Israel's services from its beginning in the early 1950s to the summer of 2026. It shows how one officer, James Angleton, came to hold the Israel account inside the CIA's counterintelligence staff, and what passed through that channel, from Khrushchev's secret speech to captured Soviet equipment. It then sets out the CIA's own record of concern: the uranium missing from NUMEC, a 1979 report on Israel's intelligence services, and the damage assessment after the Pollard arrest. Finally it follows the partnership into Wye River, the cyberattack on Iran's Natanz plant, and the hostage negotiations after 7 October 2023, and asks what the CIA can and cannot see of what Israel does with what it receives.
