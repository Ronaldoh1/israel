This part gathers the evidence that fits the book's worst-case reading least. The first section sets out, by date and in each side's own words, the public splits among the people this book maps: over skilled-worker visas, tariffs, money, the government's direction and the theory itself. The second collects the claims about the network that circulate and fail against the record, graded on the same marks used throughout, and applies the same test to the network's own claims. The third lists what was looked for and is not there, marks each absence as strong or open, and says what would change it, before the part closes with the worst-case reading, tested.
