Pakistan: The Unrecognised Relationship

Since 1947 Pakistan's passport has carried the line "valid for all countries of the world except Israel". In October 2025 an Indian channel claimed the clause had been removed; three Pakistani ministries said within a day that it had not, and the foreign ministry added that Pakistan "has never recognised Israel". [cite:wiki_pakistani_passport] [cite:dawn_no_change_passport_validity] [cite:dawn_pakistan_denies_troops_gaza] Bangladesh did remove its equivalent clause in 2021, without lifting its travel ban, and restored it on 13 April 2025, the day after a mass March for Gaza. [cite:wiki_bangladesh_israel_relations] [cite:wiki_march_for_gaza]

Pilots and a brigadier

Pakistani pilots flew for Arab air forces. Saiful Azam, an adviser to Jordan's air force, is credited with downing Israeli aircraft in June 1967 flying for Jordan and then for Iraq, though other pilots in the same fights were also credited, and Pakistan's broader claim of about ten kills has not been independently confirmed. [cite:paradigmshift_azam_alvi] [cite:aviationgeekclub_saiful_azam_obituary] In April 1974 Sattar Alvi, flying a Syrian MiG-21, shot down an Israeli Mirage. [cite:wiki_sattar_alvi] Brigadier Muhammad Zia-ul-Haq, later Pakistan's ruler, headed its training mission in Jordan and took command of a Jordanian division in the crackdown on Palestinian fighters in September 1970; Moshe Dayan later stated that King Hussein sent in his army "with help from Zia-ul-Haq". Thousands of Palestinians were killed; the numbers given vary widely. [cite:wiki_battle_of_amman_1970] [cite:foreignexchanges_black_september_1970]

Secret contacts

In the early 1980s, by several accounts, Zia allowed Pakistan's intelligence service to open a liaison channel with Mossad during the war in Afghanistan. [cite:timesofisrael_blog_secret_diplomacy] [cite:infogalactic_israel_pakistan_relations] In 2005 Foreign Minister Khurshid Kasuri said on the record that the two countries had held secret contacts for more than ten years. [cite:tribuneindia_kasuri_secret_links] A 2020 report that a Pakistani adviser had met Mossad's chief in Tel Aviv was denied and was not confirmed. [cite:pressreader_jc_bukhari_fake_news]

The bomb

Declassified American cables show that India's air force studied a strike on Pakistan's Kahuta enrichment plant in 1981, concluding it could destroy the site at the risk of full war. [cite:brookings_iaf_kahuta_feasibility] A former CIA officer said in 2025 that he had heard of a proposed joint Indian–Israeli strike that Indira Gandhi declined; he said he was not involved and that it never happened, so it stands as a second-hand account. [cite:tribuneindia_barlow_kahuta_shame] [cite:jpost_indira_refused_kahuta] Pakistan's nuclear scientist A. Q. Khan later supplied enrichment technology to Iran, Libya and North Korea. [cite:britannica_aq_khan] [cite:middleeasteye_islamic_bomb_iran]

In 2026 Pakistan's defence minister rejected President Trump's call to join the Abraham Accords, saying Pakistan's position remained tied to a Palestinian state with East Jerusalem as its capital; Senator Lindsey Graham called Pakistan's role as a mediator with Iran "more than problematic".
