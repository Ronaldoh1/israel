What Is Counted, and How

The figure most often quoted for American aid to Israel is the Congressional Research Service's: about $174 billion from 1946 to 2025, in the dollars of each year. [cite:tal_crs2025_web] It is accurate, and it understates what the aid is worth today, because a dollar appropriated in 1974 bought about seven times what a dollar buys in 2026. A total that adds 1974 dollars to 2026 dollars adds unlike things. This part converts each year separately and then adds the years together.

What goes into the total

The total counts money the United States government appropriated or lent for Israel, year by year. The backbone is the year table the Congressional Research Service has kept since 1949, as published by the Jewish Virtual Library, which divides each year into military aid, other defence funding such as missile defence, economic aid, money for resettling immigrants, grants to schools and hospitals, and everything else. Its columns add to the published totals to the cent. [cite:us1_jvl_aidtable] [cite:tal_crs2025_web] Three kinds of federal money that sit outside that table are added: the anti-tunnel and counter-drone programmes funded by the Defense Department from FY2020 to FY2025; two lines of the April 2024 supplemental, $4.4 billion to replace American weapons sent to Israel from American stocks and $801.4 million for ammunition; and the appropriations for FY2026, passed by Congress on 3 February 2026. [cite:us1_jvl_aidtable] [cite:aa_hartung_2025] [cite:tal_wh_hr7148]

The first entry is a $100 million loan from the Export-Import Bank in 1949. The Research Service states its totals from 1946, but its year tables begin with FY1949, the first year with any aid to Israel, and the searches made for this part found no American government money to the Jewish community of Palestine before the state was declared on 14 May 1948. [cite:us1_jvl_aidtable] [cite:tal_crs2006] Private American money did flow before 1948, and it is shown in the last section of this part, beside the total.

How a year becomes today's dollars

Each year is converted with the consumer price index for all urban consumers, the measure the Bureau of Labor Statistics publishes every month and the one behind ordinary inflation calculators. Aid is appropriated by fiscal year, which runs from October to September, so each fiscal year's prices are taken as one quarter of the previous calendar year's average and three quarters of its own. Today's dollars are October 2026 dollars, the latest month the Bureau has published: an index of 337.511. [cite:rf_bls_cpi_oct2026] [cite:tal_mpls_cpi] [cite:tal_fred_cpi] A year's multiplier is that index divided by the year's own. For FY1974 it is about 7.0; for FY1985, 3.2; for FY2003, 1.8; for FY2025, 1.06.

Loans and grants

Until 1985 part of the aid came as loans. The Congressional Research Service's table of 2006 splits each year into loans and grants: the loans inside the total come to about $12.8 billion in the dollars of the time, almost all of it military loans between 1959 and 1984 and economic loans between 1955 and 1980. [cite:tal_crs2006] Since FY1985 all of the military aid has been grants. The Research Service reported in 2025 that, as of September 2024, Israel no longer owed any sovereign debt to the United States government. [cite:tal_crs2025_web] A loan that is repaid is not a gift, so this part gives two totals: everything, about $425 billion in today's dollars, and grants alone, about $356 billion.

By kind of aid

Military financing is the largest part by far: about $272 billion in today's dollars. Economic aid, which began in 1951 and shrank to small sums after the last bilateral economic grants of FY2008, comes to about $102 billion; missile defence and the other defence programmes to about $22.5 billion; the weapons replaced and ammunition bought in 2024 to about $5.6 billion; resettlement of immigrants to about $4.6 billion; and the remaining programmes, among them the Export-Import Bank loans and food aid of the early years, to about $18 billion. [cite:us1_jvl_aidtable] [cite:aa_hartung_2025]

The full table, every year with its multiplier and every category, can be downloaded as a spreadsheet at www.1948scam.com/data/aid-tally.xlsx; its conversions are written as formulas, so a reader can check each one or change the base month.
