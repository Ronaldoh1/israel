## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency:** the Gaza demonstrations of March 1955, organized jointly by communists and the Muslim Brotherhood, with their own demands (end resettlement in Sinai; arms for the camps; civil freedoms) and the Egyptian crackdown (cw2_gaza_raid); the fedayeen as Egyptian-organized Palestinian units; Haj Amin al-Husseini at Bandung (cw2_baghdad_bandung); the Arab riparian states' Technical Committee in the Johnston talks.
- **The strongest Zionist/Israeli case:** the Yehud killings and the MAC's own "intolerable aggression" finding before Qibya; the border incidents and the killing before the Gaza raid (Defense Ministry archive); Eban's argument that the Czech deal created an imbalance Israel had to answer; Sharett's plea about Egypt's jets; Syrian fire on fishermen before Kinneret; the activists' argument that no US guarantee came without a ban on retaliation.
- **Arab states' strongest case:** Egypt's account of the Gaza raid to the Council (S/3365); Nasser's 1954 case that Britain had cut off arms; Egypt's sovereignty over the Canal and the population argument for the dam; the Arab reasons for refusing the Johnston plan (recognition by implication; Negev settlement).
- **Jewish dissent:** Sharett's opposition to the activist course inside Israel's own government; the Egyptian Jewish operatives as the people who paid for Susannah; American Jewish dissent continues from cw1 (no new material).
- **Jews of the Arab world:** the Egyptian Jews recruited for Susannah, their trial and executions; the community's fate linked to s00e2 and Stage Three.
- **Real antisemitism named plainly:** none newly documented in this stage's sources; the stage keeps the Susannah case tied to named Israeli and Egyptian state actors and does not generalize to Egyptian Jews or to Egyptians.
- **Palestinian citizens of Israel:** the regime itself is rd6's; Kafr Qasim is cw3's. Stated as out of scope here, with links.
- **Terms examined:** "retaliation" and "reprisal" (as Israel used them, as the Council judged them); "infiltration" (link rd6); "fedayeen"; "false flag" (lv_s01's characterization, not adopted as a finding); "non-belligerence in lieu of peace" (Alpha); "national control" of oil (ira_s00b vs the consortium's structure).
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** inside Israel, Sharett vs Ben-Gurion and Dayan; inside Washington, Dulles vs Congress on Aswan (Bowie, Wilkins), the State Department's refusal of arms while allowing France to sell; inside Egypt, Nasser vs Naguib and the Brotherhood; Britain's base interests vs Egypt's sovereignty; Iraqi Hashemite aims vs Egyptian opposition to the Pact.
- **Money and alignment:** the $26 million released only after Israel suspended the Banat Yaacov works; Johnston's offer of two-thirds of development costs; the Aswan offer ($56 million at once; $1.3 billion total) withdrawn; cotton and rice paying for Czech arms; the consortium's 40/40/14/6 split and the £25 million compensation; where money did not follow alignment (no US arms to Israel despite the Czech deal).
- **Sources kept independent:** Qibya on the Council's resolution, the State Department, Sharett's diary (via the Journal of Palestine Studies) and the Israeli Jewish Virtual Library; the Gaza raid on Israel's Defense Ministry archive, Egypt's UN letter and PalQuest; Susannah on Israel's Defense Ministry archive, Ynet and an Egyptian-sourced Arab outlet; the Aswan decision on FRUS and four separate oral histories.
