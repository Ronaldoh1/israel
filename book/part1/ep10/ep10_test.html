The Test: Dead as People, as Money, or as Protection?

The question for this part can be put the way investigators put it about a cartel: when the man at the top is taken out, does someone else step up? The record allows a precise answer, layer by layer.

What the map shows

The companion map makes the same point from a different direction. Remove Epstein and Maxwell from it, and most of the documented structure still holds together as one connected piece: the four protected staff, through the 2008 agreement; the estate's managers, through his Virgin Islands company; the apartment building on East 66th Street; the Wexner money; and, since the banks' own decision-makers were added, JPMorgan, Deutsche Bank and Bank of America, joined to one another by the banker who carried Epstein's account between them, and the Swiss bank Edmond de Rothschild, joined through the Justice Department that settled with it. [cite:ep3_ta_morris2026] [cite:ep7_doj_edr2015] The part that falls away into a separate island is Leon Black's money, which reached Epstein alone; only an allegation in the Bank of America suit, settled without admission, would tie it back in. [cite:ep3_cj_boa2026] In other words, the operational core and its legal shield survive the removal of the man at the centre. The patrons and banks stay connected to each other, but only through the people who worked for or around him: his assistant, his bankers and the agreement that protected his staff.

What would show it is still running

"The network did not shut down" is documented: its people were never prosecuted, its protection still stands, and its money is still managed by the same hands. "The network is still operating" is not documented, and the book does not say it. What would establish it is specific: a payment or transfer after 2019 tied to the old operation; a named recruiter still active; a property still in use for the same purpose; or a sworn account of abuse after 2019 by someone from Epstein's circle. None of those appears in the released record. Each is exactly the kind of thing the withheld files, the New Mexico commission's final report and the Paris investigation could still turn up.

The answer to the cartel question is therefore this. No successor has been identified, and no one with authority has looked for one. The structure that would let a successor operate, unprosecuted people, an immunity no court has withdrawn and a justice system that has declined to investigate further, is still in place.
