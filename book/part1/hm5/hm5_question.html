Would Hamas Be What It Is?

No document answers this question, and the volume does not pretend to. What follows are the two readings the record supports, each labelled as an argument.

The first reading: the split and the separation made Hamas what it became. On this reading, Israel had cut Gaza off from the West Bank for years before 2007; after Hamas won the only election it contested, Israel, the United States and Europe cut its government off, and a US-backed plan to remove it by force was reported; and from 2018 Israeli governments let Qatari cash into Gaza on the reasoning, stated by Smotrich and reported of Netanyahu, that a strong Hamas kept the Palestinian Authority weak and a Palestinian state off the table. A Hamas ruling a sealed strip, funded with Israel's consent and facing no election, was the product. The Shin Bet's own summary named the quiet and the Qatari money among the conditions for October 7. [cite:hm_aj_separation] [cite:hm_dn_rose] [cite:wb_wiki_smotrich] [cite:hm_fdd_shinbet]

The second reading: Hamas made its own choices. On this reading, Hamas took Gaza by force in 2007, refused the conditions that would have ended its isolation, chose rockets and tunnels over the economy of the strip, and planned October 7 itself; Israel's separation measures answered real attacks, and the Qatari money, which Israel says it let in to prevent a humanitarian collapse, did not decide what Hamas did with its own budget. Its charter, its ideology and its armed wing predate every one of these policies.
