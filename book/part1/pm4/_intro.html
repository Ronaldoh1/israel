Part Four names the recipients. It sets out the career totals of the Congress sitting in 2026, by chamber and party, from the compilation of FEC data by the advocacy group Track AIPAC, with the cautions that compilation requires; then the cycle-by-cycle record since 2016, from the one published ranked list, for 2018. It keeps money given to a member apart from money spent on a member's behalf, and it does not treat any total as an explanation of a vote.
