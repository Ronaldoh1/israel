Part Three set the totals out. From 1990 to 2020 pro-Israel PACs gave between about $2 million and $9 million a cycle, and in 2018, the fullest pre-2021 cycle, pro-Israel donors and groups spent just over $22 million on contributions and lobbying together, most of it to Democrats. [cite:pm_jvl_pacs2020] [cite:pm_guardian_2019] After 2021 AIPAC's two committees alone spent $44 million in 2022 and $95.1 million in 2024 on OpenSecrets' count, and more than $100 million in the 2026 cycle by August; other pro-Israel committees grew too, the Republican Jewish Coalition's fourfold. [cite:pm_israelite_2024] [cite:pm_sludge_100m_2026]

What is settled is the growth, from tens of millions to more than a hundred million a cycle, and its shift toward Republican candidates in general elections and against progressive Democrats in primaries. What is contested is the right total, which depends on what is counted, and how to treat large partisan donors such as the Adelsons, whom this volume keeps separate. What the record leaves open is the full per-cycle list of recipients, which Part Four takes up.
