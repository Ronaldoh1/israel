# Volume plan (DRAFT, 28 Oct 2026): Democracy Hasn't Been Tried (working; alt. The Unfinished Republic)
Status: research only. Ron reviews the outline before any writing.
Purpose: the counter-argument to Thiel/Yarvin's "democracy failed" claim — the US system is not a full democracy (Electoral College, primaries, money); test whether finishing democracy (popular-vote mechanisms, ranked-choice voting, money out) answers the critique better than a CEO-sovereign. Companion to The Dark Enlightenment Architecture (link de7_the_other_diagnosis, de8_*, de35b_*).
Subject type: NARRATIVE/MYTH ("we live in a democracy" as taught) + INSTRUMENT (Electoral College, RCV laws, Citizens United). Same standard for every party and lobby.
Discipline notes: split "the EC failed in 2016" into the documentable design claim (Federalist 68's screening role vs bound electors, Chiafalo v. Washington 2020; popular-vote/EC splits) and the contested outcome judgment (measures + both readings); money: every major spender on the same footing (AIPAC/UDP, Fairshake, America PAC, unions, billionaires both sides, corporate); free speech: the state-action doctrine (First Amendment binds government, not platforms), Moody v. NetChoice 2024 (platforms' editorial rights), the corporate-speech asymmetry (spend freely, moderate freely), documented moderation of Palestine-related content (HRW 2023, Meta-commissioned BSR 2022) and of other viewpoints (both directions), government pressure on platforms (Murthy v. Missouri 2024, NRA v. Vullo 2024); overturning Citizens United requires an amendment or a reversal.
Draft parts: 1 The Claim; 2 What We Actually Have (EC architecture); 3 Who Really Chooses (primaries, gerrymanders, turnout, two-party trap); 4 The Money (Citizens United → super PACs → dark money; who spends; what money buys); 5 Free Speech for Whom (corporate speech vs user speech; platforms; government pressure); 6 Ranked-Choice Voting (architecture, NYC/Maine/Alaska, bans, critiques); 7 The Other Fixes (NPV compact, proportional representation, public financing, disclosure, amendments); 8 The Test (Thiel's argument under the same standard); 9 What It Would Take.
