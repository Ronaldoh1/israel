What the Record Settles

The dead are many, and most are named. Gaza's health ministry counted 74,382 dead by October 2026, with names and ID numbers for most of them; an independent check of its early lists found them largely reliable, and an Israeli military official put the toll at about 70,000 in January 2026. [cite:gz_aj_year2026] [cite:pr_airwars_lists] [cite:pr_toi_idf70k]

The count is more likely too low than too high. A household survey estimated about 75,200 violent deaths in the war's first fifteen months, when about 49,000 had been recorded, and about 16,300 non-violent deaths in the same period; Al Jazeera reported about 10,000 people missing under the rubble in April 2026. [cite:pr_almon_survey2026] [cite:pr_aj_rubble2026]

Children, women and the unborn bore much of it. The UN commission counted at least 20,179 children killed in two years; UN Women, more than 38,000 women and girls by the end of 2025; the ministry recorded about 4,000 pregnancies lost in the first half of 2026 and 466 deaths from malnutrition and starvation since 2024, 157 of them children, by 16 October 2026; an Israeli military official rejected the ministry's malnutrition-death figures. [cite:pr_nw_coi2026] [cite:pr_mm_women2026] [cite:pr_memo_starv2026] [cite:pr_jw_idf71k]

Journalists were killed in great numbers. The Committee to Protect Journalists counted 189 killed in Gaza as of 24 September 2026, after removing those it found had engaged in combat; the UN counted 242 Palestinian journalists by 11 August 2025. In 2025, the Committee's record year worldwide, it held Israel responsible for about two-thirds. Israel says it has never deliberately targeted journalists and that some of the dead were militants. [cite:gz_cpj_2026] [cite:pr_wiki_killing] [cite:pr_cpj_2025report] [cite:pr_idf_cpj2025]

The foreign press was kept out. Three years into the war, Israel had not allowed foreign journalists to enter Gaza independently; it permitted only escorted visits with its army. [cite:pr_wlrn_ban_full]
