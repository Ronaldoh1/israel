The International Court of Justice has not ruled on whether Israel committed genocide; South Africa's case, filed on December 29, 2023, is still pending. [cite:icj_report_un] What the court did, on January 26, 2024, was issue provisional measures: it found that Palestinians' rights under the Genocide Convention were plausibly at risk of irreparable harm and ordered Israel to prevent genocidal acts and allow aid. That is an emergency order, not a verdict, and it did not order a ceasefire. [cite:mee_icj_explainer] Separately, the International Criminal Court's arrest warrants of November 21, 2024 for Benjamin Netanyahu and Yoav Gallant charge war crimes and crimes against humanity, not genocide. [cite:justiceinfo_gaza]

The line blurs courts with other bodies. Amnesty International and Human Rights Watch (December 2024), the Israeli groups B'Tselem and Physicians for Human Rights–Israel (July 2025) and a UN Commission of Inquiry (September 2025) have concluded genocide or acts of genocide. Israel rejects all of these findings. [cite:jta_un_coi_genocide] [cite:ips_amnesty_hrw_genocide]
