No country has received more American aid since the Second World War than Israel. This volume follows that money: how it began, the terms on which it is given, where it is spent, what the war since October 2023 added to it, the laws that are meant to govern it and how they were applied, and the negotiation, under way as this is written, over what replaces it after 2028.

It keeps three things apart throughout. The amount appropriated by Congress is one thing; the arms Israel buys with its own money, or with that aid, are another; and the cost of American forces sent to the region is a third. Each is given its own figure and its own source. The One-Way Mirror on this shelf follows the weapons pipeline itself, the steps from request to delivery and the co-production of missile defence; this volume follows the money and the law.
