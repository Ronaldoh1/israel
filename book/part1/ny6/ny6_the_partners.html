The Partners, 1994–2026

Kach, the party of the American-born rabbi Meir Kahane, called for the expulsion of Arabs from the land; it was barred from elections in 1988, and after a Kach supporter, Baruch Goldstein, killed 29 Palestinian worshippers in Hebron in February 1994, Israel outlawed the movement as a terrorist organisation and the United States later designated it one too. [cite:gi11_wiki_kach]

Itamar Ben-Gvir, born in 1976, was a youth coordinator in Kach. His record as a teenage provocateur kept him out of the army; he was indicted dozens of times and convicted of at least eight charges, among them, in 2007, incitement to racism and support for a terrorist organisation. He became a lawyer who defended Jewish extremists and, for years, kept a portrait of Goldstein on his wall, taking it down before the 2020 election. [cite:bgv_wiki_bgvir]

Into the Knesset and the cabinet

Netanyahu needed every vote on the right. In 2020 he pressed Naftali Bennett to take Ben-Gvir's party onto a joint list, and Bennett refused. [cite:hm_toi_goldstein2020] In 2021 Ben-Gvir entered the Knesset on a combined list with Bezalel Smotrich's Religious Zionist Party, and in 2022 the joint list that Netanyahu helped arrange won 14 seats, the third-largest bloc in the Knesset. [cite:bgv_wiki_bgvir]

The coalition sworn in on 29 December 2022 opened its guidelines with the statement that "the Jewish people have an exclusive and unquestionable right to all parts of the Land of Israel", including the West Bank. [cite:axios_israel_far_right_government] Ben-Gvir became minister of national security, in charge of the police and the prisons; Smotrich became finance minister and also a minister in the Defence Ministry with authority over the Civil Administration, the body that governs planning and building in the occupied West Bank. [cite:bgv_wiki_bgvir] [cite:smotrich_planning_authority]

What they did with it

As minister, Ben-Gvir went up to the Temple Mount compound on 3 January 2023 and on later occasions, drawing international criticism; he said that one of his top goals was to worsen conditions for security prisoners. [cite:bgv_wiki_bgvir] [cite:bgv_aljaz_prison] On 30 March 2026 the Knesset passed a law imposing the death penalty by hanging for terrorist murder, a measure he had championed. [cite:us4_ap_deathlaw] On 8 February 2026 the security cabinet, on proposals by Smotrich and Defence Minister Israel Katz, opened the West Bank land registry, repealed the Jordanian-era ban on land sales to non-Arabs and moved planning authority in Hebron to Israeli bodies; Smotrich's office said "we will continue to bury the idea of a Palestinian state". [cite:dv5_ynet_wb_feb2026]

On 10 June 2025 Britain, Canada, Australia, New Zealand and Norway imposed travel bans and asset freezes on both men for inciting violence against Palestinians; Slovenia, the Netherlands and Spain followed. The United States did not. [cite:bgv_wiki_bgvir]

The veto

The partners' power lay in arithmetic. In January 2025 Ben-Gvir said that "through our political strength" his party had prevented a hostage-and-ceasefire deal "time and again" over the previous year. [cite:bgv_toi_foiled] When the January 2025 ceasefire was signed he resigned, on 19 January; when Israel resumed full-scale strikes on Gaza on 18 March, his party announced its return the same day. [cite:bgv_wiki_bgvir] [cite:bgv_aljaz_return] The Hamas Architecture follows those ceasefires in detail. Netanyahu's answer to the charge that his partners led him was the same throughout: "They are joining me. I'm not joining them." [cite:npr_netanyahu_far_right_allies]
