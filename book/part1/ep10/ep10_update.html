Update: November 2026

The tests above were set against the record as of October 2026. Several things have moved since, and each belongs on one side of the question or the other.

On the side of "never dismantled". On 10 November 2026 the Republican majority of the House Oversight Committee published a 137-page report after a fifteen-month inquiry and nineteen interviews. It found that abuse from 2005 onward could have been prevented but for failures by federal prosecutors in South Florida and Washington, faulted the "hands-off" approach of Alexander Acosta and the possible conflicts of interest of two former prosecutors, criticised banks that missed signs of trafficking and immigration fraud, and recounted the lapses at the New York jail on the night Epstein died. It recommended that the Justice Department re-interview witnesses and investigate his associates. Democrats, who take control of the committee in January, called it partisan and incomplete; the department said the recommendations had already been addressed, and Attorney General Todd Blanche said the case was not closed. [cite:ep10_gpb_oversight] [cite:ep10_aj_oversight] In a May 2026 interview with the committee, his former assistant Sarah Kellen named further men, among them the photographer Patrick Demarchelier, who died in 2022, and the hairstylist Frédéric Fekkai, who denies any assault; her allegations against two living men were referred to the Justice Department, and no one has been charged. [cite:ep10_keyt_kellen] Maxwell has applied to have her twenty-year sentence shortened; no decision has been reported. [cite:ep10_nbc_commutation] In Britain, the prosecutors' chief has said the investigations of Andrew and Mandelson, both arrested in February 2026 and not charged, may take more than a year. On the side of "not still operating". In April 2026 the Justice Department told NPR it had no credible evidence that his activities extended to a wider network, and there has been no arrest in the United States since the files were released. [cite:ep10_npr_arrests] Maxwell remains the only associate convicted.

Both statements stand side by side as of 20 November 2026. What would change the second is the open inquiries: New Mexico's commission, whose final report is due on 31 December 2026, the British investigations, and any reinvestigation the committee's recommendation produces.
