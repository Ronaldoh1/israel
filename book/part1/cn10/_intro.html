Part Ten turns from who held the book to what the book holds. The nine parts before it followed the people who chose, guarded and banned the scriptures. This part reads a handful of passages that the inner traditions have always read differently: the temple that is the body, the place of the skull, the face of God, the single eye, the serpent lifted on a pole, and the Christ who is said to live within. All of it is a reading, offered for readers who find these traditions illuminating. Each section starts from the passage itself, quoted and sourced, then says what the reading proposes and what the record does not show.
