Part Nine follows the paper. For most of the time this book covers, nearly everything the public knew about Epstein came from places other than the government: victims' lawyers, one newspaper, and judges asked to unseal what others had filed. Then Congress, by a vote of 427 to 1, ordered the Justice Department to release what it held. This part traces how the record was built before that law, what the arrest in 2019 found and what the death in custody ended, what the law required, what the department released, blacked out, exposed and withheld, and what its leaders did, in order, while the release was under way.

It asks one question at the end: what is actually blocking release? The answer is in the documents, and it is more specific than either "nothing" or "a cover-up".
