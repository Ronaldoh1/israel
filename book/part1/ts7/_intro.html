Part Seven follows the end-times system from the study Bible into the institutions that taught it and the media that sold it. It begins with the Bible institutes and seminaries, Moody Bible Institute in Chicago and Dallas Theological Seminary above all, which trained pastors, missionaries and writers in a dispensational reading of prophecy. It then turns to the mass market: Hal Lindsey's The Late Great Planet Earth in 1970, the Left Behind novels from 1995 to 2007, and the religious broadcasters, Pat Robertson's Christian Broadcasting Network among them, who carried prophecy and Israel to television audiences. The last section follows the system abroad, to the Protestant and Pentecostal churches of Latin America and Africa, and to the governments of Guatemala, Paraguay and Brazil when they had to decide where to put an embassy. Throughout, influence is measured only by what can be counted: enrolment, copies sold, census and survey shares, and dated decisions.
