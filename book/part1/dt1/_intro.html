Part One sets out the claim this book tests and grants what is true in it. It quotes Peter Thiel, Curtis Yarvin and Nick Land on democracy in their own words and dates, from Thiel's 2009 essay to Yarvin's interviews in 2025, and draws out the single inference their arguments share: that the United States is a democracy, that it governs badly, and that democracy is therefore the problem. It then sets that inference against the record on trust, on the democracy indices, on whose preferences government follows and on money, and states the question the rest of the book pursues: whether the failure belongs to democracy or to a system that was never made fully democratic.
