Part Two established that the two states are built on opposite principles. The United States has one constitution that Congress cannot change alone, 27 amendments in more than two centuries and fifty states with reserved powers; Israel has fourteen Basic Laws as of October 2026, the latest passed on 13 July 2026 by 63 to 52, most of them amendable by a simple Knesset majority, and a unitary system in which local budgets need the Interior Ministry's approval.
