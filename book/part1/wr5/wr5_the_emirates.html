The Emirates

On 19 January 2010 Mahmoud al-Mabhouh, a Hamas commander, was killed in a Dubai hotel; Dubai police published footage of a team of more than two dozen travelling on forged or misused European and Australian passports and blamed Mossad, which Israel neither confirmed nor denied. [cite:w_mabhouh] Quiet contacts grew anyway, and in 2015 Israel opened a mission to a UN energy agency in Abu Dhabi. [cite:w_isr_uae]

The Accords

Normalisation was announced on 13 August 2020 and signed at the White House on 15 September, with Bahrain. [cite:w_abraham] The Emirates presented the suspension of Israel's plan to annex West Bank land as its condition; Israeli officials later said that commitment was time-limited. [cite:dayan_accords] [cite:axios_redline] Washington then announced a package of about $23 billion in arms for the Emirates, including F-35s, despite Israel's long opposition to F-35s for Arab states; in December 2021 the Emirates suspended the talks over American conditions. [cite:w_abraham] [cite:w_isr_uae] Israeli arms flowed the other way: Bahrain and the Emirates bought nearly $900 million worth in 2021. [cite:fp_accords_conflict]

In 2016 researchers documented an attempt to infect the phone of the Emirati human-rights activist Ahmed Mansoor with NSO Group's Pegasus spyware, the case that first exposed it; Mansoor was sentenced in 2018 to ten years in prison. [cite:w_mansoor] The Pegasus Project of 2021 listed the Emirates among suspected clients. [cite:w_pegasus_project]

Through two wars

The two states signed a free-trade agreement in 2022, and goods trade passed $3.2 billion in 2024. After 7 October the Emirates condemned Hamas's attack and kept flights to Israel running when nearly every other carrier stopped. [cite:ac_accords5] It criticised Israel's conduct in Gaza and used the relationship to coordinate aid drops. [cite:cnn_redline] On 3 September 2025 its envoy Lana Nusseibeh said annexation of the West Bank would be "a red line" that would end regional integration; Israeli sources said the sovereignty discussion was then taken off the cabinet's agenda. [cite:cnn_redline] [cite:newsonair_agenda]

From 28 February 2026 Iran fired missiles and drones at American bases in the Emirates; by 1 April the Emirati defence ministry counted 438 ballistic missiles, 2,012 drones and 19 cruise missiles. [cite:w_uae_iranwar] Israel deployed an Iron Dome battery and several dozen troops to the Emirates, the system's first operational use outside Israel and the United States. [cite:newarab_irondome] [cite:iranwire_irondome] The Wall Street Journal reported Emirati strikes on Iran coordinated with Israel and the United States. [cite:aaj_closer]
