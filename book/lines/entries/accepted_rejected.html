The first half of the line is accurate; the second half compresses the sequence. When the UN General Assembly adopted Resolution 181 on November 29, 1947, by 33 votes to 13 with 10 abstentions, the Jewish Agency accepted it, with real reservations about its borders. The Arab Higher Committee and the Arab League rejected it entirely and vowed armed resistance. [cite:wiki_un_partition_plan] [cite:palquest_un_partition_1947] The Arab majority of Mandate Palestine, about 70% of the population, had no vote on the plan, which gave the Jewish state about 55% of the territory. [cite:britannica_res181]

The war did not begin with a single Arab attack. Violence escalated from December 1, 1947, as a civil war inside Mandate Palestine, with documented attacks from both sides: a car bomb in Jaffa killed 26 people in early January 1948, and Haganah and Palmach forces attacked the village of Qisarya on February 15, 1948. [cite:explaininghistory_un_partition] Neighboring Arab armies invaded on May 15, 1948, five and a half months later, the day after Israel declared independence.
