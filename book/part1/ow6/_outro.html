Part Six found the homeland-security ties to be real but thin in law. The founding DHS–Israel memorandum, signed by Michael Chertoff and Avi Dichter in February 2007, is expressly non-binding and authorizes no classified sharing of its own; Congress added a research authority in 2014, widened it in 2016, and in December 2021 authorized at least $6 million a year in joint cybersecurity grants through fiscal 2026. [cite:ow6_jvl_dhsmou] [cite:ow6_lii_8606] The police exchanges are private programmes run by GILEE, JINSA and the ADL, partly paid for with DHS grant money; their critics' strongest claims about tactics are unsupported, while their influence on surveillance is reported but not documented, and Durham, Vermont's state police and Northampton stepped away. [cite:ow6_reason_cops] [cite:ow6_aj_durham] [cite:ow6_toi_newengland] The NYPD has kept an officer in Israel since 2007, inside an Israeli police headquarters since 2012, with private funding, and ICE has bought Israeli-made phone tools whose uses it has not disclosed.
