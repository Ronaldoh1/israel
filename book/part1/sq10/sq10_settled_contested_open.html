The pattern

Read together, the claims fail in the same way. Almost every one carries a true detail: a wall, a renamed province, a paid campaign, a poll, a payment. The failure is in the step after: the true detail is asked to prove something larger, and usually something about a whole people rather than a state, an army or an institution. The record answers in the other direction each time. It names the institution that acted, the date, the sum and the source. That is the test this library applies to every claim, whichever side it flatters: check the fact, then check what it proves.
