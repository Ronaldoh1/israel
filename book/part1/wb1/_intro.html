Part One covers the two years in which the West Bank was made. It begins with the United Nations plan of November 1947, which assigned the hill country of Samaria and Judea to an Arab state, and sets out what each of the planned states was to receive, by land, by population and by kind of land. It follows the war that began the next day and ended with the hill country in Jordanian hands and much of the rest of the planned Arab state inside Israel, and it ends with the armistice line of 1949, the line now called the Green Line.
