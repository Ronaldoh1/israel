Every Branch of the Family, Checked

The Rothschilds were not one house but five, founded by the five sons of Mayer Amschel Rothschild in Frankfurt, Vienna, London, Naples and Paris. The Naples house closed in 1863 and Frankfurt in 1901, and the family never founded a house in the United States; a later head of the London bank called that one of the family's mistakes. [cite:rf_wiki_family] Because the family's roles are so often merged into one, each branch was checked for money given to the movement or to Jewish settlement in Palestine before 1948, in the family's own archive, the Jewish Telegraphic Agency's archive and the standard references. This is what the record shows, as of 27 November 2026.

Walter

The Balfour letter was addressed to Walter, who had inherited his father's title in 1915, and it is natural to ask whether he paid for that place. The record gives a different reason. The wording began with a draft he worked out with Weizmann and sent to Balfour, and the family's archive calls him "the unofficial leader of the British Jewish community." [cite:rs_rd0_jvl_walter] [cite:rf_archive_walter] His own biography, the family's archive and the press archive record no money from him to the movement. That he gave privately and unrecorded cannot be ruled out, but nothing in the record shows it, and this volume does not count it.

How much, in today's dollars

Edmond's "some $60,000,000," reported at his death in 1934, is about $2 billion in today's dollars if most of it is valued at the prices of the years before the First World War, when his colonies took most of it, and about $1.5 billion at the prices of 1934. One later article gives "about $6 million," a tenth of the figure in both reports of 1934; it is most likely an error, and the contemporary figure is used here. [cite:rf_jta_dies_1934] [cite:rf_jweekly_1997] The documented gifts of the rest of the family before 1948, from Vienna and from Robert and Édouard in Paris, come to about $1 million in today's dollars. Edmond's money was the family's money, almost entirely. [cite:rf_bls_cpi_oct2026]

Set against all the money the map now counts, about $19 billion in today's dollars from 1882 to 1948, Edmond's sum is about 11 percent of it. The largest stream is private and immigrant capital, about half, estimated from documented totals; the fund drives (the Keren Hayesod, the JNF's collections, the Jewish Agency's wartime budget, the American appeals, Hadassah and Golda Meyerson's tour of 1948) are about a third, and the transfer agreement with Germany about 4 percent. [cite:rf_bls_cpi_oct2026] Rothschild money built much of the early ground, and it came first: for its first two decades it was almost the only money there was. It was not most of the money, and it did not launch or own the political movement.

After 1948

Much of the family's giving to Israel came after the founding. In 1954, when his parents were reburied at Ramat HaNadiv, James gave 410,000 pounds to Israeli institutions, among them the agricultural research institute at Rehovot, the Hebrew University and the Technion; after his death in 1957, IL 6,000,000 built the Knesset; and his widow Dorothy, who chaired the family's charities in Israel, gave the Supreme Court building. These belong to the years after the state, in red on the network map. [cite:rf_jta_410000_1954] [cite:rf_ej_james] [cite:rs_rd0_wiki_dorothy]
