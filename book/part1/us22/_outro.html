On rules published in full, and on this scorecard's measures, Israel's system scores about 6.1 of 10 for its citizens and America's about 4.8, and that order survives every weighting tested, narrowly in one. Israel leads clearly on health, household debt, housing, family support, safety from homicide and wellbeing; the United States leads clearly on schools, and narrowly on pay, output and the gaps between groups. On liberty the two tie for citizens, and the United States leads once the Palestinians Israel governs without citizenship are counted. The scorecard does not show that American aid pays for any of Israel's advantages: it is military money, spent mostly in the United States.
