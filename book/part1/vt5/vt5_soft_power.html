A Voice Without a Vote: The Soft-Power Thesis Tested

The previous sections asked why the Holy See is treated as a state and who checks it. This one asks what it does with that standing. The explainer channel Wendover Productions, in its video "How the Vatican's Embassies Work" (27 February 2025), argued three things: that the Holy See has soft power with no hard power behind it; that it tends to back winners, choosing "broadly reasonable positions" few will contest; and that it picks its battles, letting abortion "lie" at the international level. [cite:vt5_wendover] This section keeps that thesis as the video's argument and sets the documented record beside each example it uses.

The UN seat and why there is no vote

The video ties the UN seat to the Cuban Missile Crisis and to a visit by Secretary-General U Thant to the new pope, Paul VI. The visit is documented: Paul VI received U Thant on 11 July 1963 and called the United Nations "an instrument of brotherhood between nations." [cite:vt5_sp_pvi_uthant1963] On 21 March 1964 the Holy See notified U Thant that Mgr Alberto Giovannetti would be its first Permanent Observer, and the mission dates its status from 6 April 1964. [cite:vt5_sp_wiki_giovannetti] [cite:vt5_sp_hsm_history] The status rested on the Secretary-General's letter until resolution 58/314 of 1 July 2004 set out its rights, without a vote or candidates; the Holy See and the State of Palestine are the two non-member states with standing observer missions. [cite:vt5_sp_aleteia_un2024] [cite:vt5_hsm_58314] [cite:vt5_sp_ungeneva_nms] That the missile crisis won the seat is the video's narrative; the documents show the visit, the letter and the dates, not a motive. [scene:vt5_recognition|Why it is still treated as a state]

The video's reason for the missing vote is right in substance. Article 24 of the Lateran Treaty records the Holy See's wish to remain "extraneous" to temporal disputes between states and to congresses called to settle them, unless the parties jointly appeal to it, while reserving its moral authority. [cite:vt1_va_lateran] Cordell Hull cited Article 24 in 1944 when advising against Vatican membership, and Archbishop Celestino Migliore, a former observer, has said it has no vote "because this is our choice." [cite:dv1_wiki_holysee_un] The mission gives the reason as "absolute neutrality in specific political problems." [cite:vt5_sp_hsm_history]

John XXIII and the missile crisis

On 25 October 1962 John XXIII spoke on Vatican Radio in French, begging "all governments not to remain deaf to this cry of humanity"; Pravda carried the appeal the next day. [cite:vt5_sp_crux_jxxiii] According to the historian Norman Tanner, as reported by John L. Allen Jr., Kennedy reportedly used back channels to urge the pope to speak and Khrushchev later thanked him publicly; Tanner called the appeal a "factor" rather than a "crucial intervention." [cite:vt5_sp_crux_jxxiii] The video's account fits the record; any implication that it decided the outcome does not.

Cloning: the clearest win

On 7 August 2001 France and Germany asked for a convention against reproductive cloning. On 19 November 2001 Archbishop Renato Martino told the Legal Committee that "reproductive cloning is only part of the overall issue" and that creating embryos to be destroyed for stem cells must also be banned. [cite:vt5_sp_va_martino2001] A Belgian draft banned reproductive cloning and left other forms to states; a Costa Rican draft, with 44 other countries, banned all human cloning, a position the United States, Spain and the Philippines shared with the Holy See. [cite:vt5_sp_un_cloning2003] [cite:vt5_sp_wiki_multilateral] On 6 November 2003 the committee adopted Iran's motion, backed by 56 mostly Muslim states, to adjourn for two years, by 80 votes to 79 with 15 abstentions; the Assembly put the item back on its agenda on 9 December 2003. [cite:vt5_sp_un_cloning2003] [cite:vt5_sp_un_legal_cloning] On 8 March 2005 it adopted the non-binding United Nations Declaration on Human Cloning, resolution 59/280, by 84 to 34 with 37 abstentions, calling on states to prohibit all forms of cloning "incompatible with human dignity and the protection of human life"; the United Kingdom and China voted against. [cite:vt5_sp_un_ga10333] [cite:vt5_sp_un_news2005] The video's summary, that the text matched the view of a member that could not vote, is fair. "Backing winners" fits less well: the Holy See took the harder line early and the vote split.

Abortion: where the thesis is too strong

The video says the Holy See does not pursue abortion internationally. It does, at UN conferences. Before the Cairo population conference of September 1994, papal nuncios in about 60 countries lobbied for its positions, the Christian Science Monitor reported. [cite:vt5_sp_csm_cairo1994] On 13 September 1994 Archbishop Martino joined the consensus only "in an incomplete or partial manner", declining the health chapters, and said the Holy See "can therefore never condone abortion or policies which favor abortion." [cite:vt5_sp_ewtn_cairo1994] At Beijing it stated on 15 September 1995 that the outcome's family-planning language could not be read as endorsing methods it rejects, and the 2000 "See Change" campaign cited its record at conferences since Beijing in seeking to downgrade its seat. [cite:vt5_sp_wiki_multilateral] [cite:vt5_un_seechange2000] In April 2019 Archbishop Bernardito Auza said reading a right to abortion into reproductive health "explicitly violates the language of the ICPD." [cite:vt5_sp_cwr_auza2019] The record supports a narrower claim: the Holy See works openly to keep abortion out of consensus texts or to qualify them, rather than sponsoring texts of its own.

Cuba: credit shared

On 17 December 2014 the Secretariat of State said Francis had written to Raúl Castro and Barack Obama about humanitarian questions, including prisoners, and had received delegations of both countries "last October." [cite:vt5_sp_am_cuba2014] Obama said that day, "I want to thank His Holiness Pope Francis", citing his personal appeal on the American contractor Alan Gross, and credited Canada with hosting the talks. [cite:vt5_sp_wh_cuba2014] The video says the Cuban embassy reopened on 17 December 2014; that was the announcement, and embassies reopened on 20 July 2015. [cite:vt5_sp_aje_embassies2015] On 10 May 2015, after meeting Francis, Castro said: "I thanked the pope for what he did." [cite:vt5_sp_ap_castro2015]

The video's 2025 example is right in substance but rounds the number down to 500. On 14 January 2025 the White House said it would remove Cuba from the list of state sponsors of terrorism "as part of an understanding with the Catholic Church", Cuba said it would free 553 prisoners in the spirit of the Jubilee, and Cardinal Seán O'Malley said he had carried Francis's messages to both presidents for years. [cite:vt5_sp_ncr_cuba2025] Donald Trump restored the designation on taking office on 20 January. [cite:vt5_sp_cbs_trump2025] Cuba still announced on 10 March 2025 that all 553 were free; it called them people "sanctioned for diverse crimes", and rights groups said some appeared to be ordinary offenders. [cite:vt5_sp_aje_cuba2025] [cite:vt5_sp_cna_cuba2025] The Holy See secured a release; it could not hold the American side of the bargain.

Syria and Indonesia: presence, not leverage

The Holy See and Syria established relations on 21 February 1953; the Damascus nunciature stayed open through the civil war, and when Francis made the nuncio, Mario Zenari, a cardinal in October 2016 he kept him in post. [cite:vt5_sp_wiki_syria] [cite:vt5_sp_cns_zenari2016] The video's claims that Syrian officials sought papal mediation in 2000 could not be confirmed and are not repeated. In Indonesia the Holy See sent a delegate in 1947 and recognised the republic on 4 January 1950. [cite:vt5_sp_nunc_indonesia] Francis visited from 3 to 6 September 2024 and signed the Istiqlal Declaration on 5 September. [cite:vt5_sp_wiki_indonesia2024] The video presented the visit as a source of hope on church permits; the US Commission on International Religious Freedom reported in 2025 that the 2006 joint decree on houses of worship was still used against minorities, that churches were sealed or services halted in 2024, and that one Catholic parish had waited more than two decades to build. [cite:vt5_sp_uscirf_indonesia2025]

The video also garbles the city's area, which the Governorate gives as 0.44 km², about 0.17 square miles; it counts 117 nunciatures where I.Media, reporting the January 2026 note, counted 128; its 16 dicasteries match Praedicate Evangelium. [cite:vt4_vcs_geography] [cite:vt13_aleteia_dipl2026] [cite:vt6_va_pe] [scene:vt13_appointments_nuncios|Part Thirteen: nunciatures] Against the record, the thesis survives in a narrower form. The Holy See's tools are words, presence and the trust of parties who will not talk to each other; when a stronger party reverses course, as the United States did on Cuba, it has nothing to enforce with. The same limit governs its response to Gaza, where it has protested in strong terms and kept relations with Israel. [scene:vt18_why_no_sever|Part Eighteen: why relations were not severed] Where the video says it avoids hard fights, Cairo and Beijing show that it chooses them on doctrine, openly.
