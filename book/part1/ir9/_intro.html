The Hamas and Hezbollah volumes each set out the record of the movement concerned in its own terms; this part does the same for the government of the Islamic Republic. It sets out what Iran's supreme leaders and one of its presidents have said about Israel, quoted with dates and with the dispute over the most famous of those remarks; the money and weapons Iran's institutions have supplied to allied armed groups; plots to kill people on American soil that US prosecutors have charged and courts have tried; the holding of foreigners as bargaining chips; and the killing, imprisonment and execution of Iran's own citizens from 1988 to 2026. These are the acts of named offices and institutions, chiefly the Office of the Supreme Leader, the Revolutionary Guards and the intelligence ministry, and the government's stated position is given beside each finding.
