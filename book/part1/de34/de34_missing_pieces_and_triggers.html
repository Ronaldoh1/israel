Missing Pieces and Triggers

The end-to-end map showed a pipeline that is complete in practice for non-citizens and in pieces for citizens. This section names what the worst case still lacks to run as one machine against anyone, and lists the dated events that would move a grade. Four pieces are missing: a unified identity spine, a legal trigger that reaches citizens, enforcement capacity that can lawfully act on citizens at scale, and courts that would let all of it stand. Each is examined against the record as of October 2026, in the same grades used throughout the book.

A unified identity spine

A system that carries a person from camera to cell needs one key that links all their records. The United States has no national identity card and no single population register. REAL ID, enforced at airports since 7 May 2025, sets a standard for state driver's licences; it does not create a federal database of them ([scene:de25b_who_knows_you_travel|Part Twenty-Seven]). [cite:de25b_tsa_realid] What exists instead are partial spines. The Social Security number links tax, benefit, employment and credit files. The face links DHS's biometric records. Since May 2025 the SAVE system has linked Social Security records to DHS's immigration files, so that a state can check a person's citizenship by name, date of birth and the last four digits of a Social Security number. Queries are kept for ten years, and the agreements allow DHS to use them "for any purpose permitted by law." [cite:de24_npr_save] HART, built to hold faces, fingerprints and irises and to be shared across the federal government, police and foreign partners, was scheduled in August 2026 to reach initial operation by September. GAO had found its schedule slipping by years and only five of twelve privacy requirements met. [cite:de24_bu_schutt] [cite:de28_hart_pia] [cite:de24_gao_hart]

Grades. The components of a spine are documented. That SAVE becomes a standing citizenship check for more purposes is probable, because its agreements already authorise wider use and it has been run against more than 65 million voters since May 2025. [cite:de19_npr_save] A single file joining one person's records across all six layers of Part Thirty's map is possible as a join and not supported as a fact: no document shows one. [cite:de28_brennan_consol]

A legal trigger for citizens

For non-citizens, immigration law supplies the trigger, as [scene:de34_the_repurposing_test|the repurposing test] showed. For citizens there is none of comparable reach. A citizen cannot be removed. The State Department may not deny a passport for belief without authority from Congress, under Kent v. Dulles (1958). No statute lets the government designate a domestic group as terrorist. [cite:de25b_kent] [cite:de30_dla_nspm7] The nearest thing on the record is NSPM-7 and the Attorney General's December 2025 memorandum. Together they name ideas such as "anti-capitalism," "anti-Christianity" and "opposition to law and immigration enforcement" as indicators, direct the FBI to keep a list of groups, and send Treasury and IRS investigators after funders. [cite:de30_wh_nspm7] [cite:de30_bondi_memo] On 11 February 2026 the Attorney General told the House Judiciary Committee that the list exists, said antifa was on it and declined to share it. [cite:de34_int_list] The documented terrorism prosecutions since the directive concern violence. Senators reported in September 2026 a Treasury review of three named nonprofits' tax status, but no revocation is on record. [cite:de30_kera_prairieland] [cite:de30_wyden_letter]

Grades. A designation process aimed at domestic groups is documented as a directive and a secret list. Its use to deny citizens anything for their speech alone is not supported; the Attorney General's memo itself bars investigations "based solely" on protected activity. [cite:de30_bondi_memo] The trigger would become real with a statute, a public designation with legal consequences, or the first revocation of a nonprofit's status under the directive.

Enforcement capacity that can act on citizens

The capacity is built and funded to 2029, but its legal mandate is immigration ([scene:de32_ice_as_template|Part Thirty-Four]). [cite:de32_aic_hr1] Acting on citizens at scale would need one of two things. One is the criminal process, which runs through prosecutors, grand juries and trial juries, and the record shows a grand jury reportedly declining to indict in the case of the six lawmakers' video. [cite:de32_npr_kelly] The other is the military, which the Posse Comitatus Act bars from executing the laws unless Congress or the Constitution expressly allows it. [cite:de32_govinfo_1385] The Insurrection Act is that exception. It had not been invoked as of 28 October 2026, though the president said in October, "I could use it." [cite:de32_meaww_time] The Guard quick reaction forces of more than 23,000 exist. No order to use troops against citizens' political activity is on the record, and so no soldier's refusal of one has been tested ([scene:de32_what_stops_it|Part Thirty-Four]). [cite:de32_mt_qrf] [cite:de32_stripes_orders]

Courts that would let it stand

The fourth piece is the one the theorists themselves left unsolved ([scene:de8_who_enforces_the_charter|Part Eight]). The record in 2025 and 2026 runs both ways. The government lost on the speech-targeting policy, on 42,695 tax disclosures, on every voter-roll demand decided at trial, on the Illinois Guard deployment, on the emergency tariffs and on the law-firm orders at the district level. [cite:de28_ch_aaup] [cite:us10_cpa_irs_sep] [cite:de30_bgov_voterrolls] [cite:de32_lawdork_il] [cite:us11_ks_ieepa] [cite:de24b_gma_lawfirms] It won on the removal power, on SAVE's resumption, on immigration stops in Los Angeles, on Khalil's case on jurisdiction and, for now, on Albanese's sanctions. [cite:de0_slaughter] [cite:de19_npr_save] [cite:de32_scotusblog_perdomo] [cite:de34_ccr_khalil] [cite:de34_jurist_albanese] Many of the wins came on the Supreme Court's emergency docket, at interim stages. Courts that would uphold the use of the stack against citizens for speech are not supported on this record. Courts that defer to the executive on immigration and foreign affairs are documented.

The triggers, dated

A trigger here is an event, scheduled or possible, that would move one of the grades. The dated ones are set out below. The open-ended ones matter as much: the first invocation of the Insurrection Act, the publication of the Attorney General's list or a designation with legal effect, the first nonprofit to lose its tax status under the directive, the first enforcement use of welfare records that DHS has not yet said it plans, a confirmed flow of LexisNexis data into Palantir, and a reply to the members of Congress who asked DHS in April 2026 for every Palantir dataset and any use against observers and protesters. [cite:de32_hill_time] [cite:de30_wyden_letter] [cite:de24b_bl_tanf] [cite:de28_bu_lexis] [cite:de28_goldman_letter] The full watch list is kept in [scene:de40_worst_case_scorecard|Part Forty-Three].

The worst-case reading, tested

The hypothesis is the Sophia platform's, never a finding: immigration enforcement and government cuts as the occasion, a sovereign executive without a veto as the destination. This part audited what is built, tested whether it can be turned on critics, mapped the whole pipeline and named what it lacks.

Documented: the occasion. Every link from identification to confinement exists and is in daily use for non-citizens, and the inventory was enlarged, funded and pointed at that population in 2025 and 2026. [cite:us6_brennan_obbba] [cite:de34_trac_66k] Documented too: the turning of immigration and foreign-affairs tools on non-citizen critics, including critics of a foreign government's war. A policy of targeting non-citizen academics for their speech was found unconstitutional and vacated, and visas and sanctions were used against named people for what they said. [cite:de28_ch_aaup] [cite:de34_an_ozturk] [cite:de34_gn_kirkvisas] [cite:de34_jurist_albanese] For citizens, documented cases exist: unmasking subpoenas, a revoked Global Entry whose link to face recognition is alleged and denied, revoked clearances, orders against law firms, discipline of a senator. Most were withdrawn, enjoined or are still in court. [cite:de28_aclu_doe] [cite:de25b_bu_cleland] [cite:de24b_kvia_gabbard] [cite:de32_npr_kelly]

Probable: that the identity components converge further, because SAVE's agreements already authorise wider use; that the integration layer absorbs more of the map, because work statements require it; and that the NSPM-7 indicators steer investigative attention toward critics of immigration policy, a reading of the directive's text and the reported reviews, not a finding about any person. [cite:de24_npr_save] [cite:de28_bu_lexis] [cite:de30_wh_nspm7]

Possible, as joins only: a single file per person across all six layers; list entries under the directive matched against the integration layer; HART's biometrics shared into the same platform.

Not supported: a programme turning the stack on citizens for their speech; one standing system from camera to cell outside immigration enforcement; compelled labour as state policy; a lawful route to use troops or detention against citizens' political activity; and any document tracing this machinery to the Dark Enlightenment's texts, which it resembles in its centralised executive but with which it shares no documented paper trail. [cite:de28_brennan_consol] [cite:de32_lawdork_il] [cite:de8_kofman_ny]

The record runs against the worst case in the places the four missing pieces sit. No identity spine, no statutory trigger for citizens, no invoked military authority, and courts that, case by case, still say no often enough to matter. It also runs against it in authorship. Much of the machinery was built under presidents of both parties, and the one programme that flagged unaccused travellers by algorithm was ended by the current administration. [cite:ow9_mj_icm2019] [cite:de25b_cnn_quietskies] The gap between "every piece exists" and "one machine runs them" is now made of agreements, rules and judges. Those are the brakes the worst case needs removed, and the triggers above are where to watch. What the state could do to a person if it were enforced is the subject of [scene:de35_three_layers|Part Thirty-Seven].
