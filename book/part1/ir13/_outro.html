Part Thirteen established that Israel's stated aims toward Iran are documented and have widened over time. They began with the Begin doctrine, declared after the Osirak strike of 1981 and applied to Syria in 2007 and to Iran in 2025 and 2026. By February 2026 Netanyahu was openly calling on Iranians to overthrow their government.
