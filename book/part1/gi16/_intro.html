Part Fifteen turns from the region to Washington. It asks what the $3.8 billion a year in American military aid actually is, how it is paid and spent, what share of Israel's defence budget and economy it represents now compared with fifteen years ago, and whether it frees Israeli money for wars and settlements, testing that argument against the numbers on both sides. It then sets out what the wars since October 2023 have cost each treasury, Israel's by its central bank and Finance Ministry and America's by Central Command and Brown University's Costs of War project. Finally it lists, by date, place and branch, every American service member killed in these theatres, and the American civilians killed in Israel, Gaza and the West Bank, with responsibility stated as the record states it, before weighing the argument that the United States was dragged into these wars.
