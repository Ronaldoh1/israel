Part Six turns from the Holy See's outside relations to the people who run it and to the stories told about who really does. It sets out how a pope is chosen under canon law and the conclave rules, using the conclave of May 2025 that elected Leo XIV, and how the Roman Curia has been organised since Francis's constitution Praedicate Evangelium of 2022: the Secretariat of State, the sixteen dicasteries, the economic bodies, and who holds the main offices as of October 2026. It then examines the "Black Pope", the nickname for the Superior General of the Jesuits: where the name comes from, what the office controls, who holds it now, and what happened when popes and Jesuit generals disagreed. It ends with the "Grey Pope", the claim of a hidden ruler above both, traced to its sources and graded, and with the documented answer to the question underneath all three: who decides.
