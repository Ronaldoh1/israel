The Strongest Case Against This Reading

The program's authors would reject the scenario, and their answer deserves to be stated at full strength. Their argument is that democracy diffuses responsibility until no one is answerable, and that a sovereign who owns the state, like a company accountable to its shareholders, would have every reason to stamp out a scandal that damaged the asset. Yarvin has used the phrase "accountable monarchies". [cite:de23_blockworks] On that view, the Epstein case is an indictment of the system that exists, not of the one they propose.

That argument has real force against the record in this book. The democratic system did fail Epstein's victims for more than twenty years: the FBI sat on a report from 1996, a federal prosecutor gave him immunity in 2007, and the department responsible still withholds half the files. [cite:factually_farmer] [cite:ep8_doj_opr2020] [cite:ep9_rollcall_final] No one reading this book should conclude that the present arrangement works.

But the argument fails at the point that matters. The case was not broken open by the centre of the democratic system; it was broken open by its edges, by a local police chief, a newspaper, a judge, private lawyers, a territory, a state and a Congress forced to vote by petition. The design removes exactly those edges. A shareholder model also assumes the owner's interests run against the abuse. In this case the abuse was arranged around the owners of great fortunes and their friends, and the one consistent feature of the record is that people with power protected each other. A sovereign drawn from that world would face the same pull, with nothing outside it to push back.

The present also shows the edges still working. During the federal cuts of 2025 and 2026, auditors, inspectors general and judges went on documenting what was done, and some of it was reversed. [cite:de20_bl_va] [cite:de20_ab_cfpb] That is the strongest evidence against the worst case: it has not happened, and the checks that would prevent it are still in use.
