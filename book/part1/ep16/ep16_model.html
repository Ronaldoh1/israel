How a Removal Is Weighed

To weigh a removal, the map needs a measure of what it took to run the operation. It uses seven things, each drawn from the record. Five ran it: money in, banking, places and aircraft, staff and operations, and recruitment. Two protected it: legal and official cover, and access and reputation. Every documented tie between the operation and the outside world counts toward one of them. The operation itself is defined as Epstein, Maxwell, and the companies, trusts, properties and aircraft that carried his business.

Money is weighed by the sums the record states: about $170 million from Leon Black between 2012 and 2017, assets worth roughly $100 million through Les Wexner by the New York Times count, a $25 million fee from the Edmond de Rothschild group in 2015. Where a payment's size is not stated it counts as $1 million, an assumption disclosed with every reading. Money is read year by year from 1991 to 2019, because a payment in 2015 could not have paid for 1995. Banking is weighed by the years each account ran. Recruitment is built only on what a court proved: Ghislaine Maxwell's conviction in December 2021, on five of six counts, for recruiting and grooming girls for Epstein from 1994 to 2004, and Epstein's own 2008 Florida plea for procuring a minor in Palm Beach in 2004 and 2005. It too is read year by year. A year no conviction covers is left unchanged by any removal, because no court proved anything about it, and no one named but never charged is counted.

Each of the seven follows the same curve: the first part of anything counts for more than the last, so losing part of a channel costs little and losing most of it costs a great deal. The chance that the operation runs as documented is the product of the five running channels and of the two protecting ones taken at half weight, because losing all cover would have exposed the operation rather than stopped it. On the full record the chance reads about 94 percent. That figure is a calibration, not a finding: it says only that on the full record the operation ran.

Two acts are read as decisions rather than ties: the 2008 federal non-prosecution agreement and the 2019 arrest. Each decision's chance is the documented push for it over push plus resistance. Without the 2008 deal, a federal case that year is taken to end the operation's later years, so everything documented after 2008 drops out of that branch.
