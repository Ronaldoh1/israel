This is the last volume of The Israel Architecture, and the only one that argues. The volumes before it set out the record: the movement that founded the state, the powers that carried it, the war that made it, the occupation that followed, and the money, arms and words that sustain it. This volume puts that record together as a case, and states it plainly.

The case is this. The State of Israel was not the work of a people simply going home. It was built by a secular political movement founded in 1897, which chose a sovereign state over the shared country that its own dissenters proposed; carried by an empire, a banking family and later a superpower; founded by armed force, with the expulsion of most of the Arab population from the land it took; defended since by appeals to scripture and to the memory of persecution; and sustained by American money, arms and votes. Most of the wars, uprisings and peace plans that followed, from Oslo to the Abraham Accords, exist because of those choices, and the killing has not stopped.

Each part takes one link of the case, gives the evidence from the volumes where it is documented, and where the case rests on a counterfactual, gives the odds from the network map of the Road to 1948, which removes any part of the documented network and recounts the result. The last part sets out what the case does not claim. A case that overreaches is easy to dismiss; this one is built only from what the record carries.
