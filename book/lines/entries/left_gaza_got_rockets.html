The withdrawal was real, but "gave Gaza back" overstates it. Between 15 and 23 August 2005, Israel evacuated all 21 Gaza settlements and about 8,000 settlers, mobilizing up to 55,000 soldiers and police. [cite:crs_disengagement_2005] But Israel kept control of Gaza's airspace, coastline and most crossings, which is why the UN and the International Court of Justice continued to treat Gaza as occupied, while Israel argued its occupation had ended. [cite:wiki_disengagement]

The purpose matters too. Sharon's top aide described the plan as a way of isolating Gaza and avoiding international pressure for a political settlement. Nothing was handed to a Palestinian government by agreement. Hamas won the January 2006 legislative elections, took control of Gaza in June 2007 after fighting Fatah, and Israel and Egypt then imposed a blockade. Leaving the inside while holding the edges, with no negotiated settlement, produced the arrangement every Gaza war since 2008 has been fought inside.
