The Garden

This book has spent most of its length on machinery: the texts that describe a state run like a company, the money and people around them, and the systems a government now holds to find, join, flag and confine. The last section turns to the other half of the record, the part that kept the worst case from being a finding. The title borrows an image rather than an argument. A machine is built once and then runs; a garden has to be kept, by many hands, season after season, and it fails quietly when no one tends it. The checks that held in 2025 and 2026 behaved more like the second than the first. None of them worked by itself, and each depended on particular people doing particular work.

What ordinary people can do that the record shows works

The first thing is to know where the lines are. An administrative immigration warrant, signed by an officer rather than a judge, generally does not authorise forced entry into a home, and people may generally refuse entry on one; in January 2026 the Associated Press reported an internal ICE memo, disclosed by whistleblowers, asserting that officers could force entry on such a warrant for people with a final removal order, a position the department defended and legal scholars disputed. [cite:de40_ap_memo] A passport cannot be denied for belief, under a Supreme Court ruling of 1958. [cite:de25b_kent] Messages sent through an end-to-end encrypted service, one in which only the sender and recipient hold the keys, leave little to hand over: when Signal received a grand jury subpoena, it could produce only the date an account was created and the last time it connected. [cite:de26_sw_signal]

The second is to use the data rights that exist, because most of the machinery in Book Five runs on commercial data before it runs on government files. Nineteen states had enacted consumer privacy laws by the International Association of Privacy Professionals' count in late 2024. [cite:de40_iapp_states] Since 1 January 2026 a California resident can send one deletion request to every registered data broker, more than 500 of them, and brokers must process it every 45 days from August 2026. [cite:de24_ca_drop] Washington's health-data law lets people sue over location and health data collected without consent, and Illinois's biometric law requires a written release before a company collects a face or fingerprint, though it does not bind the government. [cite:de25_wh_mhmda] [cite:de26_justia_bipa] Opting out of brokers and advertising identifiers shrinks the trail that can be bought.

The third is to ask. Under the Freedom of Information Act any person, citizen or not, may request federal records, and there is no fee to file. [cite:de40_foia_faq] Much of what this book could document arrived that way: the TSA's agreement with ICE was released in a records lawsuit, and the documents on ICE's Palantir system came from a records request. [cite:de25b_ao_moa] [cite:de28_guardian_icm] The fourth is local and electoral. A city council decided Woodburn's cameras, state attorneys general brought the Medicaid and voter-roll cases, and the midterm elections of 3 November 2026 choose the Congress that holds the purse and the oversight power. [cite:de24_katu_woodburn] [cite:de30_hipaa_chhabria] [cite:de32_votebeat_polls] The fifth is to support the people who do the checking: the plaintiffs, whether a professors' association, a free-speech group or a Minnesota observer, the reporters, the auditors and inspectors general, and the employees who say no inside their own companies. [cite:de28_ch_aaup] [cite:de26_reason_rosado] [cite:de25b_bu_cleland] The same standard applies whichever party holds office, because much of the machinery was built under both and will pass to whoever wins next.

The worst-case reading, tested

The hypothesis was the Sophia platform's and never a finding: immigration enforcement and government cuts as the occasion, a sovereign executive without a veto as the destination. This part gathered the grades. Documented: the destination as the texts describe it, in their authors' own words; the occasions, an office that cut the government by executive order and an enforcement force enlarged and funded to 2029; the machinery, every layer of which exists and several of whose joins are on paper; the thinning of the brakes; and the turning of the machinery on non-citizen critics for their speech. [cite:de28_bu_lexis] [cite:de30_wh_nspm7] Not supported: a plan, a line from the texts to any office, a programme against citizens for their speech, and an executive without a veto. The record runs against the destination in the places this section has listed, and it does so through people rather than through design. The theorists' own design never solved who removes a ruler; the American system has an answer, and in 2025 and 2026 it was used, unevenly, by judges, states, juries, reporters, owners, voters and legislators, some of them in the president's own party. [cite:de35b_nyt] [cite:de31_ap_house] The parts of the machine are real and the joins are growing. What stands between the parts and the worst case is ordinary work in institutions that still answer to the people who use them, and that work is open to anyone willing to do some of it.
