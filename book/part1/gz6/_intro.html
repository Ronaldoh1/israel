Part Six is about a single day. On 7 October 2023 armed groups led by Hamas broke through the fence around Gaza and attacked the communities, army posts and festivals beyond it, killing about 1,200 people and taking 251 hostages. The part sets out what happened, as the human rights organizations and Israeli inquiries that examined it record; the warnings that preceded it and why they were dismissed; and the accounts of the day given by Hamas, by the UN and by Israel's own army, including the parts of those accounts that remain disputed.
