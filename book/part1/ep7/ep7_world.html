The Map by Continent

A study that looked only at America, Britain, Israel and the Gulf would be choosing its answer in advance. This section applies one test to every region where the released files place Epstein: who paid him, who dealt with him, and what the documents show about each. A name in the files is not evidence of wrongdoing, and the book says so for every country alike. This is the record as of October 2026. If new documents appear, the map changes.

Who paid him

The base of his fortune was narrow. In March 2026 the estate's co-executor, Richard Kahn, told the House Oversight Committee, according to its chairman, that Epstein had five paying financial clients: Les Wexner, Leon Black, Glenn Dubin, Steven Sinofsky and the Rothschild family. [cite:ep7_aol_kahn2026] Four of the five are American. Everywhere else, the documents show something different: not clients paying for his services, but people trading with him for access, introductions, advice and favours.

Europe

Europe is where the contacts ran deepest and where the consequences were heaviest. Part Four sets out the arrests in Britain of Andrew Mountbatten-Windsor and Peter Mandelson, the corruption charges in Norway against Thorbjørn Jagland, Mona Juul and Terje Rød-Larsen, the money-laundering investigation in France into Jack Lang, the resignation of Slovakia's Miroslav Lajčák and the apology of Norway's crown princess. [cite:ep4_aj_arrests2026] The French model agent Jean-Luc Brunel, charged in France with the rape of minors, died in his Paris cell in 2022 before trial. [cite:ep4_npr_wexner_fbi2026]

Russia and Central Asia

As set out earlier in this part, he used an official who trained at the FSB's academy as a source, did a favour for Russia's ambassador to the United Nations and tried, without documented success, to reach Vladimir Putin. [cite:ep7_euronews_intel2026] His passport files show planned travel to Kazakhstan and, in 2005, to Afghanistan by way of Baku. [cite:ep7_abc_passports2024]

The Middle East

Here the files show him as a broker between states: arranging a meeting between Qatar's former prime minister and Ehud Barak, advising Dubai's port chief, fielding a question from Kuwait before a presidential trip, and invoking Saudi Arabia's crown prince, with whom he was photographed, as a reference. [cite:ep7_reason_qatar2026] [cite:ep7_mee_saudi2026] With Israel, the documented relationship is his business with Barak, not payment from any Israeli. [cite:dropsite_epstein_cote_divoire] He planned his passports around travel to Israel, Jordan and Saudi Arabia. [cite:ep7_abc_passports2024]

Africa

His most sustained foreign political project was in West Africa. From 2010 he cultivated Karim Wade, the son of Senegal's president; after Wade was convicted of illicit enrichment in 2015, Epstein's company received a $500,000 invoice from Wade's lawyer, he asked Jagland about a European court challenge, and he was asked to fund a lobbying campaign. Wade was pardoned in 2016. [cite:ep7_occrp_wade2026] A former model who helped in that effort connected him to her uncle, Côte d'Ivoire's president Alassane Ouattara, and Epstein and Barak went on to broker surveillance proposals to the Ivorian government. [cite:ep7_occrp_wade2026] [cite:dropsite_epstein_cote_divoire] A 2011 trip was planned through Senegal, Sierra Leone, Mali, Morocco and Gabon. [cite:ep7_occrp_wade2026]

Asia and the Pacific

In India, the businessman Anil Ambani corresponded with him from 2017 to 2019 and met him in 2019, and the politician Hardeep Singh Puri met him at his townhouse three times, which Puri says was business; Prime Minister Modi is named in messages but never contacted him, and India's foreign ministry dismissed the references. Australia's former prime minister Kevin Rudd appears on a 2014 schedule and denies visiting. Latin America and the Caribbean

The Miami Herald's review of the files found that in 2003 he travelled to Cuba with Ghislaine Maxwell and the former Colombian president Andrés Pastrana, a trip on which Maxwell later said they met Fidel Castro; that in 2017 he funded a state-backed neuroscience conference in Havana; that in 2009 and 2011 he advised Dubai's port company on its seized Venezuelan port; that in 2012 he discussed oil ventures with the Venezuelan businessman Francisco D'Agostino, who says nothing came of it; and that he was pitched a solar project in Chile's Atacama desert that never went ahead. [cite:ep7_herald_latam2026] Mexican billionaires and former presidents are named in the files, which by itself shows nothing. An uncorroborated 2019 tip in the files makes criminal allegations against a former American official in Mexico; he denies them, no corroboration has been reported, and the book does not repeat them. [cite:ep7_mnd_mexico2026]

The pattern is the same on every continent. The money that made him rich came from a small group, almost entirely American. The reach that made him useful extended to governments and fortunes on six continents, and in each region what the documents record is access traded for access. That is the broker's map, and it is the one the rest of this book has to explain.
