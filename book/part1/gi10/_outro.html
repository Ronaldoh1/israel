Part Ten established that the most quoted proofs of a Nile-to-Euphrates programme fall apart on inspection, and that some of them are real. Genesis 15:18 names the Euphrates, Herzl noted in 1898 a plan, never carried out, to ask the sultan for land from Egypt's frontier to that river, and a Mizrachi rabbi gave the biblical promise as "From Egypt to the Euphrates" when a UN delegate asked him in 1947; but the one formal claim the movement made before 1948, at Paris in 1919, stopped south of Sidon and at the Hejaz Railway.
