What Is Actually Blocking Release?

Nothing physical. The records exist, the department holds them, and it has already published millions of pages. What blocks the rest is a set of specific, documented features of the system, each of which can be named.

The last row matters, and it cuts against a simple reading. Some of what is withheld must be withheld, and the department's exposure of victims in January 2026 showed what happens when files go out too fast. A fair account holds both things: the law protects victims, and the same law forbids hiding anything to spare the powerful embarrassment.

The finding is the same one Part Eight reached about prosecutions. Congress ordered disclosure almost unanimously. The department responsible reports to a man named in the material, and no one outside that chain has the power to make it comply, except, slowly, the courts. Nothing in this requires a hidden plan. It requires only that the people holding the files be the ones who decide, under a law that forgot to say what happens if they decide not to.
