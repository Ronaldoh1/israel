This part tells the life of J.D. Vance, the Vice President of the United States, and tests the claim that he is the political product of Peter Thiel. It separates what independent records carry from what rests on his own account: a Middletown childhood told mainly in his own memoir, the Marines, Ohio State and Yale, where a lecture by Thiel in 2011 changed his plans. It follows the five documented ties to Thiel, each of a different type, from employment at Mithril to investment in Narya and about $15 million for his 2022 Senate race, and the one widely repeated tie, Thiel's supposed push for him in 2024, that the record does not carry. It sets out what the vice presidency allows and what he has done with it, including his statements on the courts and his eight deciding votes, lists his money and influence in a ledger, and grades eight of his public claims against the record.
