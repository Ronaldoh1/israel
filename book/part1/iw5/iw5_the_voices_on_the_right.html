The Voices on the Right

Turning Point USA was founded in 2012 by Charlie Kirk, then 18, with the businessman Bill Montgomery; by 2024 its two main nonprofit arms took in almost $100 million. Its early and continuing donors included Richard Uihlein, the Mercer family and Robert Shillman, founder of Cognex. [cite:wiki_tpusa] [cite:desmog_tpusa] [cite:harpers_tpusa] Shillman has sat on the board of the Friends of the Israel Defense Forces and funded the journalism fellowships at the David Horowitz Freedom Center through which Ben Shapiro built his early career. [cite:harpers_tpusa] Shapiro co-founded The Daily Wire in 2015 with $4.7 million from the fracking billionaire Farris Wilks; by 2022 its revenue passed $100 million. [cite:wiki_dailywire] [cite:axios_dw_revenue] Miriam Adelson, the largest donor to Trump's campaigns, publishes Israel Hayom; Washington on this shelf sets out the family's giving. [cite:wiki_miriam_adelson]

The funding is documented. That it bought anyone's views is not: Kirk described himself in 2019 as having defended Israel his whole life, and Shapiro's positions long predate his company. Donors tend to fund people who already agree with them. [cite:toi_kirk_israel] [cite:wiki_dailywire]

The split

After 7 October the movement divided. In March 2024 The Daily Wire ended its relationship with Candace Owens, who had questioned American support for Israel's war; Shapiro said Israel was not the reason, and Owens said she was targeted for her views. [cite:variety_owens] [cite:thewrap_owens] In 2025 Kirk hosted critics of Israel's policy, including Tucker Carlson. [cite:harpers_tpusa] A sponsorship agreement dated 16 May 2025, published in September 2026 and matched to its terms by a fact-check, committed the International Fellowship of Christians and Jews to $700,000 over three years for speaking slots and campus "Israel Activism Kits"; the Fellowship confirmed a sponsorship relationship but declined to discuss terms. [cite:grayzone_ifcj] [cite:sherafy_ifcj] In August 2025 Bill Ackman hosted a meeting in the Hamptons with Kirk and about 35 influencers; Owens called it an "intervention" over Israel, which Ackman called "totally false". [cite:benzinga_ackman] Harper's reported that Shillman withdrew a $2 million pledge to TPUSA two days before Kirk was shot. [cite:harpers_tpusa]

The killing and the court record

Kirk was shot dead on 10 September 2025 while speaking at Utah Valley University. Tyler Robinson was charged; on 1 September 2026 a judge found the evidence sufficient for trial, Robinson pleaded not guilty, and on 9 November 2026 the trial was set for the summer of 2028. Prosecutors allege a political motive. [cite:npr_robinson_trial] [cite:iw_kttc_robinson] Theories linking the killing to his donors or to a foreign government have no support in the court record, and none is made here. [cite:ap_robinson_trial]

Erika Kirk was elected chief executive of TPUSA on 18 September 2025, and the Fellowship's sponsorship continued under her leadership. [cite:abc_erika_ceo] [cite:tnnd_erika_vf]
