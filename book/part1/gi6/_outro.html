Part Six established that West Bank annexation has been, since 2019, a documented programme of named actors rather than a rumour. Netanyahu pledged sovereignty over the Jordan Valley in 2019; an American plan endorsed it over about 30 per cent of the territory in 2020; and the joint statement of 13 August 2020 suspended it, in a word each side read differently. [cite:gi6_ap_jv2019] [cite:gi6_crs_plan2020] [cite:gi6_dcpd_uae2020] [cite:gi6_jta_table2020] The 2022 coalition committed itself to sovereignty in principle and moved civil powers to Smotrich, who described the change as one that would not look like annexation and in 2025 proposed annexing 82 per cent of the West Bank. [cite:gi6_wiki_37gov] [cite:gi6_bg_nyt_smotrich] [cite:gi6_toi_82pct] The Knesset voted 71 to 13 for the principle and advanced two bills, 25 to 24 and 32 to 9, which Likud refused to carry and which neither became law; the United States said it would not allow annexation and the UAE called it a red line. [cite:wb_toi_71_13] [cite:gi6_toi_readings] [cite:gi6_ap_trump0925] [cite:gi6_axios_uae2025] In 2026 the government registered land in Area C and tendered homes in E1 without declaring sovereignty, until the Attorney General's Office pushed the tender past the election of 27 October 2026, which will decide who holds the ministries concerned.
