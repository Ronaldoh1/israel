This part opens Book Seven, the test, with an audit. The first section lists, layer by layer, what had been built by October 2026: the records, sensors, brokers and integration platforms of Book Five, the gates that decide who may travel, work or bank, the legal changes of Book Six, and the enlarged force. Each item is tied to the part that documents it, along with when and under whom it was built. The second section asks whether tools built for immigration enforcement have been turned on critics, including critics of foreign governments. It sets the documented cases against the absence of any documented programme and names the legal step that repurposing would need. The third lays the whole worst-case pipeline end to end, from identification to compelled labour, and grades each link with the grades of the ecosystem map. The fourth names what the pipeline still lacks and the dated events that would move a grade, and closes with the part's test of the worst-case reading.
