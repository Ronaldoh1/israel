Part Thirty established four things. The Rosicrucian manifestos of 1614 to 1616 were openly anti-papal Lutheran texts for which no order is documented before the eighteenth century, and drew one galley sentence from a Habsburg prince and a Paris panic rather than any papal condemnation found here; Freemasonry, by contrast, has been banned by Rome from In eminenti of 28 April 1738 to the Dicastery's note of 13 November 2023, which still holds membership incompatible with the faith as of October 2026.
