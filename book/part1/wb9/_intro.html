Part Nine covers the West Bank since 7 October 2023, while the world's attention was on Gaza. Two things happened at once. The violence rose: more Palestinians were killed in the West Bank in two years than in the previous decade, Israelis were killed in attacks, and in 2025 the army emptied three refugee camps in the north, in the largest displacement there since 1967. And the settlement project moved faster than at any time since it began, with record approvals, new settlements, the E1 plan and votes in the Knesset to annex. This part sets out both, with the figures of the counting bodies, Israel's stated reasons, and where things stood in October 2026.
