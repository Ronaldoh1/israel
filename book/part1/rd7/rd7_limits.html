What the Map Cannot Know

Every figure in this appendix depends on choices that the record alone does not make, and the map discloses each of them. They are listed here so that a reader who weighs them differently can change them and see what follows.

It measures the founding, not the war. The map asks whether a state is founded on 14 May 1948, not whether it survives the war that followed. Arms ties are weighed by their connections, not by the number of rifles and aircraft they carried. The Czechoslovak sales brought 34,500 rifles, over 6,000 machine guns and the first fighter aircraft through October 1948, and Ben-Gurion said that without them "we would have not survived." [cite:d_wp_czarms] [cite:d_mef_prague] The map counts their loss modestly, and that is a limit of the map, not a finding about the arms.

It counts only what is documented. A person or organization that would have stepped in but left no record cannot be a substitute. Some of what the map reads as lost years may be work that someone undocumented would have done; some of what it reads as covered may be work that a documented substitute would not have chosen to do.

Its weights are assumptions. Money counts twice; a money tie with no stated sum counts as $1 million; a reported tie counts a little over half as much as a verified one; each push and each resistance in a key decision counts once; a successor with no documented position is an even chance; each year of delay costs 2 percent of the chance; a channel starts counting lost years at 5 percent of its 1948 total. Each is the simplest choice the record allows, and each can be argued.

It reads the record of what happened. A removal shows what the documented network would have lacked, not what the people in it would have done instead. Where the map names a successor or a substitute, it names the one the record shows was there, with what the record shows he had done.

The other homelands are judgments. The shares given to Argentina and East Africa are read from the record by hand, not counted from the map, and the comparison table shows each one with its reason.
