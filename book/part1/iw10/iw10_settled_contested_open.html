Settled, Contested and Open

The architecture

Read together, the fronts of this volume share a structure. A foreign government spends openly, through registered agents, on content whose authors it does not name. American platforms, owned by a few companies, act on that government's removal requests at high rates. A definition that treats some criticism of Israel as antisemitism moves from an international body into federal policy, platform rules and a university's discipline. A private plan proposes treating a protest movement as a terror network, and within months the government revokes visas and cuts funds along the same lines, with surveillance tools to find the people concerned.
