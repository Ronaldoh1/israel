Before you argue about Israel and Palestine, check what you're carrying in.

"The Palestinians were invented in the 1960s." False. In 1911, two cousins in Jaffa founded a newspaper called *Filastin*, "Palestine." Israel's own National Library holds 6,692 issues.

"A land without a people for a people without a land." That slogan first appeared in 1843, in a book by a Scottish clergyman who had visited the land and knew people lived there.

The Baseline sorts the claims before the history begins: true, false, or a true fact attached to a conclusion it doesn't support. The words, the maps, who writes the textbooks, and the lines you'll see in every comment section, each one checked.
