Part One sets out what the Gnostics claimed and what the word "Gnostic" can honestly mean. It explains gnosis as knowing rather than believing, the three kinds of people the Valentinians described, and the rites of "redemption" their opponents recorded, and it sets these against the bishops' path of faith, works and sacraments. It then explains why leading scholars have argued since 1996 that "Gnosticism" as a single religion is a modern construction, and how this book uses the word.
