Israel is singled out in some UN bodies and shielded in the one that can issue binding decisions; the line cites only the first half. At the Human Rights Council, Item 7, on the human rights situation in Palestine and other occupied Arab territories, is the only country-specific item on the permanent agenda, and Secretary-General Ban Ki-moon voiced disappointment at the decision to single out Israel [cite:unwatch_item7]. UN Watch, a pro-Israel watchdog, counts 17 General Assembly resolutions on Israel in 2024 against 7 on all other countries combined [cite:unwatch_unga2024].

The other half: the United States has vetoed Security Council resolutions critical of Israel 45 times as of December 2023, slightly over half of its 89 vetoes since 1945 [cite:ccga_vetoes]. Many General Assembly resolutions also concern the occupation since 1967 and the UN's own role since Resolution 181 in 1947. Whether the volume of resolutions reflects bias, the length of the occupation, or both is the real argument.
