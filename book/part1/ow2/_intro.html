Part Two follows how Congress built the legal spine described in Part One: the law that governs advocacy and the foreign-agent question first raised in 1962, the shift in 2021 when AIPAC began spending directly in elections, and the recorded votes on aid, missile defence, arms sales and related measures from 1973 to 2025. It draws on, and links to, the fuller accounts in the Follow the Money volume, and it keeps to what the filings and roll calls show: who spent what, and who voted how, without attributing motives the record does not contain.
