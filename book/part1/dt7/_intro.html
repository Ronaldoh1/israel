Part Seven sets out the other remedies proposed for the departures from majority rule mapped earlier in this book. The first section takes the fixes for money: public matching of small donations, democracy vouchers, disclosure, limits on the revolving door between government and lobbying, and the constitutional amendment that would be needed to change what the Supreme Court has ruled. The second takes the fixes for structure: the compact among states to elect the president by national popular vote, independent commissions to draw district maps, open and top-two primaries, term limits, automatic voter registration and votes at sixteen. Each comes with the evidence for it, the case its critics make, and the legal route it needs.
