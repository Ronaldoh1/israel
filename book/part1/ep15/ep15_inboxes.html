The Missing Inboxes

The most searchable version of what was released was built not by the government but by two private developers. Jmail, launched in November 2025, presents the files as Epstein's own inbox, with a search tool built to answer the department's claim that searching the release was impractical for "technical limitations"; it has had more than eighteen million users. [cite:ep15_wiki_jmail] The Government Accountability Office has opened its own review of how the redactions were made. [cite:ep15_cbs_missing2026] The document that would close this blind spot is an inventory: every account and device the department holds, and for each, whether it was searched and released.
