# Simulation source conflicts found while writing Book One (Oct 2026)

The book's writers and auditors were only allowed to use the simulation's own research. Where that research contradicts itself, the book either notes the discrepancy or follows the better-documented version. Fix these in the scenes themselves, then re-run the book audit for the affected sections.

- s00e2: sabotage "designed to accelerate departures" vs. Lavon Affair entry (aim: damage Western confidence in Nasser). Book follows Lavon entry.
- Iraqi airlift: "~120,000–130,000 airlifted" vs. ~107,000 by end of 1951 + ~16,000 other routes.
- Baghdad Jewish population: 140,000 (1945, a third) vs. 140,000 (1917, 40%) vs. 90,000–140,000.
- Farhud deaths: "180 or more" (s00e) vs. at least 128 official, estimates to 180 (s00e2).
- s00b vs s00b0: whether stretching "antisemitism" to criticism of a state "doesn't hold" or is an open dispute. Book follows s00b (open dispute). Consider aligning s00b0's "today" entity wording.
- s00c: Bund membership 400,000+ (1917) vs. "tens of thousands" (Tobias).
- s00d: 1948 flag "no new design process" vs. Provisional Council rejected an alternative design.
- s00a2: ADL finding was TikTok, HRW report was Meta; synthesis says "the same platform."
- s00h: "most" named cities still populated (rec) vs. "all" (entity).
- s00b3: "every lineage / five traditions" but only four listed, no Sephardi example.
- s00h vs s00i: ~600,000 late-19th-century population vs. 381,954 (1871–72 census); also the 1871–72 census figure could not be confirmed in standard sources (see fact-check log).
