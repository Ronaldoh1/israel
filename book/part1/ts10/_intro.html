Part Ten takes the system described in the earlier parts and shows how it works in an argument, and where it stops working. The first section follows the mechanism step by step: a promise spoken to Abram in Genesis 12:3 is read as a promise about the Jewish people, then about the modern state, then about the policies of that state, so that opposing a policy can be described as opposing God. It traces each step through dated statements by named people: a senator, a Speaker of the House, an Israeli prime minister, a Dallas pastor and the International Christian Embassy Jerusalem, and it sets beside them the case of a Catholic commissioner removed from a White House commission in February 2026, where the argument ran in the other direction and was tangled with antisemitic statements. The second section records where the mechanism has failed: Christian theologians who reject the reading, an ambassador who withdrew a maximal statement, a Brazilian president who opened a trade office instead of an embassy, and a split on the American right that widened after the killing of Charlie Kirk in September 2025.
