Part Seven follows the trips. In Congress, AIPAC's charitable foundation has paid for most of the members' travel to Israel since 2012, under an exception to the 2007 ethics law that allows charities to sponsor trips lobbies cannot; about half of the House serving in 2024 had taken one. Below Congress, community groups, police-exchange programs and, in 2025, the Israeli government itself have paid for state legislators, mayors and police chiefs to travel, with far less of it on record. This part sets out the rules, the numbers, the costs, the itineraries and the gaps.
