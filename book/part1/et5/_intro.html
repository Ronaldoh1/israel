Part Five follows the two times a power from Catholic Europe tried to take hold of the Ethiopian church. The first was the Jesuit patriarch Afonso Mendes, who brought an emperor into communion with Rome in 1626 and was expelled within a decade. The second was the Fascist Italian occupation that followed the invasion of 1935 and 1936, backed by the Italian church, which killed between 1,200 and 1,600 pilgrims and clergy at Debre Libanos in 1937 and carried the obelisk of Aksum to Rome.
