The Claim of Antiquity

For a century and a half after Ficino, Renaissance thinkers generally followed his view that Hermes was the source of pagan learning. [cite:hm_enc_hermeticism] In 1614 the classical scholar Isaac Casaubon proved "the late origin of the Hermetic texts". [cite:hm_enc_hermeticism] The writings were far younger than believed: products of the Roman era, not of the age of Moses. [cite:hm_sep_ficino] The chain of ancient theology lost its first link.

The pattern is one this shelf has now met several times. The Book of Enoch carried the name of a patriarch from before the flood; the Kebra Nagast gave a dynasty an ancient pedigree; the Zohar presented a medieval Spanish work as the teaching of a second-century sage. [cite:ds_bas_enoch] [cite:et_wiki_kebra] [cite:kb_brit_deleon] The Hermetica claimed an Egyptian antiquity older than Plato. In each case age was authority, and in each case the scholarship of a later period dated the text to its real time.

The modern Hermetic revival repeated the move. The Hermetic Order of the Golden Dawn, whose London temple was founded in 1888, was built on a set of documents called the Cipher Manuscripts, which William Wynn Westcott had decoded by 1887. [cite:hm_wiki_gd] [cite:hm_wiki_cipher] Westcott said they had come with the address of a German adept, Fräulein Anna Sprengel, of a Rosicrucian order, who authorised the new order. But no evidence has ever proven that she or her lodge existed; the scholars Ronald Decker and Michael Dummett argue that the page naming her was "almost certainly not part of the original", and that Westcott probably invented her. [cite:hm_wiki_cipher] The manuscripts are on paper watermarked 1809 and refer to the Egyptian Book of the Dead, which scholars could not read before the Rosetta Stone was deciphered in 1822. [cite:hm_wiki_cipher] There is "no conclusive evidence" of their origin. [cite:hm_wiki_cipher]

Casaubon's redating did not destroy the Hermetic tradition. Hermeticism lasted into the Enlightenment, influenced early Egyptology and passed into Rosicrucianism and Freemasonry, and the founders of the Golden Dawn were all Freemasons. [cite:hm_enc_hermeticism] [cite:hm_wiki_gd] What the redating changed was the ground of authority. A teaching that had been valued because it was oldest had to be valued, if at all, for what it said. That is the ground on which this shelf has placed every inner reading: not that it is ancient, or secret, but whether it illuminates.
