How Many

Three bodies count journalists killed in the war, and their counts differ because their rules differ. The UN counted 242 Palestinian journalists killed by Israel as of August 2025. The International Federation of Journalists, which also counts media workers, put the number at 241 by July 2026, after removing eight names and adding fourteen. The Committee to Protect Journalists counted 192 killed by Israel, at least 184 of them Palestinian, by 10 August 2025. It later removed people its research found had engaged in combat, eight in a review covering 2023 to April 2025 and 19 more in June 2026, and as of 24 September 2026 counted 189 Palestinian journalists killed in Gaza, plus two killed in Israeli detention. [cite:pr_wiki_killing] [cite:gz_cpj_2026]

The removals matter. They show that the counting bodies revised their lists when their own research found a person had taken part in combat. Israel denies deliberately targeting journalists and says some of those killed were members of armed groups; the cases in dispute are the subject of Parts Six and Seven. [scene:pr6_cases|The named]

The Record Year

In February 2026 the Committee to Protect Journalists reported that 129 journalists and media workers had been killed worldwide in 2025, the most since it began counting in 1992, and that Israel was responsible for about two-thirds of them, most of them Palestinians in Gaza. It classified 47 of the year's killings as murders, journalists deliberately killed for their work, the most in a decade, and documented 28 killings of journalists by Israeli drones in Gaza. "Journalists are being killed in record numbers at a time when access to information is more important than ever," said its chief executive, Jodie Ginsberg. [cite:pr_cpj_2025report]

A study by Brown University's Costs of War project concluded that the war had killed more journalists than the US Civil War, both world wars and several later wars combined; a European journalism-education group rated the comparison "mostly false," because the database it relies on was not designed as a complete historical record. [cite:pr_wiki_killing] The Committee to Protect Journalists' own records, which go back to 1992, show no deadlier year for journalists than 2025. [cite:pr_cpj_2025report]
