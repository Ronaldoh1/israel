# Volume plan: The Divine 3+1 — The 3 Parents of Israel

Library volume (new, thematic; built in parts like The Cold War Architecture). Standard: Road to 1948 depth
(LIBRARY-PROTOCOL.md), historian's voice, inline citations, told-vs-record callouts, figures and dashboards.

## The question the volume answers
Three cities that do not run like ordinary cities, and the state they are said to have raised:
London (finance), Rome (theology), Washington (military and material support), and Israel, the fourth sovereign.
Ron's subtitle stands as a statement. "Parent" is defined by three functions, and every city gets the same
test at the end of its part: what that city's institutions actually did for the Zionist project and the state,
from the documents, with a verdict. The Vatican gets the same test as the other two, including Ron's view that
its public refusal was a front, stated in its strongest form and checked against the archives.
