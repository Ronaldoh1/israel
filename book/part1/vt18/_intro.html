Part Eighteen follows the Holy See's relations with Israel and with the Palestinians from the opening of full diplomatic relations in June 1994 to October 2026. It sets out the treaties signed after the Fundamental Agreement and the one still unsigned, the three papal visits, the nuncios and the quarrels; the Holy See's agreements with the PLO in 2000 and with the State of Palestine in 2015; what Francis, Leo XIV, Cardinal Parolin and the Latin Patriarch said about the war in Gaza from October 2023, with Israel's replies beside each statement; and why, despite those words, the Holy See has kept its relations with Israel intact. Every item from 2025 and 2026 carries its status as of October 2026.
