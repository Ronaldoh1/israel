"Most moral army in the world" is a self-description, not a finding any independent body has made, and the documented record of the Gaza war cuts against it. In September 2025 a UN Commission of Inquiry found that Israeli authorities "deliberately deprived Palestinians in Gaza of resources indispensable for their survival" and found underlying acts of genocide, findings the Israeli government disputes. [cite:un_coi_sept2025]

Israeli intelligence officers' own testimony adds detail. According to a +972 Magazine and Local Call investigation based on six officers who used it, an AI system called Lavender flagged about 37,000 Palestinians as potential targets at its peak. The officers acknowledged roughly a 10 percent error rate and described reviewing each target for only seconds before authorizing a strike, and said 15 to 20 civilian deaths were accepted per junior operative in the war's early weeks. [cite:972mag_lavender_ai_machine] [cite:aljazeera_ai_assisted_genocide_lavender] Nor is there an outside check that could confirm the superlative: no Knesset oversight committee has access to operational detail.
