# Volume II, Stage One (1896–1917): research notes

Verified 2026-10-09. Every claim in `rd1.json` comes from a page fetched in this session. WebFetch returns a model-written summary of each page, not raw text, so quotations are short. Check every printed quotation against the original page before publication.

## 1. The Basel Program and the First Congress (`basel_program_1897`)
**Agree:** The aim and four means, the dates (Congress 29–31 August; program adopted 30 August 1897), the German "Heimstätte", the founding of the Zionist Organization, and the Munich refusal followed by the July 1897 Protestrabbiner declaration.

**Disagree:** The size of the Congress. The simulation's s04 has 208 delegates from 17 countries; the Posen Library has some two hundred from seventeen countries; the Jewish Virtual Library has "some 200"; Grill (EGO) has delegates from 14 states. Whether Dreyfus was Herzl's catalyst: s04's expanded text says it was; Beller notes Dreyfus is not named in the 1895 Zionist diary.

**Not verified:** The rechtlich/öffentlich-rechtlich drafting change rests on one reference page, so it is marked "reported". I did not see the German original of the Protestrabbiner text. Posen says the First Congress adopted "Hatikvah". I did not use this, because other accounts date its adoption much later and I could not check it.

## 2. The Congresses, 1898–1913 (`zionist_congresses_1898_1913`)
**Agree:** This entry has one source (JVL). Its 1903 vote figures match the Center for Israel Education.

**Not verified:** Delegate numbers for the Congresses after 1897 were not given.

## 3. Herzl, Zangwill and the Arab population (`herzl_arab_question_1895_1905`)
**Agree:** The al-Khalidi letter (1 March 1899, via Zadoc Kahn) and Herzl's reply (19 March 1899) are given by both Wikipedia and Rashid Khalidi.

**Disagree:** Readings of the June 1895 diary passage. Arieli presents it as an early transfer idea. Beller, reviewing Penslar, says Herzl was discussing South America, not Palestine, and preferred peaceful means. Readings of Herzl's reply are also disputed.

**Not verified:** The exact day of the 1895 entry. The diary text itself: archive.org was not reachable, so the quotation reaches us through Arieli citing Said. The original source of the Zangwill 1905 quotation, which Arieli gives without a citation. The Tribune review's line "nothing will be sold back to (the Arabs)" had no date or source, so it was left out.

## 4. Ottoman Palestine (`ottoman_palestine_1872_1914`)
**Agree:** The 1872 Mutasarrifate and its districts. The Ottoman 1914–15 total of about 722,000.

**Disagree:** The Jewish population in 1914: 38,754 (Ottoman register of subjects), about 60,000 (McCarthy; PalQuest), 85,000 (the standard figure noted by Owen), or 94,000 (Bachi). The wartime decline: "one third" left (PalQuest); much less than a quarter (McCarthy); 84,000 to 56,000 (Wikipedia, unsourced, not used as a claim).

**Not verified:** McCarthy's Muslim and Christian breakdown for 1914. Young Turk-era administrative changes beyond the 1908 elections.

## 5. The Old Yishuv (`old_yishuv_1800_1914`)
This entry has one source (the National Library of Israel). I could not verify a Jewish majority in Jerusalem by the 1870s–80s, so it is left out.

## 6. Kishinev, emigration and the East Africa vote (`kishinev_emigration_uganda_1903`)
**Agree:** The 1903 vote was 295–178 with 98 abstentions (JVL and CIE). This matches s04e.

**Conflict:** gia_src_s01 gives 295–177. It should be logged in SOURCE-CONFLICTS.md.

**Not verified:** The CIE page dates Chamberlain's proposal to April 1903. I did not use this date, because the formal offer is usually dated later in 1903 and I did not verify it.

## 7. Jewish currents for and against (`jewish_currents_1885_1914`)
**Agree:** Mizrachi was founded in Vilna in March 1902 (Encyclopaedia Judaica; Grill). Agudat Israel was founded at Kattowitz in 1912.

**Not verified:** The CCAR's 1897 resolution is quoted from Wikipedia's citation of N. W. Cohen; I did not see the resolution's text. The Bund is dated only through the simulation (October 1897), because Grill gives no Bund dates.

## 8. The Second Aliyah (`second_aliyah_1904_1914`)
**Disagree:**
- The number of immigrants: about 35,000 (NLI) or 40,000 (JVL).
- Degania's founding: 28 October 1910 (CIE) or 1909 (JVL).
- Ben-Gurion's arrival: 9 September 1906 (Ben-Gurion House) or 7 September 1907 (CIE). Prefer the first, with the conflict noted.
- When the ICA took over Rothschild's colonies: "since 1899" (Jewish Encyclopedia) or 1900 (the year-ledger dashboard).
- The historians' interpretations of Hebrew labor: Shafir against Shapira.

**Not verified:** That Hashomer was absorbed into the Haganah in 1920. The JVL page gives only its dates, 1909–1920. Hashomer's membership numbers. The 1913 "language war".

## 9. Yemenite Jews (`yemenite_aliyah_1881_1914`)
**Disagree:** About 1,000 left Yemen through Yavne'eli plus several hundred more (Simon et al., via Wikipedia), against about 2,000 Yemenite immigrants in the Second Aliyah as a whole (NLI). These count different things.

**Not verified:** The Kinneret removal of 1930 and Ruppin's and Smilansky's role in it rest on a heritage website that reproduces letters. It is attributed, and it belongs to Stage Three for detail.

## 10. Arab responses (`arab_press_politics_1905_1914`)
**Agree:** Nassar's career dates come from PalQuest (Maher Charif).

**Not verified:**
- The month of the 1911 parliamentary debate.
- The text of Ruhi al-Khalidi's speeches.
- Any Zionist–Arab talks of 1913–14 beyond Nassar's attack on them.
- The full title and place of Azouri's book. The excerpt page truncates it, and only page 75 is quoted.

## 11. Al-Fula and the Emek (`fula_emek_purchases_1910`)
**Agree:** The 1910 al-Fula purchase and the Nazareth governor's opposition. Ruppin calls it 9,500 dunams; the al-Asali page says about 2,500 acres, roughly 10,000 dunams, so the two broadly agree.

**Disagree:** How the sale looked to the parties. Ruppin's memoir describes willing sellers and a trusted buyer. Al-Asali tried to block the sale.

**Not verified:** The price of the 1910 purchase. The number of tenants at al-Fula.

## 12. The First World War (`wwi_palestine_1914_1917`)
**Disagree:**
- Those displaced from Jaffa in April 1917: 10,000 including 8,000 Jews, up to 16,000 from Tel Aviv, or 8,000 Jews (US consul).
- Mortality in Mount Lebanon: 20–30% (AUB) or nearly half (Wikipedia, unsourced).
- The date of the Damascus and Beirut hangings: the AUB exhibit says 6 May 1915, while the al-Asali sources say 6 May 1916.

**Weak sources:** The JNS column is an opinion piece. Its facts are attributed to 1915–17 New York Times reports and are marked "reported". The "Aliyah and Yishuv during WWI" page is flagged for missing citations, and only its Ottomanization and Zion Mule Corps statements are used.

**Not verified:**
- Famine deaths inside Palestine itself.
- The Ottomans taking about 45% of American aid shipments.
- A primary text of Jamal Pasha's orders.
