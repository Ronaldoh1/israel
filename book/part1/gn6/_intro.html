Part Six follows the Gnostic books that came from outside the Nag Hammadi jar. It traces three codices that reached European collections between 1769 and 1896 and waited decades to be published, and reads in two of them, the Gospel of Mary and the Pistis Sophia, the quarrel between Mary Magdalene and Peter over who may teach. It then follows the Gospel of Judas through dealers, a Long Island bank box, a foundation and a television network, with the sums at each stage, and ends with a forgery made for the same market.
