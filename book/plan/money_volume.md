# Volume plan: Follow the Money — The Pro-Israel Lobby in American Government and Around the World

Library Volume 8 (takes the shelf slot of simulation chapter ch54, "Pro-Israel Money in Congress"). Requested by Ron 18 Oct 2026.
Standard: Road to 1948 depth (LIBRARY-PROTOCOL.md), inline citations, volume bibliography, figures and dashboards.

## Ground rules specific to this volume
- "Pro-Israel money" is defined by organizations, PACs, filings and stated purpose — never by any donor's religion or ethnicity. Individual donors are named only where the record names them as major donors to the vehicles studied.
- Spending figures are tied to a cycle, a vehicle (PAC contribution / conduit-earmarked / super PAC independent expenditure / lobbying) and a snapshot date; career and cycle figures are never mixed.
- Correlation is not causation: "X received" and "Y spent against X" are documented; "money caused the vote/outcome" is a separate claim requiring its own evidence.
- Counter-spending (American Priorities, J Street, etc.) and other foreign-policy and industry lobbies are reported for scale and comparison.
- Ron's hypothesis for Part Nine (other countries' lobbies serve military aid and UN standing) is tested, not assumed.
- MTG chapter: her documented record, the documented criticism and who made it, her own stated reasons for resigning, side by side.

## Parts (stage ids pm1–pm10)
1. What counts, and how it moves — OpenSecrets "pro-Israel" category; PACs, super PACs, conduit/earmarking; LD-1/2 lobbying; FARA; 501(c)(3)/(4); ethics travel rules.
2. AIPAC: the architecture — Kenen, AZCPA (1951/1954), the 1963 Fulbright hearing and FARA, the 1980s rise, the Rosen/Weissman case, budget (990s), LD-2 spend by year, AIEF, policy conference, the 2021 PAC and UDP.
3. The money, cycle by cycle — pro-Israel totals by cycle (OpenSecrets), AIPAC PAC/UDP 2022/2024/2026, other PACs (NORPAC, DMFI, RJC, J Street, Pro-Israel America), major donors to UDP; comparisons with other lobbies.
4. The recipients — top Democratic and Republican recipients per cycle 2016–2026, House and Senate; career totals for the current Congress (cm_s01–s04).
5. The unseating campaigns — 2002 (Hilliard, McKinney) precedent; 2022 (Levin, Edwards, Turner, Allam, Lee); 2024 (Bowman, Bush); 2026 (Massie, El-Sayed, Malinowski, Illinois, Wahab, others); win/loss record; counter-spending.
6. Refusing the money, and leaving under fire — members who refuse; Marjorie Taylor Greene (record, criticism, resignation and stated reasons); others found by search.
7. The trips — AIEF, House Ethics/Senate disclosures, cost per trip, counts by year; Israeli-government-funded trips for state legislators; candidates at the Wall.
8. State and local — anti-BDS laws (count, dates, model text, court cases); Israel Bonds held by states/cities; police exchanges; local resolutions.
9. The lobby abroad — UK (CFI, LFI, BICOM, register of interests), Canada (CIJA), EU/ELNET, Germany, France, Australia (AIJAC), Latin America, Africa, Asia; Israel's own state programs (MASHAV, Esther, FARA-registered agents in the US); costs where disclosed; test of the aid/UN hypothesis.
10. The whole architecture and what it costs — composite map, totals table, what is settled / contested / open.
