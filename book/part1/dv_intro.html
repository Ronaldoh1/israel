This volume asks a question that circulates widely and is rarely tested: whether three cities that do not run like ordinary cities, the City of London, Vatican City and Washington, DC, together brought a fourth sovereign, the State of Israel, into being. Its title states the claim as its author holds it. Its method is to test that claim city by city, against the documents, and to report what survives.

Each city is assigned the function it is said to have performed: London finance, Rome theology, Washington force. Each is examined first as an institution, how it is governed and how it differs from every other powerful city, and then as an actor, through what its institutions supplied to the Zionist movement and the state and what they withheld. Each part ends with the same test and a dated verdict. A fifth part examines the child itself, the government of Israel, and a sixth sets the three verdicts side by side.

The volume does not assume that the three cities acted together, and it does not assume that they did not. It reports what the documents show and names what they leave open.
