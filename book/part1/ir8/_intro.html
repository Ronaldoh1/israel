Part Eight follows the covert war between Israel and Iran from the late 2000s to the open exchanges of 2024. It begins with the campaign against Iran's nuclear programme, which was reported to involve Israel acting alone or together with the United States: the Stuxnet worm, the killing of nuclear scientists in Tehran, the theft of Iran's nuclear archive, the explosions and blackout at Natanz, and the killing of Mohsen Fakhrizadeh by a remote-operated gun. It then turns to Iran's side of the war: the 2012 bomb plots against Israeli diplomats, the attacks on shipping, cyberattacks and the detention of foreigners. It ends with 2024, the year both governments began striking each other's territory openly. Throughout, the part separates what courts and governments have confirmed from what rests on anonymous officials, and it gives each government's own account beside the charges against it.
