Part Five follows the idea of the whole Land of Israel from a movement into government, from 1977 to the end of 2022. It begins with Menachem Begin, the first Likud prime minister: the language he brought into office, the settlement plans his government adopted, the autonomy plan in which he claimed sovereignty over the West Bank and Gaza and then proposed to leave it open, the laws that applied Israeli law to East Jerusalem and the Golan Heights, and the return of Sinai. It then reads the 1977 Likud platform, the source of the line "between the Sea and the Jordan there will only be Israeli sovereignty", for what it says and does not say, and sets the later votes of Likud's Central Committee beside what Likud prime ministers did in office. The part ends with two texts that carry the claim beyond a party: the Nation-State Law of 2018, which made Jewish settlement a "national value", and the guidelines and coalition agreements of the government formed in December 2022.
