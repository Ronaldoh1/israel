The Paid Campaign

Israel has long run public-diplomacy programmes, known in Hebrew as hasbara. In 2017 its Strategic Affairs Ministry launched an app, Act.IL, that gave users "missions" to like, share and report content; it reached about 17,500 users and closed in 2022. [cite:dfrlab_actil] [cite:972_actil_closed] The scale changed in December 2024, when Foreign Minister Gideon Sa'ar secured about $150 million for global public diplomacy, around twenty times previous budgets, with money arriving from May 2025. [cite:quincyinst_eighth_front_billion] The Pressure Machine on this shelf follows the larger budgets reported for 2026.

The Esther Project

A filing under the Foreign Agents Registration Act disclosed that Bridges Partners LLC, formed in Delaware in June 2025 by two Israeli consultants as a subcontractor of Havas Media Germany working for Israel's Foreign Ministry, would run an influencer network code-named the "Esther Project" from June to November 2025 under a contract of up to $900,000, for 14 to 18 influencers posting on YouTube, TikTok and X. [cite:jta_esther_project_fara] [cite:wiki_esther_project] The influencers' names have not been disclosed; only one consultant registered as a foreign agent, and Public Citizen asked the Justice Department to investigate whether the influencers themselves should have registered. [cite:pc_fara_complaint] Estimates of the cost per post, around $7,000, are disputed: HonestReporting argues the filing is a pooled budget. [cite:rs_paid_influencers] [cite:hr_7000] It is distinct from the Heritage Foundation's "Project Esther" in Part Seven; the two share only a name. [cite:wiki_esther_project]

Clock Tower X

Brad Parscale, Trump's 2020 campaign manager, registered his firm Clock Tower X under the Act on 18 September 2025, disclosing a $6 million agreement through Havas Media on behalf of Israel "to combat antisemitism": 100 pieces of content a month, mostly for younger audiences on TikTok, Instagram and YouTube, and work on how chatbots describe Israel. A supplemental filing in May 2026 showed $15 million received in the six months to March 2026. [cite:snopes_parscale] [cite:intercept_parscale] [cite:dc_parscale_fara]

Trips

In August 2025 a trip for conservative influencers organised by Israel365 Action was funded in part through an $86,000 Foreign Ministry contract, and Israel's Diaspora Affairs Ministry took about ten influencers to sites of the Gaza Humanitarian Foundation while foreign journalists were barred from entering Gaza independently. [cite:ji_israel365_trip] [cite:newarab_gaza_tour]

Foreign governments of every kind spend money on American opinion, and registration under the Act is how the law makes that visible. What these filings show is the scale, the methods and the gap: the money is disclosed, the people paid to post are not.
