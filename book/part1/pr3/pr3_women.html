Women and Girls

UN Women, the UN agency for women, estimated in April 2026 that more than 38,000 women and girls had been killed in Gaza between October 2023 and December 2025, about 47 a day, and warned that the true number was likely higher because the systems that record deaths had collapsed. Close to 11,000 women and girls were left with lifelong injuries. The Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics, counting adult women alone, put the number killed at 12,400. The share of households headed by a woman rose from 9 to 14 percent, about 58,600 households. [cite:pr_mm_women2026]

The two counts are not added together: one includes girls and the other does not. How deaths are recorded in Gaza is set out in Part One. [scene:pr1_lists|How the dead are counted]
