The October 7 attack was an atrocity, and it also had a documented context; the record holds both. Hamas fighters killed 1,195 people in Israel, 828 of them civilians and 36 children, and took 251 hostages, the largest single-day loss of Jewish life since the Holocaust. [cite:wiki_october7_attacks] It came after 16 years of an Israeli-Egyptian blockade of Gaza, which by 2022 had left 97% of its water contaminated by UN standards and unemployment at 46.6%. [cite:blockade_unrwa] In the West Bank, the UN documented more than 2,150 settler violence incidents in 2023 alone. [cite:gzr_btselem_settlers] That context explains grievances; it does not justify the attack.

Nor was the attack unforeseen inside Israel. Israeli intelligence obtained Hamas's attack plan more than a year earlier and judged it aspirational, and Unit 8200 observers who reported unusual Hamas training were told it was routine. [cite:nyt_jericho_wall_2023] That warnings were dismissed is documented; claims that the attack was deliberately allowed to happen are not established.
