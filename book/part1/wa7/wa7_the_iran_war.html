The Iran War, from Washington

The decisions that led to it were taken in Washington over eight years. In May 2018 Trump withdrew the United States from the nuclear agreement under which inspectors had certified Iran's compliance. [cite:wiki_jcpoa_withdrawal] In June 2025, after Israel struck Iran two days before scheduled talks, American bombers struck Iran's nuclear sites. [cite:wiki_twelve_day_war] On 28 February 2026, during negotiations, American and Israeli forces struck together, nearly 900 strikes in the first twelve hours, killing Iran's supreme leader, Ali Khamenei; the two governments' stated aims included regime change and the destruction of Iran's nuclear and missile programmes. [cite:wiki_iran_war_2026] [cite:commons_iran2026] Iran answered with missiles and drones on Israel and on American bases across the Gulf. [cite:wiki_iran_war_2026]

The bill

Central Command estimated the cost at $43.6 billion as of 3 September 2026, most of it in munitions, especially interceptors; the Pentagon disclosed 19 American service members killed as of 23 September. [cite:ap_iran_cost_436] [cite:wapo_19dead] The United Nations counted more than 3,400 Iranian civilians killed, and Iran's human-rights monitor HRANA about 3,684 dead. [cite:wiki_iran_war_2026] The Iran Architecture sets out the full bill, including what the war has cost Americans at the pump and in taxes.

Where it stands

A conditional ceasefire mediated by Pakistan took hold on 8 April, and a memorandum was reached in Islamabad in June. [cite:wiki_iran_war_2026] By November 2026 full-scale fighting was on hold, but there was no peace agreement: the United States was blockading Iran's ports, Iran had ordered the Strait of Hormuz kept closed "further day by day" until its conditions were met, and Iranian forces had struck American bases in Bahrain and Kuwait several times in recent days. On 22 November Trump said strikes could resume after the New Year; on 23 November Qatar's prime minister met Rubio and Vance in Washington to revive talks. [cite:wa_aje_deadlock] [cite:wa_euronews_hormuz]

The war began without a declaration or authorisation from Congress, on the administration's claim of self-defence. [cite:wiki_iran_war_2026] Who wanted it, Netanyahu, Trump or both, is argued in The Iran Architecture; the decisions recorded here were Washington's.
