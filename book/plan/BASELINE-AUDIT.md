# The Baseline — Full Audit (14 November 2026)

## 1. Bottom line

The research was not lost. It exists in three places: the simulation's 24 baseline scenes, the Baseline book's 35 sections (about 71,000 words, 83 figures, 693 sources), and the original **Book Zero, "How the Story Is Taught"** (v1.2, 329 pages, about 65,000 words) in the israelbooks repository. The new Baseline was built from the simulation scenes. **Book Zero was never carried into it.** Book Zero was the education book: the Maxwell opening, Jewish schools, the trips, Who Holds the Pen, the nation-by-nation chapters, and the 2024–26 curriculum chapters. The new Baseline compressed all of that into roughly three sections (*How the Story Is Taught*, *The Same History, Read Around the World* and *The Holocaust in the World's Classrooms*).

Measured against Book Zero's 47 chapters, by whether each chapter's distinctive names, institutions, numbers and dates appear in the Baseline:

- **8 chapters fully carried** (60% or more of their distinctive record present)
- **21 chapters thin** (35–59%)
- **18 chapters missing** (under 35%)

The Baseline is strongest on words, peoples and the land (Parts 2–5). It is weakest exactly where Ron said: **how the story is taught, by whom, and in which countries**.
