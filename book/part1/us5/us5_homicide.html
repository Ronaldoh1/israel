Homicide Over Time

Homicide is the most reliably counted crime in either country, because a body is hard to leave out of the record. Even so, the two countries count it differently. In the United States the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) compiles police reports and the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) compiles death certificates, and both series run back for decades. In Israel the police publish counts of murder victims, the Central Bureau of Statistics records causes of death, and since the mid-2010s a civil-society group, the Abraham Initiatives, has kept a running count of Arab citizens killed in violence and crime. Their totals for the same year differ by a few per cent because of definitions: whether police killings are included, whether East Jerusalem residents are counted, and when a death is recorded. The United States: a fall, a spike and a record low

The American homicide rate stood at 10.2 per 100,000 people in 1980, the highest figure in the series compiled by the Council on Criminal Justice (CCJ). It fell through the 1990s and reached 4.4 in 2014. It then rose to 6.6 in 2020, the highest since 1997, when the number of homicides jumped by about 30 per cent in a single year, the largest one-year rise the FBI has recorded. [cite:us5_ccj_trends] [cite:us5_fox_fbi2025] The rate reached 6.8 in 2021 by The Crime Index's figures, and then fell. [cite:us5_npr_lowmurder] According to FBI data released in August 2026, it was 4.1 in 2025, after an 18.1 per cent fall in murders from 2024. That ties 1955 and 1956 as the lowest rate on record and is 60 per cent below 1980. [cite:us5_fox_fbi2025] [cite:us5_ccj_trends] The decline continued into 2026. Murders were down 18.7 per cent in the first four months of 2026 compared with the same months of 2025, and the FBI reported further falls for the first half of the year. [cite:us5_npr_lowmurder] [cite:us5_fox_fbi2025] Analysts do not agree on why. "What's driving it is about as clear as a glass of milk," said Adam Gelb of the CCJ, noting falls in cities with very different policing strategies. President Trump credited his administration: "We ended the war on police, and we declared a very strong war on crime." [cite:us5_fox_fbi2025] Even at its record low, the American rate was about double Canada's 1.9. [cite:us5_npr_lowmurder]

Guns account for most American homicides. Firearms were used in at least 59 per cent of homicides every year from 1976 to 2025, in 77 per cent in 2021 and in at least 71 per cent every year since 2015. [cite:us5_ccj_trends] The burden is also unequal. From 1976 to 2025 the homicide victimisation rate for Black Americans averaged about eight times the rate for white Americans, and about ten times in 2020. [cite:us5_ccj_trends] That gap is measured where American policing, housing segregation and the concentration of gun violence in particular neighbourhoods overlap. Part Four covers the policing side. [scene:us4_policing|Part Four: policing]

Israel: low overall, then a break in 2023

Israel's national homicide rate has long been among the lower rates in the OECD. Israel Police counted 135 murders in 2009 and 103 homicide victims in 2018, a rate of about 1.1 per 100,000. [cite:us5_wiki_crime] A Taub Center study of February 2024 gives a crude rate of 1.56 in 2019, which placed Israel 10th of 33 OECD countries; the United States, at 5.40 that year, was 4th. [cite:us5_taub_homicide] The study's series then shows 1.59 in 2020, 1.84 in 2021 and 1.50 in 2022, followed by a sudden doubling to 3.04 in 2023. [cite:us5_taub_homicide] Over these years the national figure averaged two very different rates. Among Arab citizens the rate was 4.90 in 2019 and 11.11 in 2023. Among Jewish and other Israelis it was 0.67 in 2019 and 0.85 in 2023. Taub's authors put the ratio between the two at about four to one until around 2015, about ten to one in 2020–2022 and thirteen to one in 2023. They called the rise "a sign of institutional failure". [cite:us5_taub_homicide] Israel Police data released to the Association for Freedom of Information show the same split in absolute numbers. From 1 January 2018 to 7 October 2023, 981 people were killed: 712 Arab citizens, 214 Jewish Israelis, 50 foreign citizens and 5 of unknown origin. Arab citizens, about a fifth of the population, made up 73 per cent of the victims. [cite:us5_aawsat_police]

No comparable Israeli series back to 1980 was found for this book. The figures above begin in 2009 for police counts and 2019 for Taub's rates, so the long American series stands beside a short Israeli one. The World Health Organization and the UN Office on Drugs and Crime hold longer ICD-coded series that would close this gap.
