Part One states the question this volume exists to answer: whether the Holy See's public refusal of Zionism, from Pius X's "Non possumus" to Theodor Herzl in January 1904 until the Fundamental Agreement with Israel of 30 December 1993, was a real policy or a front for private support. It sets out the public record of that refusal and its end, turns the front hypothesis already framed in The Divine 3+1 into five tests that can be checked against the private record, the timing of the Holy See's changes of position, its treaties, the flow of money and favours, and the cost of its opposition, and fixes in advance what result would count for each side. It then explains the volume's vocabulary, separating the Holy See, Vatican City State and the Catholic Church, and Zionism, the State of Israel and Judaism; the few labelled places where interpretation is offered; and the route through the nineteen parts.
