# HILLARY RODHAM CLINTON — NETWORK MAP (Draft 1)
### Documented professional, financial, and political ties, by era. Companion to the other three files.

**How this map works:** every entry names a real, documented tie and *what kind* of tie it is —
contract, employment, funding, appointment, board seat, advocacy relationship. Tiers describe how
direct the documentation is, not how much "power" a node has:

- **DIRECT** — a documented, named, individual-to-individual or individual-to-institution
  relationship (employment, client, board seat, appointment, personal friendship with financial
  dimension).
- **INSTITUTIONAL** — a documented relationship where she engaged with an institution as a whole
  (a firm's client roster, a lobbying organization, a party committee) rather than a single person.
- **SECONDARY** — a documented but one-step-removed connection (her advisor's other client; a
  connection's business partner) — named because it's relevant context, not because she personally
  transacted with it.

**What this map deliberately does not do:** it does not use "power structure," "apex," or
"mechanism" framing implying coordinated hidden control — the evidence for any single tie below
supports "this relationship existed and worked like X," not "a hidden system operated through her."
It also never establishes a tie through religious, ethnic, or national-origin identity — every
entry below is a contract, a paycheck, a donation, an appointment, or a vote.

## READING THIS MAP: WHAT ACTUALLY HOLDS UP AS "NETWORK" AND WHAT'S THINNER

**Strongest, most concretely documented threads** (real contracts/employment/funding, multiple
independent sources): the Rose Law Firm → Tyson/McDougal/Madison Guaranty cluster (1977–1992); the
Rose-to-White-House personnel pipeline (1993); the DNC Joint Fundraising Agreement + Brazile
(2015–16).

**Real but thinner** (documented, but from fewer independent sources or with unresolved gaps):
the cattle-futures profit mechanism (no proven quid pro quo, improbable returns never fully
explained); the Clinton Foundation donor-overlap (documented overlap, unproven causation); the
Wall Street speech fees (not yet deep-dived at all).

**Asked-about but not found:** any Epstein tie to her personally; any Israel-related tie beyond
ordinary advocacy-organization speeches and voting record.
