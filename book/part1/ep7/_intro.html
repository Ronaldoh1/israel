Part Seven asks the question that has drawn more attention than any other in this case: was Epstein working for someone above him? The question is a fair one. His fortune did not begin with him, his protection was unusual, and he spent his life close to people who held power. But it has mostly been answered with assertion, in both directions.

This part answers it the slow way. It begins by walking backward from Epstein along every line that reached him, a job, an introduction, money, a tax status, a legal shield, to see where each one leads and where the record stops. It then examines, on the same evidence standard, the specific claims that an intelligence service was involved: what is documented, what is reported, what is one person's word, and what has been shown to be false.
