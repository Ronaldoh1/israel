The Tests

Each test removes part of the documented network and recalculates the whole map. The figures below are the chance the operation runs as documented, 1991–2019, and the lowest and highest the same test gives when every weight the map relies on is varied: each of the seven channels at half and at double its weight, reported ties at 30 and at 100 percent, the $1 million default at a quarter and at four times that, unstated banking years at one and at ten, the weight of cover, and the decision weights.

Money and banking are what it could not run without. Without Wexner, the money the record quantifies for 1991 to 2011 shrinks to a few million dollars; without Wexner and Black together, the operation could not have been paid for. Without any bank, the money, the payroll and the property costs have no route. These are the clearest findings, and they hold under every weighting, though their exact size does not: the ranges are wide, and the published figure sits in the middle of each.

One bank alone, or one prosecutor, was replaceable. Deutsche Bank took him on in 2013 when JPMorgan let him go; removing the US Attorney who approved the 2008 deal barely moves the deal's chance, because the push for it came from a large defence team and from inside the office.

Without Maxwell, the recruitment a court proved mostly disappears. Recruitment is counted only from convictions: hers (December 2021, five of six counts), for recruiting and grooming girls for him from 1994 to 2004, and his own 2008 Florida plea for procuring a minor in Palm Beach in 2004 and 2005. People named but never charged are not counted. Without her, ten of the twelve years those convictions cover have no proven recruiter, and the operation falls to 60%. The money, the accounts and the properties were his and stay. The range is wide (39–74%) because it depends on how heavily recruitment is weighed against the other channels.

The reputation work served his standing, not the running of the operation. Weighing access and reputation at half or at double moves that test by less than a point.
