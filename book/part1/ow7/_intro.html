Part Seven follows the relationship between Israel and the US armed forces, from the strategic-cooperation agreements of the 1980s to the war with Iran in 2026. It begins with the framework: the joint planning groups set up in 1983, Israel's designation as a major non-NATO ally, the American weapons stockpile stored on Israeli soil, the American radar on a Negev hilltop, and the 2021 move of Israel into US Central Command. It then takes the services one by one, the Army, the Navy, the Air Force and the Marine Corps, and the exercises that join them to Israel's forces. It ends with the years in which American forces fought in Israel's defence: shooting down Iranian drones and missiles in 2024 and 2025, and striking Iran alongside Israel in 2026. For each, it sets out the official American rationale and what the United States has gained, as well as what it has spent.
