The Command: How Decisions Are Made

Israelis vote for party lists, not for a prime minister. After an election the president, whose office is otherwise largely ceremonial, consults the parties and gives the member most likely to command 61 of the 120 seats 28 days to form a government, extendable by 14; if that fails, another member is tried, and then a new election follows. [cite:gov_wiki] [cite:pm_brookings] [cite:president_time] Five elections were held between 2019 and 2022 because no one could reach 61. [cite:pm_brookings] In a system where the threshold is low and the largest party rarely wins more than a quarter of the seats, a party of six can decide who governs.

War decisions

Decisions on war are taken by the security cabinet, a ministerial committee, on the advice of the army, the Shin Bet and the Mossad. [cite:wiki_security_cabinet_israel] After 7 October 2023 Benny Gantz brought his party into an emergency government, and a war cabinet was formed on 11 October with three voting members, Netanyahu, Gallant and Gantz, and observers. [cite:warcabinet_wiki] [cite:netanyahu_war_cabinet_oct2023] Gantz left in June 2024, demanding a plan for Gaza after the war, and Netanyahu dissolved the war cabinet on 17 June. [cite:warcabinet_wiki] On 5 November 2024 he fired Gallant again, this time for good. [cite:gallant_dismissal_nov2024] Decisions moved back to the security cabinet, where Ben-Gvir and Smotrich sat.

The West Bank shell

The West Bank has never been annexed, so it is not governed by the Knesset's ordinary laws. It is ruled through a military structure: COGAT, a unit of the Defence Ministry set up after 1967, and within it the Civil Administration, created in 1981, which issues permits and controls planning for Palestinians in Area C. [cite:wiki_cogat] [cite:wb_wiki_civadmin] Alongside it, the Settlement Division of the World Zionist Organization, a body outside the state, has channelled state funds to settlements since 1971, and according to Peace Now operated for decades without public oversight until a law regulated it in 2016. [cite:peacenow_settlement_division_watch] The 2023 budget gave the Settlement Division about NIS 399 million. [cite:timesofisrael_wb_budget_2023] Since 2023, the authority over the Civil Administration has sat with Smotrich. [cite:smotrich_planning_authority]
