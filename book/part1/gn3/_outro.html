The Apocryphon of John, written before about 180, describes a world made by an ignorant lesser god who declares "there is no other God beside me" and rules through archons, while humanity carries a stolen light that knowledge can awaken. [cite:vt24_apjohn] [cite:vt25_iren_129] The rulers appear as gatekeepers in Origen's diagram and in rites for the dying. [cite:vt24_origen_cc6] [cite:vt24_ah1_21] Some texts turned the critique on the church itself, calling bishops and deacons "dry canals", and Pagels read the whole system as a justification for disobeying bishops, a reading her critics contest. [cite:vt25_wiki_gapocpeter] [cite:vt24_enc_pagels] The Apocryphon ends by cursing anyone who sells its teaching. [cite:vt24_apjohn] Part Four turns to the other great body of Gnostic writing: the sayings and sacraments.
