The record shows Israel has opened at least two of its major wars. On June 5, 1967, Israel struck first, destroying most of the Egyptian air force on the ground within hours; its initial claim that Egypt had attacked first was false, and US intelligence knew it at the time. [cite:war1967_adl] The threat was real: Egypt had expelled UN peacekeepers, blockaded the Strait of Tiran and massed 100,000 troops on the border. Whether that justified striking first is still argued by lawyers and historians. Israeli historians such as Tom Segev also document planning that predated the crisis. [cite:segev_1967]

In 1982, Israel invaded Lebanon after its ambassador in London was shot by the Abu Nidal Organization, a rival and enemy of the PLO. Told this by the Shin Bet, General Rafael Eitan replied, "Abu Nidal, Abu Shmidal. We have to strike at the PLO!" [cite:war1982_eitan] Real grievances existed, including 270 PLO attacks despite a ceasefire, but "only responds" does not fit the record.
