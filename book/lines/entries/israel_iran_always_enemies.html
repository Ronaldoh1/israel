Before 1979, Israel and Iran were quiet partners, and their dealings outlasted the revolution. In 1968 the two governments set up the Eilat Ashkelon Pipeline Company as a 50/50 joint venture, so secret that Israel's confidentiality order on it was still being renewed in January 2023. [cite:wiki_eapc] Iranian oil kept reaching Israel during the 1973 war. The alliance itself followed the 1953 CIA–MI6 coup that restored the Shah; Israel had no documented role in that coup. [cite:nsarchive_cia_confirms_role]

The revolution turned the pipeline into a legal dispute; in 2016 a tribunal ordered Israel to pay Iran over $1.1 billion. Yet Israel still sold the Islamic Republic roughly $500 million in arms between 1980 and 1983, mostly paid for in oil. [cite:wiki_iran_israel_relations] In 1982 Defense Minister Ariel Sharon said Israel had "a vital interest" in "Iran's victory" over Iraq. On August 20, 1985, Israel delivered 100 American-made TOW missiles to Iran, a deal that became part of the Iran-Contra affair. [cite:jewishcurrents_israel_sells_iran] The open enmity is real, but it is newer than the line suggests.
