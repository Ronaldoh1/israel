The Election of 2006

On 25 January 2006 Palestinians in Gaza, the West Bank and East Jerusalem voted for a new Legislative Council. Hamas, running as "Change and Reform", won 74 of the 132 seats with 44.45 percent of the national list vote; Fatah won 45 seats with 41.43 percent. The international observer mission of the National Democratic Institute and the Carter Center praised "a professional and impartial performance of election officials". [cite:hm_wiki_2006elect]

The Quartet of the United States, the European Union, Russia and the UN set three conditions for dealing with a Hamas government: recognize Israel, accept previous agreements and renounce violence. Hamas refused. Israel stopped transferring about $50 million a month in customs revenue it collected for the Palestinian Authority and barred Hamas legislators from travelling between Gaza and the West Bank. After militants from Gaza captured the soldier Gilad Shalit in June 2006, Israel arrested 8 Hamas ministers and 26 Hamas legislators in the West Bank on 29 June.
