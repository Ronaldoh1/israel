# The Library of Truth: showcase poster

A standalone poster for the whole library, 1080×1920 (9:16), in the same new "Library of Truth" theme as the Baseline, Road to 1948 and Network Map sets.

- Figures are counted from the library itself (5 November 2026): 26 ready volumes (18 in The Israel Architecture, 8 in the Dark Enlightenment Series), 8,939 distinct citations, about 1.6 million words, 2,093 maps, charts, tables and timelines, and 3 interactive maps (the Road to 1948 network, the Epstein network and the Semitic language atlas).
- Version A lists every ready volume. Version B keeps only the six featured books, for a cleaner look if Version A comes out crowded.
- CTA: www.tinyurl.com/thetruthlibrary · TikTok @chosenron13 · Instagram @chosen.ron13.
