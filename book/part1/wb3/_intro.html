Part Three covers the first decade of Israeli rule, from the war of June 1967 to the change of government in 1977. It begins with the war's effects in the West Bank: a second exodus across the Jordan, villages emptied and razed, and a UN resolution whose missing word has been argued over ever since. It follows the annexation of East Jerusalem and its enlarged boundary, and ends with the first settlements, built after the government's own legal adviser warned that they would breach the Fourth Geneva Convention.
