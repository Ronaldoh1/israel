Overall scores: pending. Findings are live; final scores release state by state. Why?
Every overall score depends on public records we are still pulling: federal court dockets, the Justice Department’s foreign-agent (FARA) database and some state campaign-finance filings. We don’t estimate, assume or fill gaps. A missing record is never counted as clean, so a candidate’s overall score publishes only once every record behind it has actually been searched. Until then you can see everything found so far: the money, votes, claims checked against the record, legal history and each part score. Final scores will be released as each state’s searches finish.
