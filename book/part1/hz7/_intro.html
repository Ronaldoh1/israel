Part Seven covers the fourteen months from 8 October 2023, when Hezbollah opened what it called a support front for Gaza, to the ceasefire of 27 November 2024. It sets out who fired how often, by the count of an independent monitor and by Israel's own count; the people displaced on both sides of the border; the deaths of a Reuters journalist in southern Lebanon and of twelve children on a football pitch in the Golan Heights, with each side's account; the pager and radio detonations of September 2024, how they were carried out and the legal argument they started; the air campaign, the killing of Hassan Nasrallah and the ground invasion that followed; the toll by source; and what the ceasefire text said, beside the American letter to Israel that was reported to go with it.
