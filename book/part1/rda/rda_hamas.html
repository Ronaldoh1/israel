Hamas

The Muslim Brotherhood was founded in Egypt in 1928 and opened branches in Gaza in the 1940s. After 1967 Ahmed Yassin rebuilt it there as a network of mosques and charities, which Israel licensed in 1979 and largely left alone while it fought the PLO. [cite:hm_tamimi_2007] [cite:hm_wsj_spawn] Some of the Israeli officials involved later called that a mistake; others said Israel never funded or armed it. [cite:hm_intercept_2018]

The uprising against the occupation began in December 1987, and Hamas was founded in its first weeks, by Yassin and six others. [cite:wiki_first_intifada] [cite:hm_wsj_spawn] It won the Palestinian legislative election of January 2006, was cut off by the Quartet and Israel, and took Gaza by force in June 2007; no national election has been held since. [cite:hm_wiki_2006elect] [cite:gz_wiki_battle2007] From 2018 Israel let Qatari cash into Gaza. Smotrich and a reported Netanyahu remark tied that policy to keeping the Palestinians divided; Netanyahu denies it, and the Shin Bet says some of the money reached Hamas's military build-up. [cite:wb_wiki_smotrich] [cite:hm_fdd_shinbet]

Like Hezbollah, Hamas has two documented roots: a region-wide Islamist movement that predates the occupation, and the uprising against the occupation in which it took up arms. [cite:hm_pbs_baconi] The Hamas Architecture tells this in full.
