**How to read these drafts.**
- Every fact carries its source. Where the source is a news report rather than a document, the entry says so.
- **[verify]** marks a fact that must be checked against the primary document (an EFTA page, a court record, a filing) before the chapter is set.
- **[re-check]** marks a status that changes over time (a police investigation, a lawsuit). It must be checked again on the day the book is locked.
- Tiers are the book's own: **adjudicated** (a court decided it), **sworn** (testimony under oath), **association** (documents show contact; no wrongdoing established), **allegation** (a named accuser; not tested in court), **not carried** (circulates, but the record doesn't support it).
- Book prose comes later. These are the fact files the prose will be written from, in the template's order.

## 1 · Peter Thiel

**Born 11 October 1967, Frankfurt.** At the time of contact (2014–2018): co-founder and chairman of Palantir; founder of Founders Fund; co-founder of Valar Ventures. Today: the same, plus a major Republican donor whose former employee is Vice President.

### 1.1 Before and apart from Epstein
**Early life and education.** Born in West Germany; his family moved to the United States when he was one. Part of his childhood was spent in southern Africa (South Africa and what is now Namibia) before the family settled in California by 1977. Valedictorian, San Mateo High School, 1985. Stanford: BA in philosophy, 1989; co-founded and edited *The Stanford Review* (1987) with David Sacks; president of the Stanford Federalist Society. Stanford Law School, JD 1992, where he met Alex Karp. *(Palantir log #N31, #N138: consistent across independent biographies.)*

**Law, finance and politics, 1992–1998.** Clerked for Judge James Larry Edmondson (11th Circuit); securities lawyer at Sullivan & Cromwell; derivatives trader at Credit Suisse while writing speeches for Education Secretary William Bennett. Co-wrote *The Diversity Myth* (1995) with Sacks. Founded Thiel Capital Management (1996). *(#N138.)*

**PayPal, Palantir, Facebook, 1998–2005.** Co-founded Confinity (1998), which became PayPal; eBay bought PayPal in 2002. Co-founded Palantir on 21 May 2003 with Karp, Joe Lonsdale, Stephen Cohen and Nathan Gettings; seeded it with about $2 million. The CIA's venture arm, In-Q-Tel, invested about $2 million from around 2005 (disclosed in Palantir's 2020 S-1). Facebook's first outside investor in 2004 ($500,000 for 10.2%). Founded Founders Fund in 2005. *(#A1, #N31; S-1 is the primary source.)*

**Control of Palantir.** Palantir's Class F shares sit in a voting trust controlled jointly by Thiel, Karp and Cohen, with Wilmington Trust as trustee. The trio's voting power is set at 49.99%. Thiel has been chairman since 2003. *(#N122: Palantir's own S-1, 10-K and proxy filings, and a Thiel-filed 13G/A.)* This matters for the Epstein chapter because "Palantir" decisions are, by the company's own filings, decisions three men control.

**Stated philosophy.** In "The Education of a Libertarian" (Cato Unbound, 2009) he wrote that he no longer believed "freedom and democracy are compatible" and named the extension of the vote to women in 1920 as part of the problem. He funded the Seasteading Institute. Founders Fund invested in Curtis Yarvin's company Tlon in 2013. *(#N09, #N55; library scenes de_s01, de_s02. Primary source: the essay itself.)*

**Citizenship and tax.** New Zealand granted him citizenship in 2011 after 12 days of physical presence, by ministerial decision. His Roth IRA grew from about $1,700–2,000 to more than $5 billion by 2019, tax-free (ProPublica). *(#N31.)*

**Politics.** About $50 million in political donations since 2000. 2016: RNC delegate and speaker, $1.25 million to Trump, transition committee. 2022: about $35 million across 16 federal races, including $15 million to the PAC backing J.D. Vance, his former employee, and funding primary challengers to House Republicans who voted to impeach Trump. *(#N58: FEC records.)* Vance's tie to Thiel dates to 2011 and runs through five channels: mentor, employer, investor in Vance's Narya Capital, donor, kingmaker.

**Since 2025.** Left California in late 2025 ahead of a proposed billionaire wealth tax (and gave $3 million against it); bought a house in Buenos Aires; met President Milei at the Casa Rosada on 23 April 2026. *(#N89.)* Co-founded the private "Dialog" society with Auren Hoffman in 2006; its leaked member list (2026) includes cabinet officials, tech chiefs and Reid Hoffman. *(#N68.)*

**The one serious biography.** Max Chafkin's *The Contrarian* (2021). Thiel declined to answer its fact-checking questions. *(#N138.)*

## 2 · Peter Mandelson

**Born 21 October 1953, London.** At the time of contact (2002–2011): MP, then EU trade commissioner, then Business Secretary and First Secretary of State. Afterwards: co-founder of the lobbying firm Global Counsel; UK ambassador to the United States (2025). Today: no office; under police investigation.

### 2.1 Before and apart from Epstein
**Background.** Grandson of Herbert Morrison, a Labour cabinet minister of the 1940s. Studied at St Catherine's College, Oxford. Labour's director of communications from 1985, and one of the main architects of "New Labour". **[verify dates against Who's Who / Parliament record]**

**Parliament and two resignations.** MP for Hartlepool, 1992–2004. Entered Tony Blair's cabinet in 1998 as Trade and Industry Secretary, and resigned that December over an undisclosed £373,000 home loan from fellow minister Geoffrey Robinson, whose business affairs his department was investigating. Returned as Northern Ireland Secretary in 1999 and resigned again in January 2001 over contacts on a passport application by the businessman Srichand Hinduja. A later independent inquiry (Sir Anthony Hammond) found he had not acted improperly. **[verify against Hansard and the Hammond report]** Both resignations matter for this entry: by 2002, when the Epstein friendship begins, Mandelson had twice left office over his dealings with rich men.

**Europe and Brown's cabinet.** EU Trade Commissioner, 2004–2008. Made a life peer in 2008 and brought back by Gordon Brown as Business Secretary (2008–2010), and also First Secretary of State (2009–2010). These are the years in which the files show him passing government information to Epstein.

**Global Counsel.** Co-founded the lobbying and advisory firm Global Counsel in 2010; president and chair of its advisory board. In 2018 Palantir hired Global Counsel as its UK lobbyist. *(Library epn_s04; Palantir log #N18.)*

**Ambassador.** Announced as ambassador to Washington in December 2024; in post from February 2025.
- The Cabinet Office's own due-diligence note (11 Dec. 2024) said his Global Counsel role "would have to cease". He kept a shareholding of about 21%. *(#N18.)*
- UK Security Vetting recommended **refusing** him clearance (a nine-page summary, Jan. 2025). It named concerns about China, Russia (Oleg Deripaska), Israel (bimonthly contact with former military-intelligence chief Tamir Hayman, who denies any connection) and one unnamed person. Clearance was granted anyway. *(#N78, from named Guardian sources: the summary itself is not public. [verify])*
- On 27 February 2025 he went with Prime Minister Starmer to Palantir's Washington office while Palantir was a Global Counsel client. No minutes exist, the visit isn't in the PM's register, and the Foreign Office told FOI requesters it holds no emails about it. *(#N18; Good Law Project / Foxglove FOI responses.)*

## 3 · Ehud Barak

**Born 12 February 1942, Mishmar HaSharon (born Ehud Brog).** At the time of contact (about 2003–2019): former prime minister; defence minister 2007–2013; afterwards a private investor and broker of security technology. Today: no office.

### 3.1 Before and apart from Epstein
**Military career.** Commander of the special-forces unit Sayeret Matkal. Head of military intelligence (Aman), 1983–1985. Chief of the General Staff, 1991–1995. **[verify against IDF / Knesset biography]** He is one of the most decorated soldiers in Israel's history, and his public authority on security came from this career.

**Politics, 1995–2001.** Interior and then foreign minister (1995–96). Labour leader from 1997; prime minister from July 1999 to March 2001.
- Talks with Syria reached a draft at Shepherdstown (January 2000) and failed in Geneva (March 2000) over a few hundred metres of the Sea of Galilee shoreline. *(Library sy_s06.)*
- Withdrew Israeli forces from southern Lebanon in May 2000. In 2006 he said of Lebanon: "it was our presence there that created Hezbollah". *(Library sq_s03.)*
- Led Israel's side at Camp David (11–25 July 2000). There are three documented accounts of what was offered and why it failed. Clinton credited Barak and blamed Arafat; Robert Malley and Hussein Agha recorded that no offer was put on paper. *(Library cd_s01.)*
- Lost the February 2001 election to Ariel Sharon.

**Defence minister, 2007–2013.** Served under Olmert and then Netanyahu, through Operation Cast Lead (2008–09) and Operation Pillar of Defense (2012). Pillar of Defense opened with the killing of Hamas commander Ahmed Jabari on 14 November 2012. The Israeli mediator Gershon Baskin documented that Jabari had received a draft long-term ceasefire hours earlier, and the historian Reuven Pedatzur wrote that those talks went on with Barak's knowledge. *(Library iaa_s09.)* Left government in March 2013.

**After office: the security-technology business.**
- **Carbyne (formerly Reporty):** an emergency-call data company he chaired. Epstein's money reached it through Barak's investment partnership. **[verify the amount: library and log say about $1 million]** Thiel's Founders Fund led its 2018 round.
- **FST Biometrics:** Barak and a partner invested $15 million in 2015. Its founder is the former military-intelligence chief Aharon Ze'evi-Farkash. *(Library epn_s02.)*
- **Paragon Solutions:** an Israeli spyware firm (2019) whose Graphite tool ICE contracted for in 2024. The library calls Barak a co-founder; other reporting calls him an investor and board member. **[verify his exact role before the entry is set]** *(Library ice_s04.)*
