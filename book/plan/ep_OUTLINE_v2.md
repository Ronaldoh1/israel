## The Network, the Files and Power Protects Power
### Outline v2.0, with a blind-spot check for every part · 31 October 2026

**What changed from v1.**
- Ten research files are folded in:
  - `ep_DEATHS_LEDGER`, `ep_OPERATIONS`, `ep_PATTERNS`, `ep_EVIDENCE`, `ep_SOURCES_COMPANIES`;
  - `ep_RECONCILIATION`, `ep_LEGAL_ARCHITECTURE`, `ep_ALLEGATIONS_REGISTER`, `ep_FILES_REGISTER`;
  - the network map.
- The parts are renumbered (13 parts plus back matter).
- Every part ends with **Blind spots**: what is missing, what could be wrong, and what would weaken the argument. Each is marked:
  - **[gap]**: not yet researched;
  - **[verify]**: a claim in hand that still needs its primary document;
  - **[counter]**: the strongest opposing evidence, which the book must carry;
  - **[risk]**: a legal, ethical or framing hazard.

**The book's question.** A man pleaded guilty to a sex crime involving a child in 2008. Afterwards:
- the most powerful people in the world kept him close;
- banks kept his accounts;
- a Justice Department immunised people it never named;
- in 2026 the government still withholds about half of what it found.

What kept that system running? Each part asks this of one layer: the man, the operation, the money, the people, the image, the agencies, the law, the files, the aftermath, the deaths, the precedents, and the verdict.

**Subject type.** A composite with a person-type spine. Agencies, banks and DOJ are institution-type components. Any claim about a named individual runs at the strictest ceiling in the protocol.

**The rules, stated once in the introduction:**
1. **Four tiers for a named person:**
   - adjudicated;
   - sworn allegation;
   - documented association;
   - not carried.

   A claim that someone took part in a crime needs court records, sworn testimony or case-file documents.
2. **Being in the files is not a finding.** Say what each appearance *is*: an email, a calendar entry, a flight, a payment, or a tip.
3. **What they said vs. what the files show**, side by side with dates. This is a contradiction on the record, not a finding about motive.
4. **The slide test** on every intelligence, finance and Israel passage. A named official, firm or state act may stay. Drift to an ethnic or religious group is cut.
5. **The same standard for every party**, act by act.
6. **The survivors are the subject.**
   - Abuse is described no more graphically than the courts and survivors themselves describe it.
   - Survivors are named only if they went public.
   - Every death gets its official finding first.
   - Chapters that touch on suicide end with a resource line.
