The Readiness Audit counted what has been built. This part asks what would happen to the people living under it if the end state the texts describe were actually enforced. It separates three layers: the end state in Yarvin's and Land's own words, the machinery American law already contains as of October 2026, and the gap between them. It then climbs a ladder of consequences, from being watched to compelled labour, rung by rung; goes domain by domain through what the state can already deny a person, from a bank account to a passport; sets thirteen constitutional rights beside what the texts say about each; and ends with the texts' harshest subject, people judged unproductive, before testing the worst-case reading against the whole record.
