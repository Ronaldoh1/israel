Camp David, 2000

From 11 to 25 July 2000 President Clinton hosted Prime Minister Ehud Barak and Yasser Arafat at Camp David to reach a final agreement. It ended without one, and the participants have given three accounts of why. [cite:jvl_camp_david_overview]

Clinton publicly credited Barak and blamed Arafat, although he had assured Arafat there would be no finger-pointing. [cite:modeldiplomat_camp_david] In December 2000 he set out the Clinton Parameters, proposing about 94 to 97 percent of the West Bank with land swaps; both sides answered with reservations, and talks at Taba in January 2001 ended without agreement as Israel's election approached. [cite:jvl_camp_david_overview] [cite:rethinking_camp_david_myth]

Every account agrees on where the talks broke: the holy site in Jerusalem, the refugees and the borders. They are the same three questions still unresolved.
