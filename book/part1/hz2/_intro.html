Part Two covers the three years in which Hezbollah came into being. It begins with Israel's invasion of Lebanon in June 1982: the shooting in London that Israel cited, the aim it announced and the distance its army actually went, the siege of Beirut, the departure of the PLO and the massacre at Sabra and Shatila. It then follows Iran's Revolutionary Guards into the Bekaa Valley and the Lebanese clerics and breakaway militiamen who joined them, setting out Iran's role as Iranian officials, American courts and Hezbollah itself have described it. It records the 1983 bombings of the US embassy and of the American and French barracks in Beirut, who claimed them, what US judges later found and who denied what. It ends with the Open Letter of February 1985, in which the movement announced itself, and with what changed when Hezbollah rewrote its programme in 2009.
