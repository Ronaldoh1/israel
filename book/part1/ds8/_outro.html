The community at Qumran taught that two spirits struggle in every human heart, sang itself into the temple of heaven and called its own council a holy of holies. [cite:ds_vermes_1qs] [cite:ds_wiki_sabbath] Its Teacher claimed a hidden meaning in every prophecy, a method that can open a text or close a mind. [cite:ds_enc_dss] The reading is the reader's; the scrolls, now open to anyone, are the record. The next book on this shelf turns to an inner tradition with its own ladder of ascent: Kabbalah.
