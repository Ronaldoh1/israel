Part Nine is a reading. The eight parts before it set out what the Gnostic texts say, who wrote and copied them, and who buried, sold and finally published them. This part reads the same texts the way the inner traditions read them: the rulers of this world as rulers within, the ladder of emanations as a map of consciousness, the bridal chamber as the union of opposites, and the knowing that the texts call gnosis. It ends with a note on the word that modern readers most often attach to all this, the observer, and on what physics actually says about it. Each section starts from the passage, sourced, then says what the reading proposes and what the record does not show.
