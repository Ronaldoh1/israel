The arms relationship began with the Hawk on credit in 1962 and became statutory with the Emergency Security Assistance Act of 1973, passed 66 to 9 after the Senate set aside a condition tied to Resolution 242. [cite:ow1_kramer_hawk] [cite:ow1_vv_rs587] [cite:ow1_vv_rs583] Between 1981 and 2008 the Reagan and later administrations built a strategic memorandum, a stockpile, a free trade agreement, major non-NATO ally status, early disbursement into a Federal Reserve account and a statutory qualitative military edge. [cite:ow1_wiki_1981isr] [cite:ow1_jinsa_wrsa] [cite:ow_crs_aid2025] After 2009 Congress declared Israel a major strategic partner without a recorded vote against, the 2016 memorandum pledged $38 billion, and the war after October 2023 brought the largest transfers since 1973, partly by emergency declaration. [cite:ow_jvl_2014act] [cite:ow1_wh_mou2016] [cite:ow1_state_rubio2025] The cooperative programmes show real American returns in technology and industry, and the asymmetry lies in money, terms and law rather than in the joint programmes themselves.
