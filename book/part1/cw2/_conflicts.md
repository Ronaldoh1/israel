# Stage Two (cw2): source contradictions presented in the prose

- Qibya, who carried it out: Ben-Gurion's broadcast of 19 Oct 1953 (border settlers; "not a single Army unit") vs Resolution 101, the State Department statement of 18 Oct, Sharett's diary and Sharon's own account (Israeli regular forces under Sharon). (cw2_unit101_qibya)
- Qibya toll: 69 killed (Khalidi and Caplan; JVL) vs 66 (Glubb); 45 houses blown up (Khalidi and Caplan) vs 50 (JVL). (cw2_unit101_qibya)
- Qibya, what the raiders knew: planning option of "maximum harm to the villagers" (Khalidi and Caplan; JVL) vs Sharon's later statement that he did not know the houses were occupied. (cw2_unit101_qibya)
- Banat Yaacov aid sum: about $26 million released (Press Release 604) vs "in the neighborhood of $100,000,000" (Dulles, 20 Oct 1953) vs $50–60 million (his aide). (cw2_banat_yaacov)
- Johnston plan, Israel's share: Main plan 394 vs 393 million m³ (two AquaPedia tables); Unified plan 400 vs residue of about 409 (AquaPedia). (cw2_johnston)
- Iran consortium: "ended Iranian national control" (ourhistory account) vs a settlement negotiated with Iran's government, ratified by its Senate, acknowledging "national aspirations" (FRUS; Foreign Office). (ira_s00b)
- SAVAK founding: Mossad as co-founder (ira_s00b scene) vs first documented Israeli contact an offer from Bakhtiar in September 1957 (Stage One sources). (ira_s00b)
- Sharett's diaries: a deliberate strategy of provocation, Sharett powerless (Rokach 1980) vs a real cabinet contest Sharett often won (Caplan; Shlaim). (cw2_sharett_ben_gurion)
- Motto law numbering: Public Law 84-140, also putting the motto on paper money (HISTORY.com) vs No. 140 the currency act of 11 July 1955 and the motto Public Law 851 of 30 July 1956 (Statutes at Large). (cw2_pledge)
- Cyrus's conquest of Babylon: 593 BCE (Judis) vs 539 BCE (standard date). (cw2_pledge)
- Docherty's inclusion of Muslims: paraphrased by The Daily Signal (2026), no verbatim text found; his recorded words speak of "the Christian ethic". (cw2_pledge)
- Nahhalin toll: 9 killed, 8 men and a woman (Jordan's complaint) vs 6 in the village plus 3 Arab Legion soldiers on the road (the Commission's resolution). (cw2_border_1954)
- Byroade's Dayton speech: 9 April 1954 (State Department memorandum) vs 19 April 1954 (Medoff). (cw2_border_1954)
- Bat Galim: Egypt's charge that the crew killed two fishermen vs the Commission's "unfounded" finding, then withdrawn by its Special Committee. (cw2_border_1954)
- Susannah casualties: none (Ynet 2005; Singer, Jewish Press 2022) vs deaths in the Alexandria fire of 2 July 1954 (Raseef22). (lv_s01)
- Susannah targets on 14 July: a British-owned theatre included (Singer) vs cinemas among the devices planned for 23 July (Raseef22). (lv_s01)
- Susannah, who gave the order: Lavon (denied any knowledge) vs Gibli (verbal instruction from Lavon), with Dayan blaming the minister. (lv_s01)
- Susannah, which service: military intelligence, Aman (Defense Ministry archive) vs "the Mossad's first operation in Egypt" (Raseef22 headline). (lv_s01)
- Susannah accused: 11 arrested vs 10 accounted for by verdict, plus 2 who died in custody; Bennett's death before the trial session (Raseef22) vs in prison (the older summary). (lv_s01)
- Angleton and the liaison: formal liaison and Angleton managing it from 1951 (wrmea/Cronin account in cia_s03) vs Angleton heading the Counterintelligence Staff from 1954 (Robarge). (cw2_angleton, cia_s03)
- Khrushchev's speech: passed to the Shin Bet (Siskind) vs the Mossad, or both (NEWSru.co.il, 2007). (cw2_angleton)
- Eden's speech of 9 Nov 1955: the "Mansion House" speech (US Embassy, London) vs the Guildhall speech (ECF database). (cw2_alpha)
- Bandung invitation: U Nu insisted on inviting Israel (JTA) vs Nehru and U Nu both tried, with Indonesia opposed (Middle East Eye). (cw2_baghdad_bandung)
- Gaza raid toll: 38 Egyptian soldiers and 8 Israelis killed (Defense Ministry archive) vs 37 soldiers and 2 civilians killed, 32 injured (Egypt, S/3365) vs 38 killed, 31 wounded (Shlaim) vs 38 dead, 33 injured (PalQuest). (cw2_gaza_raid)
- Khan Yunis toll: 36 killed, 13 wounded (UNTSO) vs 35 killed, 10 soldiers and 25 refugees, 19 injured (Egypt's letter). (cw2_gaza_raid)
- What turned Nasser to Soviet arms: the Gaza raid (Shlaim; Allen, Oct 1955) vs the Turkish–Iraqi pact (Eban, Oct 1955), against the Soviet record of Nasser's request of 15 June 1954. (cw2_gaza_raid, cw2_czech_arms, cw2_free_officers)
- Mystères in Israel by Sept 1956: 24, "possibly" 48 (State Department) vs 48 (British sources). (cw2_czech_arms)
- Aswan, why the offer was withdrawn: Egypt's conduct and economics (memoranda of 19 July 1956) vs Congress (Bowie; Wilkins, the "real reason") vs Dulles's "impulsive" decision (Hart) vs a trap set by Nasser (Cortada). (cw2_aswan)
- Aswan cost: $1.3 billion (Dulles) vs a "$600 million project under the Russians" (Wilkins). (cw2_aswan)
- Interval to nationalization: "within a matter of ten days" (Bowie) vs seven days, 19 to 26 July (primary record). (cw2_aswan)
