# Stage Three (cw3) research notes: what the sources agree on, where they split, what is open

Verified 14 October 2026. 18 entries, 158 claims, 101 sources after the gap-closing pass of 14 October. The web-search budget ran out mid-research; pages not surfaced by a search could not be fetched, so some primary texts named in the plan were not reached (listed in part1/cw3/_blindspots.md §4).

## cw3_road_to_war_jul_oct_1956
- **Agree:** nationalization on 26 July 1956 after the US–UK aid refusal; SCUA proposed 9 September; Britain signalled force; Israel had faced fedayeen raids and the Tiran blockade.
- **Disagree:** self-defence (Ben-Gurion; the Jewish Agency's 1,300 killed or wounded 1948–56) vs a war of choice whose timing was set by the plot and whose aims included Gaza (Katif Center; Shlaim).
- **Open:** Qalqilya (10 October) figures; the cabinet minute of the October decision (only the Katif paraphrase).

## cw3_sevres_protocol_oct_1956
- **Agree:** the dates, participants, seven articles, three signatures, Eden's destruction order, the Israeli copy's survival and 1996 release.
- **Disagree:** motive and blame (Shlaim's 'French opportunism, Eden's duplicity, Ben-Gurion's paranoia' vs Israel's self-defence framing and Oren).
- **Open:** Eden's exact Commons denial (20 December 1956) not fetched; pre-Sèvres arms deals.

## cw3_kadesh_musketeer_course_casualties
- **Agree:** start on 29 October (IDF: 17:00), Gaza 2 November, Sharm 5 November, Musketeer timings from Keightley, cease-fire 23:59 GMT 6 November, exit 22 December.
- **Disagree:** Israeli dead 150 (early)/170/171/172/189; Egyptian dead ~1,030 to ~1,650; British 16 (despatch) vs 21 (Hansard, 12 December, a later count); Oren's 'at dawn' vs IDF 17:00; Nasser's 'withdrew, not defeated'.
- **Open:** Egyptian official figures; Port Said civilian toll.

## cw3_kafr_qasim_29_october_1956
- **Agree:** curfew moved to 5 p.m., warning about 30–90 minutes before, Border Police shot returning workers, censorship for weeks.
- **Disagree:** the count (47 / 48 / 49 / 'fifty'); mostly the unborn child and rounding. Notice time: 3:30 p.m. order (JVL) vs 4:30 p.m. warning (PalQuest).
- **Open:** testimony details rest on Wikipedia's summary of the record; the released transcripts were not read directly.

## cw3_kafr_qasim_trial_1957_1959
- **Agree:** eight convicted on 16 October 1958; sentences of 17, 15, 15 and 7 years; all freed within about a year or by 1960; Shadmi convicted only of a procedural offence and fined a token sum; the manifestly-unlawful-order doctrine.
- **Disagree:** the fine's wording (10 prutot = 1/100 lira; JVL calls it 1/1000; PalQuest says one pruta); release date (Nov 1959 vs 'by 1960'); number tried (11 vs 13).
- **Open:** the judgment's full text in English.

## cw3_kafr_qasim_sulha_memory_mole
- **Agree:** sulha on 20 November 1957 with 400+ official guests; compensation of IL5,000 per death; records opened 2022 after Raz's case; Peres, Rivlin and Herzog's words; the 2021 bill rejected.
- **Disagree:** whether the killing was linked to an expulsion plan (Levy's testimony, Segev, PalQuest) or the record shows only testimony about intent, the plan's text being redacted.
- **Open:** Plan Mole's own text.

## cw3_un_ceasefire_us_pressure_nov_1956
- **Agree:** US voted with the UN majority against its allies, forced a cease-fire on 6 November, organized UNEF (arrived 15 November).
- **Disagree:** principle and Soviet risk vs financial leverage as the decisive reason.
- **Open:** resolution numbers and votes; sterling and oil details.

## cw3_soviet_threat_hungary_nov_1956
- **Agree:** Soviet threats and Hungary in the same week; Bulganin–Ben-Gurion letters of 5, 8, 15, 17 November.
- **Disagree:** whether Moscow's threat or Washington's pressure ended the war.
- **Open:** the 5 November letters' texts.

## cw3_third_kingdom_retreat_6_8_nov_1956
- **Agree:** 7 November speech ('dead and buried'; no foreign force); Eisenhower's 7 November warning; 8 November reply and cabinet decision to withdraw.
- **Disagree:** what the 'Third Kingdom' rhetoric signified (annexationist/messianic vs rhetoric outrun by policy).
- **Open:** archival text and exact date of the Sharm el-Sheikh letter.

## cw3_gaza_khan_yunis_rafah_nov_1956
- **Agree:** UNRWA's lists of 275 (Khan Yunis) and 111 (Rafah); the Director could not verify; Israel denies a massacre; Morris uses 'massacre'.
- **Disagree:** cause (resistance/riot vs killing of unarmed civilians); totals (Israel 48 for Rafah; Palestinian 415+57 and 197+23; Rantisi 525; The Times 'some 60').
- **Open:** any Israeli investigation (none found); the 1957 mass grave.

## cw3_gaza_occupation_nov1956_mar1957
- **Agree:** ~300,000 people under military government; councils, schools, police rebuilt; Ben-Gurion wanted to keep the Strip; out on 8 March 1957.
- **Disagree:** retention as aim vs security as the condition.
- **Open:** the 13 January 1957 resolution text (Katif paraphrase only).

## cw3_withdrawal_tiran_unef_1957
- **Agree:** Ben-Gurion resisted through February, accepted on 1–2 March on stated assumptions, left Gaza on 8 March; UNEF stayed until May 1967.
- **Disagree:** withdrawal with assurances vs without formal guarantees.
- **Open:** 11 February memorandum, 20 February address, 2 March letter: not fetched directly.

## cw3_aftermath_reckoning_1957
- **Agree:** Eden resigned January 1957; the Eisenhower Doctrine aided named regional states; Nasser's prestige rose.
- **Disagree:** who won (Oren vs Shlaim/Fromkin, as Oren reports them; Hahn).
- **Open:** Doctrine's joint-resolution text, appropriation and votes.

## Gap-closing pass (14 October 2026)

### cw3_un_resolutions_oct1956_feb1957
- **Agree:** vetoes of the US and Soviet drafts; Res. 119 7–2; Res. 997 64–5–6 (noes: Australia, France, Israel, New Zealand, UK); 998 and 1000 57–0–19; 999 59–5–12; 1124 74–2–2.
- **Disagree:** none on the numbers; 1002's 65–1–10 rests on JVL only. UNU's claim that Britain and France 'withdrew within a week' of 997 is wrong (Keightley: 22 December) and is not used.
- **Open:** texts of 998–1001.

### cw3_feb1957_aidememoire_address_sanctions
- **Agree:** the aide-mémoire's terms; the address's wording; Dulles listed reparations, bonds and aid as levers; congressional leaders resisted sanctions.
- **Disagree:** whether this amounted to a sanctions threat (public 'pressure' and named levers vs no sanction imposed, a 'reluctant' President).
- **Open:** Eisenhower's 2 March letter.

### cw3_eisenhower_doctrine_pl85_7
- **Agree:** the text, the $200 million cap from existing funds, the votes 355–61 (30 January) and 72–19 (5 March), signature on 9 March.
- **Disagree:** anti-communism (text) vs replacing Britain and France (Office of the Historian, Hahn).
- **Open:** the Richards mission and the actual allocations.

### cw3_sterling_oil_pressure_nov_dec_1956
- **Agree:** the reserve drain, Humphrey's condition, the $561M drawing and the ~$739M stand-by (Boughton also gives $738M), the oil plan held until withdrawal.
- **Disagree:** whether money was decisive or followed the cease-fire.
- **Open:** exact November reserve loss; any French drawing amount.

### cw3_egypt_jews_1956_1957
- **Agree:** about 1,000 detained; 23,000–25,000 left by 1957–58; about 500 firms sequestered; the 23 November proclamation.
- **Disagree:** causes (state antisemitism vs decolonization and war measures; Beinin vs Julius and Krämer; Mendes in rd6). Legal details come from advocacy sources (JJAC, Julius) and should be attributed as such.
- **Open:** primary texts of Law 391 and Proclamation No. 4.
