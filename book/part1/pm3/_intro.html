Part Three counts the money. It begins with the long, steady record from 1990 to 2020, when pro-Israel contributions came from individuals and several dozen independent PACs and rarely exceeded $15 million a cycle, and follows the rise after 2021, when AIPAC's own committees multiplied the totals. It names the largest donors where the filings do, keeps general party giving by pro-Israel donors out of the pro-Israel totals, and sets the money beside other interests: smaller than finance, real estate or cryptocurrency, larger than any other foreign-policy cause in the record.
