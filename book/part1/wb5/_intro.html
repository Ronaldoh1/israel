Part Five covers the Oslo years, from the handshake of 1993 to the outbreak of the second intifada in 2000. It sets out the map drawn by the Interim Agreement of 1995, which divided the West Bank into Areas A, B and C as a temporary arrangement on the way to a final settlement that never came; what life in Area C, the largest of them, came to mean for Palestinians who wanted to build; and two developments of the same years, the closing of central Hebron after the massacre of 1994 and the spread of settlement outposts that the government's own report later found it had helped to fund.
