# Volume plan: The Hamas Architecture

**Placement:** shelf slot of ch15 ("The Hamas Architecture"), absorbing ch24 (Qatar–Israel–Hamas).
**Companion to:** Gaza, Documented; The West Bank, Documented; PRESS.
**Standard:** Road to 1948 depth, plus the three-source check (simulation, Sophia, israelbooks): see LIBRARY-PROTOCOL.md.

## What Ron asked for (19 Oct 2026)
- How and why Hamas was formed.
- Would it exist without Israel's colonization of Palestine? Would it exist without the split of the West Bank and Gaza and the blocked communication between them? Hamas as a response.
- Hamas's true power: weapons, money, resources, compared with Israel's. Include US aid ($3.8bn a year), other military aid, GDP of Israel vs Gaza, and analogies that make the asymmetry concrete.
- All research from the simulation, Sophia and the israelbooks repo.

## Parts (draft)
1. **Before Hamas.** The Muslim Brotherhood in Gaza from the 1940s; Ahmed Yassin; the Mujama al-Islami licensed by Israel in 1979; what Israeli officials later said about tolerating it.
2. **The Founding, 1987–1988.** The first intifada; Hamas's founding communiqués; the 1988 charter, its text (including its antisemitic articles), quoted and sourced.
3. **Would Hamas Exist? The Counterfactual.** Taken in three parts:
   - The Islamist movement predates 1948.
   - The armed organization was founded in response to the occupation, by its own statements.
   - What the historians argue.
   Both sides are given, and the volume says plainly what cannot be known.
4. **Bombs and Ballots, 1989–2006.** The suicide-bombing campaigns and their victims; Israel's assassinations; Oslo; the 2006 election.
5. **The Split and the Separation.** 2007; the separation policy between Gaza and the West Bank; the blockade; the Qatari money; the Israeli statements on the split. Would Hamas be what it is without them?
6. **Hamas as Its Own Actor.** Rule in Gaza; repression and executions; rockets fired at civilians; the 2017 document.
7. **October 7.**
8. **The Power Ledger.** Money, fighters, weapons and allies, Hamas against Israel. GDPs, budgets, aid, arsenals and analogies.
9. **After the War, 2024–2026.** The leaders killed; the disarmament question; who governs Gaza.
10. **Settled, Contested and Open.**

## Discipline
- Counterfactuals are labelled as arguments. The volume gives the documented basis for each side and does not assert what cannot be known.
- Hamas's atrocities and its charter are stated plainly and sourced. Israel's acts are stated plainly and sourced. No collective attribution to peoples.
- Every figure in the power comparison is sourced and dated, and estimates are labelled with their ranges.
