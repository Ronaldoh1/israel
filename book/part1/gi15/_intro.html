Part Fourteen continues the ledger begun in Part Thirteen, beyond Gaza, the West Bank and Lebanon. It counts what Israel's operations since 2013 have cost in Syria, from the long air campaign against Iranian and Hezbollah targets to the buffer zone and the Sweida strikes of 2025; in Iran, across the wars of June 2025 and 2026; and in Iraq and Yemen, where Israeli aircraft struck militia depots in 2019 and Houthi ports, power plants and leaders from 2024. Each toll is given with the body that counted it and the method it used, beside Israel's stated reason and the other side's. The part then turns to the two neighbours at peace with Israel, Egypt and Jordan, which were not attacked but bore the Rafah closure, proposals to move Gaza's people onto their soil, and losses to trade and tourism. It closes with one table drawing together all nine theatres as of October 2026.
