In the Congress of 2026, on Track AIPAC's career totals, the largest sums belong to members for whom outside groups spent heavily in primaries, Haley Stevens, George Latimer and Wesley Bell above all; among senators Susan Collins and Adam Schiff lead; the leaders of both parties in the House are on the lists; and a small group of progressive Democrats and one Republican, Thomas Massie, stand at zero. [cite:trackaipac_congress] In 2018, the last cycle before AIPAC's own committees, the largest recipients were senators of both parties, led by Bob Menendez and Ted Cruz. [cite:pm_guardian_2019]

What is settled is who received what, on each tracker's definition. What is contested is what the totals mean, since careers, states and seats shape them as much as positions do. What the record leaves open is the full set of single-cycle rankings since 2016, which are being built from the FEC's files.
