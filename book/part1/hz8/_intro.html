Part Eight covers the two years after the ceasefire of 27 November 2024. It sets out what the agreement actually said and what each government said it meant; how Israel kept five hilltop positions after the withdrawal deadline; how many violations and deaths the UN, the Lebanese government and the Israeli army each counted while the truce was nominally in force; how Lebanon's new president and prime minister tried to bring Hezbollah's weapons under the state; and how the war that began on 2 March 2026, inside the wider American and Israeli war on Iran, overturned all of it. Because nearly all of this is recent, figures are given with the date and the body that produced them, and the status of anything still moving is marked as of the latest reporting this book could verify.
