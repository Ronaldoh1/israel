Iran's programme began with an American reactor and the Shah's ambitions, was revived in secret with centrifuge designs from the Khan network, and was exposed in 2002; the IAEA found that Iran had concealed activities and had run a coordinated effort relevant to a nuclear weapon until the end of 2003, with some studies to 2009, and US intelligence judged in 2007 that the weapons work had halted in 2003.
