The Definition

The International Holocaust Remembrance Alliance adopted a "working definition" of antisemitism in 2016. Its examples include "denying the Jewish people their right to self-determination, e.g., by claiming that the existence of a State of Israel is a racist endeavor", applying double standards to Israel and comparing Israeli policy to that of the Nazis; seven of its eleven examples concern Israel. [cite:ihra_text] It has been adopted by the federal government and, by August 2024, by 35 states and the District of Columbia. [cite:foa_report_2024]

In March 2021 more than 200 scholars of antisemitism, Jewish history and the Holocaust signed the Jerusalem Declaration on Antisemitism, written as an alternative: it holds that criticism of Israel and anti-Zionism are not antisemitic on their face, and lists examples that are. A third text, the Nexus Document, takes a middle position. [cite:jda_text] [cite:thirdnarrative_three_defs] Critics of the IHRA definition, including Americans for Peace Now, say it chills speech by treating anti-Zionist views as antisemitic; critics of the Jerusalem Declaration, including Britain's Community Security Trust, say it gives too much room to hostility presented as politics. [cite:peacenow_ihra_faq] [cite:cst_jda_critique]

Into the platforms and the campuses

In July 2024 Meta updated its hate-speech policy to remove content that uses "Zionist" as a stand-in for Jews or Israelis in attacks, while allowing political discussion of Zionism. [cite:meta_policy_text] Six months later, in January 2025, Meta broadly loosened its hate-speech enforcement, and the Anti-Defamation League, which had welcomed the first change, documented antisemitic content increasing after the second. [cite:adl_2025_report] The One-Way Mirror on this shelf follows the definition in federal civil-rights enforcement; Part Eight follows it into Columbia's settlement.

Antisemitism is real and has risen sharply since 2023; that is not in dispute. The dispute is where its border with political speech about Israel lies, and which institution draws it.
