Independent research points to the Gaza Health Ministry undercounting deaths, not inventing them. The ministry is under Hamas's authority, which is why Israel disputes its figures, but the UN has generally treated them as reliable. [cite:gulfnews_lancet_toll] A study in The Lancet in January 2025, cross-checking ministry hospital lists, a ministry online survey and social-media obituaries, estimated 64,260 traumatic-injury deaths by 30 June 2024, against 37,877 reported, an undercount of about 41%. [cite:transcend_lancet_toll] A 2026 household survey in The Lancet Global Health, not relying on ministry records, estimated more than 75,000 violent deaths in the first 15 months, versus about 49,000 reported at the time. [cite:cyprusmail_lancetgh_2026]

As of August 2026, the ministry's count, as reported by UN OCHA, stood at about 73,400 killed. [cite:un_ocha_opt] The real open question is different: the totals do not separate combatants from civilians. Israel says it killed about 20,000 fighters, a figure not independently verified.
