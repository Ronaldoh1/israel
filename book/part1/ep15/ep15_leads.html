The Open Leads

The network map marks every tie the book could not yet confirm as a lead to verify rather than a fact. There are 55 of them. They fall into a few groups, and each group names the record that would settle it.

Some leads will turn out to be nothing, and the map will say so when they do. That is the point of keeping them visible. A blind spot is not a secret; it is a place no one has looked yet. The Swiss contract sat in French and Swiss reporting for months before it reached most American readers. The same question, asked of every line on the map, is how the rest will be found: what else is sitting in plain sight?
