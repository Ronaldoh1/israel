This book lays two records side by side. One is the Road to 1948: how a political movement founded at Basel in 1897 won a charter, a patron, money and a state. The other is the record of today: how support for the State of Israel is organised in American government and around the world. [cite:zionism_founding_1897] [cite:pm_sludge_100m_2026] Both records are told in full elsewhere on this shelf, in The Road to 1948, The Cold War Architecture, Follow the Money, The One-Way Mirror and The Theology Shield. This book asks one question of them: which mechanisms appear in both?

In the case this library makes. This volume shows that the machine that built the state in 1948 is the machine that sustains it now: nine mechanisms that appear in both records. The Case, the last volume, argues from it.

Nine do. Each is named here by what it does and by the institution, government or named person the record shows doing it. Zionism is a political movement, not the Jewish people or the Jewish religion, and Jewish organisations opposed several of these mechanisms on the record: when the Justice Department ordered the American Zionist Council to register as a foreign agent in 1962, the American Council for Judaism welcomed it. [cite:rs_cw5_smith_podcast]

A pattern is not proof of a plan. The record of 1948 holds exactly one documented case in which donor money is tied to a particular presidential decision, and the strongest counter-reading, that Washington backed Israel for reasons of its own, is set out in the last part. [cite:mondoweiss_judis_truman] What changes between the two eras is mostly scale and tools: letters became advertising campaigns, a hundred thousand dollars became a hundred million, and a friend at the door became an institution with a budget.
