Part Two turns to the children. It sets out how many were killed and injured in Gaza, by the UN's count, broken down by age down to infants under one year old; how that share compares with earlier wars; what the UN's commission of inquiry concluded and how Israel answered; the children of the West Bank and the Israeli children killed and taken on 7 October; and the children who survived: the amputees, the orphans, and those killed after the ceasefire.
