Part Twelve tests the first half of one of the three theses this volume examines: that Iran's government does not want a war with the United States. It sets out the evidence for that claim, which includes negotiations stretching from a disputed Swiss fax in 2003 to talks in Geneva two days before the war of 2026, retaliatory strikes on American bases announced in advance, and its leaders' own statements. It then sets out the evidence against: Americans killed by forces Iran armed and directed, from Beirut in 1983 to Tower 22 in 2024, hostage-taking, assassination plots on American soil, attacks on shipping and oil, and the lethal strikes of 2026. It ends by separating what the record shows from what remains an argument about motive.
