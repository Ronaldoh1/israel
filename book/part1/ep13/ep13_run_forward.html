Running It Forward

Here is the scenario, check by check. Each row takes a tool that moved the Epstein case and sets it against the program's stated design. Nothing in the right-hand column is a claim about what anyone is doing now; it is what the texts propose, applied.

Read against the previous section, the result is stark. Every tool that moved the Epstein case is one the design removes or places under the sovereign. What would remain is the one channel that did least in this case: the decision of whoever sits at the top. The scenario does not need anyone at the top to be corrupt. It needs only what this book has already documented happening: a network that reaches the people holding the keys. Under the design, there would be no other keys.

The capability already exists

One part of the machinery would not need building. The surveillance and data systems the library's other volumes document already exist and are already used by the state; in February 2026, for example, the Department of Homeland Security signed an agreement with Palantir worth up to $1 billion over five years. [cite:ow9_sa_dhsbpa] [cite:ow9_reg_immos] Nothing in the Epstein record shows that kind of capability being turned on the powerful; he was exposed by a newspaper and a parent, not by data. Which way such tools point, at those with power or at those without it, is a choice, and in the design the choice would belong to the owner.
