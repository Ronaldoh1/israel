Three Parents, One Child: The Tests Compared

Each of the three city parts ended with the same test and a dated verdict. This section sets them side by side, so that the reader can see the whole architecture at once, and then asks the question the volume has deferred until now: did the three cities, or the powers they house, act together?

The three verdicts. London's verdict was that the British government and the London market, not the Corporation of the City of London, were the financial and imperial parent of the Jewish national home, decisively from 1917 to 1939, and that this parent then restricted and withdrew from the child for a decade. [scene:dv2_parent_test|London's test] Rome's verdict was that the Holy See was not a parent at all: an opponent in doctrine until 1965 and in diplomacy until 1993, whose refusal was not a front, and which since 2015 has kept a treaty with Palestine alongside its relations with Israel. [scene:dv3_parent_test|Rome's test] Washington's verdict was that the United States government was not the parent at birth, when it recognized the state but embargoed its arms, and became its guardian afterwards, from the 1960s and decisively from 1973, in a role with no rival. [scene:dv4_parent_test|Washington's test]

The relay, not the triangle. Laid end to end, the three records do not form a triangle of cooperating parents. They form a relay, and one of the three runners never ran. Britain carried the project from 1917 until it turned against it in 1939 and handed the question to the United Nations in 1947, then abstained on partition. [cite:rs_rd4_wp_cojs_text] [cite:rs_rd5_cojs_bevin_1947] In 1948 the decisive help came from neither London nor Washington but from Czechoslovakia and the Soviet Union, the arms and the first full recognition. [cite:wiki_czech_arms_israel] [cite:israeled_gromyko_timeline] Washington took up the baton in stages from the 1960s. Rome stood aside throughout, opposing until 1993. The handover from London to Washington is the central fact of the architecture, and it was not a planned transfer: it happened because Britain withdrew and the United States, for its own reasons, stepped in.

Did they act together? The volume set out to test this without assuming the answer. The documented links among the three are real, and they are listed here in full. The Rothschild houses lent to the popes from 1831, and a Rothschild in London received the Balfour Declaration in 1917. [cite:dv3_wiki_rothschild_loans] [cite:wb_bl_addms41178] The Holy See lost about €140 million on a London property and is still litigating it. [cite:dv3_cbc_becciu_2023] [cite:dv3_advaticanum_becciu] A former chief executive of the Edmond de Rothschild group took over the Vatican Bank's board in 2026. [cite:dv3_citywire_pauly] The Protestant theology that made Christians in Britain and America into supporters of a Jewish return was the theology Rome condemned. [cite:wiki_scofield_bible] [cite:dv3_usccb_ccc676] And Britain's withdrawal and America's entry were sequential. Each link is documented; none of them is an instance of the three acting jointly on Palestine. On the decisive questions the three were on different sides: in 1947 Washington voted for partition, London abstained and Rome stayed silent to protect its own plan for Jerusalem; in 1949 Washington and London voted against the internationalization of Jerusalem that Rome had asked for. [cite:dv3_unproject_181] [cite:dv3_wiki_res303] The record shows convergence of different interests at different times, and open disagreement at the moments that mattered most. It does not show a single design.

The walls, kept. Several claims that circulate with the three-cities idea were tested in this library and its earlier books, and they stay out of this volume because no document supports them. The 1871 Organic Act did not turn the United States into a corporation; it reorganized the District's local government. [cite:dv4_politifact_1871] The stars on the District's flag come from George Washington's family arms, not from a three-city emblem. [cite:dv4_politifact_flag] No document in this research shows a single coordinated body directing the three cities, or makes the Rothschild and Rockefeller families one interest, or traces the Orsini family to a biblical Nimrod. Those claims remain outside the record until documents are found, and the volume will add them if they are.

The fourth sovereign. Part Five showed the child in its own terms: a state without a constitution, whose citizenship reaches abroad by descent, whose 2018 Basic Law reserves self-determination to one people, whose government in November 2026 is being formed from a Knesset in which neither bloc has a majority, and which stands outside or in dispute with the nuclear treaty, both courts in The Hague and the recognition of Palestine by most of the world. [cite:dv5_jvl_return] [cite:jta_nsl_upheld] [cite:dv5_israeled_watch] [cite:dv5_gov_za_icj_2026] Its relationship with its guardian combines dependence and defiance, and the record shows the guardian prevailing when it pressed for something concrete and American, and not otherwise. [scene:dv5_dependency_defiance|Dependency and defiance]

The composite finding. The volume's title states a claim: three anomalous cities as the parents of Israel. The record supports a narrower and more precise version. Two of the three cities house governments that, at different times and for their own reasons, did what parents do: Britain in the years of the promise and the Mandate, the United States in the decades of aid, arms and the veto. The third housed an institution that opposed the project for most of a century and then accepted it. The cities themselves, as cities, did none of it. Their anomalies are real, and Part One measured them. But what made two of them matter to Israel was not their peculiar status; it was the power of the governments seated in them.
