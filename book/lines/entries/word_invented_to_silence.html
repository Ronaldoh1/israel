The record shows the opposite. The adjective was first written in 1860 by Moritz Steinschneider, a Jewish scholar, in the phrase "antisemitic prejudices," to rebut Ernest Renan's theory that Semitic peoples, Arabs as well as Jews, were inferior to Aryans [cite:wiki_antisemitism_etym]. The word became a political label in 1879, when Wilhelm Marr, a German journalist who was not Jewish, made it the name of his own League of Antisemites, a movement against Jews [cite:wiki_marr_1879] [cite:jvl_marr].

So the word was named by the people doing the hating, not invented to protect anyone from scrutiny. What is documented and disputed today is narrower: whether the word has been stretched over criticism of a state.
