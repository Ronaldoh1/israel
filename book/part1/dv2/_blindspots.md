## 2. Series blind-spot list

- **Real antisemitism named.** The Christian oath that kept Salomons and Lionel de Rothschild out of office; the 1846 pamphlet behind the Waterloo legend (bc_s02); the false theory of "world Jewry" behind Cabinet calculation in 1917 (s06).
- **Jewish dissent.** Montagu and the Conjoint Committee (rd2_addressee) are linked, not retold.
- **Palestinian side.** Arab banks and deposits (1945), the refinery strike (1948), blocked balances; the Mandate's own administrative record (s07).
- **Sources independent.** Business-vote figures come from three sources of different kinds (a reference summary for 2009, a campaigning party for 2013, the 2025 results); campaign figures are labelled as such.
