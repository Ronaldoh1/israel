Part Nine asks who has decided Gaza's fate since October 2023. Inside Israel, the decisions rest with a coalition government whose partners have pressed it, publicly, against the January 2025 ceasefire and for the emigration of Gaza's people. Inside Hamas, most of the leaders who planned October 7 have been killed. Outside, the United States has supplied the weapons, blocked or shaped the Security Council's resolutions, and in 2025 wrote the plan that now governs the ceasefire, with Qatar, Egypt and Turkey as mediators. The part sets out what each has said and done, on the record.
