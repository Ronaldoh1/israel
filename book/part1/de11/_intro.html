This part tells the life of Elon Musk, the largest political donor in the 2024 and 2026 American elections and the public face of the government cuts of 2025. It keeps his own accounts apart from the documents: a Pretoria childhood and a grandfather whose politics were his own; a Canadian passport, an American degree finished in 1997 and a first company started, by his own 2005 email, before he had the legal right to stay; the Zip2 and PayPal fortunes and the boardroom coup in which Peter Thiel replaced him; Tesla and SpaceX as partners of the state, from NASA contracts and a federal loan to spy satellites and the Starlink network on which Ukraine's army came to depend; and the purchase of Twitter, which by 2026 sat with his AI company under SpaceX. The last two sections set out his political money as a ledger, the office and the break with President Trump that followed it, and grade thirteen of his public claims against the record, before testing the book's worst-case reading against this part's material.
