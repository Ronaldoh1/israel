Part One goes back before the strip existed. It finds Gaza as a town and a district of Mandate Palestine, follows the war of 1948 in the south as the Egyptian army advanced and was driven back, and shows how the line where the fighting stopped became the edge of a new and never quite defined territory. It ends with the people who filled that territory: the refugees, who by the end of the war outnumbered Gaza's own residents by more than two to one.
