The sayings and sermons from the jar describe a faith centred on interpretation and inner recognition: a kingdom "inside of you", a resurrection received in this life, sacraments culminating in a bridal chamber, and a divine voice that speaks as a woman. [cite:vt25_thomas_lambdin] [cite:vt24_gphil] [cite:vt25_pbs_thunder] The same idea of the kingdom within lies in Luke's entos hymōn, which the Vulgate and early Fathers read as "within you". [cite:vt24_ramelli] Cyril of Jerusalem's rule, that books not read in the churches should not be read at home, shows how such texts were kept out of reach. [cite:vt25_cyril_cat4] Part Five follows what happened when the books came back: who found them, who sold them and who controlled them.
