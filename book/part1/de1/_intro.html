Part One sets out what this book is about before it says anything about who is involved. It describes the Dark Enlightenment, also called neoreaction, as what it is on the record: a body of writing by two authors, Curtis Yarvin and Nick Land, published mostly between 2007 and 2014, with four core claims about democracy, "the Cathedral," exit and the state run as a company. It then separates the documented spine of texts, citations, money and public statements from the overlay of resemblance and association that surrounds the subject, and shows how the label has spread to people who have never claimed it. It ends by stating the working hypothesis the book tests, and the grades it uses to test it.
