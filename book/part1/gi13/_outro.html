This volume followed a phrase from a verse in Genesis to a Knesset vote. It found that the widest version of "Greater Israel," from the Nile to the Euphrates, rests on one reading of one verse, a diary note Herzl never acted on and a minority religious current, and that the inscription, the flag's rivers and the coin map offered as proof of it are myths or misreadings.
