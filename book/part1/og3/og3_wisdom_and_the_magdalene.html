Wisdom and the Magdalene

In the Greek Bible the Hebrew chokmah, wisdom, became Sophia, a female figure who, in Proverbs, "hath builded her house". Paul called Christ "the wisdom of God", most Church Fathers followed him, and the great church Justinian inaugurated in Constantinople on 27 December 537, Hagia Sophia, was dedicated to Christ as Divine Wisdom. [cite:vt36_wiki_sophia] The Gnostic teachers made Sophia a fallen and redeemed divine mother; orthodox theology gave her role to the Son. When the Russian theologian Sergei Bulgakov revived Sophia as an aspect of God, his teaching was condemned by the Russian Orthodox Church in 1935. [cite:vt36_wiki_sophia] The fullest Gnostic account of her fall and return is in [scene:vt26_pistis_sophia|The Vatican Architecture: Pistis Sophia].

The second figure is Mary Magdalene. All four gospels place her at the empty tomb, and in John she is the first to see the risen Jesus and is sent to tell the others. [cite:vt26_wiki_magdalene] In the Gospel of Mary, a second-century text that survives in a codex bought in Cairo in 1896, she teaches the grieving disciples, and Peter objects: did the Saviour really speak privately with a woman, and are they to listen to her? Levi answers that if the Saviour made her worthy, Peter has no right to reject her. [cite:vt26_mattison_gmary] [cite:vt26_wiki_gmary] The Pistis Sophia stages the same quarrel: Peter complains that "we will not endure this woman". [cite:vt26_mead_ch36]

In a homily in Rome around 591, Pope Gregory I merged three women of the gospels into one: the unnamed sinner of Luke, Mary of Bethany and Mary Magdalene. He read her seven demons as "the universality of all vices". He did not use the word prostitute, but the image of the penitent sinner that dominated Western art grew from this reading. [cite:vt26_pearse_greg33] The Eastern churches never made the merger and honour her as "Equal to the Apostles". [cite:vt26_wiki_magdalene] The Roman calendar separated the three women in 1969, and a decree of 3 June 2016 raised her memorial to a feast, recalling her old title, "apostle of the apostles". [cite:vt26_wiki_magdalene] [cite:vt26_va_feast2016]

The record shows both movements at once. The institution raised the Magdalene's rank to that of the apostles in 2016, and it holds that "the Church has no authority whatsoever to confer priestly ordination on women", a judgment declared in 1994 "to be definitively held". [cite:vt26_va_ordsac1994] The texts in which a woman teaches and Peter resents it were the texts the canon left out. The full account is in [scene:vt26_gospel_of_mary|The Vatican Architecture: the Gospel of Mary].
