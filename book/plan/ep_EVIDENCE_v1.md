# Where the Evidence Went: the Chain-of-Custody Ledger
### Research file v1.0 for *The Epstein Architecture* · status as of 30 October 2026

Ron's question: *"What did the government do with all the evidence? Some was mishandled, some put in storage… he had cameras all over the house… the evidence exists. Who has it, and what did they do with it?"*

That is the right question, and much of it can be answered from the record. This ledger takes every known body of evidence, from first seizure to today, through five columns:
1. What it is.
2. Who took it and when.
3. Who holds it now.
4. What was lost, moved, destroyed, returned or withheld.
5. Status.

**One line drawn on purpose.** Images of child sexual abuse must never be released, and the book never asks for them. The question is never "release the tapes". It is **"has anyone investigated what is on them, and who is identifiable in them?"** That is a question about investigation, not publication.

## B. What the ledger shows

**1. The evidence exists, and most of it is in federal hands.** Computers, drives, discs, recording devices, logbooks, a "document with names", employee lists and wiretap results are in FBI custody. Nothing suggests the core seized evidence is missing.

**2. The losses are specific, dated and documented**, not general:
- The Palm Beach computers, removed by the defence's own investigators days before a police search and never recovered (2005).
- A custody gap of about five days for the safe contents, held by Epstein's accountant (2019).
- The wrong jail tier preserved (July 2019).
- The master jail video destroyed by the FBI (2024).
- A ranch nobody searched for 27 years.

Each has a name and a date. That is what makes it a finding.

**3. The real question is not "where is it?" but "who has looked?"** The DOJ's July 2025 memo describes the material and says it found no basis to investigate anyone else. The recordings, the "document with names", the employee lists and the logbooks have sat in custody for seven years. **No public record shows anyone has been charged, or even questioned, on the basis of what is in them.** That is the same predication gate described in the operations file, Part D.

**4. Federal agencies are now the ones refusing to share.** A state truth commission subpoenaed SDNY for ranch-related material, and **SDNY refused or did not respond.** The DEA's memo is withheld. This is the "power protects power" mechanism working on evidence in the present tense: fully documented, with a date on every step.

**5. The blackmail question, held to the line.** Hidden cameras are documented (the clock cameras, the spycam footage), and victims allege recordings were used as leverage. The DOJ says it found **no evidence** of blackmail. Both are recorded. The question the book presses is **how DOJ reached that conclusion and who reviewed the recordings**. It does not assert that tapes of named people exist.

## C. Code panel: the object retained, never read
```objc
NSArray *evidence = [locker seizedItems];   // 40 devices, 26 drives, 70+ CDs, 6 recorders
[evidence retain];                          // kept, catalogued, stored for seven years
// [investigator review:evidence];          // the line that was never called
```
In plain language: the government kept everything and catalogued it, but the line that would have someone *read* it in order to charge anyone was never run. The evidence is retained in memory, never used, and the system reports no error.
