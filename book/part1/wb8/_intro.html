Part Eight follows the people who lose their homes. Displacement in the West Bank happens in two ways that the record keeps apart. The first is by the state: demolitions of buildings put up without Israeli permits, eviction cases in the courts, and the declaration of firing zones. The second is by settlers: attacks on herding communities, and the outposts built beside them. This part sets out the counts kept by the United Nations and by Israeli monitoring groups, four named places (Masafer Yatta, Khan al-Ahmar, Sheikh Jarrah and Huwara), what Israel's courts decided and why, how often settler violence ends in a charge, and what Israel's leaders have said about it.
