Part Ten leaves Lebanon for the other armed groups of what Iran and its allies call the "axis of resistance." It follows the Houthis from a Zaidi religious revival in northern Yemen through six wars with their own government, the capture of Sanaa and a Saudi-led war that the United States supported, then sets the documented Iranian arms trail beside the evidence that the movement makes its own decisions. It traces the Houthis' campaign against Red Sea shipping and Israel after October 2023, the American, British and Israeli strikes that answered it, the truce that took the United States out and the new war with Saudi Arabia in 2026. It then turns to the Iraqi militias, the groups that have most often fought American troops directly, from the chain of attacks that ended in the killing of Qasem Soleimani to the deaths of three soldiers at Tower 22. Throughout, it gives each government's and group's stated position beside the record, and says where the evidence does not settle who decides.
