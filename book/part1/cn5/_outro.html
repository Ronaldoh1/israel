By 1511 the office that guarded the Latin canon had been a territorial lordship for more than seven centuries, held under a title partly supported by a forged imperial grant and a forged body of papal law. [cite:cn_wiki_donation] [cite:cn_wiki_pseudoisidore] The forger of neither was ever identified; the man who exposed the Donation was tried by the Inquisition and then employed by the papacy. [cite:cn_wiki_valla] The chair itself was bought by the Tusculan family for a twenty-year-old in 1032 and sold by him in 1045, held by an Orsini whom Dante damned for enriching his kin, and contested by Orsini, Colonna and Caetani cardinals who rarely acted as one. [cite:cn_wiki_benedict9] [cite:cn_dante_inf19] [cite:vt7_wiki_conclave1305] Part Six turns from the office to its flock, and to how ordinary Christians lost the right to read the Bible in their own languages.
