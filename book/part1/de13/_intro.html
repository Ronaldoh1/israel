This part is about the people Peter Thiel and Elon Musk worked with at PayPal between 1998 and 2002, the group Fortune photographed in 2007 as "The PayPal Mafia." It names everyone in that photograph and the companies each went on to build, from YouTube and LinkedIn to Yelp, Yammer, Affirm and Founders Fund, and states the connection each has to Thiel today: business partner, former employee, investor, or simply former colleague. It shows that the group does not share one politics, since one of its best-known members is among the largest Democratic donors in the country. It then records which members of the network, and which former employees of Thiel's own firms, took federal posts in 2025 and 2026, how each post was filled, and where each person stood in October 2026.
