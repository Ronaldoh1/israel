Settled, Contested and Open

The architecture

Read across the whole period, one pattern recurs. The negotiations set aside the final questions for later, the interim periods stretched, and in the interim the map changed: settlements before Camp David in 1978, during Oslo's five years and after them, and around the edges of a Gaza left but not released. [cite:mas_settlements_history] [cite:peacenow_settlement_population] [cite:wiki_disengagement] The one agreement that succeeded, with Egypt, was the one that settled its territorial question at once. [cite:camp_david_1979_treaty] The record does not show that delay was everyone's plan; it shows that delay always favoured the side that held the land.
