This part opens Book Three, on the Department of Government Efficiency, by asking how a campaign donor came to hold an office and what that office was. It follows the sequence from Elon Musk's endorsement of Donald Trump in July 2024, through a proposal made in public during the campaign, more than $290 million in election spending and the announcement of a "Department of Government Efficiency" a week after the vote, to the executive order of 20 January 2025 that built something different from what had been announced: not an outside commission but a renamed White House office with a temporary unit, a fixed end date and teams inside every agency. It then sets out that office's legal form, who was in charge on paper and in practice, the special-government-employee status of its staff, the advisory-committee and freedom-of-information disputes, and what remained of it as of October 2026.
