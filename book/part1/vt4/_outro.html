Part Four has separated the two subjects that "the Vatican" names. The Holy See, recognized by 184 states, signs the treaties and holds the diplomatic relations; Vatican City State is a territory of 0.44 square kilometres, with 673 citizens and 882 residents, that the Holy See has ruled since 1929. [cite:vt4_press_relations2026] [cite:vt4_vcs_geography] [cite:vt4_vcs_population] Its constitution, rewritten by Leo XIV on 31 July 2026, keeps every legislative, executive and judicial power in the Pope and delegates the daily work to a commission and a Governorate that, since November 2025, need not be led by a cardinal. [cite:vt4_vn_fl2026] [cite:vt4_wipo_fl2000] Citizenship comes with an office and ends with it; the state's courts, reformed in 2020, 2021 and 2023, try even cardinals, but the state has no prison and relies on Italy for punishment and fugitives under Article 22 of the Lateran Treaty. [cite:vt4_loc_citizenship] [cite:vt4_vn_cardinals2021] [cite:vt4_lateran_text] Its currency, tax position, water and rail links all rest on agreements with Italy and the European Union, the euro agreement binding it to EU rules against money laundering. [cite:vt4_vcs_monetary] What is settled is how the territory runs; what this part did not test is whether the outside checks on the Holy See work, which is the subject of Part Five, beginning with [scene:vt5_recognition|recognition and who grants it].
