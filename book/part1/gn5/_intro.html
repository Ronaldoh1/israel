Part Five follows the Nag Hammadi books from the jar to the reader, 1945 to 1977. It sets out what is known of the discovery and how much of the famous story is doubted, then traces the books through the hands of farmers, merchants, dealers, a psychological institute in Zurich, a Swiss bank vault and the Egyptian state, with the sums that were paid. It ends with the scholars who controlled access for three decades and the campaign that finally put the library into print.
