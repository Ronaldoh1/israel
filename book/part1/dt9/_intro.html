Part Nine closes the book by turning from argument to practice. The first section sets out the six routes by which any reform in this book can be made, from a city charter to an amendment to the Constitution, what has already passed through each, and where every reform stands as of October 2026. The second sets out the dates to watch, beginning with the measures on the ballot on 3 November 2026, and what the record shows ordinary people can do, before giving the book's answer to the question it began with.
