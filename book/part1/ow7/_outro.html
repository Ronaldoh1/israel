Part Seven traced a military relationship built without a defence treaty. Executive agreements in 1981, 1983 and 1988 created joint planning bodies and port access; a classified agreement governs an American-owned stockpile in Israel, which was drawn on for Israel in 2006 and 2014 and for Ukraine in 2023; an American radar has stood in the Negev since 2008; and Israel moved into Central Command in 2021.
