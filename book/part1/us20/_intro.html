Part Twenty follows the money between the two countries in both directions and puts every sum in today's dollars. It first lists the American government's channels to Israel: the 2016 memorandum of understanding and its Foreign Military Financing, fixed in nominal dollars and shrinking in real value; the phase-out of the share Israel may spend at home; the missile-defence money that also buys Israeli systems for the US Army; the loan guarantees of 1992 and 2003, which moved credit rather than cash and were cut for settlement spending; and intelligence sharing, the one channel with no published price. It then sets beside them the private and commercial flows: tax-deductible American gifts to Israeli organisations, including settlements; Israel Bonds bought by American states and cities; investment and trade; and the weapons and defence work that move from Israel to the United States. The detail of the aid itself is in Part One and in the Greater Israel volume, and is linked rather than repeated.
