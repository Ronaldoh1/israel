# Epstein network map: coverage plan (19 Nov 2026)

Goal: the same treatment the Road to 1948 map received. Every pathway that built, funded, housed, moved, protected and exposed the operation, worldwide, one research pass each, every tie sourced; then a coverage audit and a stress test of the what-if model; then refreshed tests and cards.

**Subject type:** a network centred on a convicted person (PERSON-type ceilings for every named individual), with institutional components (banks, companies, agencies) under the ordinary institution ceiling.

## Standing rules for every pass
- A tie is a documented act between two parties: a deed, a contract, a payment, an appointment, a filing, sworn testimony. Appearing in the files, a contact book or a photograph is not a tie and is never presented as wrongdoing.
- Any claim of participation in the crimes needs the strongest basis: court records, sworn testimony, or case-file logs, or named-outlet reporting that cites them. Below that it is "not established", or it is left out.
- Family members get their own nodes; nothing passes between spouses, relatives or partners without a documented joint act.
- One source pull for every subject and every kind of connection (court dockets, FEC, lobbying and FARA, property records, SEC and bank filings, flight logs where public, news archives). No country, network or group is singled out for extra searching. "Checked, nothing documented" is a finding.
- Intelligence claims about any state stay "not established" unless they clear the documentary bar; every state on the same standard.
- Comparison cases are recorded only as comparisons of method, never as ties.
- Sales after 2019 and other ties outside the operation's life (1991-2019) carry no weight in the what-if model; seller and buyer ties are recorded without weight (cluster 'provenance').
