Part Eight followed the money. From 2018 Israel let Qatari cash, at times about $30 million a month, into Gaza, to pay Hamas-government salaries and needy families; Netanyahu called it a humanitarian step to keep the quiet, while reporting after October 7 described it as part of a policy of keeping Hamas strong, which he denies. [cite:gz_ds_qatar2018] [cite:gz_aj_qatar2021] [cite:gz_week_qatar] After 2014 donors pledged $5.4 billion and rebuilding passed through a mechanism that gave Israel the final say. [cite:gz_un_cairo2014] [cite:gz_aj_grm] After Israel's allegations against twelve UNRWA staff, donors cut funding; UN investigators found nine may have been involved, and the Knesset barred the agency. [cite:gz_crs_unrwa] [cite:gz_unrwa_oios] [cite:gz_jp_knesset_unrwa] The United States spent $21.7 billion on military aid to Israel in the war's first two years; rebuilding Gaza is estimated to need $71.4 billion.
