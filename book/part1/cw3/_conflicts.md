# Stage Three (cw3): source contradictions presented in the prose

- Gaza as a war aim: Ben-Gurion's public line of 15 Oct 1956 (Egypt "still sits in the Gaza Strip"; defensive preparation) vs the Gush Katif Heritage Center via Huberman ("[control of] the Gaza Strip" listed among war aims at the October 1956 cabinet; March 1955 proposal to retake it). (cw3_road_to_war, cw3_gaza_occupation)
- Sèvres, French copy: "misplaced" (French, per the 1996 BBC account) vs a photocopy of three pages found by Pineau's widow in 2002 (Wikipedia). (cw3_sevres)
- Mitla drop: 17:00 on 29 Oct (IDF) vs "at dawn" (Oren). (cw3_war)
- Israeli dead: about 150 (Ben-Gurion, 7 Nov 1956) vs 170 and 1 prisoner (Ben-Gurion, 5 Mar 1957) vs 171 (IDF) vs 172 (Jewish Agency) vs 189 killed, 899 wounded (Janda). (cw3_war)
- Egyptian dead: about 1,650 killed, 4,900 wounded (Library of Congress, citing observers) vs about 1,030 killed, 4,000 wounded (Janda) vs "well in excess of 1,000" military (naval-history.net). (cw3_war)
- Port Fuad dead: 100 (British estimate, per Weeks) vs "some 60 of the enemy" (Keightley). (cw3_war)
- British dead: 16 killed, 96 wounded (Keightley) vs 21 killed, 112 wounded (Commons, 12 Dec 1956, including occupation deaths). (cw3_war)
- Force balance: 45,000 Israelis vs 30,000 Egyptians (Janda) vs "over 30,000" Egyptians in Sinai (Ben-Gurion). (cw3_war)
- Egypt beaten or withdrawn: IDF "crushed" the Egyptian army (IDF; Morris) vs Egypt withdrew to defend the Canal (Nasser, per Library of Congress). (cw3_war, egi_src_s02)
- Cease-fire date: took effect 23:59 GMT on 6 Nov (Keightley) vs 7 Nov (Hahn). (cw3_un_ceasefire)
- Resolution 1002 vote: 65–1–10 (Jewish Virtual Library only; the "no" vote not named). (cw3_un_ceasefire)
- What ended the war: the Soviet threat (FRUS chapter title; Israeli accounts of the Bulganin letter) vs American political and financial pressure (Office of the Historian; Hahn). (cw3_soviet_hungary, cw3_un_ceasefire)
- Ben-Gurion's 17 Nov letter ("not ... caused harm to any civilian centre") vs the UNRWA Director's report on Khan Yunis (3 Nov) and Rafah (12 Nov). (cw3_soviet_hungary, cw3_gaza_killings)
- Khan Yunis: Israeli account (resistance, refugees part of it) vs refugees' account (resistance had ceased; unarmed civilians killed), recorded side by side by UNRWA. (cw3_gaza_killings)
- Khan Yunis dead: 275 named on UNRWA lists, "exact number ... not known" vs 415 killed, 57 missing (Palestinian sources) vs 525 Gazans (al-Rantisi via Filiu) vs about 100 shot at the caravanserai (Sacco's witnesses) vs "about two hundred" across both towns (Morris 1994/2011) vs "some 275 ... murdered" per Palestinians and UNRWA (Morris 2024, attributing a word the UNRWA report does not use). (cw3_gaza_killings)
- Rafah dead: 111 named (UNRWA) vs 48 (Israel) vs 197 killed, 23 disappeared (Palestinian sources) vs "some 60" (The Times) vs "more than 100" (Hass, UN estimates). (cw3_gaza_killings)
- "Third Kingdom" letter: annexationist claim (Walla; Avnery's recollection; Sèvres eighth point) vs soldiers' rhetoric (8 Nov letter to Eisenhower, "never planned to annex the Sinai desert"; cabinet's three aims). (cw3_third_kingdom)
- Begin's 16 Nov position describes the government as refusing to leave Sinai vs the government's 8 Nov acceptance of withdrawal in principle. (cw3_third_kingdom)
- Egypt's Jews, who was detained: many Egyptian citizens among about 1,000 detained (JTA) vs Jews of Egyptian nationality at first spared (Julius). (cw3_egypt_jews)
- Egypt's Jews, arrests: about 1,000 detained (JTA; JVL wire reports) vs about 900 arrested by 7 Nov plus a first wave of about 500 expelled (Julius); relation unexplained. (cw3_egypt_jews)
- Egypt's Jews, departures: 23,000–25,000 by 1957 (JTA) vs by 1958 (Julius) vs "almost 25,000" expelled (JVL wire reports). (cw3_egypt_jews)
- Causes of the Egyptian measures: persecution of Jews as Jews (Julius; JJAC; Krämer per Julius) vs war and decolonization, with Zionism blamed (Beinin per Julius); Mendes rejects both partisan versions. (cw3_egypt_jews)
- Gaza, January 1957: government resolution to "hold the Gaza Strip" and Knesset vote not to withdraw (Katif Center) vs agreement to pull the army out while leaving police and administration (Walla). (cw3_gaza_occupation)
- Gaza intentions: retention (Ben-Gurion's statements; cabinet and Knesset decisions; Blueprint; Nahal outpost) vs security-only (Israel's stated condition of UN, not Egyptian, administration). (cw3_gaza_occupation)
- Cabinet approval of the Gaza withdrawal: 2 Mar 1957, after Eisenhower's letter of that date (Ben-Gurion, 5 Mar) vs Meir already authorized to announce withdrawal on 1 Mar (JVL; Katif). (cw3_withdrawal)
- Sanctions threat: pressure in substance (Eisenhower's address; Dulles's list of reparations, bonds and aid) vs no formal threat (no sanction imposed; Eisenhower "reluctant"; Congress resisted). (cw3_withdrawal)
- Guarantees: Israel left with assurances (Ben-Gurion, 5 Mar 1957) vs "without formal security gains" (Bar-Joseph; Dulles's "unconditional withdrawal"). (cw3_withdrawal)
- Kafr Qasim dead: 47 (JVL; Encyclopaedia Judaica; Cook; JTA 2016) vs 48 (JTA 1957; Robinson; Times of Israel) vs 49 (village; JTA 1999; Ynet; JVL Knesset page; Herzog) vs "fifty" (Middle East Eye). (mg_s01, cw3_kafr_qasim_memory)
- Kafr Qasim order: Malinki's testimony (Shadmi: no "sentimentality," no "arrests") vs the summary in which Shadmi said anyone leaving his house would be shot vs Shadmi's denial that returning workers were discussed. (mg_s01)
- Shadmi's rank: colonel (Wikipedia, trial record) vs brigadier (other summaries). (mg_s01)
- Kafr Qasim trial, number tried: eleven (JVL; case title "Malinki and 10 others") vs thirteen (Ynet 2014). (cw3_kafr_qasim_trial)
- Shadmi's charges: procedural offences only, prosecutor did not hold him responsible for the killings (Orbach) vs acquitted of murder and convicted of extending the curfew (Wikipedia). (cw3_kafr_qasim_trial)
- Shadmi's fine: 10 prutot = 1/100 lira (Wikipedia; +972; Segev) vs 10 prutot described as "1/1000 of an old Israeli pound" (JVL, internally inconsistent) vs "a single pruta" (PalQuest) vs "a penny" (Cook). (cw3_kafr_qasim_trial)
- Release of the convicted: all out by about Nov 1959 (Wikipedia) vs by 1960 (PalQuest) vs none served over 3½ years (JVL). (cw3_kafr_qasim_trial)
- Plan Mole: killing linked to an expulsion plan (Levy's testimony via Haaretz summaries; Segev; PalQuest) vs not shown by the record (plan text redacted; Shadmi's denial; two of three units did not shoot; 1958 court's framing). (cw3_kafr_qasim_memory)
- Knesset recognition vote: 26 Oct 2021 (Wikipedia citing Haaretz) vs the day before Herzog's 29 Oct visit (Times of Israel). (cw3_kafr_qasim_memory)
- Eisenhower Doctrine's purpose: communism by its text (PL 85-7 §2) vs succeeding Britain and France (Office of the Historian; Hahn). (cw3_aftermath)
- Who won: Israel (Oren; IDF) vs Nasser (Hahn; Shlaim and Fromkin as Oren reports them). (cw3_aftermath)
