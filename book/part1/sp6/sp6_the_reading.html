The Inner Reading: One Line Through the Body

This section is a reading. It makes no claim that the record proves.

Read together, the four images draw one vertical line through the body. At the bottom is a bone the Romans and Greeks called holy, which rabbinic tradition calls indestructible, and on which the yoga traditions coil a serpent. [cite:sp_wikt_sacrum] [cite:sp_wiki_luz] [cite:cn_brit_kundalini] In the middle is the breath, which every one of these traditions uses as the lever. At the top is a single gland, alone among the brain's paired parts, that answers to light. [cite:cn_sep_pineal] [cite:sp_nlm_pineal] The reading proposes that the stories of a serpent lifted up are stories about this line: the bronze serpent raised on the pole, the Son of Man lifted up, the power raised from the base of the spine to the crown. [cite:sp_esv_jn3_2k18] [cite:sp_ww_hyp]

The top of the line has a name in Genesis. After wrestling through the night, Jacob names the place Peniel, "for I have seen God face to face." [cite:cn_esv_gen3230] Modern readers have heard the pineal in Peniel, and Jesus's saying that "if thine eye be single, thy whole body shall be full of light" seems to point to the same place. [cite:cn_wiki_mt622] This is a pun across languages, not an etymology: the Hebrew name means "face of God", and "pineal" comes from the Latin pinea, a pine cone. [cite:sp_wikt_pineal] It works as an image for a reader, and it should be held as one.

The pattern of forbidding

The record of who forbade what shows a consistent shape. The image is kept and the practice is policed. Hezekiah broke the bronze serpent because people made offerings to it, yet the story of its healing stayed in scripture, and John made it a figure of the cross. [cite:sp_esv_jn3_2k18] The Ophites who read the serpent as a teacher were condemned, while the serpent sayings stayed in the canon. [cite:sp_brit_ophites] Barlaam mocked the breath prayer and lost. Rome in 1989 allowed the methods but fenced the meaning of what they produce. [cite:sp_wiki_hesychast] [cite:sp_va_orationis] And when a government forbade a drug, a psychiatrist turned to the breath. [cite:sp_wiki_grof] In each case the authority did not abolish the map; it reserved the right to say what the map means.

The same record sets limits on the reading. The yoga texts and the Hebrew Bible come from different worlds with no documented line of contact. The sacred secretion is an American teaching of 1918, not an ancient one. The pineal's psychedelic role is untested in humans. [cite:sp_carey_antichrist] [cite:sp_sd_dmt] What the record does support is narrower and still striking: across separate traditions, people placed awakening on the same line through the body, and the breath was the means they used to move along it. The breath, at least, is now measured. [cite:sp_pmc_kox]
