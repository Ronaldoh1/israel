Part Eight measures the two sides. Hamas and Israel are often described as two parties to a war, and in law they are; in resources they are not comparable, and this part sets the numbers side by side. It compares the economies of Israel and Gaza; Israel's military spending and the aid that supports it, from the United States above all; Hamas's own money, from taxes, Iran, Qatar and investments; and the weapons and fighters on each side. It then puts the gap into everyday terms, and ends with what the gap does not mean.
