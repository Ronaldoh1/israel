This part closes Book Six with the two occasions on which the idea at the centre of this book, a chief executive who fixes a failing institution by setting aside the usual consultation, was tried in public. The first was small and voluntary: Curtis Yarvin's return in August 2024 as the self-described "wartime CEO" of the foundation behind his own network, Urbit, and the owners' vote that ended it. The second was national: the Department of Government Efficiency of 2025 and 2026, which was not his program and which he criticised. The single section sets the two cases side by side, what each promised, what happened, who checked it and what lasted, states the strongest readings against treating either as a test, and then grades the book's worst-case reading against Book Six as a whole: the pathway, the force and these two tests together.
