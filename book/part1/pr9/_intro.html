Part Nine turns to the West Bank, where journalists are not kept out but are watched, arrested and, in one case that became known worldwide, killed. It begins with Shireen Abu Akleh, the Al Jazeera correspondent shot in Jenin in 2022, and the conclusions of the Israeli army and the United States; then the arrests of journalists since October 2023, most of them held without charge; and the raid that closed Al Jazeera's bureau in Ramallah.
