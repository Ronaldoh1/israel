Part Seven established that the education promise is real on average and fragile at specific points. Public schools are free to attend, but about 27 cents of every school dollar comes from local property tax, the Supreme Court held in 1973 that unequal funding raises no federal constitutional issue, and the main studies find that districts serving the poorest pupils and most students of color receive less state and local money than their needs require, even though state aid on average more than offsets the local gap for poor pupils. [cite:ad7_cb_trends25] [cite:ad7_ace_pell] A bachelor's degree still brings about 75 per cent higher lifetime earnings, but defaults are concentrated among borrowers who did not finish or attended for-profit and two-year colleges, and degree requirements filter workers out of jobs that do not need a degree.
