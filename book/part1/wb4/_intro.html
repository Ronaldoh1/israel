Part Four covers the sixteen years from the Likud's victory in 1977 to the Oslo agreement of 1993, the years in which settlement in the West Bank became a mass enterprise. It follows the religious movement that drove it and the government that adopted its programme; the legal mechanism devised after the Supreme Court barred the seizure of private land, by which hundreds of thousands of dunams were declared "state land"; and the settler underground that bombed Palestinian mayors and plotted to blow up the Dome of the Rock.
