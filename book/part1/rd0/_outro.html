By the end of 1896 nothing that would later make a Jewish state possible had yet been decided, but most of the ground on which it would be decided had been laid. It lay in layers, each built by different hands for purposes of their own, and almost none of them for Zionism.

The legal layer was Ottoman, and it was the firmest. Palestine was a set of imperial districts under a sultanate that had promised its subjects equality in 1839 and 1856 and granted a constitution in 1876, which Abdul Hamid II suspended, with its parliament, in February 1878. [cite:rs_rd0_gulhane_wiki] [cite:rs_rd0_reform1856_wiki] [cite:rs_rd0_firstconst_wiki] Two bodies of Ottoman law now pulled against each other. The Land Code and Tabu law of 1858 and the law on foreigners' property of 1867 had created a registered, saleable deed and placed a foreign owner "upon terms of equality with Ottoman subjects." [cite:wiki_ottoman_land_code_1858] [cite:rs_rd0_frus1898_law1867] The decisions and notes of 1882, 1888 and 1894 then tried to close that door to one group, ending with the bar of 27 March 1894 on land for Jewish immigrants "for political reasons." [cite:rs_rd0_barin_2014] [cite:rs_rd0_frus1888_may] [cite:rs_rd0_frus1898_angell] Above both stood treaties the Porte could not revoke alone: the Capitulations, and Article 62 of the Treaty of Berlin, which Britain cited against the land rules in 1893. [cite:rs_rd0_cojs_fo1893] Ottoman sovereignty over Palestine is settled. What remains contested is the character of the land laws, a loophole exploited on the reading of a Marmara thesis or a wider privatization on Kark's, and the date the land ban began, 1892, 1893 or 1894. [cite:rs_rd0_marmara_thesis] [cite:rs_rd0_kark_2017] [cite:rs_rd0_landpurchase_wiki_bans]

The administrative layer is where the law met the ground, and where it leaked. The governor of Jerusalem limited pilgrims' stays and, with a "Commission for Sale" chaired by the mufti, blocked sales in 1893. [cite:rs_rd0_cojs_moore1887] [cite:rs_rd0_beska_2007] Against him stood consuls who could take foreign Jews under their protection, as Britain had declared in 1847 it would for Jews whose own states did not, and ambassadors who called the pilgrim rule "ipso facto null and void" for British protégés. [cite:rs_rd0_barin_2014] [cite:rs_rd0_cojs_moore1887] By Barın's estimate more than 50,000 Jews settled despite the bans. [cite:rs_rd0_barin_2014] The Porte could issue rules; it could not enforce them against the treaty powers.

The institutional and financial layer was European and older than any of this. Since 1694 the British state had borrowed from private subscribers through a chartered company in the City, and the London Rothschild house had lent to Prussia in 1818, supplied the Bank of England in 1825 and advanced £4,000,000 for the Suez shares in 1875, which Disraeli defended as "a highway to our Indian Empire." [cite:rs_rd0_boe_museum] [cite:jwatte_rothschild_myths_record] [cite:rs_rd0_disraeli_1876] Each tie was a contract, and the legend of a family directing governments traces to an antisemitic pamphlet of 1846. [cite:wiki_dairnvaell_satan_pamphlet] The money that reached Palestine did not follow Herzl. Edmond de Rothschild's colonies, roughly thirty by one count and never totaled in any cited source, and Maurice de Hirsch's Jewish Colonization Association, £2,000,000 of authorized capital aimed first at Argentina, were both committed before 1896 to resettlement without a state. [cite:wiki_edmond_james_de_rothschild] [cite:hadassah_mag_hirsch] Herzl ended the year with a pamphlet, a "rude telegram" and no patron. [cite:herzl_diaries_vol2_archive]

The Palestinian Arab layer was a society with its own politics. It had risen in 1834 against conscription and disarmament and been crushed, and historians still divide over whether that revolt was a first stirring of national identity, as Kimmerling and Migdal hold, or a provincial rising for the Ottoman order, as Morris argues. [cite:rs_rd0_revolt1834_wiki] It had sent Yusuf Diya al-Khalidi to the parliament of 1877, where he opposed the Sultan until he was ordered out of Istanbul. [cite:rs_rd0_khalidi_palquest] Arab notables in Jerusalem asked the Grand Vizier on 24 June 1891 to bar Russian Jews and land sales, and in 1895 a Cairo editor accused the colonists of taking Arab livelihoods. [cite:rs_rd0_antizionism_wiki] [cite:rs_rd0_beska_2007] The strongest form of its case, on this record, was that a sovereign's own rules were being evaded through foreign protection, on land often registered in the names of absentee families rather than of those who farmed it. [cite:rs_rd0_landpurchase_wiki] Whether the earlier peasant clashes carried political meaning is disputed between a Jewish Agency overview and Beška. [cite:rs_rd0_jafi_opposition] [cite:rs_rd0_beska_2007]

The Jewish layer held a case of comparable force. Some 24,000 Jews lived in Palestine in 1880 by Barın's estimate, 13,900 of them in Jerusalem, on the eve of the First Aliyah; the movement that followed answered pogroms, the May Laws and a show trial in the first European state to emancipate its Jews. [cite:rs_rd0_barin_2014_pop] [cite:wiki_may_laws] [cite:wiki_dreyfus] Yet most Jews who fled Russia went to America, and Reform Judaism, Jellinek in Vienna and Jerusalem's Orthodox community all opposed the national idea. [cite:wiki_pogroms_russian_empire] [cite:pittsburgh_platform_1885] [cite:rs_rd0_antizionism_wiki_dissent]

The regional layer showed who decided. In 1840 Britain, Austria, Prussia and Russia, not the people of Palestine, returned the country to the Sultan, Britain seeking to "preserve Turkey" on the route to India. [cite:rs_rd0_oriental1840_wiki] [cite:rs_rd0_oriental1840_ellison] Egypt had held it for nine years; Yemen sent its first Jewish emigrants in 1881–82; Argentina offered land to Hirsch's colonists. [cite:rs_rd0_revolt1834_wiki] [cite:rs_rd0_firstaliyah_wiki] [cite:wiki_moises_ville]

The American layer was thin but already double. In 1888 Oscar Straus called passport rules tied to religion "utterly repugnant" to the Constitution, a defense of citizens' equality rather than of settlement; in 1891 the Blackstone Memorial, with more than four hundred signers, asked President Harrison to support a Jewish homeland. [cite:rs_rd0_frus1888_may] [cite:wiki_christian_zionism] The sources do not record Harrison's answer.

What was settled, then, was who held power: the Sultan held it in law, and the treaty powers held it in practice wherever their consuls reached. What was contested was nearly every reading of the period, from the revolt of 1834 to the land laws, the Porte's motives and the meaning of a single page of Herzl's diary. [cite:herzl_diaries] What the record leaves open is how the pieces would move once a political movement entered the game. The next stage begins with [scene:rd1_ottoman|the country Herzl's program was aimed at], and then at Basel in August 1897, with the congress he had resolved to convene instead of the patron he could not find.
