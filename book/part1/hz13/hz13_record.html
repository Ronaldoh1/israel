What the Record Settles

Before Hezbollah. Organised, armed Shia politics in Lebanon predates Israel's 1982 invasion and Iran's revolution: Musa al-Sadr founded the Movement of the Deprived in 1974 and its armed wing, Amal, in 1975. [cite:hz1_brit_amal] [cite:hz1_wiki_sadr] Iran's 1979 constitution committed the new state to "the continuation of the Revolution at home and abroad". [cite:hz1_iran_const] Israel paid and armed a Lebanese militia from the late 1970s: Saad Haddad said Israel had given about $10 million toward his men's salaries by 1982, and the Congressional Research Service later described the South Lebanon Army as a militia Israel "fully funded and equipped". [cite:hz1_csm_haddad] [cite:hz4_crs_sla] By the defence ministry's own 1999 affidavit, Shin Bet officers gave the militia's interrogators at Khiam "professional guidance and training". [cite:hz4_hrw_khiam99]

The founding. Israel invaded on 6 June 1982 with a stated aim of 40 kilometres and reached Beirut within a week; Lebanese police counted about 18,000 to 19,000 dead by the end of August, and Israel's Kahan Commission found Ariel Sharon personally responsible for ignoring the danger of the Sabra and Shatila killings. [cite:hz2_idf_lw1] [cite:hz2b_csm_dec82] [cite:hz2b_jvl_kahan] Iran's Revolutionary Guards, about 1,000 to 1,500 of them, set up in the Bekaa the same year. [cite:hz2b_wilson_1500] [cite:hz2b_pbs_1000] The Open Letter of February 1985 named Israel and the United States as enemies and Khomeini as the movement's authority. [cite:hz2_cep_letter] [cite:hz2_wiki_ideology] In 2016 Hassan Nasrallah said that everything Hezbollah "eats and drinks, its weapons and rockets, come from the Islamic Republic of Iran". [cite:hz6_alarabiya_2016]

The killing of civilians, by both sides. Hezbollah stated its rule in 1995: when Israel shelled its villages, "we will shell northern Palestine". [cite:hz4_hrw_pawns] Human Rights Watch found in 2006 that Hezbollah fired rockets "indiscriminately and at times deliberately" into Israeli towns, and that Israel treated south Lebanon "effectively as a free-fire zone"; at least 1,109 Lebanese were killed, the vast majority civilians, against 121 Israeli soldiers and 44 civilians. [cite:hz5b_hrw_civassault] [cite:hz5_hrw_why9] [cite:hz5_wiki_war] Israel fired up to four million cluster submunitions, about 90 percent of the strikes in the last 72 hours, and its own Winograd Commission found the use out of conformity with international law. [cite:hz5b_hrw_flooding] [cite:hz5_wiki_war] In 2008 the head of Northern Command said that what happened to the Dahieh would happen "in every village from which Israel is fired on". [cite:hz5_jps_khalidi]

Hezbollah's own conduct at home. It turned its guns on Beirut in May 2008 and came away with a cabinet veto; a UN-backed tribunal convicted three of its members over Rafik Hariri's killing, and Hezbollah never handed them over; it fought for Bashar al-Assad from 2013, in sieges the UN commission found unlawful. [cite:hz6_crs_2008] [cite:hz6_aje_2008tl] [cite:hz6_almon_stl2022] [cite:hz6b_coi2013]

The war of 2023–24 and after. Hezbollah opened fire on 8 October 2023 "in solidarity" with Gaza. [cite:hz7_aj_8oct] ACLED counted 11,238 Israeli attacks on Lebanon and 2,370 by Hezbollah and its allies in the first year. [cite:hz7_aj_acled_year] Lebanon's health ministry counted at least 4,047 dead by December 2024; an Israeli institute counted 45 Israeli civilians and 83 soldiers. [cite:hz7_olj_abiad_4047] [cite:hz7_alma_summary] Netanyahu acknowledged the pager operation. [cite:hz7_toi_netanyahu_pagers] The ceasefire text of 27 November 2024 bound both sides; Israel stayed on five hilltops after the deadline, and Hezbollah fired again on 2 March 2026, after which Israeli strikes killed 2,294 people in Lebanon in six weeks by the ministry's provisional count. [cite:hz8_toi_text] [cite:hz8_national_five] [cite:hz8_am_mar2] [cite:hz8_olj_toll]

The balance of power. Israel's 2025 military budget, $48.3 billion, was more than twice Lebanon's whole 2023 economy, and the United States provides $3.8 billion a year besides. [cite:hz9_sipri_2025] [cite:hz9_ifl_rdna] [cite:hm_dn_mou] Israel itself armed or backed groups across its borders, by its own officials' statements: rebels on the Golan, whom a former army chief acknowledged arming, and clans in Gaza, which Netanyahu said Israel had "activated". [cite:hz11_toi_eisenkot] [cite:hz11_ynet_clans]

What Is Contested

Whether Israel made Hezbollah. Ehud Barak's view that Israel's presence in Lebanon created Hezbollah is attested from July 2006, when the scholar Augustus Richard Norton paraphrased it on American radio; the widely quoted wording, "It was our presence there that created Hezbollah", could not be traced to its original interview, though in July 2026 Barak told the Associated Press that the occupation was "not serving Israeli security". [cite:hz3a_npr_norton] [cite:hz3a_ap_barak] [cite:hz3_statesman_barak] [cite:hz3_itic_shapira] Augustus Richard Norton named two causes, the occupation "and Iran". [cite:hz3_npr_norton] Shimon Shapira argues the decision was taken in Tehran before the invasion, and quotes Naim Qassem saying the invasion "was not the reason Hezbollah was founded". [cite:hz3_itic_shapira] Each side rests on documented facts; the conclusion each draws is a counterfactual and stays an argument.

Who bombed the barracks. A US judge found in 2003 that Hezbollah carried out the 1983 bombing on Iranian orders, and awarded about $2.66 billion in 2007. [cite:hz2_anderson] [cite:hz2_peterson07] Iran did not appear, so the judgments were entered by default; Iran called them "baseless" and "politically motivated", and Hezbollah, Iran and Syria deny any part. [cite:hz2_jurist_2007] [cite:hz2_wiki_barracks]

Buenos Aires. Argentina's Cassation Court ruled in April 2024 that Iran ordered the 1992 and 1994 bombings and Hezbollah carried them out. [cite:hz4_bat_cassation] The investigation's first judge was convicted of paying for false testimony, the accused have never appeared, and Iran's foreign ministry called the charges "Zionist propaganda". [cite:hz4_bat_cassation] [cite:hz4_aje_2006]

Qana, 1996. The UN's investigator found it "unlikely" that the shelling of the UN compound was the result of gross error; Israel said it was a chain of mistakes. [cite:hz4_un_qana]

Majdal Shams. Israel and the United States attributed the rocket that killed twelve children on 27 July 2024 to Hezbollah, and Human Rights Watch found the damage consistent with a Falaq-type rocket; Hezbollah said it had "no connection to this incident" and blamed an Israeli interceptor, and no independent international investigation has reported. [cite:hz7_wiki_majdal] [cite:hz7_aj_majdal]

The pagers. UN experts and Human Rights Watch called the detonations unlawful booby-traps; military lawyers called them precise sabotage of military equipment. No court has ruled. [cite:hz7_ohchr_experts] [cite:hz7_npr_law]

Hezbollah's strength. Pre-war estimates of its rockets ran from 75,000 to 200,000; Israel's claims to have destroyed 70 to 90 percent were followed in April 2026 by its northern commander's admission of a "gap". [cite:hz9_usip_arsenal] [cite:hz9a_jpost_8590] [cite:hz9_toi_milo] Estimates of Iran's money range from $100 million to $1 billion a year; the most cited, $700 million, came in 2018 from US officials running a pressure campaign. [cite:hz6_wilson_record] [cite:hz9_wiki_irfund] [cite:hz6_icg_crt2018]

The truce counts. UNIFIL recorded about 7,800 Israeli air violations in the first year; Lebanon counted 331 dead, the UN verified 127 civilians, and the Israeli army said it killed more than 370 members of armed groups, which it called enforcement, not breach. The counts cannot all be complete on the same basis. [cite:hz8_un_unifil_year] [cite:hz8_aa_tabatabai] [cite:hz8_ohchr_nov25] [cite:hz8_idf_year]

Who pushed the 2026 war. Netanyahu argued for striking Iran for years, and reporting this book could not independently confirm says he lobbied for the February 2026 strike; the order was Trump's, on the stated ground of "imminent threats", while the IAEA had no evidence enrichment had resumed and in the week after the strikes 55 percent of registered voters told Quinnipiac that Iran had not posed an imminent military threat. [cite:hz12_cato_hoffman] [cite:hz8_wiki_iranwar] [cite:ow7_npr_epicfury] [cite:hz12_qu_mar2026] Which man's will was decisive is argument.
