Part Six set out Hezbollah's own record. It sat in parliament and cabinet from 1992 and 2005 while keeping the only army in Lebanon outside the state's control, and in May 2008 it turned that army on Beirut and came away with a veto over the cabinet. [cite:hz6_wilson_record] [cite:hz6_crs_2008] [cite:hz6_aje_2008tl] A UN-backed tribunal convicted three of its members of the 2005 bombing that killed Rafik Hariri and 21 others; Hezbollah denied any role and never handed them over. [cite:hz6_un_stl2020] [cite:hz6_almon_stl2022] Its supporters attacked protesters in 2019, and its leaders demanded the removal of the judge investigating the port explosion, an inquiry that, as of August 2026, had still produced no indictment. [cite:hz6_hrw_2019] [cite:hz6_aje_nasrallah_bitar] [cite:hz6_cm_port2026] It sent thousands of fighters to keep Bashar al-Assad in power and lost an estimated 1,000 to 2,000 of them, and by its own leader's account its budget and its weapons come from Iran, put by US officials at about $700 million a year.
