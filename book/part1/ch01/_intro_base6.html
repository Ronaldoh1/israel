The Baseline ends with the channels through which most people meet this history: the classroom, the historians, organized state messaging, and the platforms on which the argument is now conducted. The chapter comes last because the reader can now set those channels against the record laid out in the chapters before it, and because the contest over how the story is told is itself part of the documented history.

The first section follows the story as American students meet it: the textbooks of four publishers, a federal law that bars the Department of Education from directing curriculum, publishers who certify their own accuracy, and the day schools and Birthright trips that reinforce the story for some Jewish students.[cite:usc_20_1232a] [cite:wiki_birthright_israel] It holds the Palestinian Authority's textbooks to the same test, through a study commissioned by the European Union and published in 2021.[cite:gei_faq] The second section maps how historians argue about 1948: the older Israeli account, the "new historians" who worked in archives opened in the 1980s, their critics, and the dispute between the settler-colonial and national-liberation frames.[cite:wiki_deir_yassin] [cite:massad_settler_colonial] The third sets out coordination on the record: a pollster's language guide of 2009, a covert campaign that Israeli officials tied to a ministry which denies it, and paid influencer contracts disclosed under American foreign-agent law, alongside an Iran-linked network aimed at Israelis.[cite:luntz_pdf] [cite:toi_stoic] [cite:jta_esther_project_fara] [cite:calcalist_iran_social_media_network] The last examines content moderation: a Human Rights Watch report of 1,050 cases, Meta's own commissioned review, the takedown requests of Israel's Cyber Unit, and antisemitic content that evaded moderation in the same years.[cite:hrw_meta_broken_promises] [cite:hrw_meta_promises] [cite:adl_tiktok_antisemitism_loopholes]

The record in this chapter runs in more than one direction, and the sections say so. What it supports is narrower than either familiar story: not purely organic opinion and not a single directing hand, but named institutions, documented sums and stated limits.
