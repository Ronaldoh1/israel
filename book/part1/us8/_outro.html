Part Eight established that both states promise equality to their citizens and fall short of it in measurable, documented ways. In Israel, Arab families' poverty fell from about 53 per cent in 2015 to 37.6 per cent in 2024 and Arab women's employment rose from a third to nearly half, after budgeted plans that were underspent and, since 2023, cut. Those gaps sit alongside a Nation-State Law that gives national self-determination to Jews alone. The median white family held about nine times the wealth of the median Black family in 2025, after a 25 per cent fall in Black median wealth since 2022. Meanwhile the legal tools of the 1960s were narrowed from 2013 to 2026.
