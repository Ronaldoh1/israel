At the center of the American network stands one organization. Part Two describes it: how a one-man office opened in 1951 became AIPAC, the episodes that shaped its reputation, the four legal bodies through which it now works, how much each takes in according to its own tax returns, how little it spends on lobbying compared with its reputation, and the decision of December 2021 that turned a lobby without a campaign budget into the largest electoral spender among pro-Israel groups. The earlier history is told in other volumes and linked here rather than retold.
