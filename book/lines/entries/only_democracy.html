For its own citizens, the line holds up on the main international measures, but it leaves out millions of people Israel governs. In its 2024 report, the V-Dem Institute still ranked Israel as the only electoral democracy in the Middle East and North Africa, a region where 98% of people live under autocracies. [cite:globes_vdem] Israel holds competitive elections, has a free press, and Arab citizens vote and sit in the Knesset.

The same report demoted Israel from "liberal democracy" for the first time in about 50 years, citing attacks on the judiciary and a decline in freedom from torture; Freedom House's score fell from 80 in 2017 to 73. [cite:toi_vdem] The Israel Democracy Institute called this "troubling, but not catastrophic." [cite:idi_vdem] Both indices assess Israel without the occupied territories. Palestinians in the West Bank live under Israeli military control but cannot vote in Israel's elections; by one count, about 5.5 million of roughly 15.5 million people under Israel's control lack citizenship.
