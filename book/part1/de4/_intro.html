Part Four is about Nick Land, the English philosopher who coined the phrase "the Dark Enlightenment" in 2012. It follows him from the University of Warwick and the Cybernetic Culture Research Unit of the 1990s, through the idea of accelerationism and its split into left and right versions, to Shanghai, where he has lived and written since the 2000s. It then summarises his ten-part serial instalment by instalment, separating what he credits to Moldbug from what he added, and ends with the serial's hardest passages, on race and heredity, quoted exactly and in context, together with his 2014 "Hyper-Racism" post and the critics who answered him.
