The Ledger

Land

The Jewish National Fund, founded in 1901, leased its land rather than selling it, so that no later sale could undo a purchase; its founding resolution called the land "the eternal possession of the Jewish people". [cite:jnf_wiki] The Absentees' Property Law of 1950 placed the property of Palestinians who had left their homes under a state custodian, including the "present absentees" who stayed inside Israel but were away from their homes on the dates the law set. [cite:wiki_present_absentee] On 30 March 1976, Land Day, six Arab citizens of Israel were killed in protests against land expropriation in the Galilee. [cite:wiki_land_day] In the West Bank, excluding East Jerusalem, settlers numbered about 110,000 when the Oslo Accords were signed and about 500,000 by the 2020s; with East Jerusalem the total is now around 737,000. [cite:peacenow_settlement_population] [cite:globalsecurity_two_state] [cite:btselem_settlement_data]

Lives

Arab citizens of Israel lived under military rule from 1948 to 1966. On 29 October 1956 Israeli border police at Kafr Qasim shot dead 49 villagers returning from their fields under a curfew they had not been told of. [cite:wiki_kafr_qasim_massacre] [cite:conversation_kafr_qasim_60] In Gaza, the toll of the war since October 2023 is given in Part Five. When the US- and Israeli-backed Gaza Humanitarian Foundation replaced the UN's roughly 400 distribution points with four sites from 27 May 2025, the UN recorded more than 2,100 people killed near its sites and along aid routes by 4 September 2025; the foundation ended its mission in November. [cite:sq_aj_ghf_end] [cite:wiki_ghf] On 7 October 2023, 251 people were taken hostage into Gaza. [cite:wiki_hostage_crisis]

Israel's own count is kept by the same discipline. Its Ministry of Defense counted 25,644 soldiers and pre-state fighters killed since 1860 as of Memorial Day 2026. Its National Insurance Institute counted 5,313 civilians killed in hostile acts since 1851 by 2026, including 1,017 since 7 October 2023, of whom 778 were killed on that day, among them 58 children and 76 foreign nationals. [cite:halakha_yomhazikaron_2026] [cite:btl_nii_2025] [cite:jns_memorial_2025] [cite:tps_memorial_2025] No toll in this ledger cancels another.

Money

In 1933 the Haavara agreement between the Zionist Federation of Germany, the Anglo-Palestine Bank and the Nazi economics ministry let some 60,000 German Jews move to Palestine with part of their assets, in the form of German exports. [cite:jvl_haavara] [cite:wiki_haavara_agreement] Under the 2016 memorandum, the United States gives Israel $3.8 billion a year in military aid from 2019 to 2028, and by 2028 all of it must be spent on American-made weapons. [cite:fdd_mou_phaseout] [cite:buy_american] Israel's arms exports reached a record $14.795 billion in 2024, during the Gaza war, with Europe's share rising to 54 percent. [cite:sq_globes_exports] [cite:defensepost_exports_2024] In December 2024 Israel's foreign ministry secured a $150 million public-diplomacy budget, about twenty times the usual, and filings under the Foreign Agents Registration Act later disclosed paid influencer campaigns in the United States. [cite:jta_esther_project_fara] [cite:wiki_esther_project_israel]

Surveillance and law

In 2021 the Pegasus Project, a consortium of 17 news organisations, reported a leaked list of more than 50,000 phone numbers selected as possible targets by clients of the Israeli firm NSO Group. [cite:forbiddenstories_about_pegasus_project] [cite:wiki_pegasus_project_investigation] In 2000, under American pressure, Israel cancelled the sale of Phalcon airborne radar to China and later paid China compensation. [cite:ynet_phalcon_2000] [cite:flightglobal_phalcon_350m] The Paris Protocol of 1994 made Israel the collector of the customs and taxes due to the Palestinian Authority, the money Israel now withholds. [cite:paris_protocol_text] [cite:unctad_fiscal_leakage] In November 2024 the International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Netanyahu and his former defence minister Yoav Gallant, and for Hamas's military commander Mohammed Deif.
