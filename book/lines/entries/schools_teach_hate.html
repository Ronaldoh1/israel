The most detailed independent study found real problems in Palestinian textbooks, but not a curriculum of incitement to violence. In 2021 the Georg Eckert Institute, commissioned by the EU, published its analysis of Palestinian Authority textbooks and teachers' guides released in 2020. It found they "adhere to UNESCO standards" on human-rights education while expressing "a narrative of resistance" and "antagonism towards Israel." [cite:gei_faq] It also found some content with "escalatory potential," including in STEM subjects, and some antisemitic depictions. It found no direct calls for violence against Israelis.

The Institute's director later told the European Parliament that books containing antisemitism or incitement do not meet UNESCO standards. [cite:hansard_textbooks] IMPACT-se, a monitoring group, says the study confirms systemic incitement; [cite:impactse_gei] some European analysts say it disproved exaggerated claims. [cite:eumep_textbooks] The study did not examine Israeli textbooks, which other researchers have also criticized.
