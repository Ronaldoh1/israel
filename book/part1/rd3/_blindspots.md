## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency:** rd3_arab_politics (congresses, the women's movement, the Islamic Congress, Istiqlal, the Nashashibi and Husseini parties, choices and rivalries); rd3_qassam (armed choice, and the Mufti's refusal to back him in 1935); rd3_council_1935 (Arab hesitation and the breakup of the united front); s07a4 (the Mufti's role as Shaw found it).
- **The strongest form of the Zionist case:** us_s06 (the Jewish Agency's own 1937 economic case, labelled as advocacy); rd3_council_1935 (why the Zionists rejected minority status); s07a3 (the Revisionist case in its own terms); pf_s01 (the Mandate reading behind the campaign against Passfield).
- **Jewish dissent:** dh_s01 (the Orthodox Old Yishuv and Agudat Israel); rd3_brit_shalom (binationalists); hv_s01 (Wise, Silver and Jabotinsky against Haavara); rd3_council_1935 (Agudath Israel's qualified view).
- **Jews of the Arab world:** rd3_mena_jews (Iraq, Constantine); s07a4 (the Old Yishuv communities of Hebron and Safed).
- **Real antisemitism, named plainly:** hol_s01, hol_s02, hv_s01, rd3_mena_jews; the massacres of 1929 in s07a4.
- **Palestinian citizens of Israel:** not yet relevant; hl_s01 notes Arab admission to the Histadrut in 1959 as a forward link.
- **Terms examined:** "Hebrew labor" (hl_s01); "absorptive capacity" (rd3_aliyot, pf_s01); "citizen" and "naturalized" (rd3_mandate_1923); "binational" (rd3_brit_shalom); "terrorism" and "resistance" for al-Qassam, with both readings attributed (rd3_qassam).
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** pf_s01 (the pressure on a minority government, and the inquiries it overrode); hv_s01 (Nazi and Zionist interests that met for different reasons); us_s04 (organized support, not secret control).
- **Money followed alignment, and where it did not:** us_s03 (non-Zionist money joining the Agency); hv_s01; us_s06.
- **Independent sources:** the 1929 casualty figures, the Hebron rescue, Qassam and Brit Shalom each rest on at least two independent sources, or are flagged where they do not.
