Every country that teaches this history teaches its own version, and the differences follow a pattern. What a nation's classrooms say about 1948, the Holocaust or the occupation tracks that nation's own past and its government's present interests: German responsibility, Israeli statehood, Palestinian dispossession, Arab diplomacy, British impartiality law, French publishing politics.

The part opens with two surveys: how governments around the world read the conflict, and how the Holocaust is taught in the world's classrooms. It then turns to the classroom itself.
