Part Seven covers the six years in which the dispute over Iran's nuclear programme came closest to a settlement and then came apart. It begins with the interim agreement of November 2013 and the Joint Comprehensive Plan of Action of July 2015: what the deal actually limited, for how long, and how its limits were checked. It follows Benjamin Netanyahu's campaign against the deal, including his address to Congress in March 2015, and the lobbying effort in Washington that failed to stop it. It then sets out what the International Atomic Energy Agency reported about Iran's compliance, the archive Netanyahu presented in April 2018, the American withdrawal a week later, the "maximum pressure" sanctions that followed, and Iran's step-by-step breaches of the deal's limits from May 2019. It closes with what Israeli and American officials later said about the withdrawal, on both sides of the question, and with a comparison of the deal's limits against what the IAEA recorded in Iran in 2025.
