- The Iran–Israel conflict, built from the simulation's Iran Architecture chapter (ch16), the Israel module's Iran research and the Sophia Iran module, with the same approach as the Hamas and Hezbollah books.
- Netanyahu's decades of warnings that Iran is weeks, months or a few years from a bomb, which never came true: document the pattern from his years in the United States onward, and the same pattern among other Israeli officials.

## Parts
1. **The Alliance, 1948–1979.** The 1953 coup; Iran's de facto recognition (1950); Ben-Gurion's periphery doctrine; Mossad and SAVAK; the Eilat–Ashkelon pipeline and the oil (and the 2015 Swiss arbitration still being paid); Israeli arms and advisers under the Shah.
2. **Revolution and the Secret Arms Trade, 1979–1988.** Khomeini; Israel's mission handed to the PLO; Iraq's war on Iran; Israeli arms to Iran (Operation Seashell, the 1985–86 TOWs and Hawks, Iran-Contra); Sharon's contradictions; why Israel armed the Islamic Republic.
3. **The Jews of Iran: Paradox or Two Facts?** 2,700 years of community; numbers (census vs community estimates; the "20,000+" figure tested); constitutional recognition (Arts. 13 and 64), the reserved Majlis seat; Khomeini's reported assurances; Habib Elghanian's execution (1979); the 1999–2000 Shiraz trial; restrictions; emigration; the regime's anti-Zionism vs its Holocaust-denial conferences and leaders' statements; the community's own statements (and the limits on their freedom to speak). The sim calls it "two documented facts, not a paradox": both sides given.
4. **The Atom.** Atoms for Peace and the US-supplied Tehran reactor (1967); the Shah's programme; the NPT (Iran a party, Israel not); Israel's own undeclared arsenal (Dimona, SIPRI estimates, Vanunu); 2002 revelations; IAEA findings; the 2003 halt (US NIE 2007); enrichment to 60%; what the IAEA said before each war.
5. **Always a Year Away: Netanyahu, 1984–2026.** His US years (MIT, BCG, the Jonathan Institute, UN ambassador 1984–88) and what the record shows he warned about then; the Iran predictions, dated and quoted (1992, 1995 book, 1996 Congress, 2002 Iraq testimony, 2009, 2012 UN red line, 2015 Congress, 2018 archive, 2025, 2026) against what followed.
6. **The Other Voices.** Other Israeli officials' predictions (Peres 1992–96, Barak, Ya'alon, others) and Israel's own dissenters (Meir Dagan, Tamir Pardo, Ami Ayalon, Gabi Ashkenazi, the Mossad "Spy Cables" assessment); US officials who echoed or contradicted them; the US intelligence record (NIE 2007, 2011, Gabbard 2025).
7. **The Deal and Its Killing, 2013–2018.** The JCPOA; Netanyahu's 2015 speech to Congress; IAEA verification of compliance; the 2018 archive presentation and US withdrawal; maximum pressure; Iran's step-by-step breaches after 2019.
8. **The Shadow War, 2007–2023.** Stuxnet; the killings of nuclear scientists (2010–2012, Fakhrizadeh 2020); Natanz sabotage; the tanker and shipping war; Iran's attacks and plots abroad; drones and cyber on both sides.
9. **Iran's Own Record.** Leaders' statements about Israel; funding and arming Hezbollah, Hamas, PIJ, the Houthis and Iraqi militias (link the Hezbollah volume); Holocaust-denial conferences; hostage diplomacy; repression at home (1988 executions, 2009, 2019, 2022 Mahsa Amini, January 2026); the April and October 2024 attacks on Israel. Stated plainly.
10. **The Twelve Days, June 2025.** Israel's strikes of 13 June, two days before a scheduled sixth round of US–Iran talks; Iran's missile response; US Operation Midnight Hammer; tolls; the IAEA; the ceasefire; what US intelligence assessed.
11. **The 2026 War.** 28 February 2026 ("Epic Fury"); Khamenei killed; Hormuz; the Lebanese and Red Sea fronts; casualties (contested numbers); the international response; the June memorandum and its collapse; the blockade; status as of October 2026.
12. **Does Iran Want War With America?** The record: negotiations (2013–15, 2021–22, 2025 Oman talks), calibrated retaliation (Ain al-Asad 2020 with warning), stated positions; against it: attacks via militias, hostage-taking, plots on US soil (e.g. against Bolton, Pompeo, Trump as charged by DOJ). Labelled as argument where it is argument.
13. **Why Iran?** What Israel says it wants (no Iranian bomb, the Begin doctrine of nuclear monopoly, an end to Iran's network, regime change appeals of 2025–26); strategic and domestic-political explanations argued by analysts (labelled); documents often cited (A Clean Break 1996 — what it actually says; the Yinon article — contested reading); oil and Hormuz; the power ledger (Iran vs Israel: GDP, budgets, arsenals, US aid).
14. **Dragged In: the American Bill.** US decisions, costs and deaths in the Iran wars; who pushed; Congress; polling (links hz12, ow7_iran rather than repeating).
15. **Settled, Contested and Open.**

## Discipline
- Iran's government's statements and actions (calls for Israel's elimination, proxies, repression, plots) stated plainly and sourced. Israel's and the US's acts stated plainly and sourced. No collective attribution to Iranians, Persians, Jews, Israelis or Shia.
- Predictions are documented quotes with date and venue, set against the record (IAEA, NIEs). "False alarm" is the conclusion of a test, not a premise: where a warning was conditional ("if not stopped") say so.
- The Iranian Jewish community: both facts given (recognition and seat; restrictions, trials, state Holocaust denial); no claim that the community speaks freely; population figures sourced and dated.
- Motives (why Iran) are arguments unless documented; officials' stated reasons given beside critics' analysis.
- Currency: anything ongoing phrased "as of October 2026".
