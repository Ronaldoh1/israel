Part Five ended with a war that Hezbollah began by a raid across the border and Israel fought by bombing Lebanon. Part Six turns from the wars with Israel to what Hezbollah did with its power at home and next door: how it sat in Lebanon's parliament and cabinet while keeping an army the state did not control, how it turned its guns on Beirut in May 2008, how a United Nations-backed court convicted its members of killing a former prime minister, how it fought for Bashar al-Assad in Syria, and who paid for all of it. Hezbollah is not only a product of Israel's invasions and Iran's ambitions; its own choices are set out here in the same plain register used for Israel's elsewhere in this library.
