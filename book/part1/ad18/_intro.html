Part Eighteen brings the volume to its verdict. Seventeen parts have set the promise as taught beside the system as built, one mechanism at a time; this part sets them all in one place. The first section is a single ledger of fifteen mechanisms, from mobility and wages to the rules themselves and the belief that outlasts them, each with the version taught, the record and its key figure, whether the friction was designed or captured, who gains, who pays, and how firmly the record supports the finding. The second grades seven promises of the Dream, a better life than one's parents, a home, a secure job, health care, college as a ladder, a secure retirement and fair rules, on a scorecard whose every sub-score, weight and sum is printed so that a reader can recompute it. The third sets out the levers with documented effects, with the evidence for and against each, then gives the Sophia platform's reading of the Dream as an illusion, labelled as interpretation and answered by the strongest counter-readings, and closes by answering the volume's question in three registers: what is documented, what is probable, and what the record does not support.
