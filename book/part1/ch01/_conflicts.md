# ch01 (Volume I, The Baseline): source contradictions presented in the prose

One line each: the fact, the versions given, the scenes or sections involved.

- Jerusalem's 1922 census total: 62,578 (Israel's UN letter S/10138) against 52,081 (Jewish Virtual Library compilation, after Oesterreicher and Sinai); both give 33,971 Jews. Unresolved. [ch01_old_yishuv; research old_yishuv_jerusalem]
- Jerusalem's majority: S/10138 counts the city (Jewish majority 1922, 1931, 1944 and before), saying S/10130 (Jordan and the UAR) used the wider sub-district (Arab majority); the Arab figures are not in the sources. [ch01_old_yishuv]
- Jewish population of the land, 1880: about 27,000 (National Library of Israel) against 23,000–26,000 (most researchers); McCarthy's Ottoman-register count of 13,942 for 1878 excludes foreign nationals. [ch01_old_yishuv]
- Population of Ottoman Palestine: about 600,000 by the late 19th century (three districts) against the 1871–72 census of 381,954 (Bedouin excluded) and McCarthy's register totals (440,850 in 1878; 516,131 in 1890). [s00h; s00i; ch01_old_yishuv]
- Bar Kokhba revolt's end: 135 in some sources, 136 in others. [ah_s01]
- Why Rome renamed Judaea Syria Palaestina: punitive (Feldman; reference summaries) against administrative or Hellenizing, possibly 130 CE (Syme, Jacobson); no ancient source states it. [ah_s01; cmt_s07]
- Arafat's birthplace: Cairo, largely accurate though disputed by some sources. [sk_s00; ah_s01; cmt_s01; cmt_s07]
- Family-unification ban, terror cases: 45 linked in 2001–2020 (Interior Ministry data via IDI) against about 48 in 2001–2021 (Shin Bet testimony). [ch01_citizenship; research return_citizenship_laws]
- Druze in Israel: about 150,000 (Minority Rights Group) against about 152,000 (CBS, as reported April 2024). [ch01_communities; research other_communities]
- Population of the Negev's unrecognized villages: about 70,000 (ACRI, c. 2011), about 100,000 (MRG), about 150,000 (Adalah via Al-Monitor, May 2024). [ch01_communities]
- First official Jewish use of the Star of David in Prague: described as the community's seal and as a flag (1354 grant). [s00d]
- Bund membership: more than 400,000 by 1917 against "tens of thousands" in the early 20th century. [s00c; s00b3]
- Baghdad's Jewish population: 140,000 in 1917 (about 40% of the city) against about 140,000 in 1945 (about a third) against 90,000–140,000 at mid-century. [s00e; s00b2; irq_e01]
- Farhud death toll: 150–180 (USHMM); 128 by the Iraqi commission's count, independent estimates to 180; some sources 500 or more; s00e gives "an estimated 180 or more". [s00e2; s00e; irq_e03]
- Iraq's Jewish population before the airlift: about 135,000 (Israeli government) against about 130,000; number airlifted: 107,000+ by end 1951 plus ~16,000 by other routes, against summary figures of 120,000–130,000. [s00e2; irq_e05]
- Ezra and Nehemiah's route: via Cyprus and Iran, against via Cyprus at first then direct Baghdad–Lod flights. [s00e2; irq_e05]
- Baghdad bombings, 1950–51: attributed to Zionist agents (Iraqi government, some historians) against Israeli denial; archival review finds insufficient evidence. [s00e2; irq_e05]
- Lavon Affair: intended scapegoats given as Egyptian nationalists or communists, and as nationalists or the Muslim Brotherhood; Lavon resigned, or was forced out; who gave the order (Lavon against Gibli) unresolved. [s00e2; lv_s01]
- Yemenite Children Affair: Cohen-Kedmi Commission (no kidnapping plot substantiated; 42 unaccounted for) against Madmoni-Gerber and family testimonies. [s00e2]
- Ringworm Affair: about 27,000 children often cited, against estimates of 20,000 to more than 100,000. [s00e2]
- Operation Magic Carpet: about 48,000–49,000 against about 49,000 and "nearly 50,000". [s00e2; ch01_exodus_map; ye_s01]
- Jewish exodus total: about 850,000 (1948–72) against 800,000–900,000 (Arab countries and Iran) against Beker's 900,000 "forced out" against a range of 650,000–900,000. [s00e2; ch01_exodus_map; s00e3; me_s07]
- "Refugees": Beker's dual-refugee case (H. Con. Res. 311) against Shenhav and Amayreh, with the Stroum Center's middle line. [ch01_exodus_map; research mizrahi_exodus_by_country]
- Palestinian displacement, 1947–49: about 700,000 against the UN's about 711,000, within a range of 700,000–750,000. [s00e3; s00h; money_1948 panel]
- STOIC operation: NYT (lawmakers, especially Black Democrats; three fake sites) against NPR (younger progressive Americans; five sites found by FakeReporter); Diaspora Affairs Ministry denies involvement. [s00a3; inf_s01]
- Per-post payments to influencers: $1,500, up to $4,250, and $6,100–7,400 (the source of the circulating ~$7,000), from different campaigns and documents. [s00a3; s00a]
- Israel's public-diplomacy budget: $150 million secured December 2024 against $730 million reported for 2026 (different years and sources). [s00a3; s00a]
- Israeli day-school funding plans: NIS 100 million matched by JFNA against a separate NIS 200 million cabinet plan; overlap not established. [s00a]
- Meta's moderation: BSR review finds adverse impact on Palestinian users but no intentional bias, against HRW's "systemic censorship"; Calcalist's methodological critique of HRW. [s00a2; mt_s02]
- 2021 Palestinian textbook study: read by IMPACT-se as confirming systemic incitement, and by European analysts as disproving exaggeration. [s00a; sk_s05]
