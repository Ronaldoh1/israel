Iran: A Chain With Its Own Root

Iran recognised Israel de facto in 1950. The coup of August 1953, which the CIA's own history says was carried out under its direction, restored the Shah, under whom a hidden alliance with Israel grew. [cite:ir1_iranica_israel] [cite:ir1_afp_cia2013] The revolution of 1979 ended it in public within weeks: Israel's mission was handed to the PLO on 19 February 1979, and Khomeini named Israel an enemy. [cite:ir2_wiki_embassy] [cite:hz1_merip_khomeini]

The enmity that followed runs through Lebanon to the war of June 2025, which killed 1,060 or more in Iran by the Foundation of Martyrs' count and 1,190 by HRANA's; Iranian missiles killed 28 people in Israel. [cite:ir10_ap_toll] [cite:ir10_toi_numbers] This chain is the honest limit of the case: its first link, the coup of 1953, had nothing to do with Israel. The founding explains much of the enmity, not all of it. [cite:w_1953_coup] The Iran Architecture tells this in full.
