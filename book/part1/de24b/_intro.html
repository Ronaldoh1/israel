The tools in [scene:de24_data_and_fusion|the toolkit part on data] were bought or built; the records in this part were already in the state's hands, or one request away, because people handed them over to get help, go to school, take a job or borrow money. The first section follows benefit files: how eligibility is checked against dozens of other records, and how Medicaid, food-aid and cash-aid data moved toward immigration enforcement in 2025 and 2026. The second turns to schools, where a federal privacy law protects education records but not the cameras of a school's police unit, and to the student-visa database built for enforcement. The third follows the gates to work, from E-Verify and payroll databases to the continuous vetting of millions of federal workers and the revocation of security clearances. The fourth traces credit files, the Treasury's payment and debt machinery and a cash-reporting order aimed at the southern border, and closes with the part's test of the worst-case reading. Each section ends by grading which joins between these records are documented, probable, possible or not supported.
