# Consciousness: the section map (21 Nov 2026)

**Section:** Consciousness · **Tagline:** "The knowledge they buried, burned and banned"

Shelved in the Library of Truth right after The Dark Enlightenment Architecture and before the Israel volumes still coming soon. Every title is shown as **Coming soon** until Ron picks which to write. The Vatican Architecture (already written) is shelved here too, and stays numbered Volume XI in The Israel Architecture.

**How it's built:** the catalogue is `book/plan/consciousness_catalogue.json` (titles, kind, labels, emblem, colour). `tools/patch_lib_consciousness.py` puts the section into `tools/book_block.html`; re-running it after editing the JSON refreshes the shelf. Then `embed_book.py`, `build_dist.py`, `build_israel_web.py`.

**Sources to draw on:** Sophia's Reignite pillar (Ascension of Consciousness: Gnosticism, Sufism, Kabbalah; The Suppressed Lineages, 22 entries; The Forbidden Arts, 16 entries; The Rosicrucians module), The Vatican Architecture Book Two, and the institutional-religion deliberation files in the project.

**Label notes:** "Nag Hammadi Library" goes only on texts found in the 1945 jar. The Gospel of Mary (Berlin Codex), Pistis Sophia (Askew Codex) and the Gospel of Judas (Codex Tchacos) are labelled "Gnostic codices". The Book of Enoch and Jubilees are "Ethiopian canon" and "Dead Sea Scrolls". Esoteric readings carry the label "Interpretive".
