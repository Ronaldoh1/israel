## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency, including rivalries and choices:** rd5_arab_leadership (the AHC's dependence on the Arab League, the Mufti's demands and the League's refusals, the All-Palestine Government against Abdullah's Jericho Congress); s07i (the AHC's decision to boycott UNSCOP, stated as a choice); s09b (the Army of the Holy War); rd5_abdullah (Abdullah's separate course). The Mufti's wartime alliance with the Axis is named in rd5_arab_leadership and linked to s07a5.
- **The strongest version of the Zionist case:** s07i (the Agency's case to UNSCOP), s09 (the Agency's one-eighth argument and its acceptance of a plan it disliked), rd5_trusteeship (Ben-Gurion and Silver on the reversal), s10 (the Declaration's grounds and its equality and peace clauses), rd5_cities (the Levy account of Haifa, attributed).
- **The strongest version of the Arab and Palestinian case:** s09 (Yusuf's reasons for rejection; the Arab delegations' Charter argument), rd5_invasion (the League's own cablegram), rd5_cities and pd_s01 (Khalidi).
- **Jewish dissent from Zionism:** aac_s01 (Magnes, Ihud); rf_s01 (American Council for Judaism, American Jewish Committee, Arendt, the Einstein–Arendt letter); s07e4 and mil_s01 (the Jewish Agency against the Irgun and Lehi).
- **Jews of the Arab world:** rd5_mizrahi (riots of 1947; Egypt, Iraq, Syria, Libya, Yemen in 1948), with irq_e04 and me_s07.
- **Real antisemitism, named plainly:** Bevin's remarks (rd5_bevin), the anti-Jewish riots in Britain in August 1947 (rd5_britain_exits), Truman's 1947 diary (tr_s01), the violence in Arab cities (rd5_mizrahi). Each is tied to a named person, mob or government.
- **Palestinian citizens of Israel:** only the Declaration's promise (s10) falls in this stage; the record of those who stayed starts in Stage Six and mg_s01.
- **Terms examined:** "trusteeship", "corpus separatum", "de facto / de jure", "Nakba", "transfer" (via pd_s01's readings), "collusion" (rd5_abdullah), "terrorist" (mil_s01, where the government itself used the word for Lehi).
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** s07j, ore_s01 and rd5_trusteeship show the US government divided against itself; so_s01 and cz_s01 show Soviet interest converging with Zionist aims; rd5_abdullah shows British and Hashemite interest; rd5_bevin shows Britain's own strategic reasons.
- **Money followed alignment, and where it did not:** us_s07, s07d and cz_s01 (the money that bought the arms); s09's Firestone case; s07j's ARAMCO lobbying, which failed.
- **Independent sources:** Load-bearing claims in the addenda rest on primary documents (UN records, FRUS, the Declaration, S/745, A/286, the 1949 UNCCP report) or on named scholarship from both sides (Khalidi, Shlaim, Yusuf; Langham, Jevon; Morris as summarized by Lockman). Single-outlet claims are attributed and flagged.
