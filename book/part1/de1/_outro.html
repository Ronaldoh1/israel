Part One established that the Dark Enlightenment is a shelf of texts, not an organisation: Yarvin supplied its political vocabulary, including "the Cathedral" and neocameralism, the idea that "a state is a business which owns a country," and Land named it and credited those terms to him. [cite:de1_land_gwern] [cite:de1_land_ebook] It showed that only the two authors claim the label in their own words; that Yarvin's self-descriptions run from "restorationist" in 2008 to "Monarch is good" in 2025, alongside qualified denials; that Thiel's best-known sentence, "I no longer believe that freedom and democracy are compatible," came with a programme of exit, not seizure; and that Vance's one documented link is a 2021 citation of Yarvin by name. [cite:de1_wiki_yarvin] [cite:de1_nyt_interview] [cite:de1_cato_thiel] [cite:de1_vox_vance] It found that much of the label's later spread rests on adjacency and resemblance, and stated the book's working hypothesis and its grades. Part Two goes back to the beginning: the blog posts of 2007 and 2008, Land's serial of 2012, and the first readers who wrote about them.
