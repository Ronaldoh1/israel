The PayPal network is a web of former colleagues bound by early wealth, shared investments and personal trust, with Thiel, in Fortune's 2007 phrase, "at the center of it all"; it is not an organisation and not a political bloc. [cite:de13_fortune_2007] Its members' politics run from Reid Hoffman's millions for Democrats to David Sacks's $12 million Trump fundraiser and Musk's more than $290 million in 2024. [cite:de13_badger_hoffman] [cite:de13_wiki_sacks] [cite:de14_cnn_musk290] In 2025 people tied to Thiel or PayPal held the vice presidency, the White House science office, the AI and crypto brief, DOGE and the embassy in Denmark; as of October 2026 Musk and Sacks had left their main posts, O'Neill had left HHS for an unconfirmed nomination, and Howery and Kratsios remained in office. [cite:de13_abc_musk_exit] [cite:de13_tc_sacks_exit] [cite:de13_wiki_oneill] [cite:de13_arctic_howery] [cite:de13_wiki_kratsios] The appointments are documented; a plan behind them is not. What the money around these people bought, and where it disappears from view, is the subject of [scene:de14_network_map|Part Fourteen].
