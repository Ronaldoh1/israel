The architecture

Three features recur across the record. Decisions on Israel and the region are concentrated in a few people: a president, his son-in-law, a secretary of state holding four offices and a defence secretary who removed the officers who disagreed. [cite:yahoo_rubio_4jobs] [cite:armytimes_purge_2025] The same governments that are parties to the negotiations, Saudi Arabia, Qatar and the UAE, are investors in the businesses of the family and the envoy. [cite:washpo_kushner_pif] [cite:wiki_wlf] And the instruments of law, from congressional review of arms sales to the International Criminal Court, were set aside or sanctioned when they pointed at Israel or at the administration's own wars. [cite:aca_rubio_bypass] [cite:verfassungsblog_icc] [cite:wiki_iran_war_2026] The record documents each of these; it does not document a plan joining them, and it leaves that question open.
