The Attack

More than a year before 7 October, Israeli officials obtained a Hamas plan of about forty pages, code-named "Jericho Wall," which called for rocket barrages, drones to disable border cameras and machine guns, and gunmen crossing on paragliders, motorcycles and foot; they dismissed it as "aspirational." [cite:gz_nyt_jericho] On the morning of 7 October 2023 Hamas's Qassam Brigades, with at least four other armed groups, carried it out. Human Rights Watch counted 1,195 people killed, 815 of them civilians, in at least nineteen kibbutzim, five moshavim, the towns of Sderot and Ofakim, two music festivals and a beach; 251 civilians and members of the security forces were taken to Gaza. [cite:gz_hrw_oct7] At the Nova festival alone, the army's own count was 378 killed and 44 taken hostage. [cite:gz_wiki_nova]

What investigators found. Human Rights Watch concluded that "the planned murder of civilians and the hostage-taking were crimes against humanity," that killing civilians and taking hostages were "central aims of the planned attack," and documented war crimes including wilful killing, mutilation of bodies and sexual violence. [cite:gz_hrw_oct7] A UN mission led by Pramila Patten found "reasonable grounds to believe" that rape and gang rape occurred, and "clear and convincing information" that some hostages were subjected to sexual violence. [cite:gz_aj_patten] The UN's commission of inquiry found that forty Israeli children were killed that day and children were taken hostage, some of them alone. [cite:pr_un_coi2026]

Hamas's Account, and Israel's Failures

In January 2024 Hamas published a sixteen-page account, "Our Narrative," calling the attack "a necessary step and a normal response to confront all Israeli conspiracies against the Palestinian people," citing prisoners, settlements and the "Judaization" of the West Bank and Jerusalem, and saying its fighters "only targeted Israeli soldiers and people carrying weapons" and that many Israelis "were killed by the Israeli army and police due to their confusion." [cite:gz_aj_narrative] The investigations above found the opposite of its first claim: civilians were a central target. [cite:gz_hrw_oct7] Some Israelis were killed by Israeli fire: the army's inquiry into Be'eri found that a tank fired on a house where hostages were held, and the army said that friendly-fire incidents occurred but "there were not many." [cite:gz_idf_beeri_probe] [cite:gz_pbs_idfprobe]

Israel's own army found that it had "underestimated" Hamas's capabilities and wrongly believed Hamas "was more interested in governing the territory than fighting Israel"; its chief of staff resigned. [cite:gz_pbs_idfprobe] As of October 2026 no state commission of inquiry had been set up. [cite:gz_ynet_inquiry2026]
