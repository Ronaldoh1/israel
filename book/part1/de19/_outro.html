The record of DOGE inside the systems is now largely written by auditors and judges rather than by its critics or its defenders. It shows a standing order of access to every agency's unclassified systems; one Treasury employee with copy access to three payment systems, briefly write access to one, and unencrypted payment data sent out of the bureau; Social Security data placed on an unapproved commercial service, with contents the agency cannot determine; personnel files disclosed in violation of the Privacy Act; and six agencies whose DOGE access the auditors could not even measure.
