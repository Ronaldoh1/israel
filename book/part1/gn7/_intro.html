Part Seven follows the Gnostic outlook after the ancient schools. It traces the Manichaeans, organised into Elect and Hearers and burned by Roman law in 302, and the Mandaeans, the last surviving Gnostic religion, whose community in Iraq lost about ninety per cent of its people after 2003. It then follows the modern revival: a Gnostic church founded in Paris in 1890, Jung's sermons in Basilides' name and his institute's codex, and the bestseller that made the Gnostic gospels famous.
