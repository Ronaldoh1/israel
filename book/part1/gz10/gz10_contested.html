What Remains Contested

On several questions the record holds two documented positions, each sourced, and the volume has set them side by side without deciding between them. They are gathered here.

Some disputes are of a different kind: not between two positions, but between counts. The dead of every war in this volume were counted differently by Palestinian, Israeli and UN bodies, above all on how many had been fighting; the volume has given each count with its source.
