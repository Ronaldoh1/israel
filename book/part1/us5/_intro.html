Part Five turns to the most basic thing a state owes the people under its authority: protection from being killed. It traces homicide in both countries over time, from the American peak of 1980 to the record low of 2025, and Israel's low national rate to its break in 2023. It then follows the killings in Israel's Arab communities, which reached a record 252 dead in 2025, and sets out what the police solved and what successive governments did and cut. Finally it compares the two gun regimes, an American constitutional right and an Israeli discretionary licence, including the loosening of Israel's licensing after 7 October 2023, and what each country's figures on gun deaths and mass shootings do and do not show.
