The Long Reign, 2009–2021

After serving as finance minister from 2003 and resigning in August 2005 in protest against the withdrawal from Gaza, Netanyahu returned as prime minister on 31 March 2009. [cite:ny_wiki_bibi] On 14 June 2009, at Bar-Ilan University, he accepted for the first time a "demilitarized Palestinian state", on conditions including recognition of Israel as a Jewish state. [cite:gi5_aje_barilan] No such state was negotiated in the twelve years that followed.

The settlements

The settler population of the West Bank, outside East Jerusalem, was about 193,000 in 2000 and about 314,000 in 2010; by 2025 it was over 529,000. [cite:ny_wiki_settlerpop] Netanyahu was prime minister for about fifteen of those twenty-five years, including the whole of 2009 to 2021. The West Bank, Documented on this shelf follows the enterprise in detail; here the number is enough to show the direction.

Congress and the Arab vote, 2015

In January 2015 the Speaker of the House, John Boehner, invited Netanyahu to address Congress without consulting the White House. On 3 March, two weeks before his own election, he told a joint session that the nuclear agreement then being negotiated with Iran was "a very bad deal"; dozens of Democrats stayed away. [cite:wiki_netanyahu_2015_congress_address] [cite:npr_netanyahu_very_bad_deal] The Iran Architecture follows the speech and the deal's later killing.

On election day, 17 March 2015, he posted a video warning his supporters that Arab voters were "streaming in quantity to the polling stations". [cite:ny_wiki_arabvoters] He won, and on 23 March apologised to Arab citizens for the remark. [cite:ny_npr_arabvoters]

The Nation-State Law and the Trump years

On 19 July 2018 the Knesset passed, by 62 to 55, the Basic Law declaring Israel the nation-state of the Jewish people, under which the right to national self-determination in Israel is "unique to the Jewish people"; it contains no clause on equality. [cite:gi5_toi_nslaw] [cite:gi5_voa_nsl] The Supreme Court upheld it in 2021. [cite:jta_nsl_upheld]

The years of Donald Trump's first presidency brought the policies Netanyahu had sought: the American embassy opened in Jerusalem in May 2018; a proclamation of March 2019 recognised Israeli sovereignty over the Golan Heights; and on 15 September 2020 Israel, the United Arab Emirates and Bahrain signed the Abraham Accords at the White House. [cite:jerusalem_embassy_2018] [cite:gi8_ucsb_procl9852] [cite:ny_npr_abraham] The alliance with American evangelical leaders, which The Theology Shield follows, was part of the base for those decisions. [cite:cufi_netanyahu_relationship]

Four elections and a fall

Indicted in November 2019, Netanyahu fought four elections in two years without winning a stable majority. On 13 June 2021 a coalition led by Naftali Bennett and Yair Lapid ended his twelve-year run. [cite:ny_wiki_bibi]
