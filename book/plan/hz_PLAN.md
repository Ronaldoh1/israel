## Parts
1. **Before Hezbollah, 1943–1982.** The National Pact and confessional system; 1948 refugees; the PLO's move to Lebanon after 1970; civil war 1975; Musa al-Sadr and Amal; Israel's 1978 Litani operation, UNIFIL (UNSCR 425), Haddad and the South Lebanon Army; Iran's 1979 revolution.
2. **The Founding, 1982–1985.** Operation Peace for Galilee; Beirut siege; Sabra and Shatila and the Kahan Commission; IRGC in the Bekaa; the 1983 US embassy and Marine barracks bombings (US court findings); the 1985 Open Letter, its text quoted and sourced.
3. **Would Hezbollah Exist? The Counterfactual.** Israeli officials' own words (Barak, Rabin era), the occupation as stated cause, against Iran's export-of-revolution aim and Shia mobilisation before 1982. Both sides, labelled as argument.
4. **Occupation and Resistance, 1985–2000.** The security zone; SLA; Khiam; Accountability 1993; Grapes of Wrath and Qana 1996; April Understanding; 2000 withdrawal; Shebaa Farms; abroad: Buenos Aires 1992/1994 (court findings and denials), later Burgas 2012.
5. **The 2006 War.** The raid; the war; casualties both sides; cluster munitions; US role (expedited bombs, Rice); Winograd; UNSCR 1701; the Dahiya doctrine (Eisenkot 2008).
6. **Hezbollah as Its Own Actor.** Politics and the state within the state; the 2008 Beirut clashes; Syria intervention for Assad; Hariri assassination (STL verdicts); finance networks and US designations; contested drug-trade allegations; 2019 protests; the 2020 port blast investigation.
7. **The Support Front, 2023–2024.** 8 Oct 2023; cross-border fire; displacement both sides; Majdal Shams; pagers and radios; Nasrallah killed; the ground invasion; casualties; the 27 Nov 2024 ceasefire terms.
8. **The Broken Ceasefire and the 2026 War, 2024–2026.** The 27 Nov 2024 terms; Israel's retained positions; UNIFIL and Lebanese counts of violations; strikes after the truce; Lebanon's August 2025 disarmament decision and the army plan; the March–April 2026 war (in the wider US–Israel war on Iran from 28 Feb 2026), its toll and how it ended; status as of October 2026 (all checked live).
9. **The Power Ledger.** Hezbollah's money, fighters, arsenal (estimates with ranges) vs Israel's; Lebanon vs Israel (GDP, budgets); US aid to Israel and to the Lebanese army; analogies.
10. **The Rest of the Axis.** Iran's IRGC-QF network; the Houthis (origins, Saudi war, Red Sea 2023–, US and Israeli strikes, Oman truce); Iraqi militias (PMF, Kataib Hezbollah, Tower 22); Syria (Iranian presence, Israel's strikes, Israel's 2013–18 aid to rebels, post-Assad buffer zone); PIJ.
11. **Always the Axis?** Ron's thesis tested pattern by pattern, as Hamas Part 10 did: threat claims vs outcomes; who gains (aid, arms, politics); the "proxy" frame examined; groups Israel itself armed (SLA, Syrian rebels, Gaza militias 2025).
12. **Dragging America In.** US deaths (1983, Tower 22), deployments, Red Sea costs, supplementals, strikes on the Houthis, the Iran wars of 2025–2026, who pushed, polling.
13. **Settled, Contested and Open.**

## Discipline
- Hezbollah's attacks on civilians, its bombings and its role in Syria are stated plainly and sourced. Israel's acts are stated plainly and sourced. No collective attribution to peoples or religions (Shia, Jews, Lebanese, Israelis).
- "Used as a tool / excuse" is a thesis to test: documented instances (Israel arming the SLA, Syrian rebel aid, Gaza militias) are separated from inference about motive. Officials' stated reasons are given beside critics' analysis.
- Counterfactuals labelled as arguments. Estimates labelled with ranges and dates. Ongoing matters phrased "as of <month> 2026".
