The Dark Mirror

This section is a reading. It makes no claim that the record proves.

John Dee's spirit mirror is a disc of polished black obsidian from Aztec Mexico. For the Aztecs, mirrors were gateways to other worlds, and the god Tezcatlipoca, "Smoking Mirror", is often shown wearing them. [cite:fa_smith_deemirror] Dee and Kelley looked into such stones for angels; the angels, a witness at Prague recorded, "could only be seen and heard by Edward Kelley". [cite:ht_quil_rudolf]

The inner reading notices what a black mirror does. It shows almost nothing of the room; what the gazer sees in it comes mostly from within. Scrying, read this way, is a practice of looking past the surface of the world into the darkness of one's own mind, where images rise that the busy day does not allow. The danger is the one Dee's own record shows: what rises may be taken for an outside voice, and another person may claim to see for you.

The record of the seers supports the warning. The Fox sisters' raps were demonstrated as a trick by one of the sisters herself, and a government's remote viewers never produced a usable report. [cite:fa_wiki_fox] [cite:fa_wiki_stargate] The inner reading keeps the mirror and drops the medium: no one can scry for you.

The record limits the reading. Dee believed he was speaking with angels, the Aztecs used mirrors in their own religious system, and neither described scrying as introspection. [cite:fa_smith_deemirror] [cite:ht_brit_dee] The reading is the reader's.
