The Rothschild Email

On 28 February 2016 Epstein wrote to Peter Thiel: "As you may know, I represent the Rothschilds." The line comes from an email in the released files, as quoted by Le Monde and other outlets that reviewed it. [cite:ep15_bblv_rothschild2026] [cite:ep7_lemonde_ariane2026] It is widely shared online as proof that Epstein worked for a banking family.

The date is the part that has been missed. Ten weeks earlier, on 18 December 2015, the Swiss bank Edmond de Rothschild settled with the Justice Department, and reporting citing the files places transfers to Epstein's accounts on 17 and 21 December under a contract that paid him $25 million because the penalty came in low. [cite:ep7_doj_edr2015] [cite:ep7_pbm_edr2026] When he told Thiel he represented "the Rothschilds", he had just been paid by one Rothschild-named company, the only one the documents show paying him.

Epstein described himself in many ways to many people, as a currency trader, an adviser to billionaires and a man who knew intelligence services, and the book has treated each description as a claim. This one is unusual only because a document backs part of it. Read with its date, it is a broker advertising a client.
