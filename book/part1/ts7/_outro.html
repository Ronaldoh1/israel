Part Seven established how the end-times system spread and what can be counted of that spread. Bible institutes, more than 200 of them by 1961, and Dallas Theological Seminary, which opened in 1924 with 13 students and now counts more than 16,000 alumni, gave dispensationalism faculties, degrees, a press and, through Moody, a radio network reaching more than 700 outlets.
