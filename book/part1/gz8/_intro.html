Part Eight follows the money that moved into and around Gaza: the Qatari cash that Israel allowed into the strip from 2018 to 2023, the reconstruction money pledged after the war of 2014 and the system built to control it, the UN agency for Palestinian refugees and the fight over its funding after October 7, and the bill for the war that followed, both for the weapons and for rebuilding what they destroyed. The library's volume Follow the Money traces the American side of that ledger in detail; this part keeps to Gaza.
