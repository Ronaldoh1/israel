The Verdict, Layer by Layer

Each layer below is graded on its own. "Documented" means primary records or sworn testimony establish it. "Probable" means the documents point strongly that way but a key piece is missing. "Not supported" means the claim circulates but the record does not bear it out.

The pattern of the grades is itself the finding. The strongest claims in this book are about structure: how the money moved, how the law was used, how the institutions behaved. The weakest claims in public circulation are about secret controllers, intelligence handlers and killings. The documented story does not need them, and it is worse than they are, because it is true and it is still in place.
