Part Four followed settlement from a movement into a state enterprise. Gush Emunim, founded in 1974, forced its first settlements on a reluctant government; after 1977 the Likud adopted plans that put settlements among the Palestinian towns of the hill country, and the settler population outside East Jerusalem grew from 4,400 to 116,300 by 1993. [cite:wb_ej_gush] [cite:wb_peacenow_pop] When the Supreme Court barred the seizure of private land at Elon Moreh, the government declared 908,000 dunams "state land" under an Ottoman code, about 16 percent of the territory, which Israel holds it may administer in trust. [cite:wb_dweikat] [cite:wb_wiki_stateland] A settler underground maimed two mayors, killed three students in Hebron and planned to destroy the Dome of the Rock; its members were convicted, and some were pardoned.
