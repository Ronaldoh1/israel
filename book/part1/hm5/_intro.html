Ron's question for this part was direct: would Hamas be what it became if Israel had not split Gaza from the West Bank and cut the two apart? Part Five sets out the record that bears on it. It begins with how the split happened in 2006 and 2007, including the American plan to remove Hamas by force that was reported later. It then sets out Israel's separation of the two territories, which began long before Hamas took Gaza; the Qatari money that Israel let into Gaza from 2018; and what Israeli leaders said about all of it, including Prime Minister Netanyahu's denials. It ends with the two readings of what this means, labelled as arguments.
