On 13 June 2025, two days before the United States and Iran were due to hold a sixth round of nuclear talks in Muscat, Israel began bombing Iran. Over the next twelve days Iran fired hundreds of ballistic missiles at Israeli cities, the United States dropped its heaviest bunker-busting bombs on Iran's enrichment plants, and Iran struck back at an American base in Qatar after warning first. The war ended in a ceasefire announced by President Trump. This part sets out, day by day and from named sources, the diplomacy that the strikes cut across, what each government said it was doing and why, the dead on both sides as each source counted them, and the dispute that followed over how much damage was done and where Iran's near-weapons-grade uranium went.
