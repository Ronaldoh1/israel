The Protocols are a documented forgery. The text was first serialized in the St Petersburg newspaper Znamya in 1903, and in August 1921 The Times of London showed it was largely copied from Maurice Joly's satire of 1864 [cite:rs_ch01_ushmm_protocols]. A Swiss court in Bern ruled against its publishers in 1935, and a Russian court ruled it a forgery in 1993 [cite:rs_ch01_ushmm_protocols].

Exposure did not stop it. Henry Ford's newspaper ran a 91-part series based on it, Hitler cited it in Mein Kampf, and it kept circulating after the war, including in Article 32 of the 1988 Hamas charter [cite:rs_ch01_ushmm_protocols]. Its full path, 1903 to 2003, is told in [scene:ch01_hatred|An old hatred, before and after its name].
