The last part takes the volume's findings in the groups used for the Hamas, Hezbollah and Iran volumes. It first sets out what the record settles about "Greater Israel" from Herzl's diary of 1898 to October 2026, sorted into four kinds of statement: programmes that named movements, parties and governments stated or legislated; patterns that can be shown by setting events side by side over time; arguments about intent, with the case on each side; and the myths and misreadings that Part Ten named. To these it adds what Parts Thirteen to Seventeen established: the human toll of the wars since October 2023, each figure with the body that counted it and its date; the bill, and the American service members killed; what the United Nations, the courts and other states found and did; and the test of whether the programme depends on the founding of 1948. It then lists what is still open a week before Israel's election of 27 October 2026, from the annexation track and E1 to the zones held in Lebanon, Syria and Gaza, the successor to the American aid agreement, the court cases still pending and the end of the UN force in Lebanon. It ends by answering the question the volume set out to test, whether there is a programme of territorial expansion beyond the 1967 lines and who holds it, territory by territory and actor by actor, with the counter-record set beside the verdict.
