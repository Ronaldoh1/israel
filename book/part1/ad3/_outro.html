The mobility record confirms the promise for one generation and qualifies it for every generation since. Ninety-two per cent of children born in 1940 out-earned their parents; half of those born in 1980 and 1984 did, a fall that reached every state and hit children of middle-income parents hardest, and that the economists who measured it trace mainly to how growth was divided rather than how fast it came.
