Part Thirteen begins a ledger that the rest of the book has so far kept apart from its subject: what Israel's wars and operations since October 2023 have done to the places nearest it. It takes Gaza, the West Bank and Lebanon in turn. For each it sets out the operations, with the earlier rounds of 1982, 2006, 2008–09, 2014 and 2021 in brief; the dead and injured, with the body that counted them and how, and with women and children only where that body gives them; the people driven from their homes and the homes, hospitals, schools and water systems destroyed; and what the fighting did to the governments and economies left behind. Beside each it puts Israel's stated reasons and the attacks on Israel that preceded or accompanied its operations: the Hamas attack of 7 October 2023, killings of Israelis in the West Bank, and Hezbollah's fire on the north and the Israelis it displaced. Figures from different sources and periods are given side by side, never added together, and the status of each front is stated as of October 2026. The question whether these operations served territorial expansion is treated as an argument, with the evidence on both sides, because this book found documented expansion programmes only for the West Bank and the Golan.
