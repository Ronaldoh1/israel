# Every Test on the Road to 1948 Map, Ranked

Status as of 27 November 2026. These are the figures after the audit fixes, which are saved but not yet on the live map: cause and effect followed before 1948, lost work counted from 1882, substitutes only from the same circle, and lost years counted across all channels together. They will change if the founding-years rule changes.

Every test is ranked from the least likely outcome to the most likely. "Chance" is the chance the state is founded on time, in 1948; the full record gives 93%. "Ever" counts later attempts. Tests come from three lists on the map: tests of the map, tests of the case, and whose money (two of the money tests, on private and immigrant capital, are new).
