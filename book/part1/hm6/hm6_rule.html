How Hamas Ruled Gaza

From 2007 Hamas governed Gaza as a one-party authority. In 2012 Human Rights Watch documented arbitrary detention, torture of detainees and executions carried out on the basis of confessions obtained under torture, with no one held to account. [cite:hm_972_hrw2012] In March 2019, when thousands of Gazans protested against the cost of living under the slogan "We Want to Live", Hamas's forces broke up the protests; the Independent Commission for Human Rights, the Palestinian rights ombudsman, said more than 1,000 people were arrested. Hamas's own figures showed it had arrested 4,235 people between January 2018 and March 2019. The Commission received 213 complaints of torture and mistreatment by Hamas forces in that period. [cite:hm_hrw_2019]

The Palestinian Authority did the same in the West Bank: the Commission received 242 complaints of torture and mistreatment by its forces over the same period. Eric Goldstein of Human Rights Watch said the two were "bitterly divided, but unified in a common approach to crushing dissent." [cite:hm_hrw_2019]
