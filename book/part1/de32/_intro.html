A sovereign executive without a veto, the end state the texts of Book One describe, would need people to carry out its orders, and the book's worst-case reading holds that it would repurpose the forces that already exist rather than build new ones. This part examines those forces as they stood in October 2026. The first section follows Immigration and Customs Enforcement through the hiring surge, the borrowed agents and the city operations of 2025 and 2026, and the deaths and court findings that came with them. The second turns to local police deputised under the 287(g) programme and the state laws that require or refuse it. The third sets out the law that keeps soldiers out of policing and the Guard deployments, threats and military detention sites that tested it. The fourth follows the money through the private detention companies, which also monitor the people they release. The fifth asks what has actually stopped any of it, and tests the worst-case reading against the record.
