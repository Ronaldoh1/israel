Part Twelve turns from the economy as a whole to the industries inside it. It measures research spending in both countries since 1996, the weight of Israel's tech sector in its output, exports and jobs, and the boom and slump of venture money in each, before following American companies such as Intel and Nvidia into Israel and Israeli founders sold to American buyers. It then sets the two arms industries side by side: Israel's announced export records since 2022 and America's sales through government and commercial channels, measured also on SIPRI's common scale, and the co-production, aid and subsidiaries that join them. It ends with four industries that households pay into every day, natural gas, food, medicines and banking, where the comparison turns on how few firms supply each market and what each state has done about it. Figures are given with their source, year and definition, and the part says where only a snapshot exists. Status lines are as of October 2026, before Israel's election on 27 October and the American midterms on 3 November.
