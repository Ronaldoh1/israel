Part Nine covered the West Bank since 7 October 2023. The UN counted 1,001 Palestinians killed there in two years, 213 of them children, and 59 Israelis killed by West Bank Palestinians. [cite:wb_ohchr_1001] Operation Iron Wall emptied three refugee camps of more than 33,000 people, who had not returned by July 2026; the UN said it may amount to forcible transfer, and Israel calls it counter-terrorism. [cite:wb_jurist_ohchr2026] Settlement moved faster than ever: a record 27,941 units advanced in 2025, 102 settlements approved since December 2022, and E1 approved with a minister's promise to "bury the idea of a Palestinian state." [cite:wb_pn_mirabilis] [cite:wb_itv_e1] The Knesset voted for annexation in principle; the US President said it would not happen, after revoking the sanctions on violent settlers.
