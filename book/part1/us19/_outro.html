Part Nineteen established that the two states raise their armies by opposite laws: Israel conscripts most Jewish citizens under the 1949 Defense Service Law and leans on a reserve whose average member had served 233 days of the war by mid-2026, while the United States has relied on volunteers since 1973, with an active force that fell from 2.1 million in 1989 to about 1.28 million.
