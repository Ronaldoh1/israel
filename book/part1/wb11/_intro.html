Part Eleven turns to the division of the Palestinian territories between two rival governments: the Palestinian Authority, led by Fatah, in the West Bank, and Hamas in Gaza. It sets out how the split happened, why it has never been healed, and why no Palestinian election has been held since 2006. It then takes up an argument made by many critics of Israeli policy: that the division served Israel, by keeping one territory at war and the other, where settlement advanced, under a weakened authority. This part sets out the statements and actions of Israeli leaders that support that argument, the counter-arguments and Israel's stated reasons, and the limits of what the record can show about intent.
