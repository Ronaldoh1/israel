Part Nine leaves the United States. It begins with the one actor present in every country studied, the government of Israel, whose budget for public diplomacy rose to about $730 million for 2026. It then follows the national organizations and parliamentary networks that advocate for Israel in Britain, Europe, Canada, Australia, Latin America, Africa and Asia, giving amounts where they are known and naming the gaps where they are not. It ends by testing a common explanation of all this activity: that it exists to keep arms and aid flowing to Israel and to protect its standing at the United Nations.
