Enoch and Jubilees were read widely before and after Christ, fragments of both were found among the Dead Sea Scrolls, and the Letter of Jude quotes Enoch. [cite:vt27_wiki_enoch] [cite:vt27_wiki_jubilees] The Latin West set Enoch aside largely on Augustine's ground that it was not in the canon "preserved ... by the diligence of successive priests", and the full text returned to Europe only with Bruce's Ethiopian manuscripts of 1773. [cite:vt27_wiki_enoch] [cite:vt27_hessayon] No deliberate suppression is documented; non-canonical books simply stopped being copied.
