Where the Machine Stalled

The record of the first two decades holds American presidents who said no. In 1953 the Eisenhower administration held back aid until Israel suspended its water works at Banat Yaacov; about $26 million in aid was then recommended. [cite:rs_cw2_frus_pr604] In 1956–57, after the Suez war, Washington joined Moscow in asking for Israel's withdrawal from Sinai, carried the General Assembly against its own allies and held back money and oil from Britain and France until they withdrew; Ben-Gurion told Eisenhower Israel would leave Sinai once a UN force was in place. [cite:rs_cw3_boughton_fd] [cite:rs_cw3_frus16_d612] [cite:rs_cw3_jvl_bulganin1] Kennedy pressed for inspections at Dimona, and his Justice Department ordered the American Zionist Council to register. [cite:rs_cw5_nsa_letters] [cite:israellobby_org_fara_doj_order] Even Truman, who recognised Israel against his advisers, threatened a "revision" of policy in 1949, though he never enforced it. [cite:jvl_truman_note_may1949]

The resistance points today are of another kind. They are in elections and parliaments rather than in the Oval Office: the super PAC's defeats in Michigan, California and Illinois in 2026; members of Congress who refuse the money; Marjorie Taylor Greene, who broke with the lobby in 2025 and resigned after the president withdrew his support; and Canada's decision to bar lobby-sponsored travel for MPs. [cite:pm_prospect_illinois] [cite:pm_npr_mtg_resign] [cite:pm_maple_cija]

A reading of the pattern

What follows is an interpretation, not a documented finding. Across 130 years the same mechanisms recur: secure the top patron, move to the rising power, turn money into access, keep the line between a foreign state and a domestic lobby blurred, recruit a religious constituency, write the definitions, and absorb the consequences when the law is broken. The tools scale with the era. The clearest change is in who is able to refuse. Before 1967 the pressure was aimed at presidents, and presidents overrode it on the record several times. Today it runs mainly through Congress and primaries, the agreements are written into law, and documented refusals at the top have become rare.

The strongest reading against this one is in the same record. In 1948 the State Department, the Pentagon and the CIA opposed recognition on strategic grounds, and Truman decided against them. [cite:trumanlibrary_recognition] [cite:cia_ore55] By 2014 Congress had declared Israel a major strategic partner by a vote of 410 to 1. [cite:ow_jvl_2014act] On this reading Washington came to see the alliance as serving its own interests, in the Cold War and after, and the organised money follows positions that members and voters already hold. [cite:factcheck_udp_2024] Both readings rest on documented facts; the record of 1948 shows one case of money tied to a decision, and the record of today shows disclosed spending whose effect on any single vote cannot be measured. [cite:mondoweiss_judis_truman]
