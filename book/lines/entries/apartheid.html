"Apartheid" has a legal meaning, and several serious bodies have applied it to Israel, though no court has convicted anyone of the crime. Under the 1973 Apartheid Convention and the Rome Statute, apartheid is an institutionalized regime of systematic domination by one racial group over another. Human Rights Watch and the Israeli group B'Tselem concluded in 2021 that Israel commits apartheid, and Amnesty International reached the same conclusion in February 2022. [cite:fara_amnesty_explainer]

In July 2024 the International Court of Justice, in an advisory opinion on the occupied territory, found that Israeli policies breach the prohibition on racial segregation and apartheid. [cite:11kbw_icj_ao] Israel rejects the label. So the honest answer depends on the wording: "human-rights groups and the ICJ have found apartheid-related violations" is accurate; "a court has ruled Israel guilty of the crime of apartheid" goes further than the record, and "it is a slur with no legal basis" ignores it.
