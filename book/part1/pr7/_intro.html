Part Seven sets out the argument behind the cases in Part Six. Israel says it has never deliberately targeted journalists, and that some of the dead it calls journalists were members of Hamas or Islamic Jihad using press work as cover. Press-freedom groups answer that Israel labels journalists as fighters after killing them, without evidence. This part sets out what the laws of war say about journalists, Israel's argument in its own words, the cases in which the counting bodies accepted it, the reporting on an army unit tasked with finding such links, and the record of the same claims before this war.
