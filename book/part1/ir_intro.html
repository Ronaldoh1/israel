This volume is about Israel and Iran: how two states that were quiet allies for three decades became the region's central enmity, and how that enmity drew the United States into two wars. It was written to answer direct questions. How long has Benjamin Netanyahu been warning that Iran is about to have a bomb, and what happened each time? What did other Israeli leaders, and Israel's own intelligence chiefs, say? Does Iran want a war with the United States? What does Israel want from Iran, and why Iran? And how does a state that recognises its Jewish community in its constitution and reserves it a seat in parliament end up as the target of the Jewish state's longest campaign?

The record answers some of these questions and not others. It shows that Iran recognised Israel de facto in 1950, sold it oil through a pipeline the two built together, and bought Israeli arms, and that Israel kept selling arms to the Islamic Republic after 1979, including the missiles of the Iran-Contra affair. [cite:ir1_iranica_israel] [cite:ir1_wiki_eapc] [cite:ir2_walsh18] It shows Iran's constitution recognising its Jews, its census counting fewer than ten thousand of them, and the same state executing a Jewish leader in 1979 and hosting a conference that questioned the Holocaust. [cite:ir3_const_en] [cite:ir3_afp_census2012] It shows Iran enriching uranium to 60 percent while Israel holds an undeclared arsenal outside the treaty Iran signed. [cite:ir4_ap_may2025] [cite:ir4_fas_israel] It sets Netanyahu's dated warnings, from 1992 to 2026, beside what followed, including his own conditions and the US intelligence assessments that contradicted him. [cite:ir5_li_un2012] [cite:hz11_ap_gabbard] And it states Iran's own record plainly: its leaders' calls for Israel's end, the armed groups it funds, its plots against Americans, and its repression at home. It ends with the wars of June 2025 and of 2026, the American bill, and a verdict on three claims tested across the volume: that the warnings were false alarms, that Iran does not want war with America, and that America was dragged in. The volume draws on the Israel Architecture simulation, the research files of the Sophia project and the earlier Israel books, and every claim taken from them has been traced to a citable source. It is a companion to The Hamas Architecture, The Hezbollah Architecture and The One-Way Mirror.
