The last part takes the volume's findings in the groups used for the Hamas, Hezbollah and Iran volumes. It first sets out what the record settles about "Greater Israel" from Herzl's diary of 1898 to October 2026, sorted into four kinds of statement: programmes that named movements, parties and governments stated or legislated; patterns that can be shown by setting events side by side over time; arguments about intent, with the case on each side; and the myths and misreadings that Part Ten named. It then lists what is still open a week before Israel's election of 27 October 2026, from the annexation track and E1 to the zones held in Lebanon, Syria and Gaza, the end of the UN force in Lebanon and practice on the Temple Mount. It ends by answering the question the volume set out to test, whether there is a programme of territorial expansion beyond the 1967 lines and who holds it, territory by territory and actor by actor, with the counter-record set beside the verdict.
