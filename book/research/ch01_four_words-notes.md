# Baseline, ch01: the four words (Hebrew, Jewish, Israeli, Zionist), research notes

Verified 2026-10-17. Every claim in `ch01_four_words.json` comes from a page fetched in this session. WebFetch returns a model-written summary of each page, not the raw text, so quoted phrases are short. Check every quotation against the original before printing.

## Corrections to the draft brief

1. **"Hebrew was used only in prayer and scripture": wrong.** The Academy of the Hebrew Language ("Medieval Hebrew") says Hebrew stayed in wide literary use until the revival. Its uses included liturgical and secular poetry (piyyut, Shmuel HaNagid, Judah HaLevi), some legal contracts, philosophy and science (translations from Arabic), and grammar and lexicography. Some travellers and schools also kept speaking it.
2. **When Hebrew stopped being spoken is a range, not a date (marked disputed).** Sources disagree:
   - Wikipedia/Sáenz-Badillos: Mishnaic Hebrew was spoken c. 1–200 CE and began falling out of use within about a century of c. 200; the infobox gives the 4th century for its death as a native language.
   - EBSCO: it had ceased by 200 CE.
   - Academy of the Hebrew Language: from the 5th century no one used it as an everyday language.
   - Britannica: Aramaic began supplanting it from about the 3rd century BCE.

   Use "gradual decline; usually dated c. 200–400 CE".
3. **2018 nation-state law and Arabic.** The law's own text (Adalah's unofficial translation, section 4) gives Arabic a "special status" and says Arabic's existing practical status is not harmed. The claim that Arabic was "downgraded" from an official language is NPR's characterisation. Keep it attributed. The Knesset's official English PDF redirected to a maintenance page and could not be fetched.
4. **CBS no longer gives a separate headline count of Jews.** Its 2026 releases report "Jews and others" as one group. The 78.3% and 21.7% in the Rosh Hashanah 2026 release are shares of the roughly 10.0 million Israeli residents, *excluding* the 304,000 foreign nationals: 7.828 + 2.173 = 10.001 million. As shares of the 10.305 million total they are about 76% and 21%. Do not print "78% Jewish". The latest separate figure for Jews found here is 7,208,000 (2023, about 73%), via Wikipedia citing CBS.
5. **The Basel Program's wording varies by translation.** The Jewish Encyclopedia (1906) gives "publicly and legally assured home in Palestine"; Wikipedia, citing the 1898 Protocol, gives "home… secured under public law"; Britannica gives "publicly guaranteed homeland". Pick one and cite it.
6. **Neturei Karta's founding date.** The 1938 date in the brief is in Wikipedia's infobox, but the article's body says Blau split from Agudah in 1937. The JVL page gives no founding year. Write "founded 1938 (some accounts 1937)", or attribute the date.
7. **"Uganda" offer.** The territory was in British East Africa, in present-day Kenya (Wikipedia), not the modern state of Uganda.

## Disputes recorded

- **Etymology of ʿIvri** (Eber vs "other side"): unresolved, already argued in Genesis Rabbah 41.
- **ʿIvri and Ḥabiru/ʿApiru:** no consensus (Friedberg, TheTorah.com; Britannica says "might").
- **Jews of colour share in the US:** Pew 2020 found 8% of Jewish adults non-White or Hispanic (15% of 18–29s); the JoCI/Stanford meta-analysis says "at least 12–15%". The definitions differ: JoCI includes household members and uses different identity questions.
- **Kaifeng:** estimates run from about 500 to 1,000 people of descent, of whom 40–50 are active. The Song-dynasty dating is disputed by Yu (2017). China does not recognise them as a minority, and Orthodox authorities require conversion.
- **Blackstone Memorial signatories:** 431 (Wikipedia "Blackstone Memorial", citing Merkley 1998) vs 413 (Wikipedia "Christian Zionism"). Check Merkley.
- **US Jews calling themselves "Zionist":**
  - JFNA (fielded March 2025): 37%.
  - Milwaukee federation: 43%.
  - Chicago federation (2020): 40%.
  - JPPI opt-in panel (June 2024, n=634): about 80% "Zionist or slightly Zionist".

  The results depend on wording and on what respondents take the word to mean. Present the range, not a single figure.
- **Share of world Jewry living in Israel:** the CBS April 2026 release says about 45% (end-2024); the CBS Rosh Hashanah 2026 release, as reported by i24, says about 46% (1 January 2026).

The cbs.gov.il homepage was blocked, and the English i24 page needs JavaScript. Replace these with the CBS PDF when available.
- **Jewish Agency / DellaPergola world estimate for Rosh Hashanah 2025:** not found. The latest Jewish Agency release fetched is October 2024 (15.8m as of January 2024; 7.3m in Israel; 6.3m in the US). The 2026 figure of "nearly 15.9m" is the CBS's, reported second-hand. The American Jewish Year Book 2025 was not consulted.
- **Religion breakdown for 2025/26 from the CBS directly:**
  - Muslims (1.849m, 18.6%, end-2025): from JNS.
  - Christians (184,200, 1.9%, December 2025): from an Israeli-government "IsraelFact" page filed under FARA, summarising CBS release 413/2025.
  - Druze: about 150,000 (end-2022, CBS 129/2023) is the latest CBS figure fetched. No 2025 Druze figure was found.
  - Do not use the JVL "population by religion" table for 2024: its rows do not add up.
- **Ethiopian-origin population.** 168,800 (end-2022) comes from a government summary of CBS 362/2023. The 177,600 (2024) figure is from a JNS headline only; the article body was not readable. Get the CBS Sigd 2025 release.

- **Pittsburgh Platform:** the American Jewish Archives page summarises the plank. The full sentence ("…and therefore expect neither a return to Palestine…") was not fetched verbatim.
- **"Palestine or Argentine?" section of Der Judenstaat:** the Gutenberg text was truncated in fetch. The Argentina claim rests on Wikipedia's summary of the text.
- **Moses Hess date:** Britannica gives 1862. A Leo Baeck Institute page gives 1864 in its body text (apparently an error) and 1862 in its title. 1862 is used.
- **Ahad Ha'am's essay date:** Wikipedia's "Types of Zionism", as summarised, gave "1880" for "This Is Not the Way"; the usual date is 1889. No date is used in the JSON.
- **Abayudaya (Uganda):** optional; not researched.

## Source cautions

- The `fw_israelfact_*` sources are Israeli-government (LaPam) informational pages filed with the US DOJ under FARA. They quote CBS releases accurately as far as checked, but cite the CBS release numbers (413/2025 and 362/2023) as the authority.
- Wikipedia is used only where it cites named scholarship. Each label says so.
- The CUFI membership figure (10 million, December 2020) is CUFI's own and was not independently verified.
