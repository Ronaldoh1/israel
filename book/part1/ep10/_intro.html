Part Ten asks the question every reader arrives with: with Epstein dead and Maxwell in prison, is it over? The question has three separate answers, because "the network" means three different things. It means people: the staff, associates and clients around him. It means money: the fortune, the companies and the properties. And it means a mechanism: the routes by which girls and young women were found, moved and silenced. Each can be tested against the record as of October 2026.

One rule governs this part. "The network was never dismantled" and "the network is still operating" are different claims. The first is documented. The second is not, and the book says exactly what evidence would establish it.
