# Ron's videos and article, checked (4 Nov 2026)

This file covers four YouTube transcripts and one article that Ron brought in, each claim checked against sources.

**Sources reviewed**
1. History Meets Finance: "The Rothschild Who Bankrolled the Birth of Israel"
2. An untitled video on the Rothschild role in creating Israel
3. The Sovereign Ledger: "Where The Rothschilds Moved Their Money Before The 1930s Collapse"
4. Ryan Chapman: "How Israel Was Created"
5. Rimon Wehbi, "Semitic Languages," Yawna (2021, rev. 2023)

**Verdict key**
- ✅ confirmed by a primary or strong source
- 📰 reported by a named reference
- ⚠️ disputed
- ❌ wrong
- ❓ not found / unsupported

**How to read these sources**
- None of the sources keeps the Baseline's terminology. They use "antisemitism" without noting the misnomer.
- They treat Jews as one lineage and "the Jews" as having all left the land.
- The library already corrects these points: The Baseline covers "Semitic" and "antisemitism" (s00b, s00b0), the lineages, and the Jewish communities that remained (ch01_old_yishuv, rd0_aliyah).

|---|---|---|
| Edmond slipped into Palestine "in secret" in 1887 to avoid the Ottomans | ⚠️ / ❓ | He arrived anonymously on 2 April 1887, by ordinary steamer. *Hamelitz* reported it at once ("the secret was revealed immediately"). No source states the reason; avoiding the Ottomans is the video's reading (Ramat Hanadiv, five visits). |
| He visited and expanded his involvement over decades | ✅ | Five visits: 1887, c. 1893, Jan 1899, 1914, May 1925 (Ramat Hanadiv). |
| Born 19 Aug 1845, youngest son of James Mayer | ✅ | Matches the book (bc_s04). |
| Married his cousin Adelheid in 1877 | 📰 | Adelheid is recorded; the year was not separately checked. |
| ~30 settlements | ✅ | Matches the book (Schama; Wikipedia). |
| ~50,000 hectares reclaimed | ❓ | No source found for this figure. The book gives PICA ≈ 500,000 dunams (≈ 50,000 hectares) by the 1930s, from one account. The figures overlap but are not the same claim. |
| Honored with a state funeral in 1934 | ❌ | Buried in Paris in 1934. The **state funeral was in 1954**, at reburial at Ramat HaNadiv: naval frigate, 19-gun salute, eulogy by Ben-Gurion (Wikipedia). |
| Edmond took over the JCA's leadership after Hirsch died in 1896 | ❌ | Unsupported. The JCA began work in Palestine after Hirsch's death; Edmond *handed his colonies to* it. |
| End of 1899: colonies transferred to the JCA with 15 million francs | ✅ / ⚠️ | Wikipedia: end of 1899, 15M francs. Encyclopaedia Judaica: 1900, 14M francs, about 250,000 dunams and 12 settlements. Both are now in bc_s04. |
| 1910: Ottoman deputy Ismail Hakki tied the loan crisis to Zionism | ❓ | Not confirmed. The documented debate on Zionism in the Ottoman parliament is **1911** (Ruhi al-Khalidi, Sa'id al-Husayni). Already in rd1 fil_s01. |
| 1924: PICA founded, son James leads it | ✅ | President for life (Wikipedia; Ramat Hanadiv). |
| PICA bought "more than 125,000 acres" | 📰 | Matches the book's "≈ 500,000 dunams (125,000 acres)" from one account. |
| After 1934, leadership passed to Walter Rothschild in Britain | ❌ | Walter's role was 1917 and he died in 1937. The work passed to Edmond's **son James** and then James's wife **Dorothy**. |
| Walter was president of the English Zionist Federation | ❓ | Not found. Weizmann led the EZF in 1917. Walter was "unofficial leader of the British Jewish community" (Rothschild Archive). |
| The Balfour text names only "non-Jewish communities" | ✅ | The text is quoted in rd2 s06. |
| Wineries at Rishon LeZion and Zikhron Ya'akov; Hebrew schools | 📰 | Consistent with the sources; not separately checked. |
