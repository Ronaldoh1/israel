Part Five followed four wars and a season of protest. In each, Israel stated its aim as stopping the rocket fire, and in 2014 the tunnels; in each, the Palestinian dead were counted differently by Palestinian, Israeli and UN bodies, most of all on how many had been fighting. Cast Lead killed between 1,166 and 1,440 Palestinians and 13 Israelis; the war of 2014 killed more than 2,100 Palestinians and 73 people in Israel; May 2021 killed at least 256 in Gaza and 13 in Israel. [cite:gz_wiki_castlead] [cite:gz_wiki_2014] [cite:gz_wiki_2021] The UN inquiries found possible war crimes on both sides in 2009 and 2014, and, at the fence in 2018, found the protests civilian and the shooting of marked journalists, medics and children possibly criminal; Israel rejected or declined to cooperate with them, and the Goldstone report's own chief author reconsidered part of it while his colleagues stood by it.
