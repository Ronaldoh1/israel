The record of Israel's own governments does not support the idea that the only obstacle is on the Palestinian side. On 21 February 2024 the Knesset voted 99 to 9 to back Netanyahu's opposition to "unilateral" recognition of a Palestinian state, and on 11 September 2025, at the Maale Adumim settlement, Netanyahu said "there will be no Palestinian state."
