Part Eight tests the Dark Enlightenment's cure, a state run as a company under a chief executive answerable to its owners. It checks the states the texts name as models, Singapore and Dubai, against how they are actually governed; sets the design against comparative research on one-person rule, corruption and succession; asks what happened when real companies failed and when companies actually ruled countries; and then turns to the question every form of government must answer, who removes a ruler who goes wrong. It closes with the worst-case reading tested for Book One as a whole.
