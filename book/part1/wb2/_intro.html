Part Two covers the nineteen years in which the West Bank was part of the Hashemite Kingdom of Jordan. It sets out how Jordan absorbed the territory, the citizenship it gave its people and the recognition it did not receive; what Jordanian rule meant for East Jerusalem and its holy places; and the border war along the Green Line, from the infiltrations and reprisals of the 1950s to the raid on Samu in 1966, which set the West Bank against its own king months before the war that ended his rule there.
