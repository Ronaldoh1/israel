Part Seven examines the two moments, between 2023 and 2025, that put the phrase "Greater Israel" back into world headlines through the words and props of Israel's prime minister rather than through law. The first is a run of maps: the "New Middle East" map Benjamin Netanyahu held up at the UN General Assembly in September 2023, on which no Palestinian territory was marked; the map behind him at a Gaza press conference in September 2024; and the "Blessing" and "Curse" maps he showed at the UN later that month. The second is a television interview broadcast in August 2025, in which he was handed a map of the "Promised Land" and asked whether he felt connected to the vision of Greater Israel. For each the part sets out what was shown or said, as exactly as the record allows; what Netanyahu gave as his purpose; how Jordan, Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the Arab League and others answered; whether Israel's government clarified; and what the episodes establish and what they do not.
