The Shield of Faith

The pairing of prophecy and strategy is documented early. In the late 1830s the English evangelical Lord Shaftesbury lobbied the Foreign Secretary, Lord Palmerston, to sponsor Jewish settlement in Palestine. [cite:wiki_shaftesbury] Palmerston's dispatch of 11 August 1840 asked the Sultan to admit Jewish settlers as "a check upon any future evil designs of Mehemet Ali", the ruler of Egypt, and made no appeal to scripture. [cite:ts2_cojs_palmerston1840] In 1891 the Chicago evangelist William Blackstone presented President Benjamin Harrison with a memorial signed by more than four hundred people urging support for a Jewish homeland. [cite:wiki_christian_zionism] In 1896 the Anglican chaplain William Hechler read Herzl's book and opened doors for him to the Grand Duke of Baden and the Kaiser. [cite:ts2_wiki_hechler] [cite:wiki_kaiser_levant_1898]

The same constituency is organised today on a larger scale. Christians United for Israel was founded in 2006 with "over 400 pastors and ministry leaders" and announced in December 2020 that it had passed ten million members, a figure it counts itself and has not opened to checking. [cite:ts8_wiki_cufi] [cite:ts8_hfl_10m] Its Action Fund campaigned in Kentucky against Senator Rand Paul in 2018 and against Representative Thomas Massie in 2026. [cite:ts8_cufi_ky2018] [cite:ts8_ji_massie] When the American embassy opened in Jerusalem on 14 May 2018, the pastors Robert Jeffress and John Hagee gave the prayers. [cite:ts12_cnn_pastors] The president's official statement in 2017 gave legal reasons, a 1995 Act and failed waivers; in 2020 he said the move was "for the evangelicals." [cite:ts12_toi_trump2017] [cite:ts9_almon_trump2020]

Restorationist preaching and commercial interest appear together at Whitehall as early as 1655, and the pairing recurs in every later episode. [cite:ts2_crome_cdamm] What changed is the size of the constituency and where it votes. In 1840 it was a handful of peers and clergy; today it is a claimed membership in the millions with its own political fund. Many Christians reject this reading of scripture, and The Theology Shield records their dissent.
