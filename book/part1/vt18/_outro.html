Part Eighteen has established that the relations opened in 1994 were kept, argued over and used for thirty-two years: the Legal Personality Agreement of 10 November 1997 is in force and was invoked by the Latin Patriarchate as late as 31 December 2025, while the economic and fiscal agreement promised in 1993 is still unsigned as of October 2026. [cite:vt18_jvl_lpa1997] [cite:vt18_caritasjr_2025] [cite:vt18_press_2017] The Holy See signed with the PLO on 15 February 2000 and with the State of Palestine on 26 June 2015, and in both texts and in its statements to September 2026 it held to two states and an internationally guaranteed Jerusalem. [cite:vt18_va_plo2000] [cite:vt18_va_bull2015] [cite:vt18_ncr_unga2026] On Gaza, Francis spoke of terrorism and cruelty and asked for an investigation of the genocide question, Parolin called the 7 October attack "inhuman and indefensible" and Israel's war "disastrous and inhuman" in its consequences, and Leo XIV called for an end to "the barbarity of the war", while Israel's government protested and the relationship held. [cite:vt18_ncr_terror2023] [cite:vt18_ncreg_genocide2024] [cite:vt18_vn_parolin2025] [cite:vt18_vn_angelus2025] The Holy See's own treaty commitments of 1929 and 1993, and its practice in Berlin, Damascus and Managua, account for that combination of loud words and unbroken relations without any hidden alignment, though critics on both sides contest the balance it struck.
