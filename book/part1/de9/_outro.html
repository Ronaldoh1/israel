The life sets the scale and the limits of the man at the centre of the network. Thiel built his fortune from PayPal, which eBay bought for $1.5 billion in 2002, from a $500,000 stake in Facebook that returned more than $1 billion, and from Palantir, which he incorporated in 2003 and still chairs; most of the companies attached to his name in the news are held by funds with outside investors, not by him. [cite:de9_wiki_thiel] [cite:de9_wiki_ff] He paid in secret, and lawfully, for the lawsuit that bankrupted Gawker, became a New Zealand citizen after twelve days in the country, and grew a retirement account from about $1,700 to about $5 billion, which tax specialists dispute but no authority has found unlawful. [cite:de9_tc_gawker] [cite:de9_nbr_12days] [cite:de9_pp_roth] He wrote in 2009 that freedom and democracy were no longer compatible, then spent about $32 million in 2022 behind two former employees' Senate campaigns, one of which made J.D. Vance a senator and, later, Vice President; and in 2026, after meetings with Javier Milei, he moved to Buenos Aires.
