The Same History, Read Around the World

The version of this history an American reader learned is one of several. Governments and publics elsewhere read the same events through their own pasts: a debt of guilt, a memory of colonial rule or apartheid, a diaspora with family in the region, an alliance with Washington. This section is a short survey, not the full record; later volumes of this library tell each country in full.

Germany reads the conflict through the Holocaust, and its commitment began as reparation. In 1952 Chancellor Konrad Adenauer signed the Luxembourg Agreement, committing West Germany to pay 3 billion Deutsche Marks to Israel and 450 million to Jewish organizations; total German reparation payments reached about €82 billion by 2022. [cite:beyondberlin_staats] On 18 March 2008 Angela Merkel told the Knesset that Germany's historic responsibility is part of its Staatsräson, its reason of state, a moral and political compact rather than a legal doctrine. [cite:conv_staats] Per SIPRI, Germany supplied about a third of Israel's arms imports in 2020–2024, second only to the United States, and it approved €485.1 million in military equipment between 7 October 2023 and 13 May 2025. [cite:npr_germany_halt] [cite:dropsite_nicaragua] Nicaragua's 2024 case against Germany at the International Court of Justice continued after the Court declined emergency measures in April 2024, by the latest report in this record. [cite:dropsite_nicaragua] On 8 August 2025 Chancellor Merz halted exports "that could be used in the Gaza Strip," leaving missile-defense and naval exports open; its status since has not been re-verified for this edition. [cite:npr_germany_halt] [cite:fp_merz2025] At home, the Bundestag declared on 17 May 2019, in a non-binding motion, that "the arguments and methods of the BDS movement are antisemitic," and at least 99 Palestine solidarity protests were banned between 7 October and 19 November 2023, according to the Transnational Institute, some later overturned in court; officials cited a sharp rise in antisemitic incidents against German Jews. [cite:elsc_bds] [cite:voelker_bds] [cite:tni_germany] [cite:wiki_de_censorship] By April 2026 polling showed that only a relatively small share of Germans still saw Israel's security as reason of state. [cite:fp_germany2026] The arms record is told in [scene:wr2_s02|Germany's arms and the Hague case].

Spain carries two histories. In 1492 the Catholic monarchs ordered Spain's Jews, at least 200,000 by historians' estimate, to convert or leave. Ben-Gurion rejected Franco's reported 1948 overture over his Nazi ties, and Spain opened relations only on 17 January 1986, the last Western European state to do so, as a condition of joining the European Community. [cite:israeled_spain_1986] Its 2015 law offering citizenship to descendants of the expelled Sephardim drew 132,226 applications by the 2019 deadline, about 20,000 from Mexico alone. [cite:euronews_sephardic] [cite:yahoo_sephardic_close] On 28 May 2024 Spain, with Ireland and Norway, recognized the State of Palestine on the pre-1967 lines; Israel recalled its ambassador and said the three were rewarding Hamas. [cite:catalannews_spain_recognition] [cite:wiki_palestine_spain] Pro-Israel commentators see hostility with historical echoes; the government cites two states and international law.

South Africa reads the conflict through apartheid, and the record runs in an unexpected direction. Drawing on more than 7,000 pages of declassified South African documents, Sasha Polakow-Suransky documented a secret security agreement signed on 3 April 1975 by Shimon Peres and P.W. Botha. South Africa became Israel's largest arms buyer, about 35% of its military exports, while Israel publicly condemned apartheid. [cite:fp_unspoken_alliance] [cite:jc_unspoken_alliance] Minutes record Peres saying a "correct payload" was available "in three sizes"; the Guardian read this as an offer of nuclear warheads, and Peres denied it. [cite:jc_peres_denies] On 4 December 1997 Nelson Mandela said that "our freedom is incomplete without the freedom of the Palestinians," in a sentence that also named East Timor and Sudan; South Africa's Jewish Report argues the line is often quoted without that context. [cite:polity_mandela_1997] [cite:sajr_mandela_context] In January 2024 South Africa brought its genocide case against Israel to the ICJ. The pact is told in [scene:wr_s03|South Africa and the 1975 pact].

Latin America holds a large Palestinian diaspora, much of it Christian. Chile's community is estimated at 300,000 to 500,000, the largest outside the Middle East, and El Salvador's at about 100,000; its president, Nayib Bukele, of Palestinian descent, has been publicly pro-Israel, which divided the community. [cite:fp_chile_diaspora] [cite:mee_elsalvador_diaspora] The region also remembers Israeli arms. Declassified Israeli and US cables show that after the Carter administration halted military aid to Guatemala in 1977 over human-rights abuses, Israel supplied rifles, aircraft, armored vehicles and counterinsurgency training; a Guatemalan ambassador said "Israel saved Guatemala." In 1981 El Salvador received $21 million in Israeli arms credits at the US request. [cite:thewire_guatemala_cables] [cite:mee_elsalvador_diaspora]

Brazil split between its president and its public. In February 2024 President Lula called Gaza "a genocide" and compared it to Hitler's extermination of Jews; Israel's foreign minister, Israel Katz, summoned Brazil's ambassador to Yad Vashem and declared Lula persona non grata, and Brazil recalled its ambassador. [cite:toi_lula] A CNN Brasil poll found 83% of Brazilians disagreed with the comparison. [cite:cebri_lula] In August 2024 Lula suspended a $210 million contract for 36 Elbit howitzers and Brazil joined South Africa's ICJ case. [cite:mee_bolsonaro] His predecessor, Jair Bolsonaro, had pledged in 2018 to move the embassy to Jerusalem, but opened only a trade office there in December 2019, after the farm lobby warned that Arab meat markets were at risk. [cite:arabnews_bolsonaro_office] [cite:bloomberg_bolsonaro] [cite:newarab_bolsonaro_dec]

France is home to Europe's largest Jewish community, about 440,000 to 500,000, and to its largest Muslim population, an estimate because the census does not record religion. [cite:wiki_jews_by_country] [cite:f24_crif_2023] Recorded antisemitic acts rose from 436 in 2022 to 1,676 in 2023, the highest since tracking began in 2012, then 1,570 in 2024 and 1,320 in 2025; in 2024 more than 30% referenced Palestine and 192 occurred in schools. [cite:ejc_crif_2024] [cite:cep_france] [cite:f24_crif_2024] Anti-Muslim discrimination is also documented, and protest restrictions in October 2023 drew civil-liberties criticism. On 22 September 2025 President Macron recognized the State of Palestine at the UN, calling it a defeat for Hamas. [cite:air_macron]

The United Kingdom, once the Mandate power, took partial measures. On 2 September 2024 it suspended about 30 of roughly 350 arms export licences to Israel over a "clear risk" of serious violations of international humanitarian law, but exempted F-35 components; the High Court refused permission for a judicial review on 30 June 2025, and by 2026 more than 80 licences had been suspended or refused. [cite:cnbc_uk_licences] [cite:steptoe_uk] [cite:malay_f35] [cite:jc_uk80] On 21 September 2025 Prime Minister Keir Starmer recognized the State of Palestine. [cite:commons_recog]

Canada and Australia recognized Palestine the same day; Canada and the UK became the first G7 members to do so. [cite:cnn_recog] Canada stopped approving new arms permits on 8 January 2024, but earlier permits stayed valid: 164 were used in 2024, and reporting found at least C$28.5 million approved in the war's first months; the government says no lethal weapons have been approved since January 2024. [cite:cjpme_motion] [cite:global_canada] [cite:cbc_canada] When the International Criminal Court issued warrants for Netanyahu and Yoav Gallant on 21 November 2024, Justin Trudeau said Canada would "abide by all the regulations and rulings of the international courts," though his foreign minister would not say whether Canada would make an arrest; Netanyahu's office called the warrants "an antisemitic decision." [cite:cbc_trudeau_icc] [cite:ctv_trudeau_icc] [cite:algemeiner_trudeau] In Australia the war became personal on 1 April 2024, when Israeli drone strikes on three marked World Central Kitchen vehicles killed seven aid workers, among them the Australian Zomi Frankcom; Israel called it unintentional and dismissed two officers. [cite:gzr_npr_wck] [cite:gzr_wck_wiki]

Muslim-majority Asia did not take one path. In July 2025 Indonesia, the most populous Muslim-majority country, and Malaysia joined a 12-country "Hague Group," with South Africa, Colombia, Bolivia and others, committed to halting arms transfers to Israel. [cite:newsweek_hague] Both have long tied any normalization to a Palestinian state, while the UAE, Bahrain and Morocco normalized relations in 2020. [cite:commons_recog]

Ethiopian Jews are a case readers often ask about, as a test of how Israel treats its Black citizens. Operation Moses flew about 8,000 from Sudanese camps in 1984–85, after an estimated 4,000 died on the trek; Operation Solomon carried 14,325 people to Israel in 36 hours on 24–25 May 1991. [cite:wiki_operation_solomon] [cite:wjc_operation_moses] In 1996 Maariv revealed that Magen David Adom had secretly discarded blood donated by Ethiopian immigrants since about 1991 over HIV fears. [cite:jweekly_blood_1996] [cite:toi_blood_2017] Reports of disproportionate Depo-Provera injections led to a 2013 Health Ministry directive; the ministry denied wrongdoing, and investigations were inconclusive. [cite:ahram_depo_provera] [cite:ethiopanorama_depo] Rescue and discrimination are both documented; see [scene:wr_s04|Ethiopian Jews].

Set side by side, these readings follow the histories of the countries doing the reading: guilt in Germany, expulsion and late recognition in Spain, apartheid in South Africa, diaspora and civil war in Latin America, two large communities in France, alliance and its limits in Britain, Canada and Australia. None of them is the neutral version. What the record does not settle is how far each government's policy reflects its public; in Germany and Brazil the documented polling and the policy point in different directions, and for most countries here no comparable measure exists in this record.
