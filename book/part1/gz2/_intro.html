Part Two covers the thirty-eight years between the armistice and the first uprising. For nineteen of them Egypt governed the strip without annexing it, and the strip became the front line of a border war; for most of the rest, Israel occupied it, counted its people, put down an insurgency, drew its workers into the Israeli economy and planted settlements among its refugee camps. The Cold War volume of this library tells the years to 1967 in detail; this part gathers what that volume found about Gaza and carries the story to the eve of December 1987.
