This volume is about the people in Washington who have made American policy toward Israel and the region since 2017, and about what the record shows of their own interests: the president and his family, his son-in-law and envoy, his secretary of state, and his secretary of what he renamed the Department of War.

It applies the rule that governs every account of a living person on this shelf. A conviction, a verdict, a filing or a signed document is stated as fact. An allegation is stated as an allegation, with the person's denial. And where money and policy sit side by side, the volume shows both and says plainly when no document links the one to the other. Other volumes carry parts of this story in full: the plans for Gaza in Gaza, Documented, the war with Iran in The Iran Architecture, and the aid itself in Aid and Arms.
