The record shows several legally disclosed advocacy groups that work differently in each country, not one secret body in control. In the US, AIPAC is a registered lobby with a reported budget over $100 million [cite:wikipedia_aipac], and the largest pro-Israel group by membership is Christians United for Israel, an evangelical organization claiming over 10 million members [cite:wikipedia_cufi]. In Britain, Conservative Friends of Israel and Labour Friends of Israel work inside both parties, but unlike AIPAC they do not spend on elections [cite:greatrep_uk]. Germany's support rests mainly on a stated government doctrine tied to its historical responsibility for the Holocaust, not on a lobby.

The kernel of truth: these groups are real and sometimes cross lines. In 2017 an Israeli embassy official in London was filmed discussing how to "take down" a British minister, and he left his post [cite:mee_patel]. Mediapart reported in November 2025 that the Israeli government directly reimbursed the European group ELNET for a French Senate event [cite:mediapart_senate]. That is documented influence, not control of every Western government.
