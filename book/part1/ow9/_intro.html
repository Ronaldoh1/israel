Part Nine follows the American data company most often named in accounts of Israel inside the American state: Palantir Technologies. It begins with the company's origins in the CIA's venture fund and its growth into a contractor for the Army, the Pentagon's Maven programme and the Department of Homeland Security. It then follows Palantir's longest civilian relationship, with Immigration and Customs Enforcement, through the contracts, the data those platforms run on and the legal doctrine that lets agencies buy data they could not seize. It ends with Palantir's January 2024 partnership with Israel's Ministry of Defense: what is documented, what Israeli reporting says, what the company denies, and how investors responded.
