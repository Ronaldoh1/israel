The figure is verified through several independent methods that converge on the same range, and the Holocaust is the best-documented genocide in history [cite:ushmm_evidence_documentation]. Historian Raul Hilberg's demographic breakdown estimated 5.1 million Jewish deaths; Lucy Dawidowicz's 1975 research into prewar birth and death records produced 5,933,900 [cite:wiki_destruction_european_jews]. Yad Vashem's ongoing Book of Names project has individually identified about 4.8 million victims by name, and the work continues [cite:wiki_book_of_names]. At Auschwitz alone, at least 1.1 million people were murdered [cite:wiki_auschwitz_camp].

The perpetrators' own paperwork is part of the record. On July 31, 1941, Hermann Göring ordered Reinhard Heydrich to prepare the "Final Solution" [cite:yadvashem_wannsee_final_solution], and the Wannsee Protocol of January 20, 1942, listing about 11 million Jews across Europe, later became evidence at Nuremberg and at Eichmann's 1961 trial [cite:ushmm_wannsee_protocol]. The kernel of truth is only that "six million" is a rounded figure: scholars' estimates differ in detail, but they all fall in the same range.
