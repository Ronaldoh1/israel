The record includes real Palestinian and Arab rejections, but "every offer" leaves out the other half. Arab leaders did reject the 1937 Peel plan and the 1947 partition plan. At Lausanne in 1949, Israel offered to take back 100,000 refugees, but "only as an integral part of a general and final peace settlement"; the Arab states demanded return first, and Israel later withdrew the offer. [cite:un_ccp_fourth_report]

Camp David in July 2000 is the case most people mean, and its participants disagree. Clinton blamed Arafat for rejecting reported proposals of a state on roughly 91–92% of the West Bank and all of Gaza. Robert Malley, Clinton's own Middle East director at the talks, wrote with Hussein Agha that errors were shared and no offer was ever put in writing. [cite:independent_malley_barak] Palestinian negotiators said the real territory was smaller. The Clinton Parameters followed, and both sides answered with reservations; Taba in January 2001 ended without agreement. [cite:jvl_camp_david_overview]
