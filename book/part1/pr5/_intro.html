Part Five turns to the people who reported the war. Because Israel did not let foreign reporters into Gaza on their own, the war was told to the world almost entirely by Gaza's own journalists: staff of local and Arab outlets, and freelancers working for the international agencies. This part sets out how many of them were killed, by the counts of the main press-freedom organizations and why those counts differ; the record year of 2025; and what it meant to report while hungry, displaced and bereaved.
