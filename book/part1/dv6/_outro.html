The Divine 3+1 set out to test whether three anomalous cities were the parents of Israel. Its finding is that the cities are genuinely anomalous, each in its own direction, and that the governments two of them house did, at different times and for different reasons, what parents do: Britain from 1917 to 1939, the United States from the 1960s to the present. The third, the Vatican, opposed the state for most of a century and then accepted it, yet it is the root parent of the Christian theology that became Israel's strongest support abroad, the church the Protestant branch grew from and the voice that stayed silent as it grew; the claim that its opposition was a front does not survive its own private papers. [cite:dv3_jvl_vatican] [cite:dv3_dawn_irish_files] The three did not act as one; at the decisive moments of 1947 and 1949 they were on different sides. [cite:dv3_unproject_181] [cite:dv3_wiki_res303] The child they are said to have raised is, in November 2026, between governments, outside or in dispute with most of the rules other states follow, and renegotiating the terms of its guardian's support.
