The stages of this book follow the road that was taken. This appendix follows the roads that were not. Between 1891 and 1948 eight other lands were proposed, offered or studied as homes for Jews: Argentina, the northern Sinai, British East Africa, the Soviet Far East, Madagascar, Alaska, the Dominican Republic and Suriname. Each is told from its own documents, then measured on the same six things the state needed and given a chance of statehood on the same formula as every test on the network map.
