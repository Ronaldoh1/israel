This part goes inside the federal computer systems DOGE was ordered to reach, and then into the courtrooms where that access was fought over. The first section takes each system in turn, the Treasury's payment systems, the personnel files at the Office of Personnel Management, Social Security's master file of numbers, the tax agency, the labour board and six more agencies, and keeps three kinds of record apart: what auditors, inspectors general and courts found; what whistleblowers alleged under their own names; and what was reported from unnamed sources. The second section follows the main lawsuits and is exact about what each court did and did not decide, including the Supreme Court's emergency orders of June 2025 and the records case it agreed to hear in full, before testing the book's worst-case reading against this part's material.
