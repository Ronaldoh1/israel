Who Says What

"'Free Palestine' is code for antisemitism"

Jewish organisations such as Jewish Voice for Peace campaign for Palestinian rights; Orthodox traditions that oppose a Jewish state on religious grounds predate Israel; and Israeli groups such as B'Tselem document the occupation. [cite:w_jvp] Antisemitic incidents by some activists are also documented, and where criticism of a state ends and hostility to a people begins is a real debate, followed in The Information War. The record supports judging speech by what it says, not by the slogan it uses. Verdict: false as a rule; true of some speakers.

"All Jews support the war" or "Jewish critics speak for no one"

The Washington Post surveyed 815 American Jews from 2 to 9 September 2025, with a margin of error of 4.7 points. 61 percent said Israel had committed war crimes against Palestinians; 39 percent said it had committed genocide and 51 percent said it had not; 68 percent disapproved of Netanyahu. In the same poll 76 percent said Israel's existence is vital to the Jewish future, 94 percent said Hamas had committed war crimes and 91 percent blamed Hamas for prolonging the war. Among those aged 18 to 34, half said genocide. [cite:wapo_jews_poll] [cite:jta_jews_poll] [cite:toi_jews_poll] In February 2024 Pew had found 62 percent calling Israel's conduct acceptable. Other polling finds about 90 percent supporting Israel as a Jewish and democratic state while only 37 percent call themselves Zionist. [cite:forward_zionist] Verdict: both false; the community is divided, and attached to Israel while critical of its war.
