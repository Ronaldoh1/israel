The canon that the bishops closed still holds the inner sayings: the temple that is the body, the kingdom within, the single eye, the serpent lifted up and Christ in you. [cite:cn_bh_1cor316] [cite:vt20_audit_esv_luke17] [cite:cn_wiki_mt622] [cite:cn_esv_num21jn3] [cite:cn_esv_col127] The inner reading proposes that these are stations of an inward event, from the base of the spine to the place of the skull, and that the gate the office held outside every reader has a twin within. The record shows the sayings; the reading is the reader's. The books that follow on this shelf take up what the list left out.
