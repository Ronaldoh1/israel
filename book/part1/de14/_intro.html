This part follows the money around the people of Book II. It begins with a map on which every line is a documented transfer, job, investment, board seat, contract or appointment, with the amount, the date, the type of connection and how direct it is, from Peter Thiel's $32 million for two Senate candidates in 2022 to Elon Musk's $290 million in 2024 and the federal contracts held by Palantir and SpaceX. It then tests the loops often drawn on such maps, the chains in which money is said to return to where it began, and separates the one that closes on documents from the candidates that do not. It looks at the vehicles through which money becomes hard or impossible to trace: the Thiel Foundation, the donor-advised fund DonorsTrust, a nonprofit with no donor list and a donor network with no public face. It ends with Jeffrey Epstein's $40 million investment in Valar Ventures, a fund Thiel co-founded, and Thiel's documented contacts with Epstein: what the released files show, what they do not, and where the release of those files stood in October 2026.
