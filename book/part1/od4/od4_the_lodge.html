The Grand Lodge and the Popes

On 24 June 1717 four London lodges met at the Goose and Gridiron ale-house in St Paul's Churchyard and constituted themselves a Grand Lodge. [cite:od_wiki_pgle] Little is known of its first Grand Master, Anthony Sayer. James Anderson's Constitutions, published in 1723 by order of the Grand Master, gathered a legendary history of the craft, its charges and its regulations. [cite:od_wiki_pgle] The lodges used the tools and building lore of the medieval stonemasons' guilds as an allegory of moral self-improvement, worked their members through graded rituals and required secrecy about them. The descent of these "speculative" lodges from the working masons' guilds is documented; a secret transmission from the Templars or from ancient mysteries is not. [cite:vt30_wiki_pgle]

The secrecy did not last long. In 1730 Samuel Prichard published Masonry Dissected, an exposure containing "a recognisable representation of all three degrees", and the Grand Lodge changed its ritual in response. [cite:od_wiki_pgle] In 1813 the two rival English Grand Lodges united as the United Grand Lodge of England, which remains the governing body today. [cite:od_wiki_pgle]

Rome acted early. On 28 April 1738 Clement XII issued In eminenti, forbidding Catholics to join or support the lodges under pain of excommunication. Its grounds were the members' oath of secrecy, their private rules and the suspicion that secrecy itself created; it reasoned that "if they were not doing evil they would not have so great a hatred of the light". [cite:vt30_wiki_ineminenti] A state had already moved: the Dutch States General banned Masonic meetings in 1735. [cite:vt30_wiki_ineminenti] Benedict XIV confirmed the ban in 1751, and Leo XIII's Humanum genus of 1884 placed the Freemasons on the side of "the kingdom of Satan". [cite:vt30_wiki_providas] [cite:vt30_va_humanum]

The ban has never been lifted. The 1983 Code of Canon Law dropped Freemasonry's name, but a declaration of 26 November 1983, signed by Cardinal Joseph Ratzinger, said the Church's "negative judgment" was "unchanged" and that Catholics who join "are in a state of grave sin and may not receive Holy Communion". [cite:vt30_va_cdf1983] A note approved by Pope Francis on 13 November 2023 restated that "active membership in Freemasonry by a member of the faithful is prohibited". [cite:vt30_vn_ddf2023] The full sequence is in [scene:vt30_rosicrucians_masons|The Vatican Architecture: Rosicrucians and Freemasons].

Across these documents Rome's stated objection is consistent, and it is not fear of a hidden wisdom. It is the secret oath, the admission of men of every faith to the same table, which it reads as religious indifference, and the political hostility to the Church that it found in some lodges, above all on the continent. [cite:vt30_wiki_providas] [cite:vt30_va_humanum] The conflict was between two institutions that each asked for a man's loyalty under oath.
