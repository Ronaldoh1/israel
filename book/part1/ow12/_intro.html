Part Twelve examines Jeffrey Epstein using only court records, official releases and named reporting on them. It first sets out what the courts and the Justice Department established, from the 2008 Florida agreement to the July 2025 memorandum, the Maxwell interview and the files released under the Epstein Files Transparency Act. It then takes the claim that Epstein worked for an intelligence service apart into its pieces: the documented fact of Robert Maxwell's funeral in Jerusalem, the single-source report of Alexander Acosta's remark, and the theories with no document behind them. Finally it sets out Epstein's documented business with Ehud Barak, including the money that reached the 911 company Carbyne, and with Peter Thiel's Valar funds, and draws the line between documented association and an intelligence operation, which is not established.
