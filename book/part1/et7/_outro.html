The Ethiopian church held between ten and twenty per cent of cultivated land before 1974, much of it under rights to tribute from peasants granted by the throne. [cite:et_cs_land] [cite:et_wiki_landreform] On 4 March 1975 the Derg nationalised all rural land without compensation, and the church "lost all its land". [cite:et_cs_land] The patriarch Theophilos, who consecrated bishops without government approval, was imprisoned and strangled in 1979, and his successor was chosen under the Derg's direction.
