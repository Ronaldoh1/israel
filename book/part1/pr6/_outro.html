Part Six followed four cases. In each, the army said afterwards that the dead were fighters or that a legitimate target was present; in each, the evidence it published was thin or contradicted, and no findings had been released by the time of writing: a drone with no soldiers in its footage, a rank awarded to a ten-year-old, a dossier the Guardian called unconvincing, a hospital camera with no link found to Hamas.
