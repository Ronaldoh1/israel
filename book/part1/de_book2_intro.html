This is the second book of the Dark Enlightenment Series. The first set out the ideas: a body of writing, begun by Curtis Yarvin under the name Mencius Moldbug and named by Nick Land, which argues that democracy has failed and that a state should be run like a company. This book follows the people and the money around those ideas. It gives documented lives of Peter Thiel, Elon Musk and J.D. Vance, each closing with a ledger of political money and a ledger of public claims set against the record. It then maps the PayPal network, follows the money between the people in it, compares the two Senate campaigns Thiel funded in 2022, and separates the ideas' real neighbours from the movements they are often confused with.

The book's discipline is the series' discipline. A person's beliefs are taken only from that person's own words. A relative's record is not a person's own. The money and the offices are documented; no document shows a plan, so the book reports convergence, not coordination. Every claims ledger uses the same four grades for every person: red for a claim contradicted by the person's own documents or sworn record, orange for a promise not delivered or not verifiable, yellow for a reversal or concealment, grey for a genuine dispute.
