Part Fourteen found that the most important open questions are not about famous names. They are about records: the inboxes and investigations that were never released or never joined up, the computers that left a house before the police arrived, the tax breaks and early investments no one has traced, and the staff and lawyers who sit at the centre of the documented network. Each blind spot names the document that would close it.

Part Fifteen closes the book: the architecture, stated plainly, layer by layer, with what holds and what does not.
