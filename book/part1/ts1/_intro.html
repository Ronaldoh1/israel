Part One fixes the terms the rest of the volume uses. It defines Christian Zionism, distinguishes it from the things it is often confused with (restorationism, dispensationalism, evangelicalism as a whole, Catholic teaching and Jewish Zionism), and joins the two senses of the phrase "theology shield" into one working definition, stated as the volume's frame rather than its finding. It then takes the verse at the centre of the subject, Genesis 12:3, "I will bless them that bless thee", and sets out the two ways Christians read it: as a promise to Abraham's descendants that now applies to the Jewish people and the modern state, and as a promise fulfilled in Christ and the church. For each reading it names who holds it, from Scofield's 1917 note to a United States senator in 2025, and what it does and does not commit them to.
