Part Ten turns from what has happened in Gaza to what is planned for it. Since early 2024, American and Israeli leaders have said in public what they want the strip to become, and since late 2025 those statements have taken the form of a board, a budget and a master plan. This part sets out what each of them has said, in their words and with their dates; what the plans contain; what they leave out, above all the more than two million people who live there; and who sits on the bodies that will decide.
