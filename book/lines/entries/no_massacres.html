Massacres in 1948 are documented, much of the evidence coming from Israeli archives, historians and veterans themselves. On 9 April 1948, Irgun and Lehi forces, supported by the Haganah and Palmach, attacked Deir Yassin, west of Jerusalem, and killed about 110 villagers, many of them women, children and elderly people; a Red Cross representative reported seeing some 150 bodies in a cave. [cite:wiki_deir_yassin] At Tantura in May 1948, Israeli veterans in their nineties described killings and a mass grave in a 2022 documentary; others deny it, and the number killed cannot be established. [cite:npr_tantura]

One number in this story was inflated: the Deir Yassin toll was long given as 254, and current scholarship puts it at 107–120. Atrocities ran both ways; four days later, a reprisal attack on a convoy to Hadassah Hospital killed 78 Jews, most of them doctors and nurses. [cite:wiki_deir_yassin] The displacement itself is not in doubt: between 700,000 and 750,000 Palestinians were displaced and about 500 villages depopulated. [cite:morris_1948]
