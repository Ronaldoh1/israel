This volume is a field guide to the arguments that fill comment sections, street interviews and group chats about Israel and Palestine. Each part takes the claims people actually repeat, sets each one beside the documented record, and gives a plain verdict: true, false, partly true, disputed, or not established. The claims come from every direction. Some flatter Israel and some flatter its enemies; some are slogans, some are numbers, some are slurs dressed as history. The same test is applied to all of them.

The test has two steps. First, is the fact in the claim real? Second, does that fact prove what the claim says it proves? Most viral claims pass the first step and fail the second. The final parts add up the record as a ledger, counting land, lives and money from named sources on both sides, and sort what is settled from what is still argued.
