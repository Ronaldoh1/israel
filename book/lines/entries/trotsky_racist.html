Trotsky did not coin it. The French "raciste" was in print by the 1890s, and the first English use of "racism" recorded by the Oxford English Dictionary is Richard Henry Pratt's, in 1902, in an argument against racial segregation [cite:rs_ch01_npr_racism]. Trotsky used the word in his History of the Russian Revolution in 1930 and again in 1933, decades after it was already in circulation [cite:rs_ch01_se_racist].

The line is often paired with the claim that the word "antisemitic" was invented to silence critics. Both claims put a modern argument back into a history that the dated record does not support.
