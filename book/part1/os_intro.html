This volume covers the years between two wars and two peace processes: from the six days of June 1967, which put the West Bank, Gaza, East Jerusalem, Sinai and the Golan under Israeli control, to the withdrawal from Gaza in 2005, with an epilogue on what has become of the idea of two states. In between come a change of government that turned settlement into policy, the first peace with an Arab state, a secret alliance with apartheid South Africa, an invasion of Lebanon, the lobbying fight that made AIPAC a permanent power, two uprisings, the Oslo Accords and the summit that failed.

Several of these events have volumes of their own on this shelf: the West Bank and Gaza in their Documented volumes, the founding of Hamas and of Hezbollah in their Architectures, Rabin's assassination in The Netanyahu Architecture. Here they are joined into one sequence, so that the reader can see how each step set the ground for the next. Where participants give different accounts, as they do of 1967 and of Camp David in 2000, the accounts are set side by side.
