# rd6: source contradictions presented in the prose

- Who appointed Bernadotte: the UN's 17 Sept 1948 release says the five permanent Council members acting as a General Assembly committee under the GA resolution of 14 May 1948; Britannica says the Security Council (rd6_bernadotte; research bernadotte_mediation_1948).
- Date of the Emergency Regulations on absentees' property: 2 December 1948 (Fischbach, from Israeli sources) vs "December 12, 1948" (US document cited by Fischbach) (ap_s01; research land_regime_1948_1953).
- Start of the military government: 1949 (wiki_kafr_qasim_massacre) vs October 1948 with five military governors (Korn) (rd6_military_government, mg_s01; research military_government_1948_1966).
- Refugee totals: 520,000–530,000 (Israel, official) / about 711,000 (UNCCP) / 726,000 (UN Survey Mission and UNRWA) / "close to 800,000" (Eytan, privately, late 1950) / 810,000 (Britain, Feb 1949) / 900,000–1 million (Arab officials); UNCCP's 1951 property report used ~900,000 as a working figure; round ~700,000 elsewhere (rr_s01, ud_s01, lau_s01, dsp_s01; research res194_refugee_count).
- Depopulated localities: 369 (Morris 1987) / 399 (al-Arif) / 418 (Khalidi 1992) / 443 (Nijm and Muammer) / 472 (Saleh) / 531 (Palestine Land Society); differences mostly definitional (rr_s01; research res194_refugee_count).
- Recognition of Jordan's 1950 annexation: Britain and Pakistan (Library of Congress) / Britain only or Britain and Pakistan (customary Israeli accounts, per Kramer) / Britain, Iraq and Pakistan, with Britain excluding Jerusalem (sixdaywar_jerusalem) (rd6_arab_palestine, ijr_src_s05; research arab_palestine_1948_1951).
- Wording of the 24 April 1950 union resolution: "complete unity between the two sides of the Jordan and their union into one State" vs Kramer's "complete unity between the two banks of the Jordan, the eastern and western, and their union in one single state" (same meaning) (rd6_arab_palestine, ijr_src_s05).
- Date of the Law of Return: 5 July 1950 (law's own dating, Encyclopedia of the Modern Middle East; Kassim: bills submitted 3 July, adopted 5 July) vs 3 July 1950 (Encyclopaedia Judaica) (rd6_law_of_return; research law_of_return_nationality_1950_1952).
- Start of US military aid: $0.4M military loan in 1959 (CRS table via JVL) vs first military aid in 1962 (MERIP) (rd6_financing, iaa_s01; research financing_new_state_1949_1953).
- Value of German reparations: $845M over 14 years, $100M to the Claims Conference (JVL, citing Foreign Ministry 1953 and EJ) vs goods and services worth 3.5 billion marks over 12 years plus 450 million marks (NLI) (rd6_financing; research financing_new_state_1949_1953).
- AIPAC's beginning: founded as AZCPA by Kenen in 1953–54 and renamed AIPAC in 1959 (cjh_kenen_papers, britannica_aipac) vs AIPAC emerging in 1963 from the AZC's restructured lobbying (aipac_wiki) (s07g, s11, us_s08).
- Iraqi Jewish emigration: community ~135,000 reduced to ~6,000 by 1952 (gov.il) / 121,512 of 130,000 arrived (Jewish Agency) / 124,226 immigrants from Iraq 1948–52 (State Archives table) (rd6_exodus, rd6_ingathering, irq_e04, irq_e05).
- Yemeni Jewish emigration: ~49,000 airlifted (Magic Carpet) / "nearly all of Yemen's 45,000" (Jewish Agency) / 48,375 from Yemen and Aden (State Archives table) (rd6_exodus, rd6_ingathering, ye_s01, mz_s02).
- Regional total of Jews leaving: 800,000–900,000 from Arab countries and Iran, 1948–1970s (me_s07) vs "nearly one million" Mizrahim in about a decade after 1947–48 (Mendes) (rd6_exodus; research jews_arab_world_1948_1956).
- Baghdad bombings of 1950–51: Iraqi government (and some later historians) attribute them to Zionist agents; Israeli officials deny any involvement; independent review finds insufficient evidence of Israeli involvement without closing the question (rd6_exodus, irq_e05).
- Iraq as sole non-signatory: Israeli government sources call Iraq the only Arab combatant to refuse an armistice, while Israel's Foreign Ministry and isa_src_s02 record that Saudi Arabia, which sent a contingent under Egyptian command, also never signed (rd6_exodus, rd6_armistices, irq_e04, isa_src_s02).
- Nature of "infiltration" 1949–56: 90%+ economic and social, 2,700–5,000 killed, mostly unarmed (Morris via Shlaim) vs fedayeen organized by Egyptian intelligence from 1955 (JVL) (rd6_return_barred; research return_barred_infiltration_1948_1956).
- Responsibility for DMZ clashes on the Syrian front: documented Syrian fire on farmers and fishermen vs Dayan's 1976 claim (published 1997) that at least 80 percent were provoked by Israel (rd6_syria_1949, sy_s02).
- US role in Za'im's March 1949 coup: documented contacts vs debated direction (rd6_syria_1949, me_s08).
- Israel's assurances at UN admission: Eban said to have pledged to honour Resolutions 181 and 194 (wiki_res_194) vs the UN committee record consulted, which documents only the 6 May 1949 agenda, and Resolution 273, which only "takes note" (rd6_un_admission, rr_s01; research un_admission_jerusalem_1949_1950).
