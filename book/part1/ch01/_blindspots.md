## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency:** pr_s02 and s00i (national identity built by Palestinians themselves: Filastin, the congresses). The Mufti's Axis broadcasts are named in ch01_hatred, and his full story is linked to Vol. II.
- **The strongest form of the Zionist case:** ah_s01 (ancient presence and the stelae), ch01_old_yishuv (a Jewish community, and a Jerusalem plurality, before Zionism), ch01_citizenship (the Law of Return as a refuge law), s00c (peoplehood), s00e2 and ch01_exodus_map (persecution of the Jews of the Arab world).
- **Jewish dissent:** s00c and s00b3 (Reform, the Bund, Satmar, Neturei Karta, Buber, Magnes, Arendt, the Mizrahi critique); gx_s04 (current polling).
- **Jews of the Arab world and Iran:** s00b2, s00e, s00e2, ch01_exodus_map; Iran is linked.
- **Real antisemitism, named plainly:** ch01_hatred, s00b0, s00b1 (record incident levels), and the Khazar trope in s00b2.
- **Palestinian citizens of Israel:** pr_s02 (with mg_s01, nsl_s01 and population_now) and ch01_citizenship (the 1952 residence conditions; the family-unification ban).
- **Key terms examined:** "Semitic" and "antisemitism" (s00b, s00b0); "occupation", "apartheid" and "genocide" (pr_s03); "indigenous" (s00i); "settler colonialism" (hd_s01, though the coinage is a gap); "refugee" (ch01_exodus_map dispute). "Transfer" and "terrorism" are not examined in this volume: gap.
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** s00a3 names the state bodies and the contracts, and gives Iran-linked networks the same rigor; s00a2 runs both directions.
- **Money and alignment:** s00a (Birthright and day-school funding), s00a3 (STOIC $2M; the $150M budget; the FARA contracts), ch01_exodus_map (Yachin's payments).
- **Independent sources:** The Aden and Aleppo figures rest on one outlet and are attributed. The Hadrian dispute rests partly on a secondary blog and is attributed. The exodus totals come from advocates on both sides and are presented as disputed.

## 3. The reader's questions

Each writer lists three to five per section and answers them in the prose, or says the record does not answer them. Likely ones to check in the audit:
- pr_s02 / ch01_citizenship: Can a Palestinian refugee become an Israeli citizen? Can an Arab citizen's Palestinian spouse live in Israel today?
- ah_s01: Is "Palestine" a Roman insult? When does "Israel" first appear outside the Bible?
- ch01_old_yishuv: Were there Jews in the land before Zionism, and how many?
- ch01_communities: Do the Druze serve in the army? Why are some Bedouin villages "unrecognized"?
- ch01_exodus_map: Were the Jews expelled, or did they choose to leave? How many, and where did they go?
- s00e3: Did Arab states take in the Palestinian refugees?
