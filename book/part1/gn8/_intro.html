Part Eight draws the threads together. It sets out, era by era, who controlled access to Gnostic knowledge, from the bishops of the second century to the foundation and the television network of the twenty-first, and shows how the mechanism traced in The Canon repeated itself and where it failed. It ends with a section, marked as a reading, that looks at the same history through the Gnostic texts' own picture of rulers who stand between the person and the source.
