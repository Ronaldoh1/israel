Part Ten turns from an American contractor selling to Israel to Israeli-founded companies selling to, or operating in, the United States. It follows NSO Group, whose Pegasus spyware was licensed by Israel's Defense Ministry, used against American diplomats, blacklisted by the Commerce Department and tested by the FBI; Paragon Solutions, whose Graphite spyware ICE bought, paused and revived after the company passed into American ownership; and five other firms, Cellebrite, Elbit Systems' American subsidiary, Cognyte, Black Cube and Carbyne. For each it sets out the owners, the American customers and contract values, the role of Israeli export control, the controversies and the company's answer.
