What Actually Caught Him

Before asking what would survive, the book has to be exact about what worked. The earlier parts show that the federal agencies with the most power did the least, for the longest. What moved the case was a scattered set of outsiders and lesser institutions.

Every item on this list is a check that sits outside the executive branch, or below it, or both. That is the finding the scenario starts from: in this case, accountability came from the edges of the system, not its centre.
