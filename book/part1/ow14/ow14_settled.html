What the Record Settles

The law. The relationship was built by statute and executive agreement over five decades: the Emergency Security Assistance Act of 1973, the strategic memorandum of 1981, major non-NATO ally status in 1987, early disbursement into an interest-bearing account at the Federal Reserve from 1991, the qualitative military edge written into law in 2008, the 2012 and 2014 cooperation acts, and three ten-year memoranda culminating in the $38 billion agreement of 2016. By the Congressional Research Service's count, American aid to Israel from 1946 to 2025 came to about $174 billion in current dollars. [cite:ow_crs_aid2025] [cite:ow_wiki_sca1981] [cite:ow_jvl_2014act] It passed with large bipartisan majorities in both chambers, and every attempt to condition it failed. [scene:ow2_votes|Part Two: the votes]

The intelligence agencies. The CIA's liaison with the Mossad dates from 1951 and rests on no published agreement. [cite:ow3_robarge] Under a 2009 memorandum, the NSA passes Unit 8200 raw intercepts, including Americans' communications, and the memorandum creates no legally enforceable rights; the NSA's own documents also describe Israel as a counterintelligence threat that "also targets us", which Israel denies. [cite:ow_aj_nsamou] [cite:ow4_alternet_guardian] [cite:ow_td_spying2015] One Israeli spy, Jonathan Pollard, was convicted in an American court. [cite:ow13_wiki_pollard]

The military and the weapons. Without a defence treaty, the United States keeps a war-reserve stockpile in Israel, a radar in the Negev and, since 2024, a THAAD battery; Israel moved into Central Command in 2021; and since October 2023 American forces have fought in Israel's defence. [cite:ow7_js_wrsa] [cite:ow7_sat_xband] [cite:ow7_dod_thaad2024] [cite:ow_inss_centcom] Arms sales to Israel face a shorter congressional review than most countries', and since 2023 that review has been skipped more than once by emergency declaration. [cite:ow8_crs_review2021] [cite:ow_crs_aid2025]

The contractors. Palantir, seeded by the CIA's venture arm, holds ICE contracts justified as sole-source and a strategic partnership with Israel's Defense Ministry; Israeli-founded firms such as Cellebrite, Paragon and Elbit's American subsidiary sell to ICE and Customs and Border Protection, while NSO Group was blacklisted by the Commerce Department after its spyware was used against State Department employees.
