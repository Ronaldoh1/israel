# Stage Two (cw2), Retaliate, 1953–1956: research notes

Verified 14 October 2026. 15 entries, 144 claims, 78 distinct source URLs, 11 disputes. Search budget ran out before Ma'ale Akrabim, Nahalin, Khan Yunis and the Bat Galim could be fetched; see the unverified list in `part1/cw2/_blindspots.md`.

## cw2_unit101_qibya_1953
- **Agree:** Unit 101 founded August 1953 under Sharon and merged into battalion 890 in January 1954 (Israel's Defense Ministry archive). Regular forces carried out Qibya on 14–15 October 1953 (Resolution 101; the State Department; Sharett's diary). Ben-Gurion's broadcast publicly denied any army role and blamed settlers. Resolution 101 censured Israel and also asked Jordan to stop crossings; 9–0–2.
- **Disagree:** houses destroyed (45, Khalidi/Caplan and rd6; 50, Jewish Virtual Library); dead (69; Glubb's 66). Who carried out the raid: Israel's 1953 public line vs every other source, including Sharett's private record.
- **Unverified:** the exact wording of the operational order beyond the planning options Khalidi and Caplan report from Benziman.

## cw2_banat_yaacov_aid_1953
- **Agree (all FRUS):** works began 2 September; Bennike's request of 23 September; Dulles withheld Mutual Security funds; Resolution 100 (27 October, unanimous); works stopped at midnight on 28 October; aid released the next day.
- **Disagree:** the sum at stake ($26 million released for half a year vs Dulles's recalled ~$100 million total vs his aide's $50–60 million). These are different measures, not a conflict of fact; the writer should say so.

## cw2_johnston_plan_1953_1955
- **Agree:** Eisenhower's 16 October 1953 mandate; the TVA/Main study; technical acceptance in 1955; memoranda of 5 July and 11 October 1955; political refusal by the Arab League; later de facto adherence.
- **Disagree:** reasons for Arab refusal (political recognition vs substantive suspicion of Israeli benefit); allocations differ slightly between AquaPedia (Israel 400; Main 394 vs 393 in its own table) and the State Department summary (Israel 25 from the Yarmuk plus the residue). Logged.
- **Unverified:** the date of the Arab League decision rests on AquaPedia alone.

## cw2_sharett_ben_gurion_1953_1956
- **Agree:** dates of Ben-Gurion's leave, resignation, return as defence minister (21 February 1955) and premiership (3 November 1955); Sharett's resignation on 18 June 1956.
- **Disagree:** what the diaries show. Rokach (1980) reads them as proof of a deliberate strategy of provocation; Caplan and Shlaim read a real internal contest in which Sharett often won until US arms were refused. Rokach's study is an argued reading from an advocacy publisher; label it.
- **Unverified:** the Sixth Government's start date as listed by the Prime Minister's Office; a reported "35 to 7" vote (dealer's catalogue).

## cw2_lavon_susannah_1954_1955
- **Agree:** Unit 131 of Egyptian Jews recruited by military intelligence; targets including American libraries and British- and American-owned cinemas; arrests after 23 July; trial 11 December 1954–27 January 1955; Marzouk and Azar executed 31 January 1955; Lavon resigned February 1955; Israel recognized the operatives on 30 March 2005.
- **Disagree:** who gave the order (Gibli's claim of a verbal instruction vs Lavon's denial; Dayan's blame; Sharett's "preparing the ground"); whether anyone died (Ynet: no casualties; Raseef22: deaths on 2 July).
- **Note:** the intended attribution differs across sources (Brotherhood, Communists, "local nationalists" in the Defense Ministry archive; lv_s01 says nationalists or communists; s21b says the Brotherhood). The archive's list covers all three.

## cw2_egypt_free_officers_1952_1956

- **Unverified:** the exact date the republic was proclaimed and Farouk's departure date (not in the sources fetched).

## cw2_gaza_raid_fedayeen_1955
- **Agree:** the raid on 28 February 1955 by regular forces; the MAC finding; Resolution 106 (unanimous); the March protests in Gaza and their suppression; fedayeen units from September 1955.
- **Disagree:** the toll (38 and 8; 37 soldiers and 2 civilians; 38 dead and 31 or 33 wounded); and the consequences (Shlaim and Allen: the raid pushed Egypt toward Soviet arms; Eban: the Baghdad Pact did; the Soviet record of 15 June 1954 shows an earlier request).
