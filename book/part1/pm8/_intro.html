Part Eight goes below Congress. In the states, pro-Israel advocacy has produced two results that federal politics did not: laws against boycotts of Israel, passed or ordered by thirty-eight states in less than a decade, and public treasuries that bought more than $1.7 billion of Israeli government bonds in the nine months after 7 October 2023. This part sets out how each came about, what each requires or risks, and the disputes over both, in the courts and in public.
