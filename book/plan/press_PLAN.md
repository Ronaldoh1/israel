# Volume plan: PRESS

**Title:** PRESS
**Subtitle:** The Dead of Gaza and the West Bank, and the Journalists Killed Counting Them
**Cover:** two numbers side by side, each with source and date: 241 journalists and media workers killed in Gaza (IFJ, July 2026) and 74,382 killed in Gaza (health ministry, 12 Oct 2026). Re-check both at build time.

**Placement:** Vol XVIII, after *The West Bank, Documented*.
**Companion to:** *Gaza, Documented* (Part Eleven, "What Americans Were Shown") and *The West Bank, Documented*. Reuses their research keys, and expands them.
**Subject:** two things in one book.
1. The journalists of Gaza and the West Bank who were killed, injured, detained or blocked while reporting since October 2023, named one by one where the record allows, and the organizations that keep reporting under obstruction and financial strain.
2. What they were trying to report: the dead, counted and uncounted, with the children, women, pregnant women, infants and the starved broken out from the counting bodies.
**Method:** the same as the other volumes. Each part is researched claim by claim, written, then fact-checked by a separate agent. Every contested point carries both documented positions. Every figure is dated.

## The parts (draft)

1. **The Count.** How the dead of Gaza are counted: the health ministry's lists, ID numbers, hospital and family reports; the Lancet and survey estimates of the uncounted; Israel's challenges and the IDF's reported acceptance of ~71,000; what an undercount means (bodies under rubble, non-violent excess deaths).
2. **The Children.** Children, infants and newborns killed; amputations and orphans; UNICEF and ministry figures, dated; Israel's position on Hamas's use of civilian areas.
3. **Women, Mothers and the Unborn.** Women killed; pregnant women, births without care, pregnancy losses (ministry: ~4,000 in the first half of 2026), anaemia; maternity wards and the hospitals.
4. **Hunger.** The IPC famine finding (Aug 2025), starvation deaths (466, 157 children, Oct 2026), malnutrition; the aid record; Israel's denial and its aid figures; the ICC's starvation-as-method warrants.
5. **The Journalists.** Who they were: Gaza's local press, freelancers and stringers; the counts (CPJ, IFJ, RSF, UN) and why they differ; the deadliest conflict for journalists in CPJ's records.
6. **The Named.** Individual cases, each with the record: Hamza Dahdouh, Ismail al-Ghoul, Anas al-Sharif, the Nasser hospital double strike (Aug 2025), and others; the IDF's stated reason in each, the evidence it gave, and what investigations found.
7. **"Journalists Are Not Terrorists."** Israel's argument that some were militants; the "Legitimization Cell" reporting; CPJ's and IFJ's removals after review; the laws of war on journalists and on targeting.
8. **The Gate.** The foreign-press ban, the Foreign Press Association's petitions, embeds under military escort, the censor; why no foreign reporter has entered Gaza independently (as of Oct 2026).
9. **The West Bank Press.** Shireen Abu Akleh (2022) and the findings on her killing; arrests and administrative detention of journalists; raids on newsrooms; the Al Jazeera ban; Israel's stated reasons.
10. **Keeping the Lights On.** The organizations still reporting: their funding, power, internet, equipment and insurance; press-freedom groups' support funds; losses of income; the cost of a vest and a satellite phone.
11. **What Reached America.** How the casualty and press stories were carried, or not, in US outlets (links to *Gaza, Documented* Part Eleven): the memos, the coverage studies, the hot-mic question still awaiting a source.
12. **Settled, Contested and Open.**

## Discipline notes

- **Numbers.** Every casualty figure gives its source, date and what it counts (killed by violence vs excess deaths; civilians vs all). Estimates are labelled as estimates, with ranges.
- **Journalists vs combatants.** Where Israel says a named journalist was a militant, the volume gives the claim, the evidence offered, and the rebuttals and investigations, side by side. The counting bodies' own removals are reported.
- **No collective attribution.** Acts are attributed to named units, officials and governments as the record shows, not to peoples.
- **Hamas.** Hamas's control of the health ministry, its treatment of journalists, and October 7 are stated where relevant.
- **Live checks.** All counts are re-checked at build time and marked "as of [date]".
