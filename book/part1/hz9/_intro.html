Part Nine measures the two sides, as Part Eight of the Hamas volume did for Gaza. Hezbollah is often described as the strongest non-state armed group in the world, and Lebanon and Israel are formally two states at war; in resources neither pairing is close. This part first sets Hezbollah's fighters, rockets, missiles, drones and Iranian money, all of them estimates, beside Israel's army, air force, missile defences, budget and American aid, and records how Israel's own estimates of what it destroyed changed between 2024 and 2026. It then compares the two economies: Lebanon's, which lost most of its dollar value after 2019 and was damaged again by war, and Israel's, which grew through two years of fighting. It closes by putting the gap in everyday terms.
