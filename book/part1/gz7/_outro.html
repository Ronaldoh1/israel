Part Seven followed the war from the siege of October 2023 to the year after the ceasefire of October 2025. A first truce in January 2025 collapsed in March; the second freed the last living hostages and some 1,900 Palestinian prisoners and detainees, but by October 2026 nearly 1,500 more Palestinians had been killed, aid moved at a third of the agreed rate and Israel held nearly two-thirds of the strip.
