Part Fifteen has established a short record and a long list of claims. Emanuela Orlandi, a Vatican citizen, vanished in Rome on 22 June 1983, forty days after Mirella Gregori. Callers demanded Ağca's release and the Vatican opened a line to them, but no one ever proved they held her. [cite:vt15_cwr_senate] [cite:vt15_csm_1983] [cite:vt15_ilpost_2013] De Pedis's tomb was opened in 2012 and the Teutonic Cemetery's tombs in 2019, and both searches found nothing of her. [cite:vt15_ap_2012] [cite:vt15_vn_2019] The Marcinkus, abuse and John Paul II claims rest on witnesses a judge found unreliable or on no evidence at all. The 2017 memo is unauthenticated and denied by the Holy See. [cite:vt15_fq_2015] [cite:vt15_vn_francis] [cite:vt15_crux_2017] Rome archived its second investigation in 2015. Three inquiries opened in 2023: the Vatican's, Rome's and Parliament's under Law 202. As of October 2026 none has charged anyone or issued a report. [cite:vt15_fp_cass2016] [cite:vt15_law_202] [cite:vt15_agi_dec2025] The case remains open, and the family still holds that the Vatican knows more than it has said.
