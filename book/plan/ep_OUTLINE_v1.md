**The book's question.** A man pleaded guilty to a sex crime involving a child in 2008. Afterwards the most powerful people in the world kept him close; banks kept his accounts; a Justice Department immunised people it never named; and in 2026 the government still holds back roughly half of what it found. What, exactly, kept that system running? Each part asks this of one layer: money, people, agencies, the DOJ, and the release.

**Subject type.** A composite with a person-type spine. The throughline is one man's network. The agency, bank and DOJ parts are institution-type components with their own baselines. Every claim about a named individual runs at the person-type ceiling, the strictest in the protocol.

**The rules, stated once in the book's introduction and applied to every name:**
1. **Four tiers for any named person**, carried over from ch34's method scene (epf_s13):
   - **Adjudicated:** a verdict, ruling or settlement.
   - **Sworn allegation:** testimony under penalty of perjury.
   - **Documented association:** contact established; no conduct alleged.
   - **Not carried:** an assertion with no document behind it. It is left out entirely, not written up as "contested".

   Any claim that a named person took part in a crime needs court records, sworn testimony, or case-file documents. Without them it is not written.
2. **Being in the files is not a finding.** "Appears 7,000 times" is an association count. The book says what each appearance *is*: an email, a calendar entry, a flight, a payment, or a tip someone phoned in.
3. **What they said vs. what the files show.** When a public figure's own on-record statement conflicts with a dated document, both go side by side with dates. That is a documented contradiction. It is not a finding about motive or conduct.
4. **The slide test** runs on every intelligence and Israel passage.
   - A claim about a state, an agency or a named official is checkable and may stay.
   - A claim that drifts into an ethnic or religious group is not a claim; it is cut.
   - Israel appears where named Israeli officials, firms and state acts appear in documents: Barak, Carbyne, the Maxwell state funeral, the NSA–Unit 8200 memorandum.
5. **Same standard for every party.** Trump and Clinton; Bondi and the Biden-era DOJ; Mandelson (Labour) and the Republican Speaker. The release fight is documented act by act, date by date, for whoever did it.
6. **The survivors are the subject, not the backdrop.**
   - Abuse is described at the level courts and survivors' own public testimony set, never more graphically.
   - Victims' names are used only where they themselves went public.
   - Every death is reported with the official finding first, then any documented dissent.
   - Chapters touching suicide end with a resource line.

**The standard (*The Road to 1948*):** a table of contents, introduction, figure key, notes and bibliography. Each section draws on every scene in the library that bears on it, not just its own chapter. Every chapter gets a coverage ledger, written before drafting and checked after. New facts enter as research addenda (`book/research/ep*.json`), each verified live and queued back into the simulation. *The Road to 1948* runs 7 parts and about 175 sections; this outline proposes 11 parts and about 150 sections.
