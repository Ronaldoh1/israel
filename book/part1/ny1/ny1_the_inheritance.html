The Inheritance, 1910–1976

Benzion Netanyahu, born Mileikowsky in Warsaw in 1910, was a historian and a lifelong Revisionist Zionist who served as secretary to Ze'ev Jabotinsky, the founder of Revisionism, and worked for the movement in the United States after Jabotinsky's death in 1940. [cite:wiki_benzion_netanyahu] His field was the Spanish Inquisition, and his politics stayed on the hard edge of the Israeli right for the rest of his long life.

The doctrine Jabotinsky left the movement was set out in a 1923 essay, "The Iron Wall". It argued that no native population had ever accepted colonisation willingly, that the Arabs of Palestine would not be the exception, and that Zionist settlement could therefore proceed only behind "an iron wall" of force which they could not break, until they were ready to negotiate. [cite:gi2_jvl_ironwall] The son has claimed the idea in his own words. In a speech reported in June 2025 he said that "Jabotinsky established the principle of the iron wall" and that "security control over the entire territory west of the Jordan must remain in our hands"; at the state memorial for Jabotinsky in July 2026 he said that "today's iron wall is not a static wall". [cite:ny_ynet_ironwall] [cite:ny_ina_jab2026] He has also dismissed attempts to explain him through his father.

The American years and the army

Benjamin Netanyahu was born in Tel Aviv on 21 October 1949. His father's academic posts took the family to Cheltenham Township, outside Philadelphia, from 1956 to 1958 and again from 1963 to 1967, and he graduated from the local high school. [cite:ny_wiki_bibi] He returned to Israel in 1967, joined the special forces unit Sayeret Matkal, fought at Karameh in 1968, was wounded in the 1972 rescue of the hijacked Sabena Flight 571, left as a captain and came back for the 1973 war. [cite:ny_wiki_bibi] [cite:sayeret_matkal_history]

He then went back to America, to the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, where he took a degree in architecture in 1975 and a master's in management in 1976, and to the Boston Consulting Group, where he worked from 1976 to 1978. [cite:ny_wiki_bibi] [cite:ir5_enc_bio]

Entebbe

On 4 July 1976 Israeli commandos freed the hostages of a hijacked Air France flight at Entebbe in Uganda. The only Israeli soldier killed was the commander of the assault force, Benjamin's elder brother Yonatan. [cite:entebbe_operation_1976] The death ended Benjamin's doctoral studies and set the direction of his next decade. He returned to Israel in 1978 to run the Jonathan Institute, founded in his brother's name, whose conferences on terrorism in Jerusalem in 1979 and Washington in 1984 brought together politicians, officials and journalists and made the younger Netanyahu a known voice on the subject before he held any office.
