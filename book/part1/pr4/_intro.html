Part Four turns to hunger. It follows the decisions that governed what food entered Gaza, from the siege announced on 9 October 2023 to the blockade of March 2025 and the aid sites that replaced the UN's distribution; the finding of famine in August 2025, Israel's rejection of it, and the arrest warrants that named starvation as a war crime; the deaths from starvation, about a third of them children; and the easing after the ceasefire, by the UN's account and by Israel's.
