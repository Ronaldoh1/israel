Read from the land, the road to 1948 is a story of warnings that were heard and set aside. The people of Palestine said in writing in 1899 what they feared, said it again in their newspapers, their parliament, their congresses, their delegations and their courts, and were answered each time by a power that had promised their country to someone else. The record also holds their divisions, the violence some of them did, and a leader who chose the wrong side of a world war. What it does not hold is a people who failed to see what was coming. They saw it first.
