Part Eleven is about the deaths, and it is written under rules of its own. Lists of "people connected to Epstein who died" circulate constantly, and the public is right to ask about them. This part answers case by case. Every death begins with the official finding. Then come the documented dissents and anomalies, then the documented failures of whatever institution was responsible, and finally what remains open, with the date it was last checked. A death is never treated as proof of guilt, and never as proof of murder. The details of how anyone died go no further than the official record requires.

The finding, stated at the start so no reader has to wait for it: the documents show no pattern of killing. They do show another pattern, fully documented and in some ways more troubling: testimony lost before anyone took it, and institutions that failed the people and the evidence in their care.
