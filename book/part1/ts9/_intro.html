Part Nine asks what each side of the alliance gained from it. On Israel's side it follows the documented courtship of American Christian Zionist leaders by Israeli governments, from Menachem Begin's award to Jerry Falwell in 1980 and Benjamin Netanyahu's meeting with Falwell in 1998 to the Foreign Ministry's paid church campaigns of 2025, and it counts what came back in visitors, charitable money and parliamentary allies. On the American side it sets out what the polls show of the white evangelical vote, what a president said about the reason for one decision, what prophecy publishing earned, and which named donors funded the organisations in between. Motives are kept apart from money and votes: the reasons given by the people concerned and the readings of their critics are presented side by side as arguments, not as findings.
