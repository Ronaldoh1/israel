The Other Signatories

Bahrain

The US Fifth Fleet has been based in Bahrain since 1995. [cite:w_fifth_fleet] Bahrain signed the Accords at the White House on 15 September 2020. [cite:w_abraham] In November 2023 its parliament announced that Israel's ambassador had left, Bahrain's had been recalled and economic ties were halted; the government confirmed the ambassadors' departure. [cite:w_isr_bahrain] Bahrain has not left the Accords, and its ambassador attended the American-hosted sixth-anniversary gathering in September 2026. [cite:dayan_accords] [cite:aj_morocco_embassies]

Morocco

Morocco's Jewish community, rooted for two thousand years and numbering in the hundreds of thousands in the 1940s, largely emigrated after 1948. Between 1961 and 1964 Operation Yachin brought about 97,000 Moroccan Jews to Israel; historians record payments by Israel and American Jewish organisations to the Moroccan government to allow the departures. [cite:w_yachin] Liaison offices opened after Oslo in 1994 and closed in 2000. [cite:w_isr_morocco]

In December 2020 Morocco agreed to normalise, and the United States became the first country to recognise Moroccan sovereignty over Western Sahara, a territory Morocco has held since Spain left in 1975 and that the Algerian-backed Polisario Front claims for independence. [cite:brit_abraham] [cite:w_western_sahara] Israel later recognised the claim too. [cite:vin_morocco] On 16 September 2026, at a meeting hosted by the American ambassador to the UN, the two foreign ministers agreed to raise their missions to embassies, appoint ambassadors and restore direct flights before the end of 2026. [cite:aj_morocco_embassies] [cite:mwn_morocco_upgrade] The ties drew protests in Morocco during the Gaza war; defence and intelligence cooperation continued. [cite:mwn_morocco_upgrade] [cite:levant_morocco] Status as of 26 November 2026: the upgrade has been agreed; the exchange of ambassadors had not been reported.

Sudan

Khartoum was where the Arab League adopted the three no's in 1967. [cite:w_khartoum] After the fall of Omar al-Bashir, General Abdel Fattah al-Burhan met Netanyahu in Uganda in February 2020, and normalisation was announced that October. [cite:w_isr_sudan] Washington removed Sudan from its list of state sponsors of terrorism in December 2020, and Sudan signed the Accords declaration in January 2021. [cite:brit_abraham] The agreement was never ratified. The army seized full power in October 2021, and in April 2023 war broke out between the army and the Rapid Support Forces, one of the world's largest humanitarian crises. [cite:w_sudan_war] [cite:w_abraham]

Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan, which already had full relations with Israel, announced in November 2025 that it would join the Accords, the first new member of President Trump's second term. [cite:brit_abraham]

In each case the decision was made by a monarch or a military ruler; no referendum or vote of an elected legislature approved normalisation in Bahrain, Morocco or Sudan. None of the signatories has formally left. [cite:passblue_accords]
