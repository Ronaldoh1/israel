Read from within, Sufism offers a heart that shows God when its rust is polished, a self that dies before death and returns, and a reed whose song is its longing for the bed it was cut from. [cite:sf_aa_mirror] [cite:sf_brit_fana] [cite:sf_ic_reed] The reading is the reader's; the tradition, with its martyrs, its orders and its poets, is the record.
