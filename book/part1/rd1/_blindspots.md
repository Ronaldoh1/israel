## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency:** fil_s01 (Azouri, Nassar, Filastin, the Jerusalem deputies, Paris 1913), s04j (al-Asali at al-Fula; Wadi al-Hawarith later), rd1_arabquestion (al-Khalidi's letter). The research gives no Husseini–Nashashibi rivalry for this period. Internal differences appear as Nassar attacking 1913 understandings between some Arabs and Zionists.
- **Strongest form of the Zionist case:**
  - s04e: refuge after Kishinev; Herzl's "Zion this certainly is not".
  - rd1_arabquestion: Herzl's reply and Altneuland, in their own terms.
  - rd1_second_aliyah: Shapira's reading.
  - s04j: Ruppin's account of willing sellers.
  - s07a: the institutions built in the open.
- **Jewish dissent:** s04 (Protestrabbiner, Adler, the Bund, Orthodox anti-Zionism, Ahad Ha'am, Edmond de Rothschild); rd1_currents (Reform, the CCAR, Frankfurt Orthodoxy, Agudat Israel, the AZK, the League of British Jews); s04ga (Schiff, Felix Warburg); zorg_s01 (the Board of Deputies, May 1917).
- **Jews of the Arab world and Iran:**
  - rd1_second_aliyah: the Yemenites, including Shafir's "paid Arab wages" point and the Kinneret group.
  - rd1_ottoman: the Sephardi Old Yishuv; Iraqi Jewry via irq_e01.
  - s04j: Antébi.
- **Real antisemitism named plainly:** s04e (Kishinev: 49 dead; the Bessarabets blood libel; the role attributed to Plehve); s04ga (the Mullins myth); s04 (Dreyfus).
- **Terms examined:** "home" vs "state" (the Basel Program's Heimstätte); "transfer" (the 1895 diary passage and the dispute over it); "a land without a people"; "Palestinian" as a name in print (Filastin).
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** hz_s01 (each power's own interest); s04ga/s04gb (the Fed, with no line to Zionist finance); s05 (OUP's ordinary committee process).
- **Money and political alignment:**
  - s04c and s04d: mass small-donor finance.
  - s04j: founders' shares (wide ownership, narrow control).
  - s05: the Untermyer association, stated as documented association, not causation.
- **Independent sources:** load-bearing figures carry at least two sources where possible (Kishinev: EJ only. **Single-source**, flagged). Disputed counts are given as ranges.
- **Palestinian citizens of Israel:** not applicable before 1948.
