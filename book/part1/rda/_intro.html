The state was declared on 14 May 1948, and the road this volume has followed reached its end. What the founding set in motion did not end there. This stage follows it from 1949 to the present, one chain at a time: the refugees, the war of 1967 and the occupation, Lebanon and Hezbollah, Hamas, the peace plans, the American patron, Iran, and Gaza now. Each chain is told briefly, with its sources, and each points to the volume of this library that tells it in full.

The same events appear in red on the network map, with a switch that shows the years up to 1948, after it, or both. Every tie in red is one the later volumes document. The last part of this stage tests the chains on the map: which later events lose every documented cause when the founding, the displacement or the occupation is taken away, and which keep a cause of their own.
