Hamas was founded in December 1987, but the movement it grew out of is sixty years older. Part One follows that movement from its founding in Egypt in 1928 to the eve of the first intifada: its branches in Gaza, its suppression under Egyptian rule, its revival under Sheikh Ahmed Yassin, and the charity network that Israel licensed in 1979 and then largely left alone. It ends with what Israeli officials said about that choice afterwards, because they did not agree.
