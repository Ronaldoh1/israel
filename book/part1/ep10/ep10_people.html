The People Who Were Never Charged

A network is people before it is anything else, and the record on the people is clear. Epstein is dead. Maxwell is the one person convicted. Jean-Luc Brunel, the model agent charged in France, died in custody in 2022. [cite:ep2_aj_brunel2022] The four women the 2008 agreement named and protected, Sarah Kellen, Lesley Groff, Adriana Ross and Nadia Marcinkova, have never been charged. [cite:ep2_cbs_coconspirators2019] The unnamed "potential co-conspirators" the agreement also protected have never been identified. The FBI's own list of alleged co-conspirators from August 2019 produced no charge against anyone on it but Maxwell. [cite:ep4_npr_wexner_fbi2026] The fourteen people investigated with Epstein by the DEA from 2010 are still unnamed. [cite:ep7_cbs_dea2026]

The people closest to the operation have only recently begun to speak, and to Congress rather than to prosecutors. Sarah Kellen, who has said she was also a victim, told the House Oversight Committee in May 2026 that she had been abused by Maxwell and by two men she named, the former Miami Beach mayor Philip Levine and the hairstylist Frédéric Fekkai. Fekkai's spokesman said he "never abused anyone". [cite:ep10_cbs_kellen2026] These are allegations made to Congress, not tested in any court. The committee's Republican chairman, James Comer, and four other Republicans asked the Justice Department to use "all available tools" to investigate them; no response from the department has been reported.
