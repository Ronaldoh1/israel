The cuts were real and large: USAID's programmes cut by 83 per cent and the agency closed as an implementer of aid; about 25,000 probationary employees dismissed by a direction a judge later held unlawful; layoffs allowed by Supreme Court stays that left their legality undecided; and a federal workforce about 256,000 smaller on GAO's net measure, or about 317,000 departures on OPM's gross count.
