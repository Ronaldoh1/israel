Part Six asks who may join each state and what happens to those who arrive without permission. It sets Israel's Law of Return and Nationality Law, which give every Jew and certain relatives of Jews an immediate right to citizenship and give citizenship at birth only to the children of citizens, beside the American rule of citizenship by birth on the soil and naturalisation after five years of permanent residence, which the Supreme Court upheld in June 2026. It counts immigrants to Israel and naturalisations in the United States over four decades, and names who has no route in each country: Palestinian spouses barred since 2003, East Jerusalem's permanent residents and the people whose citizenship the state can now revoke, and America's unauthorised population. It then compares how the two governments treated asylum seekers, from Israel's Holot facility and its 2018 deportation plan to America's refugee ceilings, immigration courts, deportations and detention.
