Part Six asks whether the warnings tested in Part Five were one man's or a state's. It sets out the dated statements of other Israeli leaders, generals and intelligence services on how close Iran was to a bomb and whether Israel should strike, from Shimon Peres in 1992 to the army's case for war in June 2025, and the American officials who echoed them. It then gives the other side of the same establishment: Mossad and Shin Bet chiefs and army commanders who disputed the timelines or warned against an attack, the orders to prepare strikes in 2010 and 2011 that some of them resisted, the leaked Mossad paper of 2012, and the US intelligence record from the 2007 estimate to the hearings of 2025 and 2026. Forecasts, claims about capability and arguments about policy are kept apart throughout.
