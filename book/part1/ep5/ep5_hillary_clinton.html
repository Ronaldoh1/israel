Hillary Clinton

Born 1947; grew up in Park Ridge, Illinois. At the time of contact: first lady, then senator from New York and Secretary of State. Today: no office; not charged or sued over Epstein.

Before Epstein

Hillary Rodham was born on 26 October 1947 and grew up in Park Ridge, Illinois. She went to Wellesley College and entered Yale Law School in 1969, joined the Rose Law Firm in Little Rock in 1976, and was first lady of Arkansas for twelve years. In 1993 her husband, by then president, asked her to chair his task force on health-care reform. She was elected senator from New York on 7 November 2000, ran for the Democratic presidential nomination in 2008, and was Secretary of State from 2009 to 2013. [cite:ep5_wh_hillary]

The record with Epstein

Her entry is short because her record is short, and the book keeps it apart from her husband's. A marriage does not pass one person's record to the other; only a joint act does. Three items concern her directly.

The first is a donation. In 1999, while she was preparing to run for the Senate, Epstein gave $20,000 to her joint fundraising committee with the Democratic Party, according to federal election filings. [cite:ep5_gma_donations2019] It is a record of his money reaching her campaign. It is not evidence that she met him or knew him.

The second is a room. She attended the September 1993 White House reception at which Epstein and Maxwell were photographed greeting the president, according to the Clinton Library's caption. [cite:ep5_snopes_1993] No photograph released so far shows her with Epstein.

The third is a guest. Ghislaine Maxwell attended her daughter Chelsea's wedding in July 2010. Clinton says Maxwell came as "the plus-one, the guest of someone who was invited", without saying whose guest. [cite:ep4_ap_hillary2026]

What she said

She was deposed by the House Oversight Committee in Chappaqua, New York, on 26 February 2026. Her opening statement said: "I had no idea about their criminal activities. I do not recall ever encountering Mr. Epstein. I never flew on his plane or visited his island, homes or offices." [cite:ep5_rollcall_hillary2026] To reporters afterwards she went further: "I never met Jeffrey Epstein, never had any connection or communication with him", and she described Maxwell as a casual acquaintance. [cite:ep5_npr_depositions2026] The two statements differ: the first, given in the deposition, says she does not recall; the second, given to the press, says it never happened. The 1993 reception sits between them. Being in the same room at a donors' reception is not an encounter anyone would necessarily remember, and nothing released shows that she met him there.

What the news got wrong

The most viral claim about her is that the New York police found evidence on Anthony Weiner's laptop that she visited Epstein's island at least six times. Fact-checkers rated it false in January 2024: her name is not in the flight logs, the court papers that list her do so only as a possible witness in Giuffre's suit against Maxwell, and the emails from that laptop concern talks with foreign leaders, not Epstein. [cite:ep5_usat_hillary2024] On the record as it stands, the documented exposure in this family belongs to Bill Clinton. Hers consists of a campaign donation, a reception, a wedding guest and her own statements.

What remains unestablished: who brought Maxwell to the wedding, and whether the donation was solicited by anyone who knew Epstein.
