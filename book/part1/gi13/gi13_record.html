What the Record Settles

The last part sorts the volume's findings into the four kinds of statement Part One promised to keep apart: programmes that named actors stated or legislated, patterns that can be shown over time, arguments about intent, and myths or misreadings. [scene:gi1_phrase|Part One: how this volume reads the record] Each finding below rests on the documents already cited in Parts One to Twelve; nothing new is introduced here.

Documented programmes

Both banks of the Jordan, 1925–1965. The Revisionist Union's founding programme of 1925 called for a Jewish majority "on both sides of the Jordan," sung by Betar and drawn on the Irgun's emblem, and Herut carried the claim in opposition until it merged into Gahal in 1965. [cite:gi2_ej_revisionists] [cite:gi1_wiki_eastbank] [cite:gi3_jvl_herut] It was the programme of a named minority, never of the Zionist Organization or of an Israeli government, and Likud's 1977 platform dropped the east bank. [scene:gi2_bothbanks|Part Two: both banks]

The whole land, 1967–1977. The Movement for the Whole Land of Israel declared on 22 September 1967 that "no government in Israel is entitled to give up this wholeness"; it won 0.6 per cent in 1969 and passed into Likud. [cite:gi4_972_twoads] [cite:gi4_posen_manifesto] [cite:gi4_wiki_mgi] Gush Emunim, founded in 1974, stated as its aim the permanent incorporation of the whole land, and its teacher, Zvi Yehuda Kook, held that "no prime minister has the authority to renounce any part of the country." [cite:gi4_lustick_gush] [cite:gi11_wiki_zvikook] [scene:gi4_movement|Part Four: the Movement]

Party and state, 1977–2022. The 1977 Likud platform stated that "between the Sea and the Jordan there will only be Israeli sovereignty." [cite:gi5_jvl_likud1977] The Knesset applied Israeli law to East Jerusalem in 1967 and entrenched it in 1980, and on 14 December 1981 applied "the Law, jurisdiction and administration of the State" to the Golan by 63 votes to 21. [cite:wb_wiki_ejannex] [cite:gi5_wiki_golanlaw] Likud's Central Committee voted unanimously in 2017 to extend sovereignty to "the areas of liberated settlement." [cite:gi5_toi_likud2017] Clause 7 of the Nation-State Law of 2018 made "Jewish settlement" a "national value" without saying where. [cite:gi5_toi_nslaw] The guidelines of the 37th government opened with "an exclusive and inalienable right to all parts of the Land of Israel," and the Likud–Religious Zionism agreement of 1 December 2022 committed the prime minister to a sovereignty policy in Judea and Samaria at a time of his choosing. [cite:gi5_jl_guidelines] [cite:gi5_cgtn_sov] [scene:gi5_nationstate|Part Five: the Basic Law and the coalition]

The annexation track, 2019–2025. Netanyahu pledged sovereignty over the Jordan Valley on 10 September 2019; the Trump plan of January 2020 assigned Israel about 30 per cent of the West Bank. [cite:gi6_ap_jv2019] [cite:gi6_crs_plan2020] Smotrich declared 2025 "the year of sovereignty," described his civil powers as a change to the system's "DNA" that would not look like annexation, and on 3 September 2025 proposed annexing 82 per cent of the West Bank on the principle of "maximum territory and minimum Arab population." [cite:gi6_aje_2025year] [cite:gi6_bg_nyt_smotrich] [cite:gi6_toi_82pct] The Knesset backed the principle by 71 to 13 and advanced a bill on all settlements by 25 to 24. [cite:wb_toi_71_13] [cite:gi6_toi_readings] [scene:gi6_votes|Part Six: the votes]

Every one of these texts names the land west of the Jordan, Gaza in the 1977 version, or the Golan. None claims territory in Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Iraq or Syria beyond the Golan. [cite:gi5_jvl_likud1977] [cite:gi5_jl_guidelines]

Patterns over time

Holding and settling where the claim applied. Settlers in the West Bank outside East Jerusalem numbered 1,500 in 1972, 4,400 in 1977, 23,700 in 1983 and 60,300 in 1987. [cite:wb_peacenow_pop] When the court closed the seizure of private land in the Elon Moreh case of 1979, about 908,000 dunams were declared state land by 1992; when sovereignty was suspended in 2020, civil powers moved to a minister in 2023 and Area C land registration began in February 2026. [cite:wb_dweikat] [cite:wb_wiki_stateland] [cite:gi6_dcpd_uae2020] [cite:gi6_fmep_feb2026] Formal sovereignty has been declared over no part of the West Bank outside East Jerusalem. [cite:wb_wiki_ejannex]

Withdrawing under pressure or by treaty elsewhere. Israel left Gaza and Sinai in March 1957 after Eisenhower's public demand; pulled back on the Suez and Golan fronts in 1974–75; completed the return of Sinai on 25 April 1982 and of Taba in 1989; left south Lebanon on 24 May 2000; and removed every Gaza settlement in 2005. [cite:rs_cw3_app_20feb57] [cite:rs_cw3_morris_gaza] [cite:gi12_jvl_syrdis] [cite:gi12_ied_sinai2] [cite:gi1_csm_sinai1982] [cite:gi8_enc_taba] [cite:hz4_crs_shebaa] [cite:gz_wiki_disengagement] Most of those steps came under pressure or as part of an exchange; Sharon called Yamit "the red line of our concessions," and his adviser called the Gaza disengagement "formaldehyde" for the peace process. [cite:gi1_csm_sinai1982] [cite:gi12_un_weisglass] [scene:gi12_withdrawals|Part Twelve: the withdrawals]

The buffer belt, 2024–2026. In Syria, Lebanon and Gaza, the first statement was temporary and later ones were not: "deployed temporarily" became "for an indefinite period"; five posts held "at the moment" became a zone kept until Hezbollah disarms; a line "frozen" pending withdrawal became, to the chief of staff, "a new border line," with the share of Gaza behind it rising from about 53 per cent to between 64 and 70 per cent. [cite:hz10s_cnn_480] [cite:hz10s_ih_hermon] [cite:hz8_national_five] [cite:hz8b_national_tampa] [cite:gi9_olj_zamir] [cite:gi9_aj_maps] [cite:gi9_unsco_jun26] No government decision has claimed sovereignty over any of the three. [cite:gi9_nahar_saar] [scene:gi9_gaza|Part Nine: the three zones]

Maps without correction. In September 2023 and September 2024 the prime minister displayed maps on which the West Bank and Gaza appeared inside Israel; no government clarification was found. [cite:gi7_jpost_map2023] [cite:gi7_aje_phil2024] [cite:gi7_ynet_germany]

What is argument

Intent beyond the stated aims. Whether Ben-Gurion's partition as "the beginning" in 1937 and his eighth point at Sèvres in 1956 were a programme or a posture; whether Labor's tolerance of the Sebastia settlers showed consent or weakness; whether Netanyahu shares the purpose Smotrich described; whether the buffer zones are perimeters or frontiers: each rests on documents on both sides, and this volume has given both. [cite:gi2_wiki_bgletter] [cite:rs_cw3_shlaim_sevres] [cite:rs_cw3_jvl_ike_bg] [cite:gi4_lustick_gush] [cite:gi6_bg_nyt_smotrich] [cite:gi9_carnegie_ring] The disputed sentence in the Amos letter carries no finding on its own. [cite:gi2_comm_morris] [cite:gi2_hnn_karsh] Netanyahu's "very much" of August 2025 documents attachment to a vision he did not define; that it reveals a policy toward Jordan, Egypt, Lebanon, Syria or Iraq is an inference. [cite:gi7_afp_i24] [cite:gi7_olj_pendant]

"From the Nile to the Euphrates" as a programme. The phrase rests on one reading of Genesis 15:18. [cite:gi1_bg_gen1518] [cite:gi1_ebg_brookegypt] Its documented carriers are a 1898 diary note never acted on, a minority of religious nationalist groups, a rabbi's answer to a UN committee in 1947 (which he narrowed, when pressed, to exclude Egypt, Syria, Lebanon, Iraq and Transjordan), a minister's remark about Jerusalem expanding "to Damascus" and an ambassador's "It would be fine if they took it all," which he called hyperbolic. [cite:gi1_pipes_calumny] [cite:gi1_wiki_greaterisrael] [cite:gi10_djn_fishman] [cite:gi11_newarab_damascus] [cite:gi11_aje_huckabee] Hamas's covenant asserts it as Zionist aim. [cite:hm_avalon_charter] No law, platform, coalition agreement or government map found for this volume adopts it.

What is myth or misread

Part Ten's ledger stands: no one has produced the Knesset inscription, which the Christian Science Monitor withdrew in 1980; the flag's stripes come from the prayer shawl; the 10-agora coin copies a coin of Mattathias Antigonus; Yinon's essay forecast fragmentation and named only Sinai as an Israeli aim; "A Clean Break" urged keeping the Golan, not taking more. [cite:gi10_csm_1980] [cite:gi10_mfa_flag] [cite:gi10_boi_coins] [cite:ir13_mb_yinon] [cite:ir13_km_cleanbreak] [scene:gi10_myths|Part Ten: the ledger] The research also corrected claims circulating on the other side and in this library's own sources: the Herzl note is authentic, not fabricated; "Third Kingdom" is not in the Knesset speech of 7 November 1956; the United States voted for Resolution 497; the Litani clause of 1919 was the mainstream's water request, not a Revisionist frontier; and Colombia joined the United States in recognising the Golan annexation in August 2026.
