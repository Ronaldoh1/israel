## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency:** pp_s01, rd2_mufti and rd2_council, including internal rivalry where the sources document it.
- **The strongest form of the Zionist case:** rd2_faisal (the agreement as Zionist evidence of Arab consent), s07 (the Mandate as international legitimacy) and rd2_council (why the Zionist side accepted the council).
- **Jewish dissent:** Montagu in s06; anti-Zionist and non-Zionist positions in us_s01 and us_s02 where the sources give them.
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** Britain's own motives (or_s01, me_s03); the State Department's opposition to Lodge–Fish (us_s02).
- **Terms examined:** "mandate", "sacred trust" and "national home" are defined from the documents (blg_s01, s07).
