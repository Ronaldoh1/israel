1. Herzl's audience with Pius X: 25 January 1904 (Jewish Virtual Library) vs 26 January 1904 (bc_s08). Prose uses 25 January.
2. François Pauly's term as CEO of the Edmond de Rothschild group: 2019–2023 (bc_s08) vs from June 2021 (Citywire, Paperjam). Prose uses June 2021, notes the other date.
3. The 1831 Rothschild contract: "exclusive right of first refusal on all Vatican sovereign debt" (bc_s03) vs notice and preference on equal terms (Wikipedia, Rothschild loans to the Holy See). Prose uses the narrower terms.
4. Banco Ambrosiano payment: US$224 million (Wikipedia, IOR) vs $244 million (earlier Book 9 notes). Prose uses $224 million.
5. Vatican City area: 44 or 49 hectares (carried from dv1).
