The vote follows the money. The United States has vetoed Security Council resolutions critical of Israel 45 times as of December 2023, slightly over half of all its vetoes since 1945, so the one UN body that can bind states has been largely closed to the question. [cite:ccga_vetoes] At home, AIPAC and its super PAC, the United Democracy Project, formed in January 2022, topped $100 million in election spending for a second cycle running in 2026; in the races studied, its advertising rarely mentioned Israel.
