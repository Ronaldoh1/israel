Part Fourteen established three things. The Christian canon was not chosen at Nicaea: that council, attended by 250 to 300 bishops by contemporary reports (318 by later tradition), whose surviving signature lists hold 200 to 220 names, settled the Arian dispute and the date of Easter, while the list of 27 New Testament books first appears in Athanasius's letter of 367, was accepted at Hippo and Carthage, and was fixed for Catholics only at Trent on 8 April 1546 by 24 votes to 15.
