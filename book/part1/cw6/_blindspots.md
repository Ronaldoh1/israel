## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency:** the PLO's founding congress and Charter in its own words (cw6_summits_plo); Fatah's own dating of its revolution (cw6_fatah); al-Ard and the Yeredor case (cw6_al_ard); Hussein–PLO rivalry. Rivalries among Arab governments (Syria vs Jordan vs Egypt) are told from documents, not assumed.
- **Strongest Zionist/Israeli case:** Israel's fear of a first strike and the cabinet's 23–27 May deliberations; Fatah raids and the three soldiers killed before Samu; Nasser's 26 May words; the defensive-democracy reasoning in Yeredor.
- **Jewish dissent:** Justice Cohn's dissent; Webber's and Kissinger's warnings come from US officials, not Jewish dissent. Jewish dissent from Zionism in 1963–67 is not researched here (gap).
- **Jews of the Arab world:** not covered in this stage (gap above).
- **Real antisemitism:** the 1964 Charter's denial of Israel's legitimacy and Nasser's "destroy Israel" statement are quoted exactly; neither is generalized to Arabs or Palestinians.
- **Palestinian citizens of Israel:** the end of military government with what stayed in force (Akevot, ISA file GL-17005/6); al-Ard.
- **Key terms examined:** "terrorism" as the CIA and U Thant used it (cw6_fatah); "inspection" vs "invited guests" (s11d); "defensive democracy" (cw6_al_ard).
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** Washington's internal split is shown (Rusk against the planes; Rostow against Samu; Komer brokering); the Soviet-warning debate is given three positions; no implied causation from NUMEC.
- **Money and alignment:** the German kits on MAP credit, the 140 million DM settlement, the $42 million tank deal, Skyhawk terms, Jordan's cash-for-F-104s "the oil-rich Arabs are footing the bill", P.L. 480 to Egypt.
- **Independent sources:** load-bearing claims rest on FRUS, UN documents and the ISA/IDF archives; GlobalSecurity, Fanack and +972 are used only for colour or as one side of a logged dispute.

## 3. Unverified (kept out of the prose)

- **NUMEC:** the National Security Archive and Foreign Policy pages cited by fis_s07a could not be fetched (permission timeouts); writers use only what the scene itself states, with its own citations.
- The date the Luxembourg reparations payments ended (1965/66): not found in the fetched sources.
- The exact inauguration date of the National Water Carrier (only "1964").
- The vote count of the 4 June 1967 decision: the IDF archive page reads "a majority of 18" and no second source was fetched; give only the wording.
- Begin's 8 August 1982 National Defense College remark on 1967 ("we decided to attack him"): the Israel MFA text could not be fetched (permission timeout) and only secondary pointers were seen; Rabin's 1968 and Peled's 1972 remarks were not found. Keep out.
