Part Seven covers the war that followed October 7, from the siege announced two days later to the ceasefire of October 2025 and the year since. It sets out the course of the fighting and the two ceasefires; the dead, the destruction and the famine, and how each was measured and disputed; and the cases brought in the international courts and the findings of UN investigators, with Israel's response to each. Much of it is recent, and the figures here are dated to the sources that give them, as of October 2026.
