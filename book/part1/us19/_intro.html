Part Nineteen compares how the two states raise, pay for and look after their armed forces. It sets Israel's conscript army and large reserve beside America's volunteer force, follows the yeshiva-student exemption to the High Court's ruling of June 2024 and the fight over enforcing it ahead of Israel's election, and measures the burden that the wars since 2023 have placed on reservists and their families. It compares the care each state gives its veterans, the American Department of Veterans Affairs and the Israeli Defence Ministry's rehabilitation system. It then traces defence spending as a share of each economy from the 1980s to 2025, and closes by asking precisely who has paid for Iron Dome and Israel's other missile defences.
