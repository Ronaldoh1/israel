Part Ten tests a question many people ask: how can a movement this small still be the reason for everything? For two years Israel named Hamas as the cause of each strike, each closure and each refusal, and as the obstacle to ending the war. This part sets the claim against the record. It compares the forces; counts the Hamas members killed, detained and charged, and the hostages rescued; asks how a state with Israel's surveillance failed to finish its enemy; checks the claims made in Hamas's name against what was later found, in both directions; and sets out the documented incentives Israel's prime minister had to keep the war going, beside the military explanations his own generals and American officials gave. Where the evidence ends and argument begins, the part says so.
