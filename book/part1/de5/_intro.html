This part tells the life of Curtis Yarvin, the software engineer who, as Mencius Moldbug, wrote the texts at the centre of this book. It separates what rests on his own account from what independent reporting carries: a childhood in a foreign-service family and three grades skipped, Brown at eighteen and a doctorate abandoned at Berkeley; a programming job at the dawn of the mobile web and a payout that bought years of reading; the blog he began in 2007 and ended in 2016; and Tlon, the company he founded in 2013 with money from Peter Thiel's and Marc Andreessen's venture firms, left in 2019 and took back as a "wartime C.E.O." in 2024. The last section follows his public years under his own name, from the newsletter he launched in 2020 to the debates, interviews and inaugural parties of 2025, names each documented contact with the type of connection it was, and tests the claim that the theorist is connected to the people who now hold power.
