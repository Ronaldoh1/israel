This volume set out the record of Hamas from the founding of the Muslim Brotherhood in 1928 to the unfinished disarmament of 2026: a movement older than Israel that took up arms against the occupation in 1987, killed civilians by bomb and rocket, won an election, seized Gaza and ruled it harshly, and was shaped by Israeli policies of separation and, from 2018, by Qatari money Israel let in.
