Part Five followed the Oslo years. The Interim Agreement of 1995 divided the West Bank into Areas A, B and C, leaving about 72 to 74 percent under full Israeli control as a temporary step; the handovers stalled, and by 2013 Area C still covered about 63 percent, while the Palestinian areas formed 165 separate enclaves. [cite:wb_wiki_oslo2areas] In Area C, less than one percent of the land had an approved Palestinian plan, 2 percent of permit requests were granted, and more than 14,000 demolition orders were issued. [cite:wb_ocha_areac] The settler population outside East Jerusalem grew from 116,300 to 198,300 during the peace process. [cite:wb_peacenow_pop] After the Hebron massacre of 1994 it was Palestinians who were barred from the city's main street, and a government report found the state had helped fund 105 unauthorized outposts.
