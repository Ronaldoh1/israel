The three bodies are each tied to the pope, and each tie runs from the pope downward. The Jesuits vow obedience to the pope "with regard to missions"; a pope abolished the order in 1773, another restored it in 1814, and John Paul II overrode its leadership in 1981; it has shrunk from 36,038 members in 1965 to 13,768 in 2025, and its first pope, Francis, was succeeded by an Augustinian. [cite:vt8_od_members] [cite:vt8_va_charisma] [cite:vt8_ncr_leo_statutes] [cite:vt8_lcmdp_2026] The Order of Malta is a sovereign without territory, recognised by 118 countries by its own count and a UN General Assembly observer since 1994, yet as a religious order it "depends … on the Holy See", as Francis's removal of its Grand Master in 2017 and his imposed constitution of 2022 showed.
