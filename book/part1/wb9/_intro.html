Part Nine covers the West Bank since 7 October 2023, while the world's attention was on Gaza. Two things happened at once. The violence rose: the UN counted 1,001 Palestinians killed in the West Bank in two years, 43 percent of all those killed there in twenty years, [cite:wb_ohchr_1001] Israelis were killed in attacks, and in 2025 the army emptied three refugee camps in the north, in what researchers describe as the largest displacement there since 1967. [cite:wb_wiki_ironwall] And the settlement project moved faster than at any time since it began, with record approvals, new settlements, the E1 plan and votes in the Knesset to annex. This part sets out both, with the figures of the counting bodies, Israel's stated reasons, and where things stood in October 2026.
