Part Twelve follows the theology into government between 2017 and 2026. It sets out the decisions most often credited to Christian Zionist influence in the first Trump administration (the recognition of Jerusalem and the embassy's move, the recognition of Israeli sovereignty over the Golan Heights, and the 2019 statement on West Bank settlements), the reasons the officials gave for them, and the Latin American governments that followed or declined to follow. It then takes the officials one by one, from Vice President Mike Pence and Secretary of State Mike Pompeo to Ambassador Mike Huckabee, Speaker Mike Johnson, Defence Secretary Pete Hegseth and Senator Ted Cruz, and records what each said, in his own words, about faith and Israel. The question throughout is narrow: what did office-holders say and do, and what does that show, and not show, about the theology's effect on policy. Statements and statuses are given as of October 2026.
