## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency.** Shukairy's UN statement of 14 November 1963, in his own terms, with the US reply (cw5_shukairy_un). The Arab Federation proclamation of 17 April 1963 is given as the Arab governments' stated aim (s11b). The Arab League and Fatah material for 1963 is a gap, queued.
- **The strongest form of the Zionist and Israeli case.**
  - Ben-Gurion's 12 May 1963 letter ("another Holocaust", the request for a guarantee, arms parity) is stated in its own terms (s11b).
  - The domestic-lobby case is stated in its own terms: Neumann's "vindication", Barda's "absolute" separation, Kenen's affidavit and speech, and the domestic-funding defence (cw5_jewish_agency_hearing).
- **Jewish dissent.** The American Council for Judaism and the 1964 "peoplehood" exchange (cw5_dissent). The Council welcomed the 1962 order, per Smith.
- **Real antisemitism, named plainly.** Barda documents that claims tying AIPAC to the assassination circulate, and calls them baseless (s11c). Under the ruling below, the book treats them as claimed and unsupported, and never generalizes from them to a people.
- **Terms examined.**
  - "Foreign agent" under FARA: a disclosure requirement, not a finding of disloyalty or a crime (s11a; cw5_fulbright_inquiry).
  - "Ultimatum": the letters named no sanction (s11b).
  - "Special relationship": Kennedy's own phrase, with his stated limit (cw5_palm_beach).
- **Convergence, not conspiracy.** Each section names which part of the US government acted and who disagreed inside it:
  - the embassy against the estimates;
  - State's draft schedules against Komer's "opening bid";
  - the Justice Department against the officials the Wall Street Journal reported weighing Jewish opinion.
- **Money, and where it did not follow alignment.** The Jewish Agency's payments to the AZC are disputed in size ($5.1M against $1.5M), so both figures appear. Kennedy armed Israel (the Hawk loan) while pressing hardest on Dimona.
- **Sources kept independent.** No load-bearing FARA claim rests on Smith alone. His details are marked *reported* and paired with Barda's opposing reading and the contemporary JTA reports.
