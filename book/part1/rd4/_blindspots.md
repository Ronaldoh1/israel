## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency, rivalries and choices.**
  - s07a5: the committee's cross-party founding, the strike's own committees, the Nashashibi split and the peace bands, and al-Husseini's direction of the revolt from exile.
  - rd4_woodhead: the named Palestinian delegation in London.
  - rd4_white_paper: Arab relief and the formal rejection, side by side.
  - rd4_mufti_war: al-Husseini's Axis alliance as one man's record. Its fuller treatment is queued.
- **The strongest Zionist case.**
  - rd4_white_paper: the Jewish Agency's legal argument under Articles 2, 6 and 15.
  - ev_s01: the closed doors.
  - s07b2 and ab_s01: the ships.
  - s07b: why Biltmore asked for a state.
- **Jewish dissent.**
  - Rabbi Lazaron's testimony (us_s05).
  - Wise against Bergson, and the mainstream's denunciation of Lehi (bg_s01, lh_s01).
  - Biltmore's internal contest (s07b).
  - Ihud and Arendt by link to s00b3. The American Council for Judaism is queued.
- **Jews of the Arab world.** The Farhud (rd4_mufti_war, drawing irq_e03), with Volume I's s00e2 linked.
- **Real antisemitism named plainly.** Kristallnacht, Wannsee, six million, Évian's 'No one wants them', and al-Husseini's alliance.
- **Terms examined.**
  - 'transfer' and 'exchange of population' (pl_s01; rd4_labour's 1944 resolution)
  - 'commonwealth' against 'national home' (s07b)
  - 'terrorism': Irgun and Lehi attacks and rebel attacks are named by act, on both sides (s07a5, lh_s01)
  - 'foreign agent' (tv_s01, fa_s01)
- **Convergence, not conspiracy.**
  - Inside the US government: War, State and the White House disagreed (us_s05, s07c2).
  - Inside the British government: Churchill and Labour against the White Paper (rd4_white_paper).
  - Inside the movement: Weizmann against Ben-Gurion, Silver against Wise, the mainstream against Bergson.
- **Money.**
  - Kofer HaYishuv and the 1939 defense line (br_s01, via dashboard).
  - The United Jewish Appeal's wartime giving (s07f, via dashboard).
  - The Lateran settlement as context, not motive (vat_s00b).
  - In the American political campaign the record shows organization, not purchase (azc_s02's own finding).
- **Independent sources.**
  - The revolt toll rests on several independent sources, and the scene's outlier figure is flagged.
  - The Farhud counts differ between USHMM and s00e2.
  - Cia_s01's interpretive claims ('deliberate tolerance') are briefed to be attributed, not stated as fact.
