## 0. Classification and guardrails (Sophia protocol)

- **Subject type:** COMPOSITE / MACRO-SYSTEM. Build it as a linked series, not one flat module. "The Rothschilds" is not one entity. It is a family across eight-plus generations, five original banking houses, several separate modern firms that compete with each other (Rothschild & Co; Edmond de Rothschild Group; RIT Capital Partners; E.L. Rothschild LLC), plus foundations. Each component gets its own single-type module:
  - the historical banking house: SYSTEM-grade INSTITUTION;
  - each modern firm: INSTITUTION;
  - named individuals: PERSON, with the family-conflation guardrail;
  - "Rothschild control" as a belief: NARRATIVE / MYTH.
- **Financial-power-structure guardrail (2B.2) applies at full strength.** "Who really controls the money" has carried antisemitic banking conspiracy theory for nearly two centuries. The Rothschild name is its single most-used vehicle. Rules:
  - Document facts and conspiratorial inferences are scored separately.
  - No conspiracy-coded language: "cabal", "pulls the strings", "layer above governments", "who owns our media".
  - Run the slide test on every scene. Is the claim about a named person or a named firm's documented act, or has it slid to "the family", "the bankers", or Jews as a group?
  - Network ties come from documents only. Never from surname, marriage, religion or ethnicity. One Rothschild's act is not another's (family guardrail, 2B.14 §1).
- **Zionism ≠ Judaism; a Rothschild ≠ "the Rothschilds" ≠ Jews.** These are the analytical prerequisites. Getting them wrong produces bad analysis in either political direction.

## 5. NOT ESTABLISHED / REJECTED, and why

- **"Epstein operated as the Rothschild family's financial representative."** The record shows one contract with one bank (EdR) and one family branch (Ariane), plus introductions in Lynn Forester's circle. Nothing ties Rothschild & Co, the British branch or "the family" to Epstein's business. Epstein's own "I represent the Rothschilds" is a claim he made, not proof.
- **"A layer of power above governments… Rothschilds, Rockefellers, Warburgs, Schiffs… who owns our media."** This is the classic trope in its standard wording, not a finding. No document supports it. Excluded.
- **"All roads lead to Israel" / Israeli-intelligence verdict (Overlords grade "A+").**
  - The documents show private business: Barak's Carbyne loan, a political donation and surveillance brokering.
  - The grading turns documented money ($1.04M) plus association into an intelligence conclusion.
  - The article itself concedes: "No formal tasking document or agency payment has been found."
  - Keep the documented spine. The verdict is an inference, not a finding.
- **"Mega Group = espionage vehicle."** The Mega Group's existence and membership are documented (WSJ 1998). The espionage characterisation is one former NSA officer's 2024 assertion. Treat it as a lead at most. Never link members by shared religion or ethnicity.
- **"Robert Maxwell's intelligence world → Ghislaine's operational control."** Maxwell's funeral and burial are documented. That he was an intelligence asset is contested and unproven. Transferring that to his daughter is family-conflation, which is barred.
- **Death-footage "edited" claims.**
  - Documented: Wired / Hany Farid metadata analysis (July 2025): Premiere Pro, at least two source clips, "not raw".
  - Not documented: "Someone edited the footage to hide something".
  - Already handled in ep11.
- **"Rothschild in every scandal / the same fingerprints."** Pattern-by-surname is exactly what the slide test exists to stop.
