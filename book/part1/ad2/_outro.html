The promise holds in documented and specific ways. Real median household income reached $87,460 in 2025, the highest on record, and the American median disposable income ranked second in the OECD in 2021; official poverty among full-time, year-round workers was 1.6 per cent. [cite:us14_census_story2025] [cite:ad2_oecd_median] [cite:us18_crs_pov2025] Children of immigrants have out-climbed children of American-born parents for more than a century, Hispanic Americans move up strongly, and the earnings gap between white and Black children from low-income families narrowed by 30 per cent between the 1978 and 1992 birth cohorts. [cite:ad2_abramitzky_aer] [cite:ad2_oi_race_hisp] [cite:ad2_changing_opp] For children born around 1940, nine in ten out-earned their parents. [cite:ad2_chetty_fading] The same record sets the limits: the top gained about twice as much as the middle since 1967, the GI Bill widened the racial gap in the South, and the share of children out-earning their parents fell to about half for those born in the 1980s (60 to 68 per cent on alternative measures), while the odds of climbing from the bottom fifth to the top stayed near 8 or 9 per cent. [cite:us14_census_p60] [cite:ad2_turner_bound] [cite:ad2_reeves_winship] [cite:ad2_chetty_trends] Which of those figures a reader trusts depends on the measure, and the measures are now defined.
