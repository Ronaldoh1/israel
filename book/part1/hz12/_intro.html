Part Twelve turns from the region to the United States. Ron's thesis holds that the groups in this book serve, among other things, as the reason the United States is drawn into Israel's wars. This part counts what that involvement has cost Americans, in lives and in money, from the bombing of the Marine barracks in Beirut in 1983 to the war with Iran that began on 28 February 2026, and separates the deaths these groups caused from the costs the United States took on by choosing to fight, deploy and defend. It then follows the two wars with Iran of 2025 and 2026: who argued for them, who decided, what the administration said they were for, what its critics said, how Congress voted, and what the public thought.
