Part Three examines the object at the centre of the Solomonic legend: the Ark of the Covenant, which the Church of Our Lady Mary of Zion in Aksum says it holds. It sets out the rules of access as the record describes them, a single guardian monk appointed for life by his predecessor, tabots hidden from all but priests, women barred from the old church, and weighs two readings of the same arrangement: reverence for the holy, and protection of the office that guards it.
