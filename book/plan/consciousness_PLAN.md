# Consciousness: the section map (21 Nov 2026)

**Section:** Consciousness · **Tagline:** "The knowledge they buried, burned and banned"

**Shelf:** after The Dark Enlightenment Architecture. 14 titles coming soon (13 books, 1 booklet), consolidated from an earlier 59-title draft so that related topics share one book. The Vatican Architecture is written and is shelved here as well as in The Israel Architecture (still Volume XI there).

**How it's built:** the catalogue is `book/plan/consciousness_catalogue.json` (titles, kind, labels, emblem, colour, and the topics each book includes). `tools/patch_lib_consciousness.py` puts the section into `tools/book_block.html`; re-running it after editing the JSON refreshes the shelf. Then `embed_book.py`, `build_dist.py`, `build_israel_web.py`. Library search also matches the included topics, so searching "Enoch" finds The Ethiopian Bible.

**Sources to draw on:** Sophia's Reignite pillar (Ascension of Consciousness; The Suppressed Lineages; The Forbidden Arts; the Rosicrucians module), The Vatican Architecture Book Two, and the institutional-religion files in the project.

**Discipline:** documents and their history first; esoteric readings marked as readings; name institutions, not peoples.
