## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency:** the All-Palestine Government and the rival Hashemite congresses (rd6_arab_palestine), the Arab delegations' positions at Lausanne (lau_s01), Arab citizens' first vote (rd6_military_government), the refugees' own crossings as return rather than raiding (rd6_return_barred). Shlaim's verdict that Arab states made the Gaza government a "phantom" is given, not hidden.
- **The strongest Zionist/Israeli case:** Israel's Lausanne sequence (peace first, then refugees), its reading of Resolution 194, the security case for military government and for retaliation, the fedayeen, Ben-Gurion's case for the Law of Return, Za'im's instability as a reason to decline, and the reparations as justice for the murdered.
- **Jewish dissent:** the Einstein–Arendt letter (np_s01); Sharett's moderates against Ben-Gurion's activists (rd6_return_barred); Begin's and others' opposition to reparations (rd6_financing); the Communist vote against the Jerusalem declaration; Orthodox, Reform and non-Zionist positions on the Law of Return.
- **Jews of the Arab world and Iran:** rd6_exodus, rd6_ingathering and mz_s02, with real antisemitism named where documented (Iraq's 1948 law, Ades, Oujda and Jérada, Cairo 1948, Egypt 1956) and the disputed Baghdad bombings kept disputed.
- **Palestinian citizens of Israel:** rd6_military_government, ap_s01 (present absentees), rd6_law_of_return (the residence test of 1952).
- **Terms examined:** "absentee", "infiltrator", "armistice line" (not a border, by its own text), "return" (in Resolution 194 vs the Law of Return), "refugee" (UNRWA vs the 1951 Convention).
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** inside the US government, Marshall and State against Clifford (tr_s02); in Israel, Sharett against Ben-Gurion; among Arab states, Egypt against Abdullah (rd6_arab_palestine).
- **Money followed alignment, and where it did not:** the Feinberg case at its documented size; US aid economic only and modest until the 1960s; reparations opposed by part of Israel's own public; bonds sold to diaspora buyers.
- **Sources kept independent:** the refugee count is given from Israeli, UN, British and Arab figures; land figures from BADIL and Adalah are attributed and set beside scholarship; no load-bearing claim rests on one advocate.

## 3. The reader's questions

Each writer lists these per section and answers them in the prose. They are checked in the audit. Questions this chapter must not leave hanging: Why did the refugees not go home after the war? Was the armistice line a border? Who owned the land afterwards, and how? Did the Arabs who stayed become citizens, and on what terms? Why did the Jews of Iraq, Yemen, Morocco and Egypt leave? How did a state of 870,000 people absorb 700,000 newcomers? Who paid?

## 5. Contradictions between scenes, found while planning (for SOURCE-CONFLICTS.md)

- **AIPAC's origin:** s07g says the AZC's lobbying functions were spun off into AIPAC in 1963; s11 says the American Zionist Committee for Public Affairs was founded in 1953–54 by Isaiah Kenen and renamed AIPAC in 1959.
- **First US military aid:** iaa_s01 says 1962; the CRS table (rs_rd6_jvl_usaid_table) shows a $0.4 million military loan in 1959.
- **Recognition of the 1950 annexation:** ijr_src_s05 says Britain, Iraq and Pakistan; the Library of Congress study says Britain and Pakistan.
- **Yemeni airlift:** ye_s01 says about 49,000; the Jewish Agency says nearly all of 45,000; the State Archives table gives 48,375 for Yemen and Aden (1948–52).
- **Iraqi emigration:** irq_e05 gives roughly 135,000 reduced to about 6,000; the Jewish Agency gives 121,512 of 130,000; the State Archives table gives 124,226 (1948–52); Mendes gives 120,000 in 1951.
- **Military government start:** mg_s01 and ap_s01 date it from 1949 and 1948 respectively; Korn dates its official establishment to October 1948.
- **Jewish exodus total:** me_s07 gives 800,000–900,000 (1948–1970s, including Iran); Mendes gives "nearly one million" from Arab countries in the decade after 1948.
- **Law of Return date:** 5 July 1950 (the law's own date) vs 3 July 1950 (Encyclopaedia Judaica).
