Part Six describes the physical system that, since 2000, has determined where Palestinians in the West Bank can go: a barrier whose route runs mostly inside the territory, a network of checkpoints, gates and roadblocks that by the end of 2025 numbered more than nine hundred, and roads built across Palestinian land that Palestinians have been barred from using. It sets out Israel's stated reason, security, beside each part of the system, and the judgment of the International Court of Justice on the barrier.
