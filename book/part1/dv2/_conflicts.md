# Part Two (London): source conflicts

Status as of 18 October 2026.

- First oil at Haifa: 14 October 1934 vs 16 October 1934 (dv2_wiki_kirkuk_haifa vs rs_rd3_gulbenkian_pipeline). Both recorded in dv2_mandate_plumbing; not resolved.
- Mayor of London in 1215: accounts differ on whether William Hardell or Serlo le Mercer held the office when Magna Carta was sealed (dv2_wiki_magna_carta, dv2_col_mayoralty). Both recorded in dv2_square_mile.
- The Remembrancer's place in the Commons: library bc_s01 and bc_s06 said he sits on the floor, "the only non-member permitted" there; the Remembrancer sources (dv2_wiki_remembrancer, dv2_wanderer_remembrancer) say he watches from the under-gallery by the Speaker's permission, with no access beyond the bar. bc_s01 and bc_s06 corrected October 2026.
- Business vote size: about 24,000 (2009, reference summary) vs some 32,000 business against about 9,000 resident votes (2013, Green Party, a campaigner's figures) (dv2_how_it_governs).
- Tax-haven measures: Tax Justice Network indices (2020, 2024) vs HMRC's reply on Crown Dependency ownership registers (dv2_money_machine).
