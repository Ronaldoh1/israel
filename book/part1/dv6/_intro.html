The volume began with a claim stated as its author holds it: that three cities which do not run like ordinary cities raised a fourth sovereign, the State of Israel. Five parts have tested it. Part One measured what each city is and set them against cities that are ordinary. Parts Two, Three and Four applied the same parent test to London, the Vatican and Washington, each ending with a dated verdict. Part Five examined the child itself. This last part puts the pieces together: the three verdicts side by side, the question of whether the three acted together, the claims that remain outside the record, and what the reader can take from the whole.
