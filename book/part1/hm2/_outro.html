Part Two set out the founding. Hamas was founded in December 1987 by Ahmed Yassin and six others, in the first weeks of the uprising against the occupation, and carried out its first attack in 1989. [cite:hm_wsj_spawn] Its 1988 charter claimed all of Palestine, rejected any solution but jihad, and repeated antisemitic conspiracy claims; its 2017 document spoke of a conflict with Zionism, not Jews, and accepted a 1967 state without recognizing Israel, which Israel dismissed as an attempt to fool the world.
