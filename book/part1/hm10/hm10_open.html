What Is Contested

Whether Israel "created" Hamas. Israeli officials who served in Gaza disagree: one called Hamas "Israel's creation", another said Israel never financed or armed it. The record shows licensing and tolerance of a charity network, not the creation of an armed movement. [cite:hm_wsj_spawn] [cite:hm_intercept_2018]

Why the Qatari money flowed. Smotrich's 2015 statement and the reported 2019 Netanyahu remark tie it to keeping the Palestinians divided; Netanyahu calls that "a big lie" and says the money prevented a humanitarian collapse. [cite:wb_wiki_smotrich] [cite:gz_harkov_2019] [cite:hm_ynet_biglie]

What happened in June 2007. Fatah calls it a Hamas coup; Vanity Fair reported a US plan to remove Hamas by force, which the State Department called "absurd". [cite:hm_dn_rose] [cite:gz_wiki_battle2007]

Whether the 2017 document changed Hamas. Scholars such as Khaled Hroub read it as a real shift; Israel called it an attempt to fool the world, and the 1988 charter was never revoked. [cite:hm_jps_hroub2017] [cite:hm_cnn_2017doc]
