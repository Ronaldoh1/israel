This book set out to document the Epstein network from the record rather than from rumour, and to hold every claim to the same standard whoever it touched. The record shows a broker who sat where money, power and abuse met; institutions that, one after another, stopped short; a legal structure that protected his helpers and still does; and a release that exposed survivors while withholding half the files. It does not show a secret controller, an intelligence handler or a pattern of killing. It shows something more ordinary and more durable: a structure that protects the people close to those who hold its keys. That structure outlived him. The documents that would show how far it reaches are named in this book, and they are held by people who could release them tomorrow.

The survivors broke this case open, together with a few people at the edges of the system who refused to let it close. This book is dedicated to them.
