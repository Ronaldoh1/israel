The Gulf Money, 2021–2026

Affinity Partners

After leaving the White House, Jared Kushner founded Affinity Partners in 2021. Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund committed $2 billion after its own screening panel cited his lack of investing experience and concerns about the fund's terms; according to documents reported in the American press, the fund's board, chaired by the crown prince, overruled the panel. Qatari and Emirati sovereign money followed. [cite:washpo_kushner_pif] [cite:wiki_affinity_partners] In February 2026 Kushner became a formal US special envoy on Gaza while still running the fund, whose Gulf backers are expected to help pay for Gaza's reconstruction; no specific Affinity investment in Gaza has been documented. [cite:npr_gaza_masterplan] [cite:wiki_affinity_partners] Kushner has said his public and business roles comply with the law.

The family business

The Trump Organization has licensed its name for a Trump Tower in Dubai, another in Jeddah, projects in Riyadh and a development in Oman, mostly with Dar Global, a subsidiary of the Saudi developer Al Arkan, and Qatari Diar, owned by Qatar's sovereign fund, is a partner in a golf development near Doha. [cite:ap_trump_mideast_business] In May 2025 the administration accepted a Boeing 747-8 valued at about $400 million from Qatar's royal family, to serve as Air Force One and later pass to Trump's presidential library; legal experts raised the Constitution's emoluments clause, and the White House said the gift complied with the law. [cite:factcheck_qatar_jet]

The stablecoin

Days before the 2025 inauguration, an entity tied to Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed, the UAE's national security adviser, agreed to buy 49 percent of the family's cryptocurrency venture, World Liberty Financial, for $500 million; in May 2025 MGX, an Abu Dhabi state-backed fund he chairs, used World Liberty's USD1 stablecoin for a $2 billion investment in the exchange Binance. [cite:wiki_wlf] Senators wrote to the president that the administration then moved to loosen rules on exporting advanced chips to the UAE. [cite:senate_banking_wlf]

No public document shows that any of these payments changed a specific decision about Israel or the Palestinians. The conflicts themselves, the same men negotiating with governments that pay their businesses, are documented.
