A Map of the Record

The companion network map holds 570 nodes and 1,095 ties as of 20 November 2026. Of the ties, 834 are verified, meaning the cited page states the act directly, and 165 are reported, stated indirectly or in someone else's account. A further 51 rest on a single source and are marked as still to be checked; they count for nothing until a second source confirms them. Eighteen are recorded as not established, among them every claim that Epstein worked for an intelligence service, whichever state the claim names.

A tie is an act between two parties: a deed, a contract, a payment, an appointment, a filing, sworn testimony. Appearing in the files, in a contact book or in a photograph is not a tie and is never presented as wrongdoing. Family members are separate nodes, and nothing passes between spouses, relatives or partners without a documented joint act.

In November 2026 the map was widened in a series of passes, each searching one pathway worldwide with the same sources for every subject: the properties and who sold and bought them; the legal architecture, from the Palm Beach work release to the sex-offender registries; the banks and the money, including the bulk transaction records the Senate Finance Committee described; aviation, customs and immigration; recruitment through modelling agencies in Latin America and the Baltics; universities and paid press; governments country by country; the people who fought the case; what happened after 2019; and documented comparison cases. Where a search found nothing, that is recorded too: no other island owned or sought is documented, and Germany, Japan, Turkey and Ireland had no official proceeding on file.
