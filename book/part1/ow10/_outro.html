Part Ten found that Israeli-founded surveillance firms reach the American state along two different roads. Firms whose ownership or operations moved substantially outside Israel sell to American agencies under contracts that are public in outline: Cellebrite to ICE, CBP and the military police; Elbit's subsidiary, renamed Twenty-Six Defense in September 2026, to CBP on the Mexican border; Paragon to ICE under a contract signed before, and revived after, its purchase by AE Industrial Partners; Carbyne to local 911 centres before and after its sale to Axon.
