Delayed Choice, Bell and the Prize of 2022

The choice made late

In 1978 the American physicist John Wheeler proposed a variant of the double-slit experiment in which the decision whether to measure the photon's path, or to let its possibilities interfere, is made only after the photon has passed the slits. [cite:ob_wiki_delayed] The experiment was realised in 2007 by a French team led by Vincent Jacques, and the photons behaved according to the measurement chosen at the end, as quantum mechanics predicts. [cite:ob_wiki_delayed]

The result is often described as the present changing the past. Wheeler did not describe it that way. What the experiment rules out is the picture in which the photon is a little wave or a little particle all along and decides which to be at the slits; Anton Zeilinger's group and others read the results as ruling out that naive realist picture, not as signals sent backwards in time. [cite:ob_wiki_delayed] In some interpretations, such as Bohm's, the photon's past is never altered at all. [cite:ob_wiki_delayed] In every case the choice is made by apparatus, often by a random number generator; no one's attention is part of the experiment.

The prize for entanglement

The Nobel Prize in Physics for 2022 went to Alain Aspect, John Clauser and Anton Zeilinger "for experiments with entangled photons, establishing the violation of Bell inequalities and pioneering quantum information science". [cite:ob_nobel_2022] Their experiments showed that two particles created together can share correlations that no local account, in which each particle carries its own hidden instructions, can explain. It is one of the most important results in modern physics, and it is often cited as proof that everything is connected and that consciousness is part of it.

The prize citation says nothing about consciousness, and the experiments do not need it. The measurements are made by detectors and recorded by machines; the strangeness lies in the correlations, not in who reads them. [cite:ob_nobel_2022] Entanglement is also the reason decoherence happens: the same connection that the prize honours is what spreads a particle's record into its surroundings and makes the everyday world look settled. [cite:ob_sep_decoherence]
