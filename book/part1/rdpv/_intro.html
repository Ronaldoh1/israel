The six stages of this book follow the road that built a state. This part walks the same road from the fields beside it, through the eyes of the people who already lived there: the Arabs of Palestine, Muslim and Christian, who watched the first colonies planted under Ottoman rule, were promised independence and then left out of another promise, asked for an elected government and were refused, saw land sold from under their tenants, rose in revolt, and in 1948 lost most of their homes. It is told from their own documents where the record keeps them, and it keeps the same rules as the rest of the book: their leaders' failures and the violence some of them did are part of their record too.
