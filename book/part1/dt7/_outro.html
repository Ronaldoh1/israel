Part Seven established that every remedy on offer has been tried somewhere and that the evidence for each is real but modest. New York City's eight-to-one match brought more small donors from more neighbourhoods, Seattle's vouchers drew users more representative than cash donors, and independent commissions are associated with more competitive House races; but presidential public funding has fallen out of use, top-two primaries have had an inconsistent effect on moderation, and Oregon's automatic registration added voters in the tens of thousands, not millions.
