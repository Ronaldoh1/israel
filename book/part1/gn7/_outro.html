The Gnostic outlook outlived its first schools in two religions. The Manichaeans were organised into Elect and Hearers, counted Augustine among their Hearers for nine years and were ordered burned with their books by Diocletian in 302. [cite:vt24_wiki_manich] The Mandaeans, revering John the Baptist, survive today, but about ninety per cent of Iraq's 60,000–70,000 Mandaeans were killed or fled after 2003. [cite:gn_wiki_mandaeans] In the modern West the revival ran from Doinel's church of 1890 through Jung to Pagels's bestseller, while scholars showed that a single "Gnosticism" is a construction whether admired or condemned.
