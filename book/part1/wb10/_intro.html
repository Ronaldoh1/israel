Part Ten turns to the law. It is often said that nobody does anything about the settlements. The record shows something more specific: a rule written in 1949, a series of rulings and resolutions that have applied it to the West Bank, and a set of reasons, each documented, why none of them has been enforced. This part sets out the rule, Israel's legal position and how it has changed, the shifting positions of the United States, the rulings of the UN Security Council, the International Court of Justice, the General Assembly and the International Criminal Court, and, for each, what happened next.
