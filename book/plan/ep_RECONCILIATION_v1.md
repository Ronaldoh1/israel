## D. Corrections suggested *to the master document itself*

1. **§0 and §5, the CVRA case:** the Eleventh Circuit's en banc decision in *In re Wild* came in **April 2021**, not 2020; the 2020 decision was the panel's. *Check the citation before publishing.*
2. **§0 and §7.3, Acosta "denied under oath":** the committee calls it a *transcribed interview*. The book should say "denied in a transcribed interview" unless the transcript shows an oath. (Lying to Congress is a crime either way.)
3. **Hillary network map, Era 5:** Epstein's donations are listed as "Bill's tie, not Hillary's". The **$20,000 in 1999 went to her own joint fundraising committee**, so it belongs on her map as an *institutional* tie: money to her campaign, no contact shown.
