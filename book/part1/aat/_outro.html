From FY1949 to FY2026 the United States government gave or lent Israel about $186.5 billion in the dollars of each year, about $425 billion in October 2026 dollars. Of that, about $12.8 billion was loans that Israel has repaid, leaving about $356 billion in grants in today's dollars; seven dollars in ten went to military financing, missile defence and weapons. More than half of the whole, measured in today's dollars, was given between 1974 and 1998.
