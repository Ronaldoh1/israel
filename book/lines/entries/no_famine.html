Famine in Gaza was formally declared by the body that exists to make that call. On August 22, 2025, the UN-backed Integrated Food Security Phase Classification (IPC) declared famine in Gaza Governorate, the first ever declared in the Middle East and only the fifth since the system was created in 2008. A declaration requires three thresholds to be breached at once: extreme food deprivation, acute malnutrition and starvation deaths. [cite:ipc_famine_aug2025] The IPC called it "entirely man-made." [cite:gzr_ipc_famine_wiki] WHO had recorded 206 starvation deaths in 2025 by July, and 12,000 children were identified as acutely malnourished in July alone. [cite:gzr_who_famine]

The famine followed more than 60 days in spring 2025 when all humanitarian and commercial supplies were blocked from entering. [cite:ipc_may2025_blockade] Some data points have been challenged: the Times of Israel disputed parts of the IPC's data, though not the existence of severe food insecurity, and an earlier famine claim was revised down. January 2026 brought a brief period when aid met basic food needs.
