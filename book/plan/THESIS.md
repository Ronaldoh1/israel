# The thesis of The Israel Architecture

Stated in full in Volume XXVI, The Case. Every Israel-shelf volume opens with a passage ("In the case this library makes") naming the link it carries.

## The seven links
1. **A secular movement chose the Holy Land.** Basel 1897; Herzl and Ben-Gurion secular; Orthodox opposition; every other homeland 0–2% on the map. (Road to 1948, Divine 3+1)
2. **An empire, a bank and a superpower carried it.** Balfour to Walter Rothschild; Edmond's colonies; map: Rothschilds 34%, Britain 36%, US 24%, both 5%, finance houses 0%. (Road to 1948, Same Machine, Cold War)
3. **A shared country was proposed and refused.** Old Yishuv; Brit Shalom, Ihud, Magnes, Buber, Arendt; Herzl's "spirit the penniless population"; Za'im offer declined. (Baseline, Road to 1948)
4. **Founded and held by force.** No armed groups = 0%; Plan Dalet, Deir Yassin, Lydda; 700–750k displaced; Absentees' law; military rule; Kafr Qasim; occupation and settlement. Map: no expulsions = 93%, so the expulsions decided who would live in the state, not whether it existed. (Road to 1948, West Bank, Gaza)
5. **Scripture and persecution as shield.** Christian Zionism; Amalek; IHRA in US enforcement. (Theology Shield, Information War, Netanyahu)
6. **American money, arms and votes sustain it.** ~$298bn aid; $3.8bn/yr; $21.7bn 2023–25; 45 vetoes; $100m+ a cycle. (Aid and Arms, Mirror, Money, Washington, US vs Israel)
7. **The wars and accords follow from it.** Refugees, occupation, Hamas, Hezbollah, Oslo: downstream. Accords: downstream, driven also by Iran and US rewards. Iran: partly (1953 coup). Region's borders: came first. (1967–Oslo, Hamas, Hezbollah, Iran, Wider Region)
Plus the present: Gaza toll, famine, ICC warrants, ICJ case. (Gaza, Press, Street Questions)

## Lines the case does not cross
- Institutions, not a people. Zionism ≠ Judaism.
- No questioning of who is a Jew (Khazar claim fails).
- Other sides' violence stays on the record (Hebron 1929, 1936, Hadassah convoy, 7 October).
- Not "no Israeli leader is religious": the shield is the political use of faith.
- Not "every regional war": borders, oil and 1953 came first.
- Counter-evidence inside the volumes is kept, not cut.
