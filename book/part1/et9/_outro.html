The Ethiopian Bible kept the books that carry the inner reading furthest: Enoch's ascent through houses of crystal and fire to the throne, the Watchers whose descent brought knowledge without wisdom, the heavenly tablets and their true year, and an ark that no one needs to see. [cite:et_cojs_enoch14] [cite:vt27_ccel_enoch1] [cite:vt27_je_jubilees] [cite:et_esv_jer316] The prophet who said the ark would not be remembered also said the covenant would be written on the heart. [cite:et_esv_jer3133] The reading is the reader's; the books are Ethiopia's gift. The next book on this shelf goes to the caves where Enoch's oldest fragments were found: the Dead Sea Scrolls.
