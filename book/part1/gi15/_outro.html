Part Fourteen established that Israel's operations beyond Gaza, the West Bank and Lebanon have been air, missile and raiding campaigns with stated reasons about weapons, armed groups and attacks on Israel, and that their tolls can be counted only through sources with limits that must be stated. In Syria, Airwars' provisional count of civilians alleged killed by Israeli strikes since 2013 is 60 to 108, and the deadliest week in the south, at Sweida in July 2025, was mostly Syrians killing Syrians, by SOHR's count.
