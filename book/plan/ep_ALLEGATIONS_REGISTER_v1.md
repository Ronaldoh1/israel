# The Allegations Register: Who Survivors Have Accused, and What Happened
### Research file v1.0 for *The Epstein Architecture* · status as of 31 October 2026

Ron's request: *list the men the victims have accused.*

**The rules for this register (non-negotiable, and printed with it in the book):**
1. **An allegation enters only if it is on the record**:
   - a court filing;
   - sworn testimony or a deposition;
   - a government memo;
   - a survivor's own published account under her own name.

   Rumour, social media, "the list", and secondary compilations are **out**.
2. **Every allegation is printed with the man's response and the legal outcome**, at the same size and in the same row.
3. **An allegation is not a finding.** Of the men below, none except Epstein and Maxwell has been charged or found liable for these acts.
4. **Survivors are named only if they have named themselves publicly.** Jane Does stay Jane Does.
5. **Unnamed men stay unnamed.** Where a survivor described someone without naming him, the book does not guess. Guessing is how innocent people get named.
6. **The same standard applies to every party.** A man's politics does not move him between tiers.

## The survivors' own list
- **3 Sept 2025:** at a press conference with members of Congress, survivors said they would compile the names themselves. Lisa Phillips said: "We will confidentially compile the names we all know are regularly in the Epstein world."
- **Status:** **not released publicly** as of 31 Oct 2026, to this check.
- **Why it is confidential:** defamation law (see `ep_LEGAL_ARCHITECTURE`, D4). In court, an accusation is privileged. In public, the accused can sue, and these men can afford to.
- **What the book says:** a list of names compiled by survivors deserves a proper channel: Congress, a prosecutor, a court. The book reports **that the list exists, who made it, and why it is private**, and adds names to this register only when they reach the record.

## What the government had, and didn't use
- The **SDNY memo of December 2019** interviewed more than two dozen alleged victims and potential subjects.
- It described Epstein working "with others, including employees and associates", who helped by "contacting victims and scheduling their sexual encounters with Epstein".
- It weighed Maxwell, Lesley Groff (who denies wrongdoing), Brunel, Black and Staley, plus other names from finance, fashion and Hollywood that remain unidentified.
- Its legal analysis is **almost entirely redacted**.
- **Only Maxwell was charged.**
- DOJ's position, from Blanche: "if we had information… about men who abused women, we would prosecute them."
- The register sets that sentence beside the memo, and leaves the reader to weigh the two.
