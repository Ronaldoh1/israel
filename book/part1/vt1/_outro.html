The public record is clear at both ends: Herzl's diary records Pius X's refusal in January 1904, Pius XII asked in 1949 for an internationally guaranteed Jerusalem, and the Holy See recognized Israel only by the Fundamental Agreement of 30 December 1993 and the protocol that opened relations on 15 June 1994, relations still in place as of October 2026.
