Where They Sit Now

A network of former colleagues becomes a political question only when its members hold public power. This section records which people from the PayPal network, and from Peter Thiel's own firms, took government posts in Donald Trump's second administration, what each post was, how each was filled, and where each stood as of October 2026. It keeps two groups apart. The first is PayPal alumni in the strict sense: people who worked at the company. The second is people who never worked at PayPal but worked for Thiel at Clarium Capital, Thiel Capital, the Thiel Foundation or Mithril Capital. Reporters often fold both into "the Thiel network," and Fortune did so in December 2024 and May 2025. [cite:de9_fortune_net24] [cite:de9_fortune_net25] The connection types differ, so the record is kept separate here.

David Sacks: from "czar" to adviser

Trump named David Sacks, PayPal's former chief operating officer, as White House "AI and crypto czar" on 5 December 2024, a post created for the occasion. [cite:de13_wiki_sacks] He served as Special Advisor for AI and Crypto from 20 January 2025 as a special government employee, a status that limits service to 130 days in a year, does not require Senate confirmation and carries lighter public financial disclosure than a confirmed post. [cite:de13_wiki_sacks] Executive Order 14177 of 23 January 2025, which re-established the President's Council of Advisors on Science and Technology (PCAST), wrote him into a second role by title: "The APST and the Special Advisor for AI & Crypto shall serve as Co-Chairs of the PCAST," the APST being the president's science adviser, Michael Kratsios. [cite:de13_eo14177]

The terms of his service drew the most scrutiny of any network appointment. Sacks received two White House ethics waivers that allowed him to keep certain stakes while shaping federal policy on artificial intelligence and crypto. [cite:de13_tc_sacks_exit] On 30 November 2025 the New York Times reported that his disclosures listed 708 technology investments, 449 of them in AI companies, and that his filings did not show the remaining value of the holdings he had said he would sell, or when he sold them. [cite:de13_tc_nyt_sacks] Sacks called the story a "nothing burger"; the White House called him "an invaluable asset." [cite:de13_tc_nyt_sacks] These are documented facts about disclosure and waivers. Whether any specific policy he worked on benefited a specific holding is a separate claim that the published record does not settle; it is set out as a candidate loop in [scene:de14_loops|Part Fourteen].

On 26 March 2026 his non-consecutive 130 days ran out and his czar role ended. [cite:de13_tc_sacks_exit] He stayed on as PCAST co-chair, a body that studies and recommends but does not make policy, and said the post let him "make recommendations on not just AI but an expanded range of technology topics." He remained a partner at Craft Ventures. [cite:de13_tc_sacks_exit] Status as of October 2026: PCAST co-chair; no longer a special government employee in the czar role.

Ken Howery: Denmark and Greenland

Ken Howery, PayPal's first chief financial officer and a co-founder of Founders Fund, had already served Trump once, as ambassador to Sweden from 2019 to January 2021. [cite:de13_wiki_howery] On 22 December 2024 Trump announced him as ambassador to Denmark, writing in the same announcement that "ownership and control of Greenland is an absolute necessity." [cite:de13_abc_greenland] [cite:de13_wiki_howery] The Senate confirmed him 51 to 47 in an en-bloc vote on 7 October 2025, and he took up the post on 5 November 2025. [cite:de13_arctic_howery] [cite:de13_wiki_howery] In December 2025 he travelled to Nuuk for the annual joint committee of the United States, Greenland and Denmark, and in January 2026 he hosted the embassy's New Year reception in Copenhagen. [cite:de13_hnn_nuuk] [cite:de13_amcham_2026] Status as of October 2026: ambassador, with no departure reported. His appointment places a Thiel business partner of twenty years in the diplomatic post that handles the president's stated Greenland ambition; it is documented as an appointment by the president, and no document shows Thiel or anyone else in the network seeking it for him. Thiel's own spokesperson has said that "Peter isn't involved in any plans or discussions regarding Greenland." [cite:de13_newsweek_greenland]

Elon Musk: DOGE, 130 days

Musk, PayPal's first chief executive, served as a special government employee leading the Department of Government Efficiency from January 2025. On 28 May 2025 he posted, "As my scheduled time as a Special Government Employee comes to an end," and the White House began off-boarding him; his 130-day limit fell as early as 30 May. [cite:de13_abc_musk_exit] What DOGE was in law, who staffed it and what it did are the subject of Book III, beginning with [scene:de17_money_to_office|the deal from campaign money to an office]; his later dealings with the administration are in [scene:de11_twitter_x_xai|Musk's life].

The Thiel firms, not PayPal

Two senior appointees came from Thiel's own companies rather than from PayPal. Michael Kratsios was chief financial officer at Clarium from 2010 and later a principal at Thiel Capital and Thiel's chief of staff; he served as United States chief technology officer from 2019 to 2021, was nominated to direct the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy on 22 December 2024 and was confirmed 74 to 25 on 25 March 2025. [cite:de13_wiki_kratsios] Status as of October 2026: OSTP director and PCAST co-chair. [cite:de13_wiki_kratsios] [cite:de13_eo14177] The vote, with a large bipartisan majority, is part of the record: his confirmation was not a party-line event.

Jim O'Neill was chief executive of the Thiel Foundation from 2009 to 2012 and a managing director at Clarium, Thiel Capital and Mithril. [cite:de13_wiki_oneill] He was confirmed as deputy secretary of Health and Human Services in June 2025 by 52 to 43, served as acting director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention from 28 August 2025, and left both posts on 13 February 2026 in a reshuffle at the department. [cite:de13_wiki_oneill] [cite:de13_cnn_oneill] Trump nominated him to direct the National Science Foundation on 2 March 2026; in May 2026 the scientific society AAAS wrote to the Senate committee asking it to schedule his still-unheld confirmation hearing. [cite:de13_wiki_oneill] [cite:de13_aaas_nsf] Status as of October 2026: nominee, not confirmed.

Who holds no office

The absences are part of the finding. Keith Rabois, Luke Nosek, Max Levchin, Roelof Botha and Jeremy Stoppelman hold no federal appointment, and none is on record for 2025 or 2026. [cite:de13_wiki_rabois] [cite:de13_wiki_levchin] Reid Hoffman, the network's largest Democratic donor, is on the other side of the administration. [cite:de13_badger_hoffman] Peter Thiel himself holds no office: he served on Trump's transition executive committee in 2016 and has held no government post since. [cite:de9_cnn_transition] Vance's path from Thiel's employ to the vice presidency is told in [scene:de12_the_thiel_years|his life], and is not repeated here.

The worst-case reading, tested

The hypothesis this book tests reads the 2025 cuts and immigration enforcement as the occasion for building an executive without a veto. On the narrow question of this part, whether people from one network were placed in positions of public power, the answer is documented: six people with ties to Thiel or to PayPal, direct or one step removed, held federal posts in 2025, among them the vice president, the president's science adviser, the official writing AI and crypto policy and the head of DOGE. [cite:de13_wiki_sacks] [cite:de13_wiki_kratsios] [cite:de13_abc_musk_exit] Also documented: that the most prominent of these, Sacks, served under a status that avoided Senate confirmation and with ethics waivers whose full effect the public filings do not show. [cite:de13_tc_nyt_sacks]

Probable, on the record: that shared history made these appointments easier, since the people were known to the president's circle through Vance, Musk and Thiel. The record does not show the mechanism, and the grade stays at probable. Not supported: that the network acted as one bloc, or that the appointments followed any plan. The posts went through ordinary channels, including confirmation votes with Democratic support for Kratsios; [cite:de13_wiki_kratsios] several of the people in the 2007 photograph hold no office, and one of them funds the opposition. [cite:de13_badger_hoffman] Running against the hypothesis, too, is the short tenure of the two most powerful posts: Musk left after 130 days, and Sacks's czar role ended after fourteen months. [cite:de13_abc_musk_exit] [cite:de13_tc_sacks_exit] What the money behind these people bought, and where it is visible and where it is not, is the subject of [scene:de14_network_map|Part Fourteen]; the final grades go to [scene:de40_worst_case_scorecard|Part Forty-Three].
