The Children Who Lived

Amputees. By the end of 2024 Gaza's hospitals had recorded 4,500 amputations, about 800 of them on children. [cite:pr_newarab_amput] The UN commission found in 2026 that Gaza "now has the highest concentration of child amputees in the world." [cite:pr_un_coi2026]

Orphans. In April 2025 the Palestinian Central Bureau of Statistics counted 39,384 children in Gaza who had lost one or both parents, about 17,000 of them both. [cite:pr_aj_orphans2025]

After the ceasefire. The ceasefire of October 2025 did not end the killing of children. UNICEF reported that at least 300 were killed in its first 300 days. "A ceasefire that leaves an average of one child dead each and every day is failing children," said its regional director, Edouard Beigbeder, in August 2026. [cite:pr_unicefusa_2026]

These were the stories Gaza's journalists filed day after day: the child pulled from the rubble, the ward of amputees, the family with no parents left. Part Five turns to who those journalists were.
