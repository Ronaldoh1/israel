Part Seventeen brings the mechanisms of the whole book down to the scale of a household. Its first section runs the published budgets of the average household in four fifths of the American income ladder, from the Bureau of Labor Statistics' Consumer Expenditure Survey for 2024, through taxes, housing, transport, health care and debt, shows with the arithmetic what is left over at each level, and tests what one medical bill, job loss or rent increase would do to that margin. Its second section follows the taught life script from childcare to Social Security, giving the documented typical figure at each stage, from school funding and college debt to the first paycheck, the first rent or mortgage, the 401(k) and the retirement benefit, and pointing to the part of the book that explains the mechanism behind each number. No invented families appear in either section; every figure comes from a named survey or administrative source.
