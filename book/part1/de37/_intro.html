This part maps the network's ties to Israel and only those a document shows, on the same footing as its ties to any other state. The first section adds to what the library has already set out in The One-Way Mirror: what Palantir has said about its partnership with Israel's Ministry of Defense, what a United Nations rapporteur and one biography allege, what the company denies, and which investors and customers left and for what stated reasons. The second section tests the claims about Israel that circulate and fail, states the difference between Zionism and Judaism on which the subject depends, names antisemitism where the network's own record shows it, and sets Israel's place beside the Gulf states, China and others measured by the same rule.
