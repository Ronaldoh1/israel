Name by Name

How the ministry counts. Gaza's health ministry, part of the Hamas-run government, records deaths reported by hospitals and health workers, and matches them where it can to the national ID numbers of the dead. In October 2023, after President Biden said he had "no confidence" in its figures, it began publishing the names of everyone it said had been killed. It does not record whether the dead were civilians or fighters. [cite:pr_walrus_counters] By January 2026, according to the Times of Israel, its records included names and ID numbers for more than 90 percent of the bodies. [cite:pr_toi_idf70k] As of 12 October 2026 its count stood at 74,382 killed since October 2023, 1,493 of them after the ceasefire of October 2025. [cite:gz_aj_year2026]

Testing the lists. Airwars, a British group that monitors civilian harm in war, checked the ministry's first list against its own open-source archive of the war's first seventeen days. Of 2,993 civilians it had identified by full name, 2,236, three in four, appeared on the ministry's list. It judged the early recording "largely reliable" for that period, and noted that later recording became harder as hospitals stopped functioning. [cite:pr_airwars_lists] The statistician Michael Spagat said the figures up to 26 October 2023 were much more reliable than those since, as hospitals were destroyed. [cite:pr_walrus_counters]

What Israel Says

Through most of the war Israel's government rejected the ministry's figures as the product of a Hamas-run body. In January 2026 a senior Israeli military official told reporters, anonymously, that the army believed about 70,000 Gazans had been killed, close to the ministry's count of 71,667 at the time, and that the share who were fighters was still under review. The army had said before the ceasefire that it had killed at least 22,000 combatants, and its officials had put the ratio at two to three civilians for each fighter. [cite:pr_toi_idf70k] [cite:pr_jw_idf71k] The next day the army's spokesman, Nadav Shoshani, said reports that the army had accepted the toll "do not reflect official data." [cite:pr_newarab_iddeny]
