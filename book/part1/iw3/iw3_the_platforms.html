The Platforms

The Cyber Unit

Israel's Cyber Unit, within the State Attorney's Office, sends platforms what it calls voluntary requests to remove content it considers incitement or support for terrorism. Between 7 October and 14 November 2023 it sent about 9,500 requests, 60 percent of them to Meta, with a compliance rate an Israeli official put at 94 percent. [cite:cjr_cyber_unit] [cite:eff_cyber_unit] Leaked internal data reported by Drop Site News in 2025 indicated that Meta had removed more than 90,000 posts at Israel's request, often quickly and without human review. [cite:dropsite_meta_leak] Israel describes the unit as countering incitement, a legitimate aim; Meta's Oversight Board has urged the company to publish how it handles government requests. [cite:eff_cyber_unit]

What Meta removed

In December 2023 Human Rights Watch reviewed more than 1,050 takedowns and other suppression of content from over 60 countries; 1,049 involved peaceful pro-Palestinian content. HRW describes the cases as reported to it, not a comparative sample. [cite:hrw_meta_promises] A review Meta itself commissioned, published in September 2022, found its actions during the May 2021 war had harmed Palestinian users' rights, and did not find intentional bias. [cite:bhrrc_hrw_meta] Meta disputes that it suppresses any voice systemically. [cite:hrw_meta_promises] A University of Chicago study found that pro-Palestinian and pro-Israel content had similar engagement but that only pro-Palestinian content showed signs of algorithmic reduction after 7 October. [cite:uchicago_algorithmic_reduction_study]

TikTok

On 26 September 2025, at Israel's consulate in New York, Netanyahu told a group of influencers that the most important weapons were on social media, and that "the most important purchase that is going on right now is TikTok". [cite:aa_tiktok_weapon] The joint venture that took over TikTok's American operations was formed on 22 January 2026: Oracle, Silver Lake and Abu Dhabi's MGX each hold 15 percent, ByteDance keeps 19.9 percent, and Oracle runs data security and oversees the American copy of the recommendation algorithm. [cite:techcrunch_tiktok_owners] Oracle's founder, Larry Ellison, has given more than $26 million to the Friends of the Israel Defense Forces, including $16.6 million in 2017, and is a long-time friend of Netanyahu. [cite:influencewatch_larry_ellison] [cite:wiki_larry_ellison] No document shows a change to TikTok's moderation of Israel-related content since the sale; the ownership and the stated interest are documented.
