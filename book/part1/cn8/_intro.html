Part Eight covers 1555 to 1966, the four centuries in which the institution that had fixed the list of sacred books also kept a list of forbidden ones. It begins with the first Roman Index of 1559 and the pope who made it, Paul IV Carafa, whose nephews ran his government, were disgraced by him, executed by his successor and rehabilitated by the next pope. It then follows the Index through its editions to the last of 1948, and to the notification of 14 June 1966 that ended its force as church law.
