The Mechanism, in Plain Terms: A State Set Running Before It Was Declared

What follows is a reading, not a finding. It offers a way of picturing the sequence set out in the previous section, borrowed from computer programming. It adds no fact to that sequence, and where the comparison and the record part ways, the record governs.

The familiar statement is that Israel was created in 1948. As a statement about the declaration of 14 May 1948, it is accurate. As a statement about when the state's working parts came into being, it misleads, and the comparison is one way of seeing why 1948 can look like a beginning when the documents show it as the end of a long build.

The comparison comes from Objective-C, a programming language in which a program can contain a routine named +load. The language's runtime calls that routine on its own, at the moment a piece of the program is loaded into memory, before the program's main sequence, the part a user sees, ever begins. No one has to invoke it. In the illustrative version, four setup steps run inside that early routine, in order: capitalize a bank, buy land and hold it permanently, have an agency legally recognized, and build a lobby. By the time the main sequence starts, all four have finished. The main sequence then prints a single line, "State declared." It is the only line a casual reader of the program's output ever sees.

The same point can be made in a street version, with no code vocabulary at all. Picture a restaurant on the night of its grand opening. The opening is real, and for most people it is the first moment the place exists. But the kitchen was built in 1899. The land was bought in 1901. The permit was granted in 1922. The marketing team had been working the neighborhood since the 1940s. Everything that made the opening possible happened before the sign went up, and most of it happened out of public view.

Each part of the picture stands for a documented institution, every one of them in place before May 1948. The kitchen is the financial base: the Jewish Colonial Trust and the Anglo-Palestine Bank, founded between 1899 and 1902. The land is the permanent holdings of the Jewish National Fund, which began buying in 1901 and did not resell. The permit is Article 4 of the Mandate of 1922, which recognized a "Jewish agency" and so gave the movement's agency legal standing. The marketing team is the American political machine of the 1940s, the American Zionist Emergency Council, whose local committees and letter campaigns worked Congress district by district [cite:aja_azec_local_1944]. The declaration, in this reading, was the first line of the main program. It was not the first line of the program.

The reading has limits, and they matter as much as the picture. A program has a programmer who writes every line in advance; the build traced in this volume had no single author. Its institutions were founded by different people in different decades, in London, Palestine and the United States. A routine that runs automatically also meets no resistance, and this one met it at every step. Edmond de Rothschild, who funded the early colonies, opposed Herzl. In Washington the State Department, the Joint Chiefs of Staff and the Secretary of Defense opposed American support in writing, the CIA warned against it, and all were overruled. Britain spent about £100 million on its forces in Palestine in 1945–47 before it gave up the Mandate [cite:hmg_termination_1948]. And the outcome on the ground, the lines that the state actually held, was decided not by anything loaded in advance but by the war of 1948 and the armistices that followed it.

The reading's own summary is a single line: the state was initialized long before it was declared. So the comparison is useful for one thing only: it explains why an observer who looked first in 1948 would see a sudden beginning, when the institutions that made the beginning possible had been running for close to fifty years. It does not show that the outcome was planned from the start, or that it was inevitable. The record shows accumulation and contingency together, and the comparison should be read as a picture of the first, never as proof against the second.
