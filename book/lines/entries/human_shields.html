There is documented evidence that Hamas used civilian sites for military purposes, but under the laws of war that does not move responsibility for civilian deaths entirely onto Hamas. In November 2023 the White House said US intelligence, separate from Israel's, indicated that Hamas and Palestinian Islamic Jihad used some Gaza hospitals, including Al-Shifa, and tunnels beneath them to conceal military operations; it called this a war crime. Gaza doctors denied it. [cite:jns_us_intel_hospitals] The record also runs the other way: Israeli soldiers, former detainees and the veterans' group Breaking the Silence described Israeli units sending Palestinians, including a 16-year-old, into buildings and tunnels ahead of troops, a practice nicknamed the "mosquito protocol." The IDF says its directives prohibit this. [cite:cnn_mosquito_protocol] [cite:cbs_human_shields]

Using civilians as shields is prohibited for every party, and a shield's presence does not lift the attacker's duties of distinction and proportionality. Whether specific Israeli operations met those duties is contested. [cite:btselem_casualties]
