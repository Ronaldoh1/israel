Part Eight followed pro-Israel advocacy into the states. Thirty-eight states adopted laws or executive orders against boycotts of Israel between 2015 and 2024, from a model act drafted in 2015 and with the lobbying of AIPAC, the Israel Action Network and local federations; the courts divided, and the Supreme Court declined in 2023 to review the law the Eighth Circuit had upheld. [cite:pm_wiki_antibds] [cite:pm_aclu_waldrip] State and local treasuries bought more than $1.7 billion of Israeli bonds after 7 October 2023, from a seller that courted their officials with trips and awards. [cite:pm_icij_bonds]

What is settled is the law and the purchases. What is contested is whether the laws penalize protected political expression and whether the bond purchases served the funds' beneficiaries or a cause. What the record leaves open is how many officials who wrote the laws or bought the bonds had traveled at a sponsor's expense, since no federal body records their travel. Part Nine turns to other countries.
