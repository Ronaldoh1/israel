Gaza, Now

On 7 October 2023 Hamas-led fighters killed about 1,200 people in Israel, 778 of them civilians, among them 58 children, and took 251 hostages. [cite:halakha_yomhazikaron_2026] [cite:wiki_hostage_crisis] The war Israel then waged in Gaza is, by the documented measures, the deadliest in the history of the conflict.

By late November 2026 Gaza's Health Ministry counted 74,742 people killed and 176,227 wounded, including at least 1,675 killed after the ceasefire of 10 October 2025. [cite:sq_abna_toll] Two peer-reviewed studies found the count too low, not too high: one estimated 64,260 deaths by June 2024 against 37,877 then reported; the other, the first survey not drawn from ministry records, estimated more than 75,000 violent deaths in the first fifteen months, about 56 percent of them women, children and the elderly. [cite:gulfnews_lancet_toll] [cite:cyprusmail_lancetgh_2026] The global hunger monitor confirmed famine in Gaza City in August 2025, a finding Israel rejected; about 81 percent of the strip's structures had been damaged. [cite:gz_aj_ipc] [cite:gz_aj_year2026]

In law

The International Criminal Court issued arrest warrants for Netanyahu and his former defence minister in November 2024, and for Hamas's military commander. [cite:icc_case_database] [cite:gz_un_icc] The court rejected Israel's appeal against them. [cite:gz_toi_icc2025] South Africa's genocide case runs at the International Court of Justice, with written pleadings due in 2029; the court's 2024 order found some rights plausibly at risk and is not a verdict. [cite:gz_icj_timelimits] [cite:jta_un_coi_genocide]

The killing of Israelis on 7 October is a crime the record documents, and so are the crimes alleged in Gaza; neither erases the other. What the case adds is the chain: more than two-thirds of Gaza's 2.4 million people on the eve of the war were registered refugees from 1948 and their descendants, held in a strip whose edges Israel controls, and the war fell on them.
