The caves gave back Enoch in Aramaic and Jubilees in Hebrew: eleven manuscripts of Enoch, nine of the Book of Giants, fourteen or fifteen of Jubilees, and a solar calendar that divided a community from the Temple. [cite:ds_bas_enoch] [cite:ds_vq_nature] What Ethiopia kept in the open, Qumran had kept in the dark. Part Five asks why most of the scrolls stayed in the dark for forty years after they were found.
