Part Eight follows the end-times system into organised politics. It begins with Jerry Falwell's Moral Majority, founded in June 1979 for reasons that historians dispute but that did not centre on Israel, and with the Israeli prime minister Menachem Begin's choice of Falwell as his main American Christian partner. It then sets out three organisations built wholly around support for Israel: the International Christian Embassy Jerusalem, founded in 1980 when embassies left the city; Bridges for Peace; and the International Fellowship of Christians and Jews, a rabbi's fundraising body whose own tax returns show its scale. It ends with Christians United for Israel, founded by John Hagee in 2006: its self-reported membership, its summits and lobbying, its stated positions, the controversies around its founder, and what its finances do and do not disclose. Each organisation's own statements are set beside the record and its critics.
