This is the fourth book of the Dark Enlightenment Series. "Exit", the freedom to leave rather than to vote, is the ideas' favourite remedy. This book follows what happened when people tried to build it: floating cities, charter cities in Honduras and the Próspera project on the island of Roatán, Argentina and the campaign pledge of "Freedom Cities", and Urbit, the digital network Curtis Yarvin designed and described in his own words as "digital feudalism".

It asks whether exit works as an escape for everyone or as an enclave for the few, and who holds power inside the places built on it. The documents are the charters themselves, court rulings, arbitration filings and the founders' own published statements.
