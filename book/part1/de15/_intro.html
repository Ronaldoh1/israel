This part sets two Senate races of 2022 side by side. In Ohio and in Arizona Peter Thiel backed a former employee, J.D. Vance and Blake Masters, by giving $10 million to a super PAC formed for one candidate before that candidate had declared, adding more around Donald Trump's endorsement, and stepping back after the primary. Both men won their primaries; one became Vice President, the other lost to Mark Kelly. The part gives the amounts, the dates, the endorsements and the outcomes, names the other donors who gave to both, records the complaints that were made and dismissed, and separates the method the record documents from the machine it is often said to show.
