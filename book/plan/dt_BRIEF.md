It accepts that the US system is not a full democracy, maps exactly where (Electoral College, primaries and gerrymanders, money, corporate speech), and tests whether finishing democracy answers Thiel's and Yarvin's critique better than a CEO-sovereign. Discipline:
- Same standard for every party, lobby and viewpoint. Every major spender on the same footing (AIPAC's UDP, Fairshake, America PAC, Future Forward, MAGA Inc., CLF, SLF, unions, billionaires both sides, corporate). Every moderation complaint on the same footing (Palestine-related content; the laptop story; Trump suspension; COVID-era).
- Design facts separate from outcome judgments: "the Electoral College's screening role no longer operates (Chiafalo)" is documented; "the country is worse off since 2016" is a contested judgment — show measures and both readings, never assert it.
- Each reform gets its evidence for, its critics, and its legal route (city charter, state ballot, statute, amendment, court).
- Citizens United: 5–4 on the spending ban, 8–1 upholding disclosure; never "5–4" alone.
- Status "as of October 2026" for anything that changed in 2025–26; 3 Nov 2026 ballot measures are "on the ballot", results not yet known.
- No research-method talk, no "Ron", no QOP/AI; no hype words; no rhetorical questions; no bullets outside figures; define terms on first use (super PAC, dark money, faithless elector, instant runoff, exhausted ballot, state action).
- Israel/AIPAC: plain register, documented spending only, same footing as every other lobby; Zionism ≠ Judaism; no tropes.
