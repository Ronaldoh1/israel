Oslo, Rabin and the First Term, 1993–1999

The opposition to Oslo grew violent in 1995. At a rally in Zion Square in Jerusalem in October, weeks before the assassination, Netanyahu spoke from a balcony with other Likud leaders while posters showing Rabin in an SS uniform circulated in the crowd below; Ehud Olmert left as the mood grew alarming, and Netanyahu stayed. [cite:ny_spectator_rabin] Around the same time a young follower of Meir Kahane, Itamar Ben-Gvir, held up on television the emblem he had torn from Rabin's car and said, "We got to his car, and we'll get to him too." [cite:bgv_wiki_bgvir] On 4 November 1995 Rabin was shot dead by Yigal Amir, a Jewish extremist, as he left a peace rally in Tel Aviv. [cite:rabin_assassination_1995]

Netanyahu did not hold the posters, and he has rejected the charge, made by his critics for three decades, that he helped create the climate for the killing. [cite:timesofisrael_netanyahu_rabin_blame] What the record holds is the rally, the posters and his presence; what responsibility follows from them is argued, not settled.

The first term

On 29 May 1996, in the first election in which Israelis chose their prime minister directly, Netanyahu defeated Shimon Peres. [cite:ny_wiki_bibi] He did not tear up Oslo. On 14 January 1997 he signed the Hebron Protocol, which handed most of Hebron to the Palestinian Authority, and in October 1998 the Wye River Memorandum, which promised further withdrawals; the Knesset approved Wye by 75 to 19. [cite:ny_wiki_bibi] [cite:gi10_crs_wye] In May 1999 he lost to Ehud Barak. [cite:ny_wiki_bibi]

The attorney general who lasted two days

In January 1997 Netanyahu's government appointed Roni Bar-On, a Jerusalem lawyer and Likud activist, as attorney general; he resigned within days amid criticism of his thin qualifications. A television report then alleged that the appointment had been part of a deal with Aryeh Deri, leader of the Shas party and himself on trial for corruption, who wanted an attorney general open to a plea bargain, in exchange for Shas votes for the Hebron Protocol. [cite:wiki_baron_hebron_affair] Police recommended in 1997 that Netanyahu be indicted; the attorney general, Elyakim Rubinstein, and the state attorney, Edna Arbel, found "insufficient evidence" against him, while deciding that Deri should be charged, and Rubinstein said his decisions did not clear those involved "of all suspicion". [cite:jta_baron_affair_indicting] [cite:ny_jta_baron_sep97] The pattern of an appointment, a coalition need and a prosecution would return.

The tape

In 2001, out of office, Netanyahu was filmed speaking privately to a family of settlers. The recording aired on Israel's Channel 10 in July 2010. In it he said, "I know what America is. America is a thing you can move very easily, move it in the right direction. They won't get in the way." [cite:ny_aje_2010video] He also described how he had used the agreements' undefined "military zones" to limit what Israel handed over: "Nobody said what defined military zones were." [cite:ny_aje_2010video] He spoke as a man explaining his own method; he did not say it in public, and he has not repeated it.
