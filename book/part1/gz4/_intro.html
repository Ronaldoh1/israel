Part Four describes the system that governed Gaza from 2007 to 2023. It begins with the election of 2006 and the fighting of June 2007 that left Hamas in control of the strip, then sets out, piece by piece, how Israel and Egypt closed it: what was allowed in and out, who could leave, how far fishermen could sail and how close farmers could go to the fence. It ends with what the closure did to the strip's economy, water, electricity and health, as the UN measured it, and with the legal judgments made about it, which disagree.
