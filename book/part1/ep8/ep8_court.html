What the Courts Found

Two of the victims sued the government in 2008, under the Crime Victims' Rights Act, for keeping the agreement from them. It took eleven years to get a ruling. In February 2019 Judge Kenneth Marra found that prosecutors had violated the Act: while the government spent untold hours negotiating with Epstein's lawyers, the victims were told to be patient. [cite:marra] [cite:nbc_marra] In the same opinion he found that Epstein had directed other people to abuse the girls and had worked in concert with others to obtain minors "not only for his own gratification but for the gratification of others". [cite:marra] [cite:cbs_marra] That is a federal court's written finding that this was a network, not one man.

It did not last as a remedy. In April 2021 the full Eleventh Circuit Court of Appeals, sitting en banc, held that the Act's rights apply only once a defendant has been charged. A deal struck before any charge, however secret, falls outside it. [cite:ep8_justia_wild2021] The Supreme Court declined to hear the victims' appeal on 22 February 2022. [cite:ep8_scotus_wild2022] Reform bills to close that gap were introduced in Congress in 2019 and 2022 and did not pass. [cite:ep8_hr4729] [cite:ep8_hr9444] The court found the government broke the law; the appeals court found the law did not reach what the government did. Both rulings stand, and together they describe a right that disappears at exactly the moment it would matter.

The courts also held the other line. When the Justice Department asked in 2025 to unseal grand jury material from the Florida and New York cases, judges in both districts refused, because federal rules allow it only in narrow, listed circumstances. [cite:ep8_gpb_fl_gj2025] [cite:ep8_ap_ny_gj2025] And when Ghislaine Maxwell asked a federal judge in 2026 to throw out her conviction, Judge Paul Engelmayer found her claims "all meritless and all or almost all … frivolous". [cite:ep8_abc_maxwell2026] The courts convicted the one person prosecutors charged. They could not reach anyone prosecutors chose not to charge.
