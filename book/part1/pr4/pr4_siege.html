The Siege, and the Blockade

On 9 October 2023, two days after the Hamas-led attack, Defence Minister Yoav Gallant announced: "We are putting a complete siege on Gaza … No electricity, no food, no water, no gas – it's all closed." [cite:gz_aj_siege] When the January 2025 ceasefire broke down, Israel stopped all aid from entering on 2 March 2025, saying Hamas was stealing it; it did not publish evidence for the claim. [cite:gz_wiki_ceasefire2025] [cite:gz_aj_ghf]

The aid sites. From 27 May 2025 food was distributed by the Gaza Humanitarian Foundation, backed by Israel and the United States, at a handful of sites, replacing the UN's network. By 4 September 2025 the UN Human Rights Office had recorded more than 2,146 people killed near the foundation's sites and along aid convoy routes. The foundation said it was the only operation that delivered food reliably and safely, more than three million boxes, before it closed on 24 November 2025. [cite:gz_aj_ghf]

Famine, and the Denial

On 22 August 2025 the Integrated Food Security Phase Classification, the UN-backed system that declares famines, found that "famine is now occurring in Gaza City and its surrounding area," with more than half a million people in catastrophic conditions, and named "conflict, displacement, restricted access, and food system collapse" as its causes. [cite:gz_aj_ipc] Israel's foreign ministry answered: "There is no famine in Gaza." It called the findings "based on Hamas lies laundered through organisations with vested interests," and said "a massive influx of aid has flooded the Strip with staple foods." [cite:gz_olj_ipc]

The warrants. In November 2024 the International Criminal Court had already issued arrest warrants for Netanyahu and Gallant for, among other crimes, "starvation as a method of warfare." Netanyahu called them "absurd and false." [cite:gz_un_icc]
