The promise has not been kept evenly, and the record shows where and through which mechanisms. Black Americans' poverty fell from 41.8 per cent in 1966 to 17.3 per cent in 2025, but the median white family held about nine times the wealth of the median Black family in 2025, and a Black child born to the bottom fifth had a 2.5 per cent chance of reaching the top fifth against 10.6 per cent for a white child.
