Part Ten established how the theology shield works and where it has been refused. Its mechanism runs in five steps that can be followed in dated statements: the ICEJ calls Genesis 12:3 the Christian Zionist "biblical mandate"; Senator Cruz, on 18 June 2025, read it as being about "the nation of Israel"; Speaker Johnson and Prime Minister Netanyahu applied it to nations in 2025; and Robert Jeffress, in 2017 and again in June 2025, said that those who oppose Israel are "on the wrong side of God". [cite:ts10_icej_hedding] [cite:ts10_cp_cruz] [cite:ts10_ww_kaylor] [cite:ts10_cbn_jeffress2017] [cite:ts10_cp_jeffress2025] The verse itself, spoken to Abram, does not name Israel, and the case of Carrie Prejean Boller, removed from the Religious Liberty Commission on 11 February 2026 after denying that Catholics embrace Zionism and repeating the deicide charge at the same hearing, shows the frame used in both directions. [cite:ts10_ww_kaylor] [cite:ts10_cam_prejean] [cite:ts10_ncr_prejean] The mechanism has also failed: theologians from Fuller to Catholic Answers rejected the reading, Cruz said he does not use his faith as the reason for his policy, Huckabee called his "take it all" answer hyperbolic within two days, Brazil opened a trade office instead of an embassy, and in December 2025 Tucker Carlson told Turning Point's conference that "God is not on any country's side".
