What the Reader Now Holds

A volume that tests a popular claim owes the reader more than a verdict. It owes the tools with which the reader can test the next claim without it. This closing section sets out what the six parts have established, in a form that can be carried away, and names what the record still leaves open.

First, the difference between a city and what it houses. Every part found the same thing: the institution that acted was not the city. The Corporation of the City of London did not issue the Balfour Declaration or hold the Mandate; the British government did. Vatican City, a state created in 1929, did not refuse Herzl in 1904; the Holy See did. The District of Columbia's council has never had a foreign policy; the federal government seated in it recognized, armed and shielded Israel. [scene:dv2_parent_test|London] [scene:dv3_parent_test|The Vatican] [scene:dv4_parent_test|Washington] When a claim names a city, the first question is which institution in it acted, and what document shows it.

Second, a measure of what is anomalous. Part One set the three cities against Chicago, Atlanta and Madrid, cities that belong to the governments above them and whose residents elect those governments. [scene:dv1_ordinary_city|The ordinary city] Against that measure London has more self-government than it should, Washington far less, and Vatican City has full sovereignty and almost no self-sufficiency. Each anomaly is real, and each points in a different direction. None of them, by itself, is a source of power over other states.

Third, a test with dates in it. The parent test asks what an institution supplied that could not be had elsewhere, and when. Its most useful result is that the answer changes over time. London was decisive from 1917 to 1939 and an obstacle from 1939 to 1948; Washington embargoed Israel's arms in 1948 and became its principal supplier twenty years later; the Vatican refused for eighty-nine years and then recognized. [cite:rs_rd4_wp_cojs_text] [cite:dv4_herf_embargo] [cite:dv4_jvl_special_alliance] [cite:dv3_wiki_fund_agreement] A claim that a power "created" or "opposed" Israel, without dates, is almost always wrong for part of the period.

Fourth, the private record as the test of a front. The strongest form of the claim that the Vatican's refusal was a front made a prediction: that the private papers would contradict the public ones. They did not. The Secretariat of State's internal correspondence in 1943 and the Irish diplomatic file of 1948–1949 say what the public statements said. [cite:dv3_jvl_vatican] [cite:dv3_dawn_irish_files] The same method applies to any claim that a public position conceals a private one: find the private papers, and read them.

Fifth, convergence rather than design. The documented ties among the three cities are real, from the Rothschild loans to the popes to the Vatican Bank's new president. But at the decisive moments of 1947 and 1949 the three were on different sides, and the handover from London to Washington came about because Britain withdrew. [cite:dv3_unproject_181] [cite:dv3_wiki_res303] [cite:rs_rd5_cojs_bevin_1947] Powers with different interests can produce the same outcome without coordinating, and the record of this volume is a record of that, not of a plan.

Sixth, where to look now. The architecture is still moving, and the live questions as of 11 November 2026 are named here so the reader can follow them. Whether Gadi Eisenkot forms a government by 20 December, and whether it changes how decisions about the West Bank are made. [cite:dv5_israeled_watch] [cite:dv5_ynet_wb_feb2026] Whether the next American aid agreement ends grants, halves them or extends them to 2033, as reported. [cite:dv4_toi_rubio_nov2026] [cite:dv4_antiwar_ynet_nov2026] Whether the federal appeals court lets the National Guard stay in Washington. [cite:dv4_wbal_guard_2026] Whether the Holy See's London property case is retried in January 2027. [cite:dv3_advaticanum_becciu] And, in The Hague, the jurisdiction challenge at the International Criminal Court and the genocide case, whose pleadings now run to 2029. [cite:dv5_thesun_icc_2025] [cite:dv5_gov_za_icj_2026]

What remains open. The gaps are named so that later research can close them. The Holy See's own files on Palestine for 1939 to 1958 have not yet produced a published study, and a document could still surface that changes the reading of those years. The economic agreement between the Holy See and Israel, promised in 1993, had not been concluded at last report. How much of the Zionist movement's money passed through London between 1920 and 1948, as against New York, has not been measured. And the full itemized record of American arms transfers since October 2023 has not been made public, so every total given here is a minimum. [cite:dv3_lewy_2009] [cite:dv3_wiki_fund_agreement] [cite:ap_cow_2yr] None of these gaps, on the evidence assembled, would reverse the verdicts. Each would make them more exact.
