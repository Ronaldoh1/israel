Part Four turns to the Book of Enoch and the Book of Jubilees, Jewish writings that the Ethiopian church kept and almost everyone else let go. It summarises what the books say, notes that the Letter of Jude in the New Testament quotes Enoch, sets out in their own words why Tertullian accepted Enoch and Augustine rejected it, and follows how Europe forgot the book until a Scottish traveller brought Ethiopian copies home in 1773.
