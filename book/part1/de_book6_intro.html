This is the sixth book of the Dark Enlightenment Series. Data does not arrest anyone. This book asks how the checks on executive power have been loosened, and who would carry out a state that had none: the five documented patterns that remove brakes, from the power to fire officials to the control of spending; who benefits from each and what courts, Congress and states have blocked; ICE as the template for enforcement, local police, the military at home, and the private companies that run immigration detention.

It closes by treating Urbit and DOGE as two live tests of the idea of a chief executive without a veto, and grades what the record shows: what has been documented, what is probable, and what the vetoes that still work have stopped.
