Who Owns the News

In 1983 the journalist Ben Bagdikian counted about 50 corporations controlling most of the American media business. [cite:fair_digital_media_oligarchy] The largest single change since came with the Telecommunications Act, signed on 8 February 1996, which removed the national cap of 40 radio stations per owner; Clear Channel grew from 40 stations to about 1,240 by 2003, and the number of commercial radio owners fell from about 5,100 to 3,800 within five years. [cite:wiki_radio_homogenization] [cite:publicintegrity_fcc_dawdled_radio] Fox News launched on 7 October 1996 under Roger Ailes, a Republican media strategist who had produced television for Nixon, Reagan and George H. W. Bush. [cite:npr_ailes_obituary]

A FAIR analysis of December 2024 to November 2025 found that more than half of all American visits to major online news sites went to outlets controlled by seven families or companies, led by the Ochs-Sulzberger family's New York Times, Warner Bros. Discovery's CNN and Comcast's NBC News. [cite:fair_digital_media_oligarchy] In 2025 Skydance, controlled by David Ellison, acquired Paramount, owner of CBS. [cite:972_ellison_tiktok]

What ownership rewards

The commercial incentive is on the record. In 2016 the chief executive of CBS, Les Moonves, said of the Trump candidacy: "It may not be good for America, but it's damn good for CBS." [cite:moonves] By March 2016 Trump had received an estimated $1.9 billion in free media, more than all the other Republican candidates combined. [cite:free_media] The lesson for this volume is narrower than any claim of control: news outlets are businesses, and attention, not accuracy, is what they sell.

The failures are also on the record. In May 2004 the New York Times's editors acknowledged that its coverage of Iraq's weapons before the 2003 war "was not as rigorous as it should have been". [cite:nyt_iraq] Trust has fallen with it: in 2025 Gallup found 28 percent of Americans expressing a great deal or fair amount of trust in the mass media. [cite:fair_digital_media_oligarchy]
