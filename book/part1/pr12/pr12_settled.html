What the Record Settles

The dead are many, and most are named. Gaza's health ministry counted 74,382 dead by October 2026, with names and ID numbers for most of them; an independent check of its early lists found them largely reliable, and an Israeli military official put the toll at about 70,000 in January 2026. [cite:gz_aj_year2026] [cite:pr_airwars_lists] [cite:pr_toi_idf70k]

The count is more likely too low than too high. A household survey estimated about 75,200 violent deaths in the war's first fifteen months, when about 49,000 had been recorded, and about 16,300 deaths from indirect causes; about 10,000 people were missing under the rubble in April 2026. [cite:pr_almon_survey2026] [cite:pr_aj_rubble2026]

Children, women and the unborn bore much of it. The UN commission counted at least 20,179 children killed in two years; UN Women, more than 38,000 women and girls by the end of 2025; the ministry recorded about 4,000 pregnancies lost in the first half of 2026 and 466 deaths from starvation, 157 of them children, by October 2026. [cite:pr_nw_coi2026] [cite:pr_mm_women2026] [cite:pr_memo_starv2026]

Journalists were killed in numbers without precedent in the counters' records. Between 189 and 242, by the CPJ's and the UN's counts; in 2025, a record year for journalists killed worldwide, Israel was responsible for about two-thirds.
