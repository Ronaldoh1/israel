Pro-Israel money in American government runs through a small number of organizations, AIPAC's four bodies at the center, whose campaign money grew from about $15 million a cycle in pro-Israel contributions in 2018 to between $95 million and $127 million for AIPAC's two committees alone in 2024; it reaches members of Congress through contributions, primaries and trips, the states through laws and bond purchases, and other countries through parallel organizations and the Israeli government's own budget. What is settled is the structure and the scale, from filings the organizations made themselves. What is contested is what the money does, and how to count it. What the record leaves open is the donors who are not disclosed, the officials whose travel is not recorded, and the effect of the network on members it never had to oppose.
