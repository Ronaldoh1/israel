The Institutions as Entities

An entity diagram draws each institution as a box with its key attributes, and each documented tie as a numbered line. On the left are the powers: the British cabinet, the Mandate government, the United Nations and the American government. On the right is the movement and what it built. The red box at the end is the state.

Read down the right column and the movement builds its own institutions; read across and each step depends on a power on the left. That is the shape the network map measures: the movement and the powers together, neither alone.
