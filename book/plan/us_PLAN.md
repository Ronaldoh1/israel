# Volume plan: US System vs Israel System

**Subtitle:** Government, Rights, Money and the Cost of Living, Compared Over Time
**Placement:** shelf slot of ch40 (US ⇄ Israel — Two Systems Compared), absorbing ch60 (What About America?).
**Standard:** Greater Israel / Theology Shield volumes; three-source check; every figure re-checked from primary data, measured over time (1980–2026 where series exist); independent audit.
**Source map:** scratchpad us_leads.md (169 leads, 188 data-series rows).

## Locked chapter list (21 Oct 2026)
1. **The Question** (us1). What the aid is, what it frees up, and why people compare
2. **How the State Is Built** (us2). Constitution vs Basic Laws; executive and legislature; courts and the 2023 overhaul; federal vs central; emergency powers (Israel in a declared emergency since 1948); civil service; corruption indices
3. **Democracy** (us3). Elections and access; campaign finance and lobbying; press freedom and media ownership; speech, protest and anti-boycott laws; NGO and foreign-funding laws; indices over time (Freedom House, V-Dem, RSF, IDI Democracy Index)
4. **Criminal Justice** (us4). Policing and police violence; incarceration rates over time; death penalty; plea deals; prisons; juvenile justice; administrative detention
5. **Public Safety and Guns** (us5). Homicide over time; the Arab-community crime wave in Israel (252 Arab citizens killed in 2025, a record); gun laws (Israel licensing and the post-7 October loosening vs US law); mass shootings
6. **Citizenship and Immigration** (us6). Law of Return vs US naturalisation; the 2003 family-reunification ban; asylum seekers (Holot, deportations; US border, ICE, detention); who can and cannot become a citizen
7. **Religion and State** (us7). The Rabbinate and personal status law (marriage, divorce, burial, conversion; no civil marriage), Haredi exemptions and funding; US separation of church and state, religious exemptions and funding
8. **Equality Among Citizens** (us8). Arab citizens, Ethiopian, Mizrahi and Haredi communities in Israel; US racial gaps; gender and LGBTQ rights; disability — measured over time
9. **People Under Control Without Citizenship** (us9). Palestinians in the West Bank (two legal systems; military courts) and Gaza; East Jerusalem residency; compared with the US question of non-citizens under US control (territories, detention) where documented — the unequal comparison stated plainly. Link wb*, gz* rather than repeat
10. **Surveillance and Privacy** (us10). Shin Bet domestic surveillance, COVID phone tracking, Pegasus/police; US NSA, ICE data, Palantir; legal limits and oversight; link ow*
11. **The Economy** (us11). GDP per person and growth 1980–2026; Israel's 1985 stabilisation plan beside the US 1978–86 turn; taxes and who pays; debt and budget; central banks; concentration (Israel's tycoon families and the 2013 concentration law vs US corporate power); trade
12. **Industries** (us12). Tech and venture capital; defence and arms exports; energy and gas; agriculture and food; pharma; finance; US firms in Israel and Israeli firms in the US
13. **Labour** (us13). Minimum wage over time; unions (Histadrut vs US unions, density 1980–2026); hours and leave; pay gaps; migrant and Palestinian workers
14. **Cost of Living Over Time** (us14). Wages vs prices 1980–2026 in both countries (local currency and adjusted); housing price- and rent-to-income; food (2011 cottage-cheese protest, import monopolies); the 2011 tent protests and the Trajtenberg Committee; childcare, college, health out-of-pocket; transport, energy, phones; household debt and savings; a median family's monthly budget then and now
15. **Housing, Land and Infrastructure** (us15). Israel Land Authority ownership and leasing, JNF, zoning and building permits incl. Arab towns; US zoning, homeownership and homelessness; transit, water and desalination, energy grid, climate policy and disaster response
16. **Health** (us16). Coverage (Israel's 1995 National Health Insurance Law and the four funds vs the US mixed system), cost, outcomes over time (life expectancy, infant mortality), drug prices, mental health
17. **Education** (us17). K–12 and Israel's separate school streams (state, state-religious, Haredi, Arab), universities, cost and student debt, outcomes (PISA over time)
18. **Family Policy and the Safety Net** (us18). Parental leave, childcare, child allowances; abortion (Israel's approval committees and public funding vs post-Dobbs US); fertility and IVF; pensions, unemployment, disability; poverty rates over time (NII vs Census), elder care
19. **Military and Security** (us19). Conscription vs volunteer force; Haredi exemptions and the 2024 ruling; defence spending as share of GDP over time; veterans' care; Iron Dome and who pays
20. **Foreign Policy and the Money Flow** (us20). The MOU, FMF, offshore procurement phase-out, loan guarantees, tax-deductible donations, intelligence sharing; flows in both directions, in today's dollars; link gi16_aid rather than repeat
21. **What About America?** (us21). Synthesis: where each system serves its own citizens better or worse, by measure and over time; the aid in that light; what the comparison does and does not show; status as of October 2026 ahead of the 27 October and 3 November elections

## Rules
- Compare systems and governments, never peoples.
- The aid stated precisely: military money Israel does not raise itself, freeing Israeli money; mostly spent with US contractors; shrinking as a share of Israel's economy.
- Every statistic refreshed from primary series; snapshots labelled as snapshots.
- Show Israel's own gaps and America's.
- Status lines dated "as of October 2026"; re-check after Israel's 27 October and the US 3 November elections.

## Findings that shaped the list
- ch40 (= Sophia's module) is 2026 snapshots with ~1/3 aggregator figures and an undisclosed scorecard: structure kept, every figure rebuilt.
- Cost of living has no Israeli time series in any existing source: new research (CBS CPI and household expenditure survey, OECD price levels).
- Part 8 split (citizens vs people under control without citizenship); family policy folded into the safety net; infrastructure merged with housing and land; Israel's 1985 stabilisation plan added beside the US 1978–86 turn.
- Public safety centres the record killings in Israel's Arab community (252 in 2025).
