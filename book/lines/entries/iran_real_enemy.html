For several Arab governments, the record does show Iran replacing Israel as the main strategic concern, but that is a statement about governments, not about the countries as a whole. Through the 2010s Saudi Arabia and Israel came to share a threat assessment centered on Iran's nuclear program, missiles and allied armed groups, producing largely unofficial security contacts well before any public opening. Saudi Arabia's own war with the Iran-aligned Houthis in Yemen, ongoing since 2015, hardened that view.

The peace that exists is narrow. Egypt signed the first Arab peace treaty with Israel in 1979, and embassies have operated since 1980, but scholars call it a "cold peace": trade, tourism and cultural ties have stayed limited for over four decades. [cite:wikipedia_egypt_israel_treaty] The 2020 normalization deals were decided by monarchs or military rulers, with no referendum or ratification by an elected legislature in Bahrain, Morocco or Sudan, and protests followed in Morocco and Bahrain during the Gaza war.
