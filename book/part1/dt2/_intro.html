Part Two examines the mechanism that chooses the president. It sets out how the Electoral College is built, from Article II and the Twelfth Amendment to the state laws that give all of a state's electors to its statewide winner, and follows a single ballot through to the national count. It then separates the documented claim about Alexander Hamilton's promised check on unfit candidates, which no longer operates, from the contested judgment about whether the country has been worse off since 2016. Finally it lays out the record, five elections won by the candidate with fewer votes and campaigns concentrated in a few states, and the reforms on offer, each with its case, its critics and its legal route.
