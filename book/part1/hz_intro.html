This volume is about Hezbollah and the other armed groups that Israel and the United States have fought across the region: where they came from, what they have done, how much power they actually hold, and what the wars against them have produced. It was written to answer direct questions. Would Hezbollah exist if Israel had not invaded Lebanon in 1982 and occupied its south for eighteen years? What is it, beyond the label? What is its strength, and Lebanon's, against Israel's and the American aid behind it? And are Hezbollah, the Houthis and the Iraqi militias the standing reason for wars that do not end, for military aid that does not stop, and for an America drawn in again and again?

The record answers some of these questions and not others. It shows that Shia political mobilisation in Lebanon began before 1982, with Musa al-Sadr and Amal, and that Iran set out from 1979 to export its revolution. [cite:hz1_brit_sadr] [cite:hz1_iran_const] It shows that Israel invaded in 1982, armed and paid a Lebanese militia to hold a strip of the south, and that Hezbollah formed in the invasion's wake, with Iran's Revolutionary Guards in the Bekaa. [cite:hz2_idf_lw1] [cite:hz1_olj_sla] [cite:hz3_itic_shapira] It shows the bombings of 1983 that killed 241 American service members, attributed by US courts to Hezbollah with Iranian support and denied by both, and Hezbollah's rockets aimed at Israeli towns, stated plainly. [cite:hz2_anderson] [cite:hz12_cia_embassy] It shows the 2006 war, the support front of 2023, the pagers, the ceasefire of November 2024 and what followed it, the war of 2026, and the Houthis' and Iraqi militias' campaigns, each with the counts of the dead and how they were made. [cite:hz7_toi_text] [cite:hz7_acled_year_pdf]

It tests the claim that these groups have become the excuse for everything, the way The Hamas Architecture tested it for Hamas, and it separates what is documented, including the armed groups Israel itself has paid or supplied, from what is argued about motive. [scene:hz11_pattern|Always the Axis?] [cite:hz11_toi_eisenkot] [cite:hz11_npr_clans] What would have happened otherwise cannot be documented, and the volume labels every such reading as an argument. It draws on the Israel Architecture simulation, the research files of the Sophia project and the earlier Israel books, and every claim taken from them has been traced to a citable source. It is a companion to The Hamas Architecture and The One-Way Mirror.
