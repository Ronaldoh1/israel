Read this with book/PLANNER-BRIEF.md. It decides scope and scene allocation across the six stages, so stage planners do not collide.

## Depth standard

Volume II, The Road to 1948, is the bar (LIBRARY-PROTOCOL.md section 5, locked 14 October 2026). Each stage gets its own research file, blind-spot list, manifest, opener and closing synthesis. Aim for **10–16 research entries and 90–150 live-verified claims per stage**, with disputes logged. A stage is research-led: the simulation holds only seven scenes for this period in its own chapter (ch05), so most sections will be built on new research (section ids `cwN_<slug>`).

## Scope: autumn 1949 to 4 June 1967

Volume II's Stage Six (rd6) already tells, in full, from the primary record: the armistices; Bernadotte; Resolution 194; Lausanne; how return was barred and the "infiltration" years to 1956; the land regime to 1953; military government's structure (mg_s01, drawn for the regime); the Law of Return and Nationality Law; the mass immigration of 1948–52 and the ma'abarot; the Jews of the Arab world 1948–56; Jordan's annexation; the constitution deferred; financing 1949–53 including US loans, Israel Bonds and the opening of German reparations; the founding of the American Zionist Committee for Public Affairs in 1953 (in s07g, from s11). Volume III **links back** to these sections and never retells them. Where a thread continues (military government, the border, reparations, the lobby), pick it up where rd6 stops and say so in one sentence with a link.

The war of June 1967 belongs to Volume IV (Six Days, Two Fronts). Volume III ends on the eve: the May–June 1967 crisis is the last section of Stage Six, ending 4 June, linking forward to s12.

- **cw2 Retaliate (1953–56):** Unit 101 and Sharon; Qibya (14–15 October 1953) and the UN Security Council resolution; the US suspension of aid over the Banat Yaacov canal (September–October 1953); the Johnston Plan (1953–55); Moshe Sharett's diaries and the Sharett–Ben-Gurion split; Operation Susannah / the Lavon affair (July 1954) from lv_s01; the Free Officers 1952 and Nasser's rise; Operation Alpha (1954–56); the Gaza raid of 28 February 1955 (Operation Black Arrow) and its documented consequences; the fedayeen; the Baghdad Pact (February 1955); Bandung (April 1955); the Czech arms deal (September 1955); the Aswan Dam withdrawal (19 July 1956) and nationalization (26 July 1956); the Pledge "under God" (Docherty, February 1954; signed 14 June 1954) and the motto (30 July 1956), with the documented anti-communist motive and any Israel link labeled as interpretation; the 1953 Land Acquisition Law.
- **cw3 Strike and Withdraw (1956–57):** the Sèvres Protocol (22–24 October 1956); Operation Kadesh / Musketeer; Kafr Qasim (29 October 1956) and the trial (the "black flag" ruling, 1958); Khan Yunis (3 November 1956) and Rafah (12 November 1956), from the UNRWA special report; Eisenhower and Dulles's pressure (the UN votes, sanctions threat, the 20 February 1957 address); Ben-Gurion's "Third Kingdom of Israel" speech and retreat; withdrawal (March 1957), UNEF, the Tiran assurances (aide-mémoire 11 February 1957); the Eisenhower Doctrine (January–March 1957); the Soviet threat letters (5 November 1956); Hungary at the same time.
- **cw4 Arm (1957–62):** the French nuclear agreement (1957) and Dimona's construction; the 1960 U-2/attaché discovery and Ben-Gurion's 21 December 1960 Knesset statement; Herter, the Eisenhower–Kennedy transition on Dimona; the periphery doctrine (Iran, Turkey, Ethiopia: Trident 1958; SAVAK ties from 1957); 1958 (UAR February, Lebanon landing July, the Iraqi revolution 14 July, British paratroops to Jordan over Israeli airspace); German arms and the Strauss–Peres channel (from 1957), and the 1960 Ben-Gurion–Adenauer meeting; the Eichmann capture (11 May 1960) and trial (draw on rd4_documents and holocaust_primary_documents; do not retell the documents); Wadi Salib (July 1959) and the Mizrahi protest; the 1960–61 Lavon reopening and Ben-Gurion's fall (if not cw2); the Hawk missile decision (August 1962); Section 620I (1961); LFI (1957).
- **cw5 Confront (1962–63):** the Senate Foreign Relations Committee's Fulbright hearings (1963) on foreign agents, the Jewish Agency–AZC funding conduit and the FARA order (from s11a, fis_s06); Kennedy's Dimona letters to Ben-Gurion and Eshkol (May–July 1963), the inspection visits; Ben-Gurion's resignation (16 June 1963); Kennedy's Middle East policy (Nasser outreach, Yemen war from September 1962, the Hawk); 22 November 1963 and what the record does and does not show (s11c's rule: no implied causation without documentation).
- **cw6 Escalate (1963–4 June 1967):** Johnson and Eshkol (the 1964 visit, the first state visit); the tank deal (1965, via Germany then direct) and Skyhawks (1966); the 1964 Arab summits (January, Cairo; September, Alexandria), the PLO (May–June 1964, Shukeiri), the Arab diversion plan and the National Water Carrier (opened June 1964), the Israeli strikes on the diversion works (1965–66); Fatah's first operation (1 January 1965); the Syrian coup of February 1966; the Samu raid (13 November 1966) and UNSC Resolution 228; the end of military government (1 December 1966); West German–Israeli diplomatic relations (May 1965) and the end of reparations payments; NUMEC (1965 onward, the start); the 7 April 1967 air battle; the May crisis: the Soviet warning (13 May), UNEF's withdrawal (16–19 May), Tiran closed (22–23 May), the Egypt–Jordan pact (30 May), the unity government and Dayan (1 June), ending on 4 June.
