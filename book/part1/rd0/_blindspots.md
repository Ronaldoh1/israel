## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency:** the 1834 revolt and its leaders (rd0_ottoman); al-Khalidi as Jerusalem's elected deputy and an opposition member (rd0_tanzimat); the 1891 petition and the mufti's 1893 campaign over land sales (rd0_limits); emigration to the Americas (dsp_s01). The dispute over whether 1834 or 1891 were national or political acts is presented from both sides.
- **The strongest form of the Zionist case:** Pinsker's and Herzl's safety argument against a record of pogroms, the May Laws and Dreyfus (s02, s03); continuous Jewish presence in Jerusalem (13,900 in 1880, rd0_aliyah); religious Zionism's reading of the commandment to settle the land (oaths_s01).
- **Jewish dissent from Zionism:** Reform 1885 (s02), the Orthodox readings of the Three Oaths (oaths_s01), Jerusalem's Orthodox community in the 1880s and Jellinek (rd0_aliyah), Edmond's refusal of political Zionism (bc_s04), the four objections (s03).
- **Jews of the Arab world:** the Yemenite arrivals of 1881–82 (rd0_aliyah). Thin; the Damascus affair and Iraqi Jewry (irq_e01) are Baseline material.
- **Real antisemitism, named plainly:** the pogroms and May Laws (s02), Dreyfus (s03), the 1846 'Satan' pamphlet (bc_s02), Catholic hostility to the papal loans framed by the Rothschilds' Jewishness (bc_s03), the 'three cities' trope (bc_s06).
- **Palestinian citizens of Israel:** not applicable before 1948.
- **Key terms examined:** 'colony' (the settlers' own word, rd0_aliyah and bc_s04); 'Zionism' (Birnbaum, 1890); 'Semitic' and 'antisemitism' (s02, linked to the Baseline); 'sovereign' (bc_s01, bc_s06); 'restoration' (bc_s05).
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** Britain's stated interest in 1840 and 1875 ('preserve Turkey'; 'a highway to our Indian Empire'); the US and British protests against Ottoman rules on religious-equality grounds (rd0_limits); the City as a documented lobby rather than a hidden sovereign (bc_s01).
- **Money and political alignment:** the Bank of England's lenders and the Crown (rd0_bank1694); the Rothschild houses as creditors to states and the Pope (bc_s02, bc_s03); Edmond's money built colonies but refused the political movement (bc_s04); Hirsch's money chose Argentina (s04b).
- **Sources kept independent:** the Ottoman restrictions now rest on US and British diplomatic documents as well as a thesis and a reference summary. Several load-bearing claims still rest on one source (see §3).

## 3. Unverified, conflicting, or single-source, and kept out of the prose unless attributed

From the chapter's own scenes (for the writers and the auditor):
- **bc_s03 rests on one reference article** (Wikipedia, 'Rothschild loans to the Holy See') plus Ferguson as cited there. Not independently verified: the 30 November 1831 contract, its 'exclusivity clause', the 1834 enforcement, the Jewish Encyclopedia's 'guardians of the papal treasure'. Internal conflicts: Tosti as embezzling treasurer vs. a cardinal refinancing with six banks; 'Teresa' vs. 'Maria Luisa' Orsini; London vs. Paris house; oc_ch48 names a different house in its detail and its deep dive.
- **bc_s02 / s01 (Waterloo):** bc_s02 traces the legend to Dairnvaell (1846) and quotes the Rothschild Archive via a personal blog (jwatte.com); s01's deep dive credits an 'information advantage' and Drumont. The archive's own page was not reachable in this pass.
- **Founding dates of the Rothschild houses:** London 1809 (bc_s02) vs. 1811 (s01); Paris 1812 (s01 deep dive, fra) vs. 1817 (rothP).
- **Two men named Nathan Mayer Rothschild:** s01's rothL deep dive calls Walter the son of 'Nathan Mayer Rothschild (1st Baron)'; that is the founder's grandson, not the founder (1777–1836). Writers must not merge them.
- **Edmond's money:** '$5–6 million', '~$200 million today', '~40% of early Yishuv capital' have no citation; money_1948 records the stream as 'Not totaled'.
- **Herzl and Edmond, July 1896:** two sequences (bc_s04: request, refusal, 'rude telegram'; s01 deep dive: refused to receive him, then met via an intermediary).
- **Hirsch's death:** s04b says 1896 and both 'a year before Basel' and 'five years before'. The further £7,000,000 has no separate citation.
- **Der Judenstaat and Uganda:** s03's book entity says the 1896 text weighed Uganda; the Uganda offer dates from 1903.
- **Dreyfus:** the deep dive dates Herzl's witnessing of the degradation to 22 December 1894 and gives the crowd's cry as 'À bas les Juifs'; the narration says 'Death to the Jews'. Not checked against a source in this pass.
- **Der Judenstaat details:** '86 pages' and 'universal male suffrage for Jews only' carry no citation.
- **Herzl's 1895 diary:** the deep dive calls Herzl's private intention 'not disputed' right after presenting a dispute; keep it as disputed.
- **palSem:** 'named nowhere in the founding literature' conflicts with the scene's own Ahad Ha'am reference and with s03.
- **w_kook** cites a page on Hillel Kook, not Rav Abraham Isaac Kook.
- **ott (s01):** 'Palestinian delegates sat in the Ottoman parliament' is now supported for 1877 (al-Khalidi, sole Jerusalem deputy); the plural is not supported beyond that.
- **Migration shares** (90–95% to America, about 3% to Palestine) rest on one reference source.
