## NODE #IMMIG-01 — Zip2-era work authorization
**Claim:** Musk lacked legal work authorization for a period while building Zip2 (1995–97); Kimbal has separately admitted lying to cross the US-Canada border.

**Sources:** WaPo (Sacchetti/Siddiqui/Miroff, Oct 26 2024) — cites court records, company documents, a 2005 email from Musk himself ("no legal right to stay in the country"), on-record statement from Zip2 board member/later-CEO Derek Proudian, and Zip2 HR staff describing processing visas retroactively. Corroborated independently by CNN, Forbes (x2), citing named immigration attorneys (Kuck, Kurzban, Siskind) on the legal mechanics of J-1→H-1B and student-status violations. Musk's own on-record response confirms the J-1/H-1B sequence, which attorneys say is itself an admission of working out-of-status during the gap.

**Countervailing:** Snopes' fact-check pushes specifically on the *label* "illegal immigrant" as Kimbal's own word choice/hyperbole, not on the underlying facts — it doesn't dispute the documented timeline, it disputes the noun.

**Verdict: 📰 REPORTED, Documentary 2/3.** Multiple independent outlets, named sources, a self-authored email, and unanimous attorney read on the legal mechanics. Not 3/3 only because we haven't personally reviewed the underlying court records/company documents — that's a next step if you want it airtight (PACER/court filing pull).

**Sub-claim — Kimbal's border admission:** 📰 REPORTED, on Kimbal's own recorded statement per WaPo; immigration attorney Kurzban characterizes it as entry fraud. Same caveat — verifying by locating the original interview clip would move this to ✅.

## THE STRUCTURAL QUESTION — "why isn't ICE after them" (flagged, not yet resolved)

This is a different *kind* of claim than IMMIG-01 — it's not "did this happen," it's "why is enforcement asymmetric," and it needs its own node rather than riding on the emotional force of the first one. Two things have to be checked before that argument can be scored, or it collapses into assertion:

1. **Current status.** If Musk later naturalized as a US citizen (needs verification — flagging as open), a 30-year-old visa-status violation is not something ICE has jurisdiction to act on today regardless of wealth — it's not enforcement discretion, it's that the legal exposure window (visa violations, not a deportable status) closes on naturalization. That's a *different* explanation than "money buys immunity," and if true, it competes with the disparity claim rather than confirming it.
2. **What actually happened at the time.** WaPo's own reporting says investors lined up an immigration attorney and got him a visa *before* things escalated — i.e., the resolution mechanism was "wealthy investors paid for legal-status cleanup," which is itself a real and citable disparity (money buying a fix that an undocumented line cook doesn't have access to) — but it's a different claim than "he's currently an illegal alien ICE is deliberately ignoring." The first is well-supported; the second, as stated, isn't yet — it needs its own verification of present-day status before I can score it rather than assume it.

**Status: ❓ node opened, not scored.** I'll run this down (naturalization date/status) as its own item rather than let it inherit IMMIG-01's tier — that's exactly the discipline your protocol is built to enforce.
