The Others

Each of the following people appears on the lists that circulate. Each is set out the same way: the official finding, then what else the record shows.

Jean-Luc Brunel, 2022

The model agent, charged in France with raping minors and held since December 2021, was found dead in his Paris cell on 19 February 2022 after several earlier attempts on his own life in prison. The official investigation concluded in 2023 that it was suicide. His own lawyers asked the justice ministry for an inquiry, saying the system had failed to account for his high risk of suicide; they said his death was "not guided by guilt but a profound sense of injustice". [cite:ep11_fu_brunel2024] The case against him ended with him. Apart from Maxwell, he was the only person ever charged in any country in connection with Epstein's operation.

Virginia Giuffre, 2025

Virginia Giuffre, the most prominent of the survivors, died by suicide in April 2025 at her home north of Perth, Western Australia, aged 41. In June 2026 the state's police commissioner announced a review of how officers had handled her reports in a dispute with a former partner, prompted by a letter from her family. Her family and sixteen experts on domestic violence have asked the coroner for an inquest, and the commissioner said the coroner and the ombudsman could also investigate. [cite:ep11_1news_giuffre2026] Those processes are open as of October 2026.

Simon Andriesz, 2026

Andriesz, a former broker at BGC Partners who raised concerns about the firm and about business links between its former chief executive, Howard Lutnick, and Epstein with regulators and the FBI, died in Thailand in September 2026. No official cause has been published. His family said he died by suicide and that there was no indication of foul play. Britain's Financial Conduct Authority has appointed one of its board members to review how it handled his whistleblowing. [cite:ep11_newsweek_andriesz2026] Lutnick has described his own dealings with Epstein as "virtually nonexistent". [cite:ep11_newsweek_andriesz2026] The cause of death remains open.

Daniel Siad, 2026

The model scout, named nearly 2,000 times in the files and the subject of several complaints in France, including one of rape, which he denied, was found dead at his home near Paris on 20 July 2026, aged 69. The autopsy found no sign of recent violence and evidence of an old heart attack; further tests ruled out poisoning and found a heart condition, and prosecutors closed the case on 22 October for lack of any criminal offence. He had wanted to give investigators his account. [cite:ep11_cp24_siad2026] [cite:ep11_brut_siad2026]

Steven Hoffenberg, 2022

Epstein's former partner at Towers Financial was found dead, aged 77, in his apartment in Derby, Connecticut, in August 2022, at least a week after he died. The autopsy found no trauma and police found no sign of a struggle or forced entry. [cite:ep11_npr_hoffenberg2022]

Efrain Reyes, 2020

Epstein's last cellmate, the man who warned guards not to leave him alone, was found dead in November 2020, fifteen months later, reportedly after contracting Covid-19. [cite:ep11_wex_reyes2020] Claims that he died weeks after speaking to investigators are wrong on the dates.

Mark Middleton, 2022

The former Clinton White House aide, reported by the Daily Mail to have signed Epstein in on White House visits, died in Arkansas in May 2022. His family and the authorities described it as suicide, and his brother told a court that his depression had worsened in the months before. The first published reports said no weapon was found; a later report said one was. PolitiFact rated the claim that he was murdered false. [cite:ep11_politifact_middleton] His family went to court to keep the photographs of his death private after the family was harassed, and the book names no one else connected to him.

Arthur Shapiro, 1985

The one homicide on these lists predates Epstein's fortune. Shapiro, a Columbus lawyer whose firm represented Les Wexner's company, The Limited, was shot dead in 1985, the day before he was due to testify in a tax-fraud case. The killing is unsolved, and in 1996 the city's police chief was accused of ordering a police report on the case destroyed. [cite:ep11_ifq_shapiro2026] Its only connection to Epstein runs through Wexner's company. Claims about who ordered the killing rest on that disputed report, and the book does not carry them.

Names that do not belong

The longest circulating lists add people with no documented role in Epstein's operation who died of age or illness, among them famous scientists and public figures in their seventies and eighties, and a set of entertainers tied together only by a viral claim about a film. Including them makes the lists longer. It does not make them truer.
