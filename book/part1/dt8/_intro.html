Part Eight holds the Dark Enlightenment's argument against democracy to the standard the rest of this book applies to everything else. The first section sets out the argument in its authors' own words, breaks it into steps, and tests each step against the record, including the uncomfortable fact that this book's own title makes a claim of the same kind as theirs. The second grades their eight principal charges one by one, by a method stated in advance, and says which of them the reforms in Parts Six and Seven would answer and which they would not.
