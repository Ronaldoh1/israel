By March 1957 every army that had crossed into Egypt the previous autumn had left it, and almost nothing else stood where it had stood in July 1956. The stage's own closing section sets out the regional order of that spring and the Eisenhower Doctrine that framed it, in [scene:cw3_aftermath|the architecture in spring 1957]. What follows takes the structure layer by layer, and asks what each layer had settled.

The legal layer was rebuilt in the General Assembly. With Britain and France vetoing the American and Soviet drafts on 30 October, the Security Council used Uniting for Peace to pass the question on, and the Assembly then spoke in numbers no invader could match: 64 to 5 for the cease-fire of 2 November, 74 to 2 on 2 February 1957 to "deplore" Israel's non-compliance. [cite:rs_cw3_unu_ufp] [cite:rs_cw3_unispal_sc119] [cite:rs_cw3_unproj_997] [cite:rs_cw3_jvl_1124] Israel returned behind armistice lines that Ben-Gurion had said on 7 November had "no more validity," calling the armistice agreement itself "dead and buried." [cite:rs_cw3_jvl_bg_7nov] Its claim to the Straits of Tiran rested on a different kind of instrument: an American aide-mémoire calling the Gulf "international waters" and declaring the United States "prepared to exercise the right of free and innocent passage," and Israel's own stated "assumptions" read into the Assembly's record on 1 March. [cite:rs_cw3_jvl_aidememoire] [cite:rs_cw3_jvl_bg_mar57] Neither was a treaty. Inside Israel the war produced a doctrine of law. The Kafr Qasim court, sitting from March 1957, would rule in October 1958 that an order over which "a black flag flies" must be refused. [cite:rs_cw3_orbach_2013] [cite:rs_cw3_wiki_kq] In Egypt the war produced a law of a different kind: Nationality Law No. 391 of 22 November 1956, which barred undefined "Zionists" from nationality. [cite:rs_cw3_jjac_egypt]

The administrative layer shows how each state governed people it treated as a wartime risk. Israel's Arab citizens in the Triangle were placed under a curfew announced, at Kafr Qasim, thirty minutes before it began, and news of the killings there was censored, with the press blackout lifted only after about two months. [cite:rs_cw3_robinson_2003] [cite:rs_cw3_wiki_kq] For four months Israel also ruled Gaza's "some 300,000" people through a military government, reinstated councils and local police under the Israel Police. [cite:rs_cw3_morris_gaza] Egypt detained about 1,000 Jews and put Jewish property under administrators by military proclamation. [cite:rs_cw3_jta_2006_egypt] [cite:rs_cw3_jjac_egypt]

The institutional and financial layer is where the war was decided after the shooting stopped. UNEF, created by the Assembly in early November, reached Egypt on 15 November and stood by March on the Gaza line and at Sharm el-Sheikh, on Egyptian soil and so only as long as Egypt allowed. [cite:rs_cw3_origins_hahn] [cite:rs_cw3_jvl_bg_mar57] Britain's reserves fell below the $2 billion floor; Humphrey made support conditional on "conforming to rather than defying the United Nations"; and the $561 million IMF drawing came only once withdrawal was agreed, while the oil plan was held back, in Eisenhower's words, "until the invading powers accepted immediate withdrawal." [cite:rs_cw3_boughton_fd] [cite:rs_cw3_frus16_d612] Against Israel the levers were named but never pulled: Dulles listed German reparations, Israel Bonds and US aid on 20 February, and the leaders of Congress declined to follow. [cite:rs_cw3_frus17_d120]

The Palestinian Arab side appears in this stage mostly in the record of what was done to it, though not only: at Kafr Qasim the villagers prevented the accused from being brought to the state's reconciliation ceremony. [cite:rs_cw3_palquest_kq] The UNRWA Director received lists naming 275 people at Khan Yunis and 111 at Rafah, and could verify neither; the sources record no Israeli investigation. [cite:rs_cw3_unrwa_a3212] At Kafr Qasim forty-nine citizens are counted dead by the village's own figure, and in November 1957 the state offered IL5,000 per victim and a sulha that villagers were pressured to attend. [cite:rs_cw3_djn_comp] [cite:rs_cw3_robinson_2003] One difference between the two is documented: the killings inside Israel went to a court, and for those in Gaza no proceeding appears in the record.

The regional layer turned on Cairo. Nasser kept the Canal and, in Hahn's words, gained prestige as "a leader who had defied European empires and survived a military invasion by Israel." [cite:rs_cw3_origins_hahn] Eden resigned in January 1957. [cite:rs_cw3_milestones_suez] Between 23,000 and 25,000 Jews left Egypt after a minister's proclamation that "all Jews are Zionists and enemies of the state." [cite:rs_cw3_jta_2006_egypt] [cite:rs_cw3_fathom_julius] Israel came away with a French alliance and a reactor of twice the promised capacity, delivered in September 1957. [cite:rs_cw3_shlaim_sevres] The American layer is the plainest change of all: Washington, which had not been told of Sèvres, asked the Security Council, as Moscow did, for Israel's withdrawal, carried the Assembly against its allies, held back their money and oil, and by March had written its own commitment to the region into law. [cite:rs_cw3_unu_ufp] [cite:rs_cw3_origins_hahn] [cite:rs_cw3_pl85_7]

On this reading of the record, what was settled was who could end a war in the Middle East: not the powers that started this one, but Washington acting through the United Nations and its own Treasury. What was contested was the war's nature. Israel's strongest case was Ben-Gurion's of 17 November, that a return to the armistice meant "a return to murder, blockade, and boycott," backed by 1,300 Israelis killed or wounded across eight years by the Jewish Agency's count. [cite:rs_cw3_jvl_bulganin] [cite:rs_cw3_jafi_fedayeen] The strongest case against it rests on documents too: a secret protocol fixing the attack as a pretext for Britain and France, an annexation point Ben-Gurion sought at Sèvres, and Gaza listed, on the Katif Center's account, among the war's aims. [cite:rs_cw3_shlaim_sevres] [cite:rs_cw3_katif_gaza] The record leaves open how many died at Khan Yunis, Rafah and Port Said; whether Egypt was beaten in Sinai or withdrew; whether Israel meant to keep Gaza; and what Plan Mole, still redacted, provided for. Ben-Gurion's own warning, that the next war might be long and fought without "helping forces from abroad," returns in [scene:s12|1967]. [cite:rs_cw3_jpost_dec56]
