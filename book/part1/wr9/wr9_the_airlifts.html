The Airlifts

The Beta Israel, the "House of Israel", lived for centuries in the Gondar and Tigray regions of Ethiopia, practising a Judaism distinct from the rabbinic tradition. Israel's religious authorities disputed their status until 1973, when the Sephardi Chief Rabbi Ovadia Yosef recognised them as Jews; the Law of Return was applied to them in 1975. [cite:w_beta_israel] [cite:w_ethiopian_jews_israel] Even so, in 1985 the chief rabbinate required a symbolic conversion before full acceptance. [cite:moment_failing]

Moses, Joshua, Solomon

Thousands walked to refugee camps in Sudan, where between about 2,000 and 5,000 died of disease and hunger; the community commemorates 4,000. [cite:w_ethiopian_jews_israel] [cite:w_hadane] Operation Moses flew about 8,000 to Israel between 21 November 1984 and 5 January 1985, with Sudanese and CIA cooperation; it ended early after a press leak in Israel, when Sudan's Arab allies pressed Khartoum to stop it. [cite:w_op_joshua] [cite:w_aliyah_ethiopia] In 1985 Vice-President Bush arranged Operation Joshua, in which the US Air Force flew out the 494 still in Sudan. [cite:w_op_joshua] On 24 and 25 May 1991, as the Ethiopian government collapsed, Operation Solomon carried 14,325 people to Israel in 36 hours. [cite:w_op_solomon] [cite:unpacked_solomon] By the end of 2019 about 155,300 Ethiopian Jews lived in Israel. [cite:w_beta_israel]

After arrival

In 1996 the newspaper Ma'ariv revealed that the national blood service had been discarding blood donated by Ethiopian Israelis, setting off mass protests. [cite:moment_failing] In 2012 and 2013 a television investigation alleged that Ethiopian women had been given the contraceptive Depo-Provera without proper consent; the Health Ministry ordered that it not be given without informed consent, and a later official review did not find a deliberate policy of coercion. [cite:w_ethiopian_jews_israel] In 2015 video of police beating an Ethiopian-Israeli soldier set off protests, and in July 2019 an off-duty officer killed 18-year-old Solomon Teka, setting off protests across the country; the officer said the bullet ricocheted. [cite:moment_failing]

The airlifts came in the same years as roughly a million Jews and their relatives arriving from the former Soviet Union, and during the occupation of southern Lebanon and the First Intifada. [cite:w_soviet_aliyah] Claims that the rescues were staged to distract from the occupation are not supported: they were planned in secret, and publicity ended Operation Moses early. [cite:w_aliyah_ethiopia] The record shows the rescues served Zionism's aim of gathering Jews in, and that the welcome that followed fell short.
