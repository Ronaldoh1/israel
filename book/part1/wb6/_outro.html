Part Six followed the system of movement. The barrier begun during the second intifada runs 85 percent inside the West Bank and will cut off 9 percent of it; Israel says its sole purpose is to stop attacks, and the International Court of Justice, by fourteen votes to one, advised in 2004 that it was contrary to international law and that the settlements it protects were established in breach of that law. [cite:wb_ocha_barrier2022] [cite:wb_asil_icj2004] By the end of 2025, 925 checkpoints, gates and roadblocks restricted 3.4 million Palestinians, 43 percent more than the twenty-year average, [cite:wb_ocha_move2026] and roads such as Route 443, built on expropriated land, stayed closed to them as a main route despite a Supreme Court ruling.
