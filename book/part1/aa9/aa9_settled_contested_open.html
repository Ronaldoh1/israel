Settled, Contested and Open

The architecture

Read as a whole, the aid has the shape of a closed loop. Congress appropriates the money; it is paid to Israel early; Israel spends most of it on American weapons through contracts the Pentagon signs with American firms; those firms and the jobs they create become part of the case for the next appropriation; and the conditions written into law to govern the use of the weapons were, in the one war where they were tested hardest, not applied. [cite:aa_crs_2023] [cite:aa_mil_f15] [cite:aa_pp_620i] The loop is documented at each step. What the record does not show is that it was designed as a loop rather than built up by separate decisions over fifty years, and that question is left open.
