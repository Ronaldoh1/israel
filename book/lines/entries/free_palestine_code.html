"Free Palestine" is a political position, and the record of who says it contradicts the idea that it is always code for hatred of Jews. Jewish organizations such as Jewish Voice for Peace campaign for Palestinian rights [cite:w_jvp], Orthodox anti-Zionist traditions are older than the state of Israel, and Israeli human-rights groups such as B'Tselem document the occupation. A slogan used by these groups and by many Jewish Americans cannot, by itself, be proof of antisemitism.

There is a real kernel in the worry. Some people who use the slogan do express antisemitism, and those incidents are documented. Where criticism of a state ends and hostility toward a people begins is a subject of genuine debate. The fair test is the one the record supports: judge what a person actually says and does, not the slogan they chant.
