Part Five follows the Federal Bureau of Investigation, the agency charged with catching foreign spies and unregistered foreign agents in the United States, through eighty years of cases involving Israel. It begins with what the Bureau knew of the arms network of 1945–48 and the early counterintelligence cases, and its part in the long and unresolved NUMEC inquiry. It then sets out the three cases that reached court, Jonathan Pollard, Ben-Ami Kadish and the AIPAC case, and one that is often miscounted. Finally it places the counterintelligence warnings of 1996 to 2026, each beside Israel's denial, alongside the other track of the relationship: the FBI's office in Israel, its training ties and its own description of a partnership that runs both ways.
