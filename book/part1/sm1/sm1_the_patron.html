The Patron at the Top

The Basel Program of 1897 asked for a home "secured under public law", and Theodor Herzl spent the rest of his life looking for a power to grant it. He asked the Ottoman Sultan, the German Kaiser, the British colonial secretary and the Pope. The Sultan refused, the Kaiser's interest faded, Lord Cromer vetoed El-Arish and Pius X said "we could never sanction it." [cite:rs_rd1_basel_wiki] [cite:w_herzl] [cite:rs_rd1_jerusalemstory] The movement kept looking until Britain, at war with the Ottomans, said yes.

The Balfour Declaration of 2 November 1917 was written as a letter to Lord Walter Rothschild at 148 Piccadilly, asking him to pass it to the Zionist Federation. Its wording began with a draft he sent Balfour on 18 July 1917 after months of drafting with Zionist leaders; the final text was negotiated by others. [cite:w_balfour_decl] The address line is a documented fact. It is not evidence that one family decided British policy, and the Road to 1948 sets out why the letter went to him.

Thirty years later the decisive channel was a friendship. On 18 March 1948 Harry Truman received Chaim Weizmann at the White House. He had refused to see him officially, and the meeting happened because Eddie Jacobson, his former partner in a Kansas City clothing store, asked him to. [cite:forward_state_dept_truman_israel] Recognition on 14 May went against the written advice of the Secretary of State, the Joint Chiefs, the Secretary of Defense and the CIA, whose estimate had warned that American interests would be put in danger. [cite:trumanlibrary_recognition] [cite:cia_ore55]

Moving to the rising power

The second half of the mechanism is the switch. Britain was the patron from 1917; by 1942 the movement's centre had moved to New York, where the Biltmore Program called for a Jewish commonwealth and American politics became the target. [cite:biltmore_medoff] [cite:biltmore_enc] In the 1950s France supplied the arms and the Dimona reactor; the historian Avi Shlaim reads France as hoping to draw Israel into war with Egypt, an interpretation rather than a documented motive. [cite:rs_cw2_shlaim_arms] American arms began with the Hawk on credit in 1962.

Today the patron is bound by statute. Defense Secretary Caspar Weinberger and Defense Minister Ariel Sharon signed a memorandum on strategic cooperation on 30 November 1981. In 2008 Public Law 110-429 wrote Israel's "qualitative military edge" over its neighbours into American law. Ten-year memoranda pledged $26.7 billion, then $30 billion, then $38 billion in 2016. [cite:ow1_kramer_hawk] [cite:ow1_wiki_1981isr] [cite:ow_crs_aid2025] [cite:ow1_wh_mou2016] The same approach reaches Europe: the advocacy network ELNET has credited its own work for Germany's $3.5 billion purchase of Israel's Arrow 3 missile defence, a claim that is ELNET's own, for a decision that was the German government's. [cite:pm_intercept_elnet]

What changed is the form of the tie. In 1948 the channel to the top was one man's friendship and the policy could have gone the other way; today the tie is a set of laws and agreements that outlast any president.
