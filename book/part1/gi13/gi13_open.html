The Central Question, Answered

The volume asked whether there is a programme of territorial expansion beyond the 1967 lines, and who holds it. The answer differs by territory, and the record gives it by named actor.

The West Bank, including East Jerusalem: yes, a documented programme of named parties and governments. East Jerusalem was placed under Israeli law in 1967. [cite:wb_wiki_ejannex] For the rest, the claim is stated in Likud's 1977 platform and its Central Committee's 2017 resolution, in the 2022 government guidelines and coalition agreement, in Smotrich's 82 per cent plan, and in the votes of the Religious Zionism party, Otzma Yehudit, Noam and part of Likud. [cite:gi5_jvl_likud1977] [cite:gi5_toi_likud2017] [cite:gi5_jl_guidelines] [cite:gi5_cgtn_sov] [cite:gi6_toi_82pct] [cite:gi6_toi_readings] Netanyahu has stated "there will be no Palestinian state" and that "this place belongs to us," while his party refused the 2025 bills and his office says final status is for negotiation. [cite:gi6_toi_e1sign] [cite:gi6_bg_nyt_smotrich] What separates the programme from its completion is documented: the US veto, the UAE's red line and Likud's choice of timing. [cite:gi6_ap_trump0925] [cite:gi6_axios_uae2025]

The Golan: yes, legislated. The Begin government and the Knesset annexed it in 1981, and Netanyahu in 2016 and Bennett's coalition in 2021 restated it; Rabin and Barak negotiated over most of it between 1994 and 2000. [cite:gi5_wiki_golanlaw] [cite:gi8_mfa_cab2016] [cite:gi8_cnn_golan2021] [cite:gi8_enc_syrtalks]

Gaza, south Lebanon and south-west Syria: documented holdings with security rationales; intent contested. Each zone is held without a departure date and against the neighbour's and the UN's objection, and none is claimed by law or government decision; Sa'ar denied territorial ambitions in Lebanon. [cite:gi9_nahar_saar] [cite:hz10s_un_guterres] The case for expansion rests on the pattern, on Zamir's "new border line" and on Smotrich's Gaza "bonanza"; the case against rests on the stated purposes and on 1957, 2000 and 2005. [cite:gi9_olj_zamir] [cite:gz_toi_bonanza] [scene:gi9_lebanon|Part Nine: the buffer belt] The wars that produced these zones carried the heaviest tolls in the ledger, counted in Parts Thirteen and Fourteen; the size of a toll is a finding about the war, not evidence of a territorial aim. [cite:gz_aj_year2026] [cite:hz8b_qna_toll] [scene:gi14_gaza|Part Thirteen: the ledger]

Iran, Iraq and Yemen: wars and strikes, no territorial claim. Israel fought two wars with Iran, the second jointly with the United States, struck militia depots in Iraq in 2019 and struck ports, power plants, an airport and leaders in Yemen from 2024, each with a stated reason about weapons, armed groups or attacks on Israel, and each met by the other side's own stated reason. No Israeli map, law or platform claims land in any of the three. [cite:ir10_idf_jun14] [cite:gi15_aje_iraq2019] [cite:gi15_gv_ap46] [scene:gi15_iran|Part Fourteen: Iran]

[cite:gi8_avalon_egtreaty] [cite:gi8_enc_taba] The Revisionist claim to the east bank ended with Herut; the 1994 treaty recognised Jordan's territorial integrity, which the Foreign Ministry restated in 2023 after Smotrich spoke before a map that included Jordan. "Jordan is Palestine" was an argument by named Likud figures about where a Palestinian state should be, not a claim to Jordanian land. The counter-record, and the verdict

The counter-record is as documented as the programme. Israeli governments withdrew from captured land in 1957, 1974, 1975, 1982, 1989, 2000 and 2005, signed treaties with Egypt and Jordan that have held, and offered most of the Golan and, in 2008, all but about 6 per cent of the West Bank against land swaps. [cite:gi12_ied_sinai2] [cite:gi8_incore_jotreaty] [cite:gi8_enc_syrtalks] [cite:gi12_toi_olmert2008] Israelis opposed expansion from the Officers' Letter of 1978 to the 25 former security chiefs of 2019, Olmert declared Greater Israel finished in 2008, and the High Court struck down the Regulation Law in 2020. [cite:gi12_pn_officers] [cite:gi12_jn_25chiefs] [cite:gi1_ynet_olmert] [cite:gi12_toi_reglaw2020] The critics' reply is documented too: most withdrawals came under pressure or in exchange, the court has limited how land is taken rather than whether settlement continues, and part of the 2005 evacuation was repealed in 2023. [cite:rs_cw3_barjoseph_hdiplo] [cite:wb_wiki_stateland] [cite:gi12_toi_repeal2023]

The verdict is precise. There is a programme of expansion beyond the 1967 lines on the record, held by named parties, ministers and governments and stated in platforms, guidelines, a coalition agreement and Knesset votes; it covers the West Bank, including East Jerusalem, and the Golan, has been legislated for East Jerusalem and the Golan, and stops at the Jordan. It is not held by Israelis as a people, a majority of whom told pollsters in 2026 that they opposed annexation. [cite:gi12_jvl_polls] Beyond those lines the record shows holdings explained as security, whose future the election of 27 October 2026 and the end of UNIFIL will test, and beyond those a slogan with no government behind it. The wars of 2023 to 2026 are documented in their own right, with tolls counted by named bodies, bills carried by Israel's treasury and America's, and 28 American service members killed; on the documents they are not steps in that programme, though whether the zones they left become one is argued on both sides. [cite:gz_aj_year2026] [cite:gi16_boi_2025] [cite:ow7_inq_deaths2026] The programme presupposes the state founded in 1948 and the conquest of 1967 together, while the idea of a larger land is older than both; and the line the programme crosses, the 1967 line, is the line along which the UN's organs and the International Court separate the state they accept from the holding of land they reject. [cite:gi2_bu_zo1919] [cite:gi4_cie_june19] [cite:gi18_icj_pr2024] [scene:gi18_legitimacy|Part Seventeen: two questions of legitimacy] Part One took as a hypothesis that the record would prove worse than the rumour. It proves narrower and more concrete: the rumour fails, and the documented claim to the land between the Jordan and the sea stands. [scene:gi1_phrase|Part One: the hypothesis]
