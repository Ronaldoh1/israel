Who Ben-Gvir Is

Itamar Ben-Gvir came out of the Kach movement of Rabbi Meir Kahane, which Israel outlawed and the United States listed as a terrorist organization from 1997 until 2022; [cite:hm_aj_kach2022] he was its youth coordinator. Weeks before Prime Minister Yitzhak Rabin's assassination in 1995, he appeared on television holding an emblem taken from Rabin's car and said: "We got to his car, and we'll get to him too." He was later convicted of incitement to racism and of support for a terrorist organization, and for years kept on his wall a photograph of Baruch Goldstein, who murdered 29 Muslim worshippers in Hebron in 1994, removing it in January 2020 as he sought an electoral alliance with Naftali Bennett. How Netanyahu Brought Him In

Ben-Gvir's road to power ran through Netanyahu. In February 2019 Netanyahu pressed for a joint list that included Ben-Gvir's party, Otzma Yehudit; AIPAC, the leading pro-Israel lobby in Washington, said it would not meet "members of this racist and reprehensible party." In February 2021 a joint list of Otzma and the Religious Zionist Party, "orchestrated by" Netanyahu, brought Ben-Gvir into the Knesset; in 2022 Netanyahu urged the far-right lists to run together again so their votes would not be lost. [cite:hm_wiki_otzma] In November 2022 the joint far-right list won 14 seats, the third-largest party. [cite:hm_jvl_bengvir] On 25 November 2022 Netanyahu's Likud signed a coalition deal giving Ben-Gvir an expanded Ministry of National Security with control of the police and of the Border Police in the West Bank. [cite:hm_jta_coalition2022]

The dependence ran both ways. Netanyahu, on trial since 2020, needed Ben-Gvir's seats for his majority; Ben-Gvir needed Netanyahu for the ministry and its powers.
