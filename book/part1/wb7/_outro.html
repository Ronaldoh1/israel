Part Seven set out the law and the watchers. Israeli settlers live under Israeli law and courts; their Palestinian neighbours under military orders and military courts, which in 2010 convicted in 99.74 percent of trials, a distinction Israel's Justice Ministry defends on security grounds. [cite:wb_acri_twosys] [cite:wb_972_conviction] Thousands are held without charge, 3,577 in September 2025, [cite:wb_mem_hamoked2025] and UNICEF found the ill-treatment of detained children "widespread, systematic and institutionalized." [cite:wb_un_unicef2013] Facial-recognition systems built only on Palestinians' data watch the checkpoints of Hebron. [cite:wb_toi_redwolf] Three human rights organizations, one of them Israeli, concluded that the system amounts to apartheid; Israel called the charge "divorced from reality." [cite:wb_oj_apartheid] [cite:wb_vibes_amnesty] Part Eight turns to the people who lose their homes.
