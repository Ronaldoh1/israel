Part Ten tested whether the network ended with Epstein. Abroad, the files led to arrests and charges; at home, to none. New Mexico, the only active American criminal inquiry, found at least five women abused at the ranch, at least thirty more possible victims, 227 flights and a record of authorities who repeatedly failed to look, and federal prosecutors who would not answer its subpoenas. The people around Epstein were never prosecuted, his money survives under the same management, his properties have been sold, and the modelling route he used is under investigation in Paris, where new victims are still coming forward. The network as people and as protection did not shut down. Whether it still operates is not shown, and no authority has looked.
