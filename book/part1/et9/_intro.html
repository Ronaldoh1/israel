Part Nine is a reading. The eight parts before it followed the Ethiopian Bible through kings, missionaries, armies and a revolution. This part reads four of the things the Ethiopian church kept that the West let go: Enoch's ascent to the throne, the Watchers who brought knowledge down, the heavenly tablets and their true year, and the ark that no one sees. Each section starts from the text, sourced, then says what the inner reading proposes and what the record does not show.
