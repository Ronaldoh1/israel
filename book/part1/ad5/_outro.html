Part Five established that the federal wage floors are fixed dollar sums that lose value whenever Congress does not act: the minimum wage has been $7.25 since 24 July 2009 and in 2025 bought about 40 per cent less than in 1968 and 31 per cent less than when last raised, a full year of full-time work at that rate falls below the 2026 poverty guideline for one person, the tipped cash wage has been $2.13 since 1991, and the overtime salary line stands at $684 a week after the 2024 increase was set aside in court.
