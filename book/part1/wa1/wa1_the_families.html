The Families

Donald Trump

Before he entered politics, Donald Trump and his businesses were involved in thousands of lawsuits; a USA Today analysis counted roughly 3,500. [cite:npr_lawsuits_2016] Trump University was resolved by a $25 million settlement in 2016, and the Trump Foundation was dissolved in 2019 under a New York court agreement, with Trump ordered to pay $2 million. [cite:wiki_legal_affairs]

On 30 May 2024 a Manhattan jury convicted him on 34 felony counts of falsifying business records, making him the first former president convicted of a crime; he was later sentenced to an unconditional discharge. [cite:cbc_hush_money_verdict] [cite:wiki_legal_affairs] Civil juries found him liable for sexually abusing and defaming the writer E. Jean Carroll, awarding $5 million in 2023 and $83.3 million for further defamation in 2024; appeals courts upheld both, and in July 2026 he asked the Supreme Court to hear the larger case. [cite:gulfnews_carroll_verdict2] [cite:scotusblog_carroll_cert] The federal cases over January 6 and classified documents were dismissed after his 2024 election. [cite:wiki_legal_affairs] He denies wrongdoing in all of them and calls the prosecutions political.

The Kushners

Charles Kushner, a New Jersey developer, pleaded guilty in 2004 to 18 counts of illegal campaign contributions, tax evasion and witness tampering, the last for arranging to have his brother-in-law, who was cooperating with investigators, filmed with a prostitute and sending the tape to his sister; the prosecutor, Chris Christie, called it one of the most loathsome crimes he had prosecuted. He served two years in prison. [cite:wiki_charles_kushner] Trump pardoned him on 23 December 2020, and the Senate confirmed him as ambassador to France on 19 May 2025, by 51 to 45. [cite:wiki_charles_kushner] [cite:cbs_ckushner_france] In 2026 France restricted his access to its officials after he failed to answer a summons. [cite:france24_ckushner]

His son Jared led the family's purchase of 666 Fifth Avenue in 2007 for a record $1.8 billion. In 2018 a unit of Brookfield, a firm in which Qatar's sovereign fund holds a significant stake, took a 99-year lease that rescued the building; Senators Warren and Carper questioned the timing. [cite:wiki_kushner_companies] [cite:warren_freddiemac_letter] Jared received a top-secret clearance in 2018 over the objections of career officials. [cite:wiki_jared_kushner]

The family foundation and Israel

Tax filings first reported by Haaretz show that the Charles and Seryl Kushner Foundation gave $58,500 to settlement organisations between 2011 and 2013, including at least $38,000 to a yeshiva in Beit El, a small part of about $8.5 million it gave in that period, mostly to Jewish schools and hospitals; it also gave about $315,000 to the Friends of the Israel Defense Forces, on whose board Jared sat. Jared was a co-director of the foundation from 2006 to 2015 and did not list the position on his financial disclosure. [cite:toi_kushner_settlements] [cite:toi_kushner_nondisclose]
