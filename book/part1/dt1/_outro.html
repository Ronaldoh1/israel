Part One established the claim and the ground it stands on. Thiel wrote in 2009 that he no longer believed "that freedom and democracy are compatible"; Yarvin said in 2025 that he did not "believe in actually voting at all"; Land proposed "No voice, free exit." [cite:de1_cato_thiel] [cite:de1_audit_nyt_voting] [cite:de35b_land_ebook] Their account of the symptoms is largely right: trust in the federal government stands at 17 per cent, 69 per cent of Americans are dissatisfied with how democracy works, four democracy indices have downgraded the country, and when the affluent and everyone else disagree the affluent have an edge, though its size is disputed. [cite:de7_pew_trust2025] [cite:dt1_pew_indices2026] [cite:ad15_jr_gilens] [cite:ad15_bashir] What the record does not show is that democracy caused them; the indices record a country becoming less democratic, and the system has kept features that do not weigh each vote equally.
