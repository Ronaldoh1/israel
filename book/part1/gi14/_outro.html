Part Thirteen found the human cost of the wars since October 2023 to be documented in detail, though no single count is complete. In Gaza, after the attack of 7 October 2023 that killed 1,195 people in Israel, the health ministry counted 74,382 Palestinians killed by 12 October 2026. A peer-reviewed estimate suggests that count runs low, and of the dead identified by the end of 2025, 21,283 were children. About three-quarters of housing units were destroyed or damaged, and 94 per cent of the population needed shelter. [cite:gz_hrw_oct7] [cite:gz_aj_year2026] [cite:gz_aj_lancet] [cite:gi14_ocha_gazasnap] [cite:gz_rdna2026] In the West Bank more Palestinians were killed in three years than in the seventeen before them, with 59 Israelis killed by West Bank Palestinians in the first two years of the war. Some 42,000 Palestinians were displaced, most of them from refugee camps the army still held in October 2026. [cite:gi14_oxfam_wb] [cite:wb_ohchr_1001] [cite:gi14_ocha_wbsnap] In Lebanon the health ministry counted 8,954 people killed from 8 October 2023 to October 2026, across two wars that Hezbollah opened and the truce between them. On Israel's side, the record shows 128 deaths in the first round and 42 in the second, along with 60,000 Israelis displaced from the north. By October 2026 Israel held about 6 per cent of the country. [cite:hz8b_qna_toll] [cite:hz7_alma_summary] [cite:hz8b_npr_lines] [cite:hz7_afp_north_return] In each place Israel's stated reason was security against an armed enemy whose own attacks are on the record. Whether the operations also served expansion is documented as a programme only in the West Bank, and elsewhere remains an argument. Part Fourteen continues the ledger in Syria, Iran, Iraq and Yemen.
