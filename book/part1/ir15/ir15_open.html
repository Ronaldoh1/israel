What Remains Open

The stockpile. The last figure is the IAEA's estimate of 440.9 kg of uranium enriched up to 60 percent, as of 13 June 2025. In June 2026 the IAEA said it could not give "any information on the current size, composition or whereabouts" of it, and on 7 September Grossi told its Board that the agency had lost continuity of knowledge. [cite:ir10_ap_nov25] [cite:ir10_isis_jun26] [cite:ir11_iaea_sep7] On 9 September the Board referred Iran to the Security Council by 23 votes to 3; Iran blamed the American and Israeli attacks for the lack of access. [cite:ir11_am_referral] [cite:ir11_aj_iaea_sep8]

The treaty. A bill to leave the NPT was tabled in Iran's parliament on 28 March 2026, and the foreign ministry said Iran "has never sought, and does not seek, nuclear weapons." No formal notice of withdrawal had been reported as of mid-October. [cite:ir4_aca_npt2026] [cite:ir4_k24_npt]

The war's end. The Islamabad Memorandum of 17 June collapsed on 8 July, each side saying the other broke it first, and the American blockade of Iran's ports has been in force since 14 July. [cite:ir11_sole_mou] [cite:ir11_aj_jul8] [cite:hz12a_cbs_oct] Iran's offer to reopen the strait was rejected in September, an American counter-proposal followed, and on 8 October Trump said he would not attack Iran before the midterm elections of 3 November. As of 19 October 2026 no agreement had ended the war. [cite:ir11_egt_proposal] [cite:ir11_ap_midterms]

The succession. The Assembly of Experts named Mojtaba Khamenei Supreme Leader on 8 March 2026, a hereditary succession that, Reuters noted, the Islamic Republic's ideology frowns on. [cite:ir11_reu_mojtaba] A war whose American and Israeli authors spoke of changing Iran's government produced, in its first weeks, a son in his father's place; what his rule will become is not yet on the record. [cite:ow7_npr_epicfury]

The Three Theses, Tested

That Netanyahu's warnings were false alarms. The record supports this for every warning that carried a date: none produced a bomb, and the American agencies and the IAEA found the weapons work had stopped in 2003, with nothing credible after 2009. [cite:ir5_iaea_gov2015] [cite:hz11_wh_nie2007] It does not support the stronger claim that the danger was invented. The pre-2003 weapons work was real, the IAEA found Iran in breach of its safeguards obligations on 12 June 2025, and on capacity, a 60 percent stockpile, the record largely bore Netanyahu out. [cite:ir4_wnn_pmd2015] [cite:ir5_aj_iaea2025] [cite:ir4_ap_may2025] What remains argument is why no bomb came. Israel's government credits pressure and sabotage, and the 2007 estimate itself said the halt answered "international scrutiny and pressure"; its critics, among them Israel's own former intelligence chiefs, say the warnings outran the evidence, and in June 2025 the IAEA found no "systematic plan" to build a weapon. [cite:ir5_wh_nie2007] [cite:ir5_ap_dagan2011] [cite:ir6_arabnews_dagan2011] [cite:ir5_tbs_grossi]

That Iran does not want war with the United States. The record supports this in its narrow form. Iran negotiated with Washington in 2013–15, 2021–22, 2025 and 2026, was in talks when it was struck in June 2025 and again in February 2026, and before 2026 warned in advance of each direct strike on American bases, killing no one. [cite:ir7_usip_2231] [cite:ir12_too_sixth] [cite:ir12_euronews_feb26] [cite:ir12_aj_iraqwarn] [cite:ir12_ahram_udeid] It does not support a reading of Iran's government as peaceable toward Americans: a US court found Iran behind the 1983 barracks bombing in an undefended case, the Pentagon attributes at least 603 deaths in Iraq to militias Iran armed, its officers were convicted or charged over plots on American soil, and its strikes killed American soldiers in 2026, after the United States and Israel had attacked it. [cite:hz12_dcd_anderson] [cite:ir12_mt_603] [cite:ir12_doj_arbabsiar] [cite:hz12_ktar_deaths] Iran denies ordering those attacks. Whether its calibrated pressure through allies is defence or expansion is argument; the analyst Ali Vaez warned in February 2026 that Iran's leaders had come to regard conflict as less dangerous than accepting Washington's terms. [cite:ir12_na_forward] [cite:ir12_npr_vaez2026]

That the United States was dragged in. The record supports Israeli influence: requests to strike in 2008 and 2013, the 2015 speech to Congress, the 2018 archive eight days before the withdrawal, Israel's first strike during talks in 2025, and in 2026 Rubio's statement that the United States acted because it knew of a planned Israeli action. [cite:hz12_cato_hoffman] [cite:ir7_fox_speech] [cite:ir7_pmo_archive] [cite:ir10_an_round6] [cite:ir14_aje_rubio] It does not support compulsion. Every American operation was ordered by a president; Bush and Obama refused Israel's requests; Trump told Israel in June 2025, "DO NOT DROP THOSE BOMBS," and said in 2026 that he may have forced Israel's hand. [cite:ir14_wiki_earnestwill] [cite:ir10_kt_violations] [cite:ir14_sn_forced] Whether either war would have happened without Israel is a counterfactual: Joe Kent resigned blaming "pressure from Israel and its powerful American lobby"; Senator Mitch McConnell said the letter was tainted by antisemitism, and J Street's Ilan Goldenberg placed the responsibility with Trump. [cite:ir14_fox_kent] [cite:ir14_npr_kent] The finding that holds is narrower than the thesis: a war on a timetable set partly by Israel, against most of the public, without Congress's authorisation. [cite:ir14_sac_mar5] [cite:hz12_qu_mar2026]

The Paradox, as the Record Has It

The question is often put as a paradox: Israel and the United States at war with a state whose constitution recognises its Jews and gives them a seat in parliament. [cite:ir3_const_en] The record treats it as two facts that do not cancel. The recognition is real: Homayoun Sameh Najafabadi holds the seat, about 100 synagogues remain, and Freedom House finds the community "relatively free to worship." [cite:ir3_wiki_sameh] [cite:ir3_wiki_history] [cite:ir3_fh_2026] The community is smaller than often said: censuses counted 8,756 Jews in 2011 and 9,826 in 2016, current outside estimates run from about 8,000 to 15,000, and no current source supports "over 20,000." [cite:ir3_afp_census2012] [cite:ir3_wiki_exodus] [cite:ir3_rns_synagogue] The restriction is also real, and in wartime the community's leaders condemned Israel under pressure the State Department documented, while USCIRF reported that the authorities held Jews collectively responsible for Israel's acts. [cite:ir3_iranwire_rallies] [cite:ir3_uscirf_2026] Its members bore costs from both directions: questioning by their own government, and the Rafi'-Nia synagogue destroyed by an Israeli strike on 7 April 2026, which the Israeli military called collateral damage. [cite:ir3_toi_summoned] [cite:ir3_nw_assoc2026] No Israeli or American statement examined gave the community's protection as a war aim, and its existence settles neither whether Iran's government was a threat nor whether the wars were justified.
