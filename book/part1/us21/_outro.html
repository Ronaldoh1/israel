This volume set two states side by side over four decades and found that neither serves the people under its authority better on every measure. Israel covers every citizen and resident's health care by a 1995 law, guarantees fifteen weeks of paid leave after a birth, ties its wage floor to the average wage, and has moved from level life expectancy with the United States in 1980 to about four years ahead, with half America's infant mortality. [cite:us16_wiki_ilhealth] [cite:us18_nii_maternity2018] [cite:us13_kz_minwagelaw] [cite:us16_wb_le] [cite:us16_wb_imr] The United States pays higher average wages, scored well above Israel in the 2025 PISA round, performs civil marriages in every state and ranks far higher for press freedom, while leaving 26.7 million people uninsured, holding its federal minimum wage at $7.25 since 2009, imprisoning 542 people per 100,000 and counting 745,652 homeless on one night.
