# Stage One (cw1, 1949–1953): research notes

Verified live, 14 October 2026. 15 entries, 144 claims, 15 logged disputes. Every claim cites a page fetched in this session. Two pages could not be fetched (permission timeouts) and were replaced: the FRUS Iran retrospective preface (history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1951-54Iran/preface) and the National Security Archive's new-domain briefing book URL (the nsarchive2 mirror was used instead).

## cw1_non_identification_korea_1948_1951
- **Agree:** Israel's founding doctrine was friendship with both blocs (Basic Principles, 9 March 1949); the Korean vote of July 1950 was the break at the declaratory level; no troops were sent.
- **Disagree:** why the tilt happened. Brecher (arms and aid) and Shlaim (immigration, American Jewry, reparations) against the Israeli establishment's view that Soviet policy drove it. Labelled interpretation.
- **Not verified:** the exact UN resolution(s) Israel voted for in late June/early July 1950 (the sources speak of "the UN resolution condemning North Korea"); the State Department lists Ben-Gurion's visit as 3–8 May 1951 but says he left on 31 May.

## cw1_tripartite_declaration_1950
- **Agree:** the text (three paragraphs), date and authors; it followed the London meeting and NSC 65/3.
- **Disagree:** Israel's reception. The US report to the NSC says Israel was "pleased"; Shlaim, from Ben-Gurion's diary, says Israel rejected it. Both are documented; they may reflect public vs private reactions (interpretation, not stated by either source).
- **Not verified:** the Near East Arms Coordinating Committee's workings and actual deliveries.

## cw1_us_grant_aid_1951_1952
- **Agree:** Israel asked for $150M in March 1951; the Administration offered $23.5M; Congress raised it; FY1953 settled at $70.228M + $2.772M.
- **Disagree:** the FY1952 Israel figure: $65M (JTA obituary) vs ~$68.8M Senate authorization (Kenen's letter, partly illegible). rd6's CRS table gives 86.4 for 1952 (calendar/fiscal basis differs; do not mix).
- **Not verified:** the enacted FY1952 appropriation; Israel Hayom's "146–65" House vote (no year).

## cw1_lobby_cold_war_frame_1950_1954
- **Agree:** Kenen came to Washington in 1951 to lobby for aid; the separate lobbying committee existed by 1954; the name AIPAC came later.
- **Disagree:** dates and names: JTA (1951 committee; AIPAC 1954) vs JVL (AZCPA 1954; AIPAC 1959); rd6/s07g give 1953–54 for AZCPA. Each refers to a different step; writers must say which.
- **Note:** the Blaustein exchange is given from a secondary account (no primary text fetched; the BGU PDF redirected).

## cw1_intelligence_founding_1948_1952
- **Agree:** 30 June 1948 restructuring; the 13 December 1949 directive (primary text) under Shiloah; Harel succeeded him in 1952.
- **Not verified independently:** the 18 February 1949 legal anchoring of the Shin Bet (JVL only); GlobalSecurity's 1 April 1951 reorganization date (consistent with Shiloah's 2 March 1951 order in the Encyclopaedia Judaica).

## cw1_cia_israel_liaison_origins
- **Agree:** Ben-Gurion's 1951 visit opened the door; Angleton came to run the Israel account; the 1956 Khrushchev speech was a turning point.
- **Disagree:** the start date and formality. df_s04 says formal liaison began in 1951 under Angleton as counterintelligence chief; the CIA historian dates his CI Staff post to 1954; McConnell says "how early is a matter of dispute" and dates the no-spying understanding to the mid-1950s.

## cw1_tpajax_1953
- **Agree:** the coup happened with CIA and MI6 planning and money; the CIA's own history says it "planned and helped implement" it; some 300 died in Tehran.
- **Disagree:** approval date (June per the LOC study and Roosevelt's memoir vs 11 July per CIA documents; no written presidential authorization found); agency (Anglo-American vs Iranian actors) and motive (oil vs Soviet fear) — Byrne records both readings.
- **Cost:** $1,000,000 station budget (April 1953) and $5,330,000 grand total over three phases; Risen's $5M to Zahedi after the coup. s11's "~$5M" should be replaced by these.

## cw1_iran_israel_1947_1953
- **Agree:** de facto recognition on 6 March 1950; consulate closed 7 July 1951; no documented Israeli role in the coup.
- **Conflict to log (SOURCE-CONFLICTS.md):** Iranica's phrasing implies Turkey recognized Israel in September 1949; rd6 (tk_s03) has March 1949. Not used here.
- **Not verified:** Mossad training of SAVAK from 1957 (only the September 1957 intelligence-exchange offer is documented here).

## cw1_soviet_break_1949_1953
- **Agree:** trial, plot, bombing, break (11 February 1953), restoration (July 1953).
- **Disagree:** who drove the rupture (Sharett's view vs Bialer). Shlaim calls the Tel Aviv target a "consulate"; the Center for Israel Education says "legation"/"embassy"; use "legation" as in the Soviet note.
- **Caution:** one fetched source (Jewish Currents) wrongly says relations were not restored until 1991; it was not used.
