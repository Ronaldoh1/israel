The stages of this book follow the road that was taken. This appendix follows the roads that were not. Between 1891 and 1948 eight other lands were proposed, offered or studied as homes for Jews: Argentina, the northern Sinai, British East Africa, the Soviet Far East, Madagascar, Alaska, the Dominican Republic and Suriname. Each is told from its own documents, then measured on six of the nine things the state needed (money, land and settlers, arms, diplomacy, organization and recognition; legitimacy, people and land held are measured only against the Palestine record, and leaving them out can only flatter the other lands) and given a chance of statehood on the same formula as every test on the network map.
