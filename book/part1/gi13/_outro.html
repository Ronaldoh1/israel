This volume followed a phrase from a verse in Genesis to a Knesset vote. It found that the widest version of "Greater Israel," from the Nile to the Euphrates, rests on one reading of one verse, a diary note Herzl never acted on and a minority religious current, and that the inscription, the flag's rivers and the coin map offered as proof of it are myths or misreadings. [cite:gi1_bg_gen1518] [cite:gi1_pipes_calumny] [cite:gi10_csm_1980] [cite:gi10_mfa_flag] [cite:gi10_boi_coins] It found a narrower claim fully documented: the Revisionists' "both banks" until Herut gave it up, then the land between the Jordan and the sea in the 1967 manifesto, the 1977 Likud platform and the 2022 government guidelines, the Golan placed under Israeli law in 1981, and a West Bank annexation track pursued by named ministers and parties through 2025 and 2026.
