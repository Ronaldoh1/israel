Armed groups led by Hamas crossed the fence at more than sixty points and killed about 1,200 people, most of them civilians, and took 251 hostages; 378 were killed at the Nova festival alone. [cite:gz_hrw_oct7] [cite:gz_wiki_nova] [cite:gz_pbs_idfprobe] Israel had held the attackers' plan for more than a year and judged it beyond them, and its army has since said it underestimated Hamas; no state commission of inquiry has been set up. Israel's army was reported to have invoked the Hannibal procedure, and its own probes have found individual cases of Israelis killed by Israeli fire, without a published total.
