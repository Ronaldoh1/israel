Part Eleven follows one minister through the war's ceasefires. Itamar Ben-Gvir, the far-right national security minister, opposed the deals that brought hostages home, in 2023 and 2025, said on the record that he had used his party's leverage to stop such deals "time and again," left the government when a ceasefire began and returned the day the bombing resumed. This part sets out who he is, how Benjamin Netanyahu brought him into government and came to depend on him, how each truce ended according to both sides, and how the language used about Hamas compares with the record of its strength and of the aid that reached Israel while the war went on.
