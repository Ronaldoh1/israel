Part Two set out to find London in the making of Israel, and found four Londons, each with its own record.

The Corporation of the City of London is a real and unusual body. It holds its rights by charter and by prescription older than Parliament's reforms, it was left untouched in 1835, it gives businesses a vote, and it keeps an officer, the Remembrancer, who watches Parliament from the under-gallery on its behalf. [cite:dv2_lma_william] [cite:dv2_col_mayoralty] [cite:dv2_wiki_remembrancer] Its power is the power of a privileged local authority and a voice for the finance industry. It is not sovereign, and in the decisions that produced a Jewish national home and then a state it does not appear. [cite:wb_jc_rothschild2017]

The London market is a different and much larger thing: the state's bank since 1694, the home of chartered companies that once ruled a subcontinent, and today the largest center of currency trading in the world, ringed by offshore jurisdictions whose rules an advocacy group and the British tax authority describe very differently. [cite:rs_rd0_boe_museum] [cite:dv2_wiki_eic] [cite:dv2_boe_triennial_2025] [cite:dv2_ferret_cthi_2024] Its power is to lend, clear, price and give legal form, and the Zionist movement used it: a bank incorporated there in 1899, a land fund in 1907, a fundraising arm founded there in 1920. [cite:jct_wiki] [cite:dv2_wiki_jnf] [cite:keren_hayesod_wiki]

The Rothschild house stood inside the City's institutions, won its seat in Parliament for the City in 1858 after a long fight against a Christian oath, chaired the gold fix for eighty-five years, and received the Balfour letter as the movement's address in London. [cite:dv2_wiki_lionel] [cite:dv2_ra_goldfix] [cite:wb_bl_addms41178] And the British state, in Whitehall, issued the promise, held the Mandate, managed the country's money through a London board, and then, from 1939, restricted, abstained, cut the money off and recognized the new state late. [cite:rs_a22_frus] [cite:dv2_unccp_assets] [cite:rs_rd4_wp_cojs_text] [cite:rs_rd5_langham_exit]

What is settled, then, is that London was a parent, and which London it was: the British government and the legal and financial machinery of the London market, decisively between 1917 and 1939. What is contested, or simply unrecorded in the sources used here, is the founders' reason for choosing English incorporation, the fate of the blocked balances, and how much of the movement's money passed through London rather than New York. What the record rules out is the story of a sovereign Square Mile directing the whole. The next part turns to the second parent, a city that is a sovereign state, and to the harder question this volume has promised to test: whether the Holy See's public refusal to support a Jewish state was the whole of its record, or a front for something else.
