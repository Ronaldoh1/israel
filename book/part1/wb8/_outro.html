Part Eight followed the people who lose their homes. In 2025 Israeli authorities demolished 1,657 Palestinian structures, 93 percent for lack of a permit that Israel's planning system seldom grants; Israel's position is that unauthorized building is enforced against under the interim agreements. [cite:wb_ocha_snap2025] [cite:wb_cogat_2015] Israel's Supreme Court upheld the firing zone at Masafer Yatta and approved the demolition of Khan al-Ahmar, and East Jerusalem's courts hear eviction cases under a law that lets only one side reclaim what it lost in 1948. [cite:wb_sabrang_masafer] [cite:wb_toi_khan] [cite:wb_nrc_sj] Settler attacks rose to 1,828 in 2025; herding outposts spread across about 14 percent of the West Bank; 57 communities were emptied in little over a year after October 2023; and 93.6 percent of police files on settler violence closed without a charge, while Israel's leaders condemned the attacks as the work of a minority.
