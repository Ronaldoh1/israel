The 2026 war. As of October 2026 no agreement had formally ended the American and Israeli war with Iran; after the ceasefire collapsed on 8 July the contest had turned to a US blockade and Iranian attacks on shipping in the Strait of Hormuz, American casualties were still being added in September, and the cost stood at $43.6 billion by Central Command's estimate of 3 September. The Thesis, Tested

The thesis this volume tests holds that Hezbollah, the Houthis and the Iraqi militias serve as tools and excuses for continued war, for military aid and for American involvement. The record answers it in three parts.

What the record supports. Armed groups have been tools, on both sides. Iran funds and arms Hezbollah by Hezbollah's own account and supplies the Houthis' missiles by the UN panel's; Israel paid and armed the South Lebanon Army for a quarter-century, armed rebels on the Golan, and in Gaza, by Netanyahu's statement, "activated" clans that ACLED assesses Israel armed. [cite:hz6_alarabiya_2016] [cite:hz10_un_poe2024] [cite:hz4_crs_sla] [cite:hz11_toi_eisenkot] [cite:hz11_ynet_clans] The threat these groups pose is written into the laws that fund Israel's military, such as the April 2024 supplemental, and the United States gave at least $21.7 billion in military aid in the two years after 7 October 2023. [cite:ow_crs_aid2025] [cite:hm_an_costsofwar] Threats were described as nearer than they proved: the bomb said in 2012 to be about a year away had not appeared by 2025, when American agencies assessed that none was being built. [cite:hz11_rferl_spycables] [cite:hz11_bg_gabbard] No war ended the enemy it was fought against, and most American money was spent by decision in Washington rather than forced by attack. [cite:hz8_am_mar2] [cite:hz12_cow_2025]

What the record does not support. It contains no case of Israel or the United States creating or arming Hezbollah, the Houthis or the Iraqi militias. [cite:hz4_crs_sla] The threats were not inventions: Hezbollah fired first in 2023 and in 2026, a rocket attributed to it killed twelve children, the Houthis sank four ships, and these groups killed Americans in Beirut and at Tower 22. [cite:hz7_aj_8oct] [cite:hz8_am_mar2] [cite:hz7_wiki_majdal] [cite:hz10_olj_factbox] [cite:hz12_wiki_barracks] [cite:hz10_abc_tower22] The public record examined here holds no document showing a war prolonged to secure aid, contracts or exports. [scene:hz11_gains|Who gains] Nor did Washington always follow: it paused a bomb shipment in 2024 and left the Houthi war in 2025 while Israel's went on, and Netanyahu signed the 2024 truce over Ben-Gvir's objection. [cite:ow8_axios_pause] [cite:hz10_aje_truce] [cite:hz7_bg_ceasefire]

What is argument. Whether the groups' existence is the reason for the wars or the reason given for them is a question of motive. One reading holds that the threat is the standing justification, renewed by each campaign; the other that the groups chose their wars and the money followed the threat. Both fit the documents. The narrower finding that survives is this: the groups are real enemies who attack and kill; the wars against them have not ended them; and every round has produced beneficiaries who are documented, while their influence on the decision to fight is not.
