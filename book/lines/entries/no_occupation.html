Under international law, the West Bank is occupied territory, and the world's highest court has said so directly. On 19 July 2024 the International Court of Justice advised that Israel's continued presence in the West Bank, East Jerusalem and Gaza is unlawful, that it must end "as rapidly as possible," and that Israel must stop all new settlement, evacuate settlers and make reparation. Several of those findings passed 14 to 1. [cite:icj_press_19jul24] The court noted that occupation law sets no time limit; it was annexation-like policies and the denial of self-determination that made the presence unlawful. [cite:transcend_icj_ao]

The UN Security Council reached the same view of settlements in December 2016, voting 14 to 0 in Resolution 2334 that they have "no legal validity." [cite:deccan_unsc_2334] Israel rejects these findings, and the 2024 opinion is advisory, not an enforceable judgment. But "disputed" is Israel's position, not the finding of any court or UN body.
