The Dead, Since 7 October 2023

In the two years from 7 October 2023 to 17 October 2025, the UN Human Rights Office counted 1,001 Palestinians killed in the West Bank and East Jerusalem, 213 of them children. Israeli forces killed 968 of them; settlers, alone or together with soldiers, killed 33. In the same two years 59 Israelis, 22 of them members of the security forces, were killed in attacks by West Bank Palestinians in the West Bank and in Israel. [cite:wb_ohchr_1001]

The UN office concluded that 449 of those killed by Israeli forces were unarmed and not involved in violence when they were shot, and that Israel announced investigations only rarely. It said the pattern suggested force was being used "as a tool to control and repress." The army described many of those killed in its airstrikes as "terrorists," and in one case acknowledged it had not sufficiently verified who it struck. [cite:wb_ohchr_1001] In 2025 alone, the UN's humanitarian office counted 240 Palestinians and 17 Israelis killed in the West Bank and Israel. [cite:wb_ocha_snap2025]

Iron Wall: the Northern Camps

On 21 January 2025 the army launched Operation Iron Wall in the Jenin refugee camp, and extended it to Tulkarm within the week. The army's stated aim was to degrade the armed groups' infrastructure and remove imminent threats. The Palestinian Authority's own forces had been fighting the armed groups in Jenin camp since December 2024. In February Israeli tanks entered Jenin, the first in the West Bank since 2002, and the defense minister, Israel Katz, said the troops would stay. The army reported 102 militants killed. [cite:wb_wiki_ironwall]

More than 33,000 people were displaced from the three camps in January and February 2025, and as of July 2026 they had not been allowed back. In September 2026 the UN Human Rights Office reported that the displacement may amount to forcible transfer, a crime against humanity, and called it "large-scale, long-term and systematic." Israel describes the operation as counter-terrorism against armed groups and their weapons in the northern West Bank. [cite:wb_jurist_ohchr2026] As of October 2026 the operation was still listed as ongoing, with the army in full control of the three camps. [cite:wb_wiki_ironwall]
