Part Thirteen tests the claim in this volume's title. It first sets out the documented record of Israeli intelligence activity directed at the United States, from the Pollard case and the unresolved NUMEC question to the NSA's own assessments and the reported operations of 2015, 2019 and 2026, together with Israel's denials and the record of American collection on Israel. It then follows one item of American intelligence or one American weapon from the moment it leaves American hands, and shows where Washington's ability to verify its use ends: in Israeli systems, in a special vetting forum, and in a territory closed to independent reporters. It ends with why so little of this is followed up in the press, and with a single table of what flows each way and what the United States can see.
