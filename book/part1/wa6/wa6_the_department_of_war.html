The Department of War

Pete Hegseth graduated from Princeton in 2003, served in the Army National Guard with deployments to Guantánamo Bay, Iraq and Afghanistan, earning two Bronze Stars and the rank of major, led veterans' advocacy groups, and from 2017 to 2024 co-hosted Fox & Friends Weekend. [cite:hg_britannica_hegseth] [cite:hg_cbsnews_whois] In 2021 he was removed from the Guard unit assigned to the presidential inauguration over a Jerusalem cross tattoo that officials flagged as also used by Christian nationalist groups; he called it a Christian symbol. [cite:hg_britannica_hegseth]

The confirmation

In October 2017 a woman told police in Monterey, California, that Hegseth had sexually assaulted her; he said the encounter was consensual, and the district attorney declined to file charges in January 2018. In 2020 he paid her $50,000 under a confidential settlement, which he disclosed when asked during his confirmation. [cite:hg_pbs_dadeclined] [cite:hg_cnn_50k] A former sister-in-law gave senators a sworn statement alleging heavy drinking and that his second wife had feared for her safety; both he and his former wife denied abuse. [cite:hg_nbcwash_50k] The Senate confirmed him on 24 January 2025 by 51 to 50, with Vice President Vance breaking the tie after three Republicans voted against him. [cite:hg_npr_confirmed]

In office

On 21 February 2025 Trump fired the chairman of the Joint Chiefs, General Charles Brown, and Hegseth removed the chief of naval operations and the Air Force vice chief; in August 2025 he fired the director of the Defense Intelligence Agency, Lieutenant General Jeffrey Kruse, after a preliminary assessment found the June strikes had set Iran's nuclear programme back by months, not "obliterated" it; and in April 2026, in the middle of the war with Iran, he removed the Army chief of staff, General Randy George, and two other generals without giving a reason. [cite:armytimes_purge_2025] [cite:adn_kruse_2025] [cite:wtop_george_2026]

In March 2025 he shared the timing and weapons of imminent strikes in Yemen in a Signal chat to which a journalist had been added by mistake, and, the New York Times reported, in a second chat that included his wife and brother; he said the details were not classified, and the Pentagon's inspector general opened an inquiry. [cite:hg_atlantic_signalgate] [cite:hg_nyt_signalgate2] [cite:hg_wiki_signalgate] On 5 September 2025 Trump restored "Department of War" as a secondary name by executive order; the legal name remains Department of Defense unless Congress changes it. [cite:hg_pentagon_wardeptEO] From September 2025 the military struck boats in the Caribbean and the Pacific alleged to carry drugs, killing more than 82 people in at least 21 strikes by December; the Washington Post reported that Hegseth had told commanders to "kill everybody" aboard one boat, which he denies, and the admiral in command has said he ordered the follow-up strike himself. [cite:hg_reuters_boatstrikecount] [cite:hg_wapo_boatstrike] [cite:hg_aptop_bradleyrole]
