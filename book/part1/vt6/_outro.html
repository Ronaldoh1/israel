Part Six established that the chain of command in the Holy See is written down and has a single top. Canon law gives the pope supreme, full and immediate power with no appeal from his decree, and the cardinals under 80 choose each pope by a two-thirds secret ballot, as they chose Leo XIV on the fourth ballot on 8 May 2025. [cite:vt6_cic_331] [cite:vt6_wiki_conclave2025] The Curia as reorganised on 19 March 2022 works in his name through offices whose heads he appoints for five years and who lose office when he dies; as of October 2026 Cardinal Parolin remained Secretary of State, Archbishop Rudelli had become Substitute, and economic oversight sat with the Secretariat for the Economy under Maximino Caballero Ledo. [cite:vt6_ewtn_pe] [cite:vt6_va_parolin2026] [cite:vt6_vn_rudelli] [cite:vt6_gc_spe] The "Black Pope" is a nickname for the Jesuit Superior General, Arturo Sosa Abascal as of October 2026, who governs one religious order bound to the pope by a vow of obedience; when the two disagreed, in 1773 and 1981, the pope prevailed. [cite:vt6_wiki_supgen] [cite:vt6_gt_sosa2026] [cite:vt6_wiki_suppression] [cite:vt6_wiki_dezza] The "Grey Pope" has no office, no consistent holder and no document behind it, and is graded a myth. [cite:vt6_wiki_eminence] [cite:vt6_kettner_pepe] [cite:vt6_wiki_princeasst] Part Seven examines the family most often named as the hidden power, the Orsini, and the Roman "Black Nobility" to which it belonged.
