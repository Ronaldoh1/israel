Part Eleven sets out the Christian record against Christian Zionism: the churches, theologians and believers who rejected the system from within the faith, in their own documents and in their own words. It begins with the historic churches, from Pius X's refusal to Herzl in 1904 through Nostra Aetate and the Catholic Catechism's rejection of millenarianism to the Holy See's agreements with Israel and Palestine, the statements of the Jerusalem church heads in 2006 and January 2026, and the divestment votes of the Presbyterian Church (USA) and the United Methodist Church. It then turns to Palestinian Christians, the community most directly affected and least heard: Naim Ateek and Sabeel, the Kairos Palestine document of 2009, the Christ at the Checkpoint conferences and Munther Isaac's preaching during the Gaza war, each with the criticism it drew. Last, it follows dissent among American evangelicals themselves and the dated surveys, from 2017 to May 2026, that show a generational shift. Every position is attributed to a named church, organisation or person, and every survey figure to its survey, date and population.
