Read as a network, the record gives three plain answers. The operation ran on money that came through personal relationships, Wexner's above all, and on banks that kept him as a client after the warnings; without either, it could not have been paid for. The places, the aircraft, the staff, the lawyers and the tax breaks were real and heavy, but they served any rich client and could have served someone else. And nothing in the record shows an operation that existed before him and that he took over: it was built around his money and his properties from about 1990.
