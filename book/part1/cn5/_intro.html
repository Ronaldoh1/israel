Part Five covers 754 to 1511, when the bishop of Rome became the ruler of a state and his office became the richest prize in Italy. It follows the two forgeries that served papal claims, the Donation of Constantine and the False Decretals, and the scholar who exposed the first and was then hired by a pope. It then follows the Roman families who bought, inherited and fought over the chair, from the counts of Tusculum to the Orsini, Colonna and Caetani, and sets what the record shows about them against what legend adds.
