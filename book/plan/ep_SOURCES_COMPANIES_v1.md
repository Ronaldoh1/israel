## Part E · How to finish the registry from the DOJ library
A full vendor list (food, staff agencies, maintenance, boats, fuel, telecom) needs a **systematic pass over the ~3.5M released pages**, not searches one at a time.
1. **Keyword passes** over the EFTA sets: invoice, quote, proposal, purchase order, "Amazon list", UPS, FedEx, wire, contractor, delivery, catering, grocery, provisions, fuel, charter, security, alarm, camera.
2. **Extract** each hit as a row: vendor, service, property, date, amount, EFTA number.
3. **Tier** each row: documented purchase, documented relationship, or a mention only.
4. **Guardrail:** an ordinary vendor appears only with its documented service. No vendor is described as knowing about, or complicit in, anything unless a document says so. The food-"code-word" theory is not carried (see the outline's register).
