No court record, released government document or sworn testimony shows that Epstein worked for Israeli intelligence. The claim rests mainly on an unconfirmed, second-hand remark attributed to Alexander Acosta and on Ari Ben-Menashe, a source with documented credibility problems; Israel denies it [cite:politico_acosta_epstein_2019] [cite:wiki_ari_ben_menashe]. An FBI informant document obtained through FOIA records an informant saying Epstein was an Israeli spy, but it documents what an informant told the FBI, not anything the FBI verified [cite:fbi_epstein_spy].

Some ties are real. Epstein and former Israeli Prime Minister Ehud Barak jointly brokered Israeli surveillance technology to Côte d'Ivoire [cite:aljazeera_epstein_ivory_coast_2026]. Ghislaine Maxwell's father, Robert Maxwell, was buried in Jerusalem in 1991 with the honors of a state funeral, a documented fact about his own standing in Israel; books alleging he worked with Israeli intelligence rely heavily on Ari Ben-Menashe and secondary accounts, and a father's relationships are not evidence about what his daughter or Epstein did [cite:jta_maxwell_funeral_1991] [cite:wiki_robert_maxwell]. Roughly three million files remain withheld, so the question stays open, not proven [cite:efta_wiki_transparency_act].
