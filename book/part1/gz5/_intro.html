Part Five covers the four wars fought between Israel and the armed groups in Gaza between 2008 and 2021, and the mass protests at the fence in 2018. Each began with a sequence of events both sides describe differently, and each section sets out that sequence as the record gives it, the aims Israel stated, the dead as each counting body counted them, and the findings of the inquiries that followed. The figures differ by source, sometimes widely, and the differences are given rather than resolved.
