## 1. FAMILY / EARLY LIFE (1947–1969)

- Born **Hillary Diane Rodham, October 26, 1947, Chicago**, raised in Park Ridge, IL. Father
  Hugh Rodham ran a small drapery-fabric business; mother Dorothy Howell Rodham was a homemaker.
  Eldest of three children; brothers Hugh Jr. and Tony followed. Raised Methodist, raised
  Republican. **DOCUMENTED.** [1][2]
- Attended Maine East/Maine South HS in Park Ridge; National Honor Society, Girl Scouts.
  **DOCUMENTED.** [1]
- Wellesley College, 1965–69: president of the Young Republicans as a freshman (reflecting her
  parents' politics), shifted left over the course of the Civil Rights Movement/Vietnam/1968,
  became a Democrat by the end of the '68 cycle. Elected student-body president; first student
  ever to give the Wellesley commencement address (1969), a widely covered speech that got a
  seven-minute standing ovation. **DOCUMENTED**, contemporaneous *Life* magazine coverage. [1][3]
- Yale Law School (1969–73): editor, *Yale Review of Law and Social Action*; worked with
  children's-rights advocate **Marian Wright Edelman** at the Washington Research Project
  (predecessor to the Children's Defense Fund); met **Bill Clinton** in the law library, spring
  1971. **DOCUMENTED.** [1][2][6]

*Note for the book:* the "Republican-turned-Democrat" biographical fact is genuinely well
documented and is often used rhetorically by both admirers and critics — admirers use it as
evidence of independent-mindedness, critics use it to argue opportunism. Both are interpretive
readings of the same documented fact; neither reading itself is documented.

## 2. ARKANSAS YEARS (1974–1992)

- 1974: worked on the House Judiciary Committee's Nixon impeachment-inquiry staff, then moved to
  Arkansas to be with Bill Clinton. **DOCUMENTED.** [2][6]
- 1975: married Bill Clinton. 1976: joined the **Rose Law Firm**. 1980: Chelsea Victoria Clinton
  born. First Lady of Arkansas 1979–81 and 1983–92 (two non-consecutive terms, matching Bill
  Clinton's gubernatorial terms). **DOCUMENTED.** [6]
- **Whitewater**: In 1978, the Clintons and Jim/Susan McDougal invested in the Whitewater
  Development Corporation, an Ozark land deal that lost money. McDougal separately bought
  Madison Guaranty Savings & Loan, which later collapsed; his handling of Madison's finances (not
  the original land deal) produced the criminal exposure. Multiple investigators — the Resolution
  Trust Corporation's referral review, independent counsel Robert Fiske, then independent counsel
  **Ken Starr**, then independent counsel Robert Ray — examined the Clintons' role over ~6 years
  and **$50M+ in costs**. **Final outcome, DOCUMENTED:** in September 2000, IC Robert Ray closed
  the matter, stating there was **"insufficient evidence" to charge either Clinton** with
  criminal wrongdoing, and specifically found no evidence Hillary Clinton intentionally withheld
  her Rose Law Firm billing records (which had surfaced years late, in the White House, a fact
  that drew heavy suspicion at the time but was not established as intentional concealment).
  **DOCUMENTED — closed with no charges.** [7][8][9][10]
  - Contested texture worth keeping in the book: Starr's own 2018 memoir says he considered but
    dropped perjury charges against her over her 1995 deposition testimony ("I don't recall," by
    his count, over 100 times), calling her testimony "preposterous" but concluding it would be
    hard to prove she lied. This is Starr's characterization, on the record, but it is
    **Starr's opinion of demeanor**, not a finding of perjury — the matter was closed without
    charges. **CONTESTED characterization / DOCUMENTED outcome (no charges).** [11][12]
- **Travelgate** (1993, technically First Lady/West Wing era, included here since it's
  Arkansas-to-DC continuity): 7 White House travel-office staff fired; critics alleged the
  Clintons wanted to install cronies. Multiple investigations (DOJ, congressional panels, Starr)
  reviewed it; **Starr's independent-counsel review found no blame attached to Bill Clinton** on
  the firings specifically. **DOCUMENTED — investigated, no finding of Clinton wrongdoing.** [7]
- Separately (flagged for completeness, not yet deep-researched here): FBI-files matter
  ("Filegate") — hundreds of raw FBI background files on former Republican White House staff
  turned up in Clinton White House hands; investigated, no criminal charges resulted. **NEEDS
  DEEPER PASS** if you want it in the book. [7]

    said it — it is a claim about motive/coordination by her political opponents, not itself an
    independently verified finding. **DOCUMENTED that she said it; the substance of the claim is
    her characterization, not adjudicated fact.**

## 5. SECRETARY OF STATE (2009–2013)

- Nominated by President Obama, confirmed, served one term. **DOCUMENTED.**
- **Benghazi (Sept. 11, 2012):** attack on the U.S. diplomatic compound/CIA annex in Benghazi,
  Libya killed 4 Americans including Ambassador Chris Stevens. The House formed a Select Committee
  (chaired by Rep. Trey Gowdy), which ran ~2 years, ~$7M, 100+ witnesses, 75,000+ pages of
  documents. **Final outcome, DOCUMENTED:** the June 2016 final report found **no "smoking gun" —
  no new evidence of wrongdoing by Clinton** specific to the attack itself; it criticized State
  Department security decisions broadly and the Obama administration's crisis response, and it was
  the vehicle through which the private email server first came to public light. Democrats on the
  committee called it a partisan effort to damage her campaign; Republicans said the security
  failures were real. **DOCUMENTED outcome: no wrongdoing finding against Clinton personally on
  Benghazi. CONTESTED: whether the process itself was appropriately or excessively political.**
  [13][14][15][16][17]
- **Private email server:** discovered via the Benghazi Committee's document requests. FBI opened
  a formal investigation in 2015 into whether classified information was mishandled.
  **DOCUMENTED findings (July 5, 2016, Comey public statement):** 110 emails in 52 chains
  contained classified information at the time they were sent; the FBI found no evidence of
  intentional deletion to obstruct the investigation; Comey called Clinton and her staff **"extremely
  careless"** with classified material but concluded **"no reasonable prosecutor" would bring
  charges** — no criminal intent could be shown. DOJ (AG Loretta Lynch) formally closed the matter
  July 6, 2016, with no charges. **DOCUMENTED — investigated, closed, no charges.** [18][19][20][21]
  - Oct. 28, 2016: Comey reopened the review after classified-adjacent emails turned up on Anthony
    Weiner's laptop (Weiner being the estranged husband of top Clinton aide Huma Abedin); Nov. 6,
    2016 (2 days before the election) Comey announced nothing had changed the July conclusion.
    **DOCUMENTED.** The timing/political effect of this reopening is a major, legitimately
    **CONTESTED** part of the 2016-loss discussion (see Section 7). [21][22]
  - 2018 DOJ Inspector General report (Michael Horowitz): found Comey's handling **departed
    from FBI/DOJ norms** (e.g., making the July announcement without DOJ sign-off, and the
    "insubordinate" characterization CBS reported) but **did not conclude the investigative
    conclusion itself (no charges) was wrong** — it faulted process and optics, not the
    prosecutorial judgment. A 2025 declassified "Clinton annex" to that report (released via Sen.
    Grassley) additionally surfaced **uncorroborated Russian-intelligence-derived allegations**
    that the Obama-era DNC chair and Obama administration figures discussed protecting Clinton's
    candidacy during the probe; the same report notes the FBI/Comey/Strzok **did not seriously
    verify the underlying intelligence** before it entered their files. **This is exactly a
    STRUCTURAL-INFERENCE-vs-UNSUPPORTED case**: a document that reports an *unverified allegation*
    exists is not the same as the allegation being true — treat as **REPORTED-that-an-allegation-
    exists, NOT SUBSTANTIATED as to the underlying claim.** [23][18]

## 6. THE 2016 DEMOCRATIC PRIMARY vs. BERNIE SANDERS — DNC CONDUCT

This is the cluster you specifically flagged. Two separate things get conflated in public
discussion and should stay separated in the book:

### 6a. The leaked DNC staff emails (WikiLeaks, July 2016 — from a DNC server hack)
- Emails from ~7 DNC staffers (2015–16) showed derogatory internal commentary about Sanders
  (DNC Chair Debbie Wasserman Schultz calling his campaign manager "a damn liar," an aide
  suggesting raising Sanders' religious beliefs as a wedge issue in West Virginia, a
  communications aide floating using a voter-data dispute to paint the Sanders campaign as
  disorganized). **DOCUMENTED — these are the actual leaked emails**, published and widely
  authenticated; DNC Chair Wasserman Schultz resigned days later, right before the convention.
  [22][24]
- Separately, in October 2016, emails from **Clinton campaign chair John Podesta's** account
  (also hacked/leaked via WikiLeaks) showed acting/interim DNC chair **Donna Brazile** — while
  she was still a paid CNN commentator — forwarding at least two town-hall/debate questions in
  advance to the Clinton campaign (a Flint water-crisis question ahead of a March 2016 CNN debate,
  and a death-penalty question ahead of a March town hall). Brazile initially denied it, then,
  after the second batch surfaced, **CNN accepted her resignation** and she later admitted in a
  public essay that sending the emails was "a mistake I will forever regret." **DOCUMENTED —
  she has personally confirmed this.** [25][26][27][28][29]
- **Attribution note for the book:** U.S. intelligence agencies attributed the DNC server hack
  itself to Russian military intelligence (GRU); that's a separate, well-documented finding from
  the 2017 Intelligence Community Assessment and the 2019 Mueller Report — flag for a dedicated
  research pass if you want the full Russia-interference thread built out, since it's large enough
  to be its own section.

### 6b. The Joint Fundraising Agreement (revealed by Donna Brazile, Nov. 2017, in *Politico*/her book *Hacks*)
- **DOCUMENTED, from a real signed document Brazile says she found:** In August 2015 — four
  months after Clinton entered the race and a full year before she clinched the nomination — the
  DNC (then run by Wasserman Schultz, CEO Amy Dacey), the **Hillary Victory Fund**, and **Hillary
  for America** signed a Joint Fundraising Agreement. Per Brazile's account (which is what's
  publicly available; I have not independently reviewed the primary document myself in this pass —
  **REPORTED, sourced to Brazile's direct account of a document she says she saw**): in exchange
  for the Clinton campaign raising money for and effectively bailing out a debt-ridden DNC
  (roughly $10M, ~80% of its remaining debt, per Brazile), the agreement gave Hillary for America
  **advance consultation/right-of-refusal over DNC communications-director hiring, and final
  say on DNC staffing, budgeting, data, analytics, and mailings** — while Sanders was still
  actively competing for the nomination.
- Money mechanism, as described by Brazile: individual donors could give more to a joint
  state-party/national-committee vehicle than directly to a candidate; the Hillary Victory Fund
  collected under that higher combined limit, and functionally most of it flowed through to the
  DNC and onward to the Clinton campaign in Brooklyn.
- Brazile's own characterization: **"not illegal, but it sure looked unethical."** She called it
  "the cancer" of the party in her book excerpt. **DOCUMENTED that she said this; her underlying
  legal/ethical characterization is her opinion, not a court finding — no court or regulator ruled
  the arrangement illegal.**
- **CONTESTED counter-case, on the record:** Bernie Sanders' campaign **also signed its own joint
  fundraising agreement** with the DNC (a standard mechanism both campaigns used). DNC spokesperson
  Xochitl Hinojosa said publicly that Clinton was simply the only candidate who actually chose to
  raise money for the party this way, which "would benefit any candidate coming out of the
  primary." A former Clinton campaign staffer (Zerlina Maxwell) called the "rigged" framing
  "overstating" how joint fundraising committees legally work. Some contemporaneous political
  analysts argued the DNC's preference for Clinton was "common knowledge" well before this
  agreement and that the agreement itself was a "banal" finance mechanism being retroactively
  dramatized. **This is a live, legitimately contested framing question, not a settled one — the
  underlying financial arrangement is DOCUMENTED; whether it constitutes "rigging the primary" is
  CONTESTED opinion/interpretation, not an adjudicated legal finding.** [30][31][32][33][34]

**QOP-style verdict for this cluster:** The specific claim "the DNC had a formal financial control
agreement over its own presidential primary with one candidate, months before that candidate won
the nomination, while its own chair was privately feeding that candidate's team debate questions" —
**DOCUMENTED** as to both facts (the agreement's terms per Brazile's account, and the debate-question
forwarding, which Brazile herself confirmed). Whether that combination amounts to "the primary was
rigged" in a legal or dispositive sense is **CONTESTED** — no regulator or court adjudicated it as
rigging, and a comparable (if smaller, less financially loaded) agreement existed on the Sanders
side too. Your instinct that "this should have been disqualifying" is a values/political judgment,
not itself a documentary gap — the documentary record actually supports the underlying facts fairly
well; it's the "why didn't this end her" question that's more a story about how the Democratic base
and press treated it in a compressed campaign season than about missing evidence.

## 7. EPSTEIN — WHAT ACTUALLY CHECKS AND WHAT DOESN'T

Important and, per your framework, exactly the kind of case where the finding should be allowed to
land as **NOT SUBSTANTIATED** rather than forced into a shape you might have expected.

- **Bill Clinton**, not Hillary, is the family member with a documented Epstein connection.
  Flight logs (from Epstein's pilot, entered into court proceedings) show Bill Clinton on **26
  individual flight legs** on Epstein's planes between 2002–2003 (his camp says these represent
  4 distinct trips, tied to Clinton Foundation work in Africa/Southeast Asia; a CNN count found at
  least 16 separate flights). Pilot Larry Visoski confirmed Clinton sometimes flew without Secret
  Service. Epstein donated $1,000 to Bill Clinton's 1992 campaign and $20,000 to a joint DNC/Hillary
  Senate-campaign fundraising committee in 1999. **DOCUMENTED**, this is Bill Clinton's exposure,
  not Hillary's. [35][36][37]
- One Epstein accuser, **Virginia Giuffre**, told a UK tabloid in 2011 she'd met Bill Clinton twice,
  including once on Epstein's island, and that she dined there with Epstein, Ghislaine Maxwell,
  Clinton, and "two young brunettes" — but she **explicitly said she was not "lent out" to Clinton
  and did not witness any wrongdoing by him.** No Epstein survivor or associate has ever publicly
  alleged wrongdoing by Bill Clinton. Flight logs do **not** show Clinton on a flight to the island.
  **REPORTED (single-source tabloid account, no wrongdoing alleged) — not itself an allegation of
  misconduct.** [35]
- **Hillary Clinton specifically:** Her name does **not** appear in the Epstein flight logs
  released in 2019 or in the broader Jan. 2025/2026 DOJ document releases in connection with any
  visit to Epstein's properties. A widely-shared 2025 social-media claim that "NYPD found evidence
  on Anthony Weiner's laptop that she visited Epstein's island six times" was checked by
  fact-checkers against the actual State-Department-released Weiner-laptop emails (which do exist,
  and did contain some classified material via aide Huma Abedin) — **none of those emails mention
  Epstein or his island.** **NOT SUBSTANTIATED — this specific viral claim does not check out
  against the documents it claims to be sourced from.** [40]
- **Feb. 26, 2026 — the definitive statement on record.** Subpoenaed alongside Bill Clinton, she
  gave a ~6–7 hour closed-door deposition to the House Oversight Committee in Chappaqua, NY. Her
  opening statement, posted publicly: **"I had no idea about their criminal activities. I do not
  recall ever encountering Mr. Epstein. I never flew on his plane or visited his island, homes or
  offices."** Speaking to reporters afterward, she was more categorical still: **"I never met
  Jeffrey Epstein, never had any connection or communication with him."** She confirmed she knew
  **Ghislaine Maxwell** — but as a **casual acquaintance**, specifically citing (a) encounters at
  Clinton Foundation conferences, and (b) Maxwell's attendance at **Chelsea Clinton's 2010
  wedding**, where — per Hillary Clinton's own account to reporters — Maxwell came as another
  guest's plus-one, not the Clintons' invitee. **DOCUMENTED — sworn sworn/on-the-record statement,
  the strongest and most recent first-person evidence available; nothing in the released document
  sets contradicts it.** [38][39][41][151][152][153]
  - Context on the deposition itself: it was part of a bipartisan-supported committee action (per
    committee chair James Comer) compelling both Clintons to testify; she used the opportunity to
    publicly push back, arguing the committee should be questioning Donald Trump under oath with
    the same rigor given how many times his name appears in the broader Epstein document releases —
    a comparative point about *scrutiny*, not a claim that clears or convicts anyone. **DOCUMENTED
    that she said this; it's argumentative context, not a finding about her own conduct.** [151]

**Bottom line for the book:** the Clinton-Epstein material is real and well-documented for **Bill**
Clinton (flights, donations, photos, a jacuzzi photo he says was taken during a Clinton Foundation
AIDS-initiative stop in Brunei). For **Hillary** specifically, the documented record is much
thinner — a Maxwell acquaintance and sworn "I don't recall meeting him" — and the most-viral
Hillary-specific claim (NYPD/Weiner-laptop/"six island visits") is **not substantiated** and is
directly contradicted by the documents it purports to cite. If the book chapter on her leans on
Epstein, the honest framing is "the family's exposure is Bill's, not hers, and the strongest
document-based claims about her personally don't hold up" — not "she has an Epstein connection."

## 8. CLINTON FOUNDATION — "PAY TO PLAY"

- Central allegation: that foreign governments/corporations donated to the Clinton Foundation (or
  paid large speaking fees to Bill Clinton) in exchange for favorable access or decisions from
  Hillary Clinton's State Department (2009–2013). Peter Schweizer's book *Clinton Cash* (2015) is
  the most-cited compilation of the specific alleged instances (Colombia free-trade/logging
  rights, Keystone XL–linked speaking fees, a Boko Haram terror-designation delay). **These are
  REPORTED allegations from an advocacy-journalism source, not independently adjudicated
  findings** — treat Schweizer's book as a lead-generator, not a verified record, in the book.
- An Associated Press review **did find a documented overlap** between Foundation donors and
  people Clinton met with at State — that overlap itself is **DOCUMENTED** (AP counted it); whether
  the meetings were *caused by* the donations (pay-to-play) versus donors simply being the kind of
  prominent people any Secretary of State would meet anyway is the **CONTESTED** causal question,
  and AP's own reporting did not claim to prove causation. [45]
- FBI/DOJ opened a formal "pay-to-play" investigation into the Foundation in Jan. 2018 (Little
  Rock field office), examined possible tax-law violations and whether the Foundation misused
  tax-exempt assets. **DOCUMENTED that this investigation existed.**
- **Outcome:** this Trump-DOJ-era review **wound down with no charges**; reporting through 2019
  consistently describes prior probes as having "found no evidence of wrongdoing" prosecutable in
  court. A separate 2019 IRS criminal review (New Jersey field office, per whistleblowers Moynihan
  and Doyle) reportedly opened and then abruptly stopped contact with its own whistleblowers —
  this stoppage itself is **REPORTED** (via the whistleblowers/*Just the News*) and I have not
  independently verified an official closure statement for that specific IRS thread; flag as
  **REPORTED, not fully verified end-state.** [42][43][44][46]
- As of late 2025, Trump-DOJ officials (AG Bondi) reportedly provided Congress with new document
  caches described as reviving "pay to play" and "suppressed probe" claims — this is a **live,
  ongoing, current-political story as of this writing**, not a closed matter; treat anything from
  this thread as **PENDING/DEVELOPING**, and re-check before publication if the book chapter covers
  it. [42]

- **Policy record** — consistently pro-Israel-alignment voting/positioning as Senator and Secretary

- **Left/progressive criticism** — her 2016 AIPAC speech drew public criticism from progressive and
  Palestinian-rights organizations (the U.S. Campaign to End the Israeli Occupation, Jewish Voice

  territories entirely. Worth including for balance: **the relationship is contested even within
  the Democratic coalition, not a settled or uniform alignment.** **DOCUMENTED that these
  criticisms were made publicly.** [158]
- **What was searched for and not found in this pass:** any documented financial, intelligence, or
