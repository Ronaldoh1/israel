Founded by Force

On the network map of the Road to 1948, removing the armed groups and the arms network, the Haganah, the Palmach, the Irgun and Lehi and the buyers and smugglers who armed them, drops the chance of a state to zero, on time or ever. No other test does that except removing the organized movement itself. The state was not voted into being; it was fought into being.

Plan Dalet and the expulsions

The Haganah finalised Plan Dalet on 10 March 1948. [cite:rs_rd5_khalidi_1988] Under it the mixed and Arab cities fell before any Arab army crossed the border: Tiberias on 18 April, Haifa on 23 April, Jaffa's 60,000 people emptied in early May, Safad on 10 May, Beisan on 11 May. [cite:hyw_ch2] [cite:wiki_plan_dalet] On 9 April Irgun and Lehi fighters killed villagers at Deir Yassin; Ben-Gurion later wrote that it "had a decisive effect" on Arab flight. [cite:morris_1948] [cite:wiki_deir_yassin] In July Yitzhak Rabin signed the order to expel the people of Lydda. [cite:rs_rd5_palquest_lydda] In the war of 1947–49 roughly 700,000 to 750,000 Palestinians were displaced, the Nakba. [cite:wiki_nakba] The Israeli historian Ilan Pappé argues that Plan Dalet meets the UN definition of ethnic cleansing; Benny Morris documents the expulsions while disputing a master plan. [cite:pappe_ethnic_cleansing] [cite:morris_1948]

The map adds a hard point. Removing Plan Dalet and the expulsions leaves the chance of a state at about 93 percent, unchanged. The expulsions were not needed to found the state. They decided who would live in it. The Absentees' Property Law of 14 March 1950 then gave the refugees' property to the state, including that of Arabs who had stayed inside Israel but were away from their homes. [cite:rs_rd6_fischbach_2003] [cite:wiki_present_absentee]

Held by force

Arab citizens lived under military rule until 1966; at Kafr Qasim in 1956 border police shot dead 49 villagers returning from their fields. [cite:wiki_kafr_qasim_massacre] Since 1967 the West Bank and Gaza have been held under military rule, and settlers in the West Bank, outside East Jerusalem, went from about 110,000 at Oslo to about 500,000; with East Jerusalem the total is about 737,000. [cite:peacenow_settlement_population] [cite:globalsecurity_two_state] Arab forces killed 78 Jews, most of them doctors and nurses, in the Hadassah convoy four days after Deir Yassin; that violence is part of the same record. [cite:wiki_deir_yassin]
