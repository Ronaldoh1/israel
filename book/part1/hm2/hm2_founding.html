December 1987

In December 1987, twenty years into Israel's occupation of Gaza and the West Bank, Palestinians in both territories rose up against it, in the uprising that became known as the first intifada. Gaza, Documented sets it out. [scene:gz3_uprising|The first intifada] In the same weeks, Sheikh Ahmed Yassin, the paraplegic cleric who led the Muslim Brotherhood's network in Gaza, and six others founded a new organization. [cite:hm_wsj_spawn] They called it the Islamic Resistance Movement. [cite:hm_avalon_charter] The historian Tareq Baconi describes the moment as the point at which the Brotherhood's Islamic party in the territories "spun off" into Hamas, moving from social work to armed struggle. [cite:hm_pbs_baconi] Israel, which had licensed the Brotherhood's charity in 1979 and largely left it alone, did not move against the new group at first; Hamas carried out its first attack in 1989.
