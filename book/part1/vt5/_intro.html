Part Four showed how the Vatican governs itself. Part Five asks why the rest of the world treats the Holy See as a sovereign, and who, if anyone, examines how it uses that status. It follows three lines of outside contact: recognition by other states and the United Nations, including the campaign to remove its UN seat and the lawsuits in which its sovereign immunity was tested; the 2014 reviews by two UN treaty committees of its record on clerical sexual abuse, with the Holy See's replies; and the euro agreement with the European Union and the anti-money-laundering evaluations by the Council of Europe's MONEYVAL committee, which together answer the claim that the Vatican's bank sits outside European anti-money-laundering rules.
