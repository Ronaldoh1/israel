Part Eleven covers the war that began on 28 February 2026, when the United States and Israel launched joint strikes on Iran and killed its Supreme Leader, Ayatollah Ali Khamenei, on the first day. The first section covers the run-up: the January crackdown on protests inside Iran, the American and Israeli statements that followed, and the talks mediated by Oman that were still going on when the bombs fell. It then sets out the stated aims of each government, the succession in Tehran, Iran's missile and drone attacks on Israel, American bases and the Gulf states, its closure of the Strait of Hormuz, the tolls and how each was counted, and the response at the United Nations. The second section follows the war from the April ceasefire through the Islamabad Memorandum, its collapse in July and the American blockade, to where things stood in October 2026, with each side's stated position on each turn. The American bill in money and lives is told in full in Part Fourteen, and the Lebanese front in the Hezbollah volume; this part links to both and does not repeat them.
