Israel's state manages 93 per cent of the land and leases it; its courts barred allocation by religion or nationality in 2000 and the Attorney General opened JNF land to all bidders in 2005, but admissions committees may still screen applicants to small communities, and the Arab sector, whose 139 towns and villages sit on less than 3 per cent of the land, received 97 per cent of the administrative demolition orders issued in 2012–14.
