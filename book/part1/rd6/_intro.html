On 20 May 1948, six days after Israel declared its independence, the United Nations appointed Count Folke Bernadotte, head of the Swedish Red Cross, as its mediator in Palestine. [cite:rs_rd6_britannica_bernadotte] [cite:rs_rd6_un_bernadotte_bio] The war he was sent to stop was still being fought. Egypt, Jordan, Syria, Lebanon and Iraq had invaded on the Mandate's last day, and Jordan's Arab Legion held the Old City of Jerusalem and the West Bank. [cite:wikipedia_1948_war] [cite:wiki_jordan_annexation] The new state had been recognized by the United States eleven minutes after its declaration, but only de facto, over the written objections of the State Department and the CIA; full legal recognition would wait until January 1949. [cite:jvl_truman_marshall_dejure] Inside the lines Israel held, its statisticians would count 716,700 Jews and 156,000 non-Jews at the end of the year. [cite:rs_rd6_jvl_population] Beyond them, by December, some 700,000 Palestinians were refugees, a number disputed from the first day it was given. [cite:rs_rd6_morris_appendix]

This stage asks what became of all that once the shooting stopped. The war had produced facts: positions held, villages emptied, houses occupied, a state with an army and no borders. In the years that followed, each fact was either written into law and treaty or left in suspense, and the choice was rarely made by one party alone. The stage follows both processes, consolidation and accounting. It asks who fixed the lines, who took title to the land, who was admitted as a citizen and who was not, who paid, and what the new international order did, and could not do, for the people the war had displaced. It also closes the account on the years before the state: who funded the build, through which channels, and what the record can and cannot show about money and American politicians.

The cast is the widest in the volume. Israel's government under David Ben-Gurion and Moshe Sharett, and its first parliament, made most of the decisions that turned wartime facts into statute. The United Nations acted through Bernadotte, through Ralph Bunche, who negotiated the armistices after Bernadotte's murder, and through a Conciliation Commission of France, Turkey and the United States. [cite:rs_rd6_nobel_bunche] [cite:rs_rd6_refworld_194] In Washington Harry Truman moved between his Secretary of State, George Marshall, his political aide Clark Clifford and the donors of an election year. Among the Arab states, King Abdullah of Jordan, Egypt's government and a Syrian officer, Husni al-Za'im, each pursued a different settlement. Palestinian leaders acted on both sides of an inter-Arab rivalry, in a government proclaimed in Gaza under Ahmad Hilmi and Hajj Amin al-Husayni, and in a congress at Jericho that asked to be joined to Abdullah's crown. [cite:rs_rd6_shlaim_apg_1990] The Jews of Iraq, Yemen, Libya, Egypt and Morocco, West Germany's Konrad Adenauer, the Holy See and an American lobby in the course of renaming itself complete it.

The sections begin with the mediator's two plans and with Truman's decisions from the summer of 1948 to May 1950, including the one decision that a historian ties to a campaign contribution. They then follow how return was barred and the border hardened, who came to govern Arab Palestine, and the regime imposed on the Arabs who stayed inside Israel, before tracing the laws that turned flight into state ownership of land. One week in December 1948 produced the Universal Declaration and Resolution 194; the armistices of 1949 drew lines that, by their own text, were not borders; Lausanne failed over which should come first, peace or return. Israel's constitution was deferred, Syria's offer of peace was declined, and Israel entered the United Nations while the question of Jerusalem stayed open. The refugees' host states, the exodus of the Jews of the Arab world, the mass immigration and the Yemenite children, and the laws defining who belonged follow. The stage then closes the books: the organization that became AIPAC, how the state was paid for, how the flight was explained, how the case was put to Americans, who paid for the pre-state build, and the whole sequence from a bank to a state.
