This part sets out what DOGE and the agencies cut in 2025 and 2026, and who bore the cost. The first section goes agency by agency, from the closing of USAID and the probationary firings to the layoffs at Education, Health and Human Services and the consumer bureau, the plans that were reversed at Veterans Affairs and the Weather Service, and grants sorted for cancellation with a chatbot; it follows the court rulings that let most of the cuts proceed, and reads the three common workforce figures each on its own definition, never added together. The second section weighs the cost: the death estimates for aid cut abroad, each with its method and its critics; service at home as the agencies and their auditors measured it; the workers and contractors who lost jobs; and what the cuts saved, ending with a money trail and a test of the book's worst-case reading.
