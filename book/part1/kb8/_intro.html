Part Eight is a reading. Kabbalah is itself an inner tradition, so the inner reading here is closer to the tradition's own meaning than in any book before. It still goes further than the texts: it reads Luria's account of creation as an account of the self, and the Hasidic teaching of cleaving to God as a practice for anyone. Each section starts from the teaching, sourced, then says what the reading proposes and what the record does not show.
