Part Eight established that, between 1967 and 2023, the claim to land beyond the 1949 lines was written into Israeli law on one front only. The Golan Heights Law of 14 December 1981 applied Israeli law to the plateau; the Security Council declared it "null and void" unanimously, with the United States voting in favour; Netanyahu called the Golan Israel's "forever" in 2016; and the United States recognised it as Israeli in 2019, followed by Colombia in August 2026. Even there, Rabin and Barak negotiated over withdrawal between 1994 and 2000.
