Part Two follows the book that bound the Ethiopian throne to the Bible. It sets out how the Kebra Nagast, written by clerics between 1314 and 1322, gave the Solomonic dynasty founded in 1270 a descent from Solomon and the Queen of Sheba and claimed the Ark of the Covenant for Ethiopia. It then follows the claim from legend into the constitution of 1955, which declared the emperor's line perpetual and his person sacred, to the Rastafari movement in Jamaica, and to the coup of 1974 that ended it.
