Before any figure in this volume can mean anything, three things have to be fixed: what counts as pro-Israel money, through which legal channels it moves, and how a number drawn from those channels should be read. Part One sets them out. It defines the subject by organizations and filings, never by the religion or origin of any donor; it describes the six channels, from a $5,000 check to an unlimited super PAC campaign; and it gives five questions to ask of every figure, with examples of what goes wrong when they are skipped.
