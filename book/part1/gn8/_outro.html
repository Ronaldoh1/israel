The Gnostic texts said their knowledge was found within and could not be sold; the Apocryphon of John cursed anyone who traded it for a gift. [cite:vt24_apjohn] For eighteen centuries, access to the knowledge and then to the books was held by bishops, the empire, heresy-hunters, dealers, a Swiss institute, a bank vault, a state, a circle of scholars, a foundation and a television network. [cite:vt25_wiki_nhl] The Mandaeans show the other way such a tradition survives: lived by a people rather than kept in a vault.
