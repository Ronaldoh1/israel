Twenty Thousand

In June 2026 the UN's Independent International Commission of Inquiry reported that at least 20,179 children had been killed in Gaza between 7 October 2023 and 7 October 2025, and 44,143 injured. Of the dead, 5,031 were under five years old, and 1,029 were under one. Children were 30 percent of all those killed, against about 24 percent in the wars of 2008–2009 and 2014, and about two percent of all the children in Gaza had been killed. Save the Children estimated that another 5,160 children were buried under the rubble, not yet counted. [cite:pr_nw_coi2026]

The commission's finding. The commission concluded that there were "reasonable grounds" to find that these acts formed part of "a deliberate strategy to destroy the future of the Palestinians in Gaza by targeting their children." It listed the army units it believed responsible for specific killings, and said Israel had not answered any of its thirteen requests for information or access. [cite:pr_un_coi2026]

Israel's answer. Israel's mission in Geneva called the report a "libelous sham" that ignored "the brutal tactics of Hamas," and said it omitted Israel's part in vaccination campaigns and field hospitals; Israel's foreign ministry said the commission was "a fundamentally flawed mechanism" without "any credible verification mechanism for its claims." [cite:pr_nw_coi2026]

The Other Children

The same commission had found that 40 Israeli children were killed on 7 October 2023, hundreds were injured, and children were taken hostage, several of them alone; it attributed war crimes and crimes against humanity against them to Hamas and other armed groups. [cite:pr_un_coi2026] In the West Bank, the UN Human Rights Office counted 213 Palestinian children among the 1,001 Palestinians killed there in the two years after 7 October 2023. [cite:wb_ohchr_1001]
