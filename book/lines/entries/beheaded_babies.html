The specific claim that Hamas beheaded 40 babies has not been supported by the record, and it was walked back after spreading. In the first days after the attack, the "beheaded babies" line was amplified by US media and repeated by President Biden before it was retracted. It became part of what reached American audiences while much of the documented context, reported mainly by Israeli outlets, did not.

Debunking that number does not shrink the attack. Hamas killed 1,195 people on October 7, 828 of them civilians and 36 of them children, took 251 hostages, and committed sexual violence documented by UN rapporteur reports and Israeli forensic teams. [cite:wiki_october7_attacks] It was the largest single-day loss of Jewish life since the Holocaust. The record supports the atrocity in full; it does not support this particular, widely repeated figure.
