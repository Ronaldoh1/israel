This volume followed Israel and Iran from allies to enemies: a recognition kept quiet in 1950, an intelligence partnership and a shared pipeline under the Shah, Israeli arms sold to the Islamic Republic that called for Israel's end, and from 1992 a run of dated warnings of an Iranian bomb that did not arrive. [cite:ir1_iranica_israel] [cite:ir1_alpher_fa] [cite:ir2_walsh18] [cite:hz11b_intercept_wolf] Its record is of two governments that each did what the other accused it of in part: Iran ran weapons work until 2003, enriched to 60 percent, armed groups at war with Israel and killed its own citizens in their thousands, while Israel kept an undeclared arsenal of about 90 warheads outside the treaty Iran signed, waged a covert campaign that was never acknowledged, and, alone in 2025 and with the United States in 2026, opened war while American–Iranian talks were under way.
