By the end of 1948 the structure that had governed Palestine since 1922 was gone, and what replaced it had been built in three years by different hands, each holding something the others did not.

The legal layer was thinner than it looked. Resolution 181, adopted on 29 November 1947 by 33 votes to 13 with 10 abstaining, recommended two states, an economic union and an international Jerusalem, on a timetable that ran to 1 October 1948; Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, Iraq, Syria and Yemen said they regarded it as a recommendation and would not be bound by it. [cite:rs_rd5_un_yearbook_181] Britain, which had abstained, declined in September 1947 to impose any policy "by force of arms," so the plan's handover had no one to carry it out. [cite:rs_rd5_jvl_creechjones] The Declaration of 14 May 1948 rested the state on "natural and historic right" and on three international instruments, promised equal rights "irrespective of religion, race or sex," stated no borders, and set a constitution deadline of 1 October 1948 that passed without one. [cite:rs_rd5_jvl_declaration] Recognition came by executive act: American de facto recognition eleven minutes after the proclamation, Soviet de jure recognition on 17 May, American de jure recognition on 31 January 1949, Britain's in January 1949. [cite:trumanlibrary_recognition] [cite:wiki_soviet_arab_israeli] [cite:jvl_dejure_1949] [cite:rs_rd5_langham_exit] Resolution 194 of December 1948 affirmed a right of return that, as of 2026, has not been implemented. [cite:morris_1948]

The administrative layer was the most complete, because most of it already existed. The Jewish Agency's institutions became a government's. The order of 26 May 1948 made the Israel Defense Forces the only lawful armed force; the shelling of the Altalena on 22 June and the arrests after Lehi killed Count Bernadotte on 17 September enforced it, and an amnesty in February 1949 released those arrested. [cite:wiki_altalena_affair] [cite:wiki_bernadotte_assassination]

The financial layer was private and transnational. The Sonneborn network bought and smuggled material from 1945, some 800 shipments and about 2,400 tons, while, by one account, the Justice Department declined FBI recommendations to prosecute key figures; nine men were tried only in 1950. [cite:mexico_arms_1948] [cite:forward_oss] [cite:schwimmer_wiki] Golda Meyerson's tour was reported to have raised $50 million in about two months, spent largely on weapons. [cite:jwa_golda_50m] The library's funding ledger puts the 1948 United Jewish Appeal at a record $150 million, of which $74.7 million went to the Palestine share. Czechoslovakia sold arms with Soviet consent for almost $14.5 million by its own foreign minister's figure, though estimates run to about $90 million. [cite:jpost_czech_arms_saved_israel] Against this stood an American embargo and, by the same ledger, a British bill of about £100 million for its forces in 1945–47.

The Palestinian Arab layer was the weakest, and the record shows both choices and constraints. The Arab Higher Committee was re-established in 1946 "by a decision of the Arab League," its chairman the Mufti, who had spent the war allied with the Axis and led from Cairo. [cite:rs_rd5_shlaim_jps_1990] [cite:rs_rd5_dipc_husayni] It boycotted UNSCOP, rejected partition, and promised a "line of fire and blood." [cite:rs_rd5_unscop_report] [cite:wiki_un_partition_plan] In February 1948 the League refused it a government-in-exile and a loan; its Army of the Holy War and the League's Arab Liberation Army did not cooperate. [cite:rs_rd5_shlaim_jps_1990] [cite:rs_rd5_lodz_2019] By the end of the year two bodies claimed to speak for Palestinians, a government in Gaza and a congress at Jericho, and on the UN's technical estimate about 711,000 had become refugees. [cite:rs_rd5_unccp_1949] As early as June 1948 Israel's cabinet had reached a consensus against their return. [cite:rs_rd5_lockman_ref]

The regional layer was a coalition of states with different ends. The League's cablegram of 15 May spoke of restoring "law and order" and of a "United State of Palestine"; Lebanon chose war deliberately, Saudi Arabia sent a contingent under Egyptian command, and the Arab Legion, on the Office of the Historian's account, entered only areas assigned to the Arab state or Jerusalem. [cite:rs_rd5_un_s745] [cite:lebanon_1948_war] [cite:rs_rd5_oh_war_b] The armistices of 1949 left Egypt in Gaza and Jordan on the West Bank, with about 78 per cent of Mandate Palestine under Israeli control. [cite:morris_1948] In Arab cities the vote set off riots against Jews, and the governments of Egypt and Iraq followed with arrests, sequestrations and, in Iraq, a law against Zionism. [cite:rs_rd5_cie_arabcities] [cite:rs_rd5_anderson_1951]

The American layer was a government divided against itself. Henderson, the Joint Chiefs, Forrestal and the CIA warned in writing; the President's political advisers prevailed; the delegation retreated to trusteeship on 19 March and was still arguing for it when the President recognized the state. [cite:wrmea_henderson_warning_1994] [cite:cia_ore55] [cite:rs_rd5_avalon_austin] [cite:trumanlibrary_recognition]

What was settled was the existence of the state, its army and the lines its army held. What was contested was nearly everything about how. Zionist leaders could point to a UN plan they accepted, a rejection announced as a "line of fire and blood," and a Declaration that offered Arab citizens equality; Palestinian leaders and their historians to a plan that gave a majority of the land to a community owning under a tenth of it, to statehood for a largely immigrant minority while the majority was denied self-determination, and, in Khalidi's reading, to a displacement written into Plan Dalet's village clause. Whether that plan was a design for removal or a military plan whose expulsions were decided locally remains argued between Morris and Khalidi. So are Truman's motive, Abdullah's understanding with the Agency, and how far the insurgency drove Britain out. The record also leaves things open: a refugee count that runs from about 520,000 to 957,000 depending on who counted and how, Arab war dead not compiled in these sources, and Deir Yassin's archives still partly closed. [cite:rs_rd5_prrn_1996] [cite:wiki_deir_yassin] Above all it leaves the refugees themselves, outside the lines and refused return, the question [scene:rr_s01|Resolution 194] inherited.
