Before testing where the American Dream fails, this part sets out where it holds. Its first section gathers the documented record on the promise's side: household incomes at a record and among the highest in the rich world, low poverty among people who work full time, a century of upward mobility for immigrants' children, strong mobility for Hispanic Americans and recent gains for Black children, the places where children from the bottom still reach the top at high rates, the generation born around 1940 for whom the taught version was close to literal fact, and the falling cost of food and many goods. Its second section explains the measures needed to read the rest of the book: absolute and relative mobility, income and wealth, median and mean, real and nominal dollars, the choice of price index, households and individuals, and income before and after taxes and benefits.
