## 2. Series blind-spot list (applied)

- **Palestinian political agency:** the Arab delegations' positions at Paris in their own words (cw1_refugees); refugee opposition to the Sinai resettlement scheme; Arafat's Students League and Habash's path from Lydda to the Amman clinic; Syria's case and the Arab landowners' refusals at Huleh (cw1_huleh).
- **The strongest Zionist/Israeli case:** Eban on Huleh and on the Suez blockade; Israel's Paris case (security, the Arab states as aggressors, the Iraqi blocked accounts); Ben-Gurion's case for Western alignment and for full partnership rather than bases; the documented antisemitism of the Prague trial and the Doctors' Plot as part of the reason for the tilt.
- **Arab states' strongest case:** Egypt's belligerency argument on the Canal at full strength (Gross); Syria's sovereignty and refugee arguments; the Arab position on return and individual compensation.
- **Jewish dissent:** the American Council for Judaism; the Blaustein–Ben-Gurion exchange on diaspora allegiance; Mapam's Oren as a victim of the Prague trial; Maki's pro–North Korea stance; Begin's reparations opposition linked to rd6.
- **Jews of the Arab world and Iran:** the Iraqi blocked accounts as Israel's Paris condition (linked to rd6_exodus); Iran's Jewish community linked (ira_src_s16).
- **Real antisemitism named plainly:** the Slánský trial (12 of 14 defendants Jewish; "Zionist" charges), the Doctors' Plot ("international Jewry"; the CIA's own "clearly antiSemitic overtones"), tied to named states and officials.
- **Palestinian citizens of Israel:** the 785 Arab civilians evacuated from the Huleh demilitarized zone and the Security Council's order that they may return (cw1_huleh); regime itself linked to rd6.
- **Terms examined:** "non-identification"; "belligerency" and "visit and search"; "de facto" vs "de jure" recognition (Iran); "impartiality" (Dulles); "coup" and "acknowledgement" (CIA 2013); "founding" of the lobby (three dates, each meaning something different).
- **Convergence, not conspiracy:** inside Washington, the State Department (McGhee, Byroade) against Congress (Douglas, Taft) on aid; Ambassador Henderson's reluctance; CIA headquarters' evacuation advice that Roosevelt ignored; British oil interests and American Cold War fears as distinct motives; s11's own rule that the 1953 lobby founding and the coup share only a year.
- **Money and alignment:** grant aid arrived as Israel tilted West (Brecher's and Shlaim's readings, labelled); the parallel US money for Arab refugees and Arab states; TPAJAX's budget against the claim of "~$5M"; where money did not follow (the Administration's $23.5M against a $150M request).
- **Sources kept independent:** the coup's approval date and cost rest on CIA documents in FRUS, set against the Library of Congress study and Roosevelt's memoir; aid figures on FRUS plus JTA plus Kenen's letter; the liaison's start on three independent accounts (Melman/Raviv, McConnell, the CIA historian).
