Part Eleven followed the war to American audiences. Israel barred foreign journalists from entering Gaza independently, a ban its Supreme Court upheld in January 2024 and that remained in force in October 2026, and embedded reporters had their footage reviewed by Israeli commanders. [cite:gz_tna_fpa] [cite:gz_wlrn_ban] Gaza's own journalists were killed in greater numbers than in any war Costs of War has measured. [cite:gz_wiki_journalists] Inside American newsrooms, CNN routed coverage through its Jerusalem bureau and cautioned against Hamas statements, and the New York Times restricted words such as "genocide" and "occupied territory"; in the first six weeks, emotive language was reserved almost entirely for Israeli victims. [cite:gz_intercept_cnn] [cite:gz_nl_nytmemo] [cite:gz_aa_intercept] CBS passed to new owners who named Bari Weiss, known for pro-Israel positions, editor-in-chief.
