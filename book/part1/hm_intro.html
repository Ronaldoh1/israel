This volume is about Hamas: where it came from, what it has done, and how much power it actually holds. It was written to answer a set of direct questions. How and why was Hamas formed? Would it exist if Palestine had not been colonized and occupied? Would it be what it is if Israel had not cut Gaza off from the West Bank? Was it a response, and to what? And what is its true strength, in money, weapons and allies, against Israel's, with its economy, its own military budget and the billions in American aid behind it?

The record answers some of these questions and not others. It shows that the movement Hamas grew from, the Muslim Brotherhood, was founded in Egypt in 1928, two decades before Israel existed; that Israel licensed the Brotherhood's charity network in Gaza in 1979 and largely left it alone; and that Hamas itself was founded in December 1987, in the first weeks of an uprising against twenty years of occupation. [cite:hm_tamimi_2007] [cite:hm_wsj_spawn] It shows Hamas's charter, with its antisemitic articles, its suicide bombings and rockets aimed at civilians, its rule over Gaza and its attack of 7 October 2023, stated plainly. [cite:hm_avalon_charter] [cite:hm_wiki_suicide] [cite:hm_hrw_rockets2009] It shows Israel's separation of Gaza from the West Bank, the blockade, and the Qatari money Israel let into Gaza, with what Israeli leaders said about it and their denials, side by side. [cite:hm_aj_separation] [cite:hm_fdd_shinbet] [cite:hm_ynet_biglie] It tests the claim, often made, that Hamas has become the excuse for everything, against the record of the war. [scene:hm11_scale|Always Hamas] And it measures the gap between the two sides: before the war, Israel's economy was about 190 times the size of Gaza's. [cite:hm_fred_ilgdp] [cite:hm_aspi_gazagdp]

What would have happened otherwise cannot be documented, and the volume labels every such reading as an argument. It is a companion to Gaza, Documented, The West Bank, Documented and PRESS.
