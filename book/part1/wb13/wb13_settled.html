What the Record Settles

The West Bank was meant for an Arab state that was never created. The 1947 plan assigned the hill country to an Arab state; the war of 1948 left it in Jordan's hands, and the armistice of 1949 drew the Green Line around it. Jordan annexed it in 1950, recognized by almost no other state, and made its people Jordanian citizens. [cite:wb_ej_partition] [cite:rs_rd6_arm_jordan_text] [cite:wb_wiki_annex] [cite:wb_palquest_nationality]

Settlement was a decision of Israel's governments, taken against their own legal advice. In 1967 Israel's Foreign Ministry legal adviser warned that civilian settlement would breach the Fourth Geneva Convention; the cabinet approved Kfar Etzion the same month, presented as a military outpost. [cite:wb_gorenberg_meron] Later governments declared hundreds of thousands of dunams state land, drew Oslo's map with 60 percent of the territory left under Israeli control, and, since 2022, moved civil powers in Area C from the army to a minister responsible for settlement. [cite:wb_wiki_stateland] [cite:wb_wiki_oslo2areas] [cite:wb_pn_mirabilis]

Two populations live under two systems. Settlers live under Israeli law and civilian courts; Palestinians under military orders and military courts, which convicted in 99.74 percent of cases in 2010. Israel's Justice Ministry did not dispute the description and defended it on security grounds. [cite:wb_acri_twosys] [cite:wb_972_conviction]

Palestinians build under a system that seldom lets them. Less than one percent of Area C has been planned for Palestinian use, and in 2025 Israel demolished 1,657 Palestinian structures, 93 percent for lack of a permit. [cite:wb_ocha_areac] [cite:wb_ocha_snap2025]

Settler violence rose, and is seldom prosecuted. The UN counted 1,828 settler attacks causing casualties or damage in 2025; Yesh Din found 93.6 percent of the police files it followed closed without indictment; 57 Palestinian communities were emptied between October 2023 and December 2024. Israel's leaders have condemned the attacks as the work of a minority. [cite:wb_ocha_snap2025] [cite:wb_yeshdin_2025] [cite:wb_972_villages] [cite:wb_toi_netanyahu2025]

The international bodies that have ruled found the settlements unlawful, and none of the rulings has been enforced. The Security Council in 2016, the International Court of Justice in 2004 and 2024, and the General Assembly in 2024; Israel rejects each, and each stopped at a documented point.
