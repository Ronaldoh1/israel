Part Three covers the eighteen years from the first uprising to the Israeli withdrawal. It begins in the Jabaliya camp in December 1987, where an uprising that spread across the occupied territories began; the same week, a new Islamist movement in the strip named itself Hamas. It follows the Oslo agreements into Gaza, where a Palestinian Authority took over the cities while Israel kept the settlements, the borders and the permits, and it ends with the removal of the settlements in 2005 and the argument, still unresolved in the record, over whether the occupation ended with them.
