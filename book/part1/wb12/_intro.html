Part Twelve follows the money. Two flows run through the West Bank. The first is the Palestinian Authority's: most of its revenue is collected for it by Israel, under an economic agreement of 1994, and Israel can, and does, withhold it. The second is the settlements': state budgets, security money for outposts, roads, private donations from abroad and the businesses that supply them. This part sets out both, with Israel's stated reasons for its decisions and the dates on which the figures were counted.
