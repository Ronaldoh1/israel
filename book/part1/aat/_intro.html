Part Two adds it all up. It counts every dollar of American government aid to Israel year by year, from the first loan in 1949 to the money Congress enacted for 2026, and converts each year into today's dollars using that year's own prices. It then sets beside the total, without adding them in, the sums that are often quoted alongside it: loan guarantees, Israel Bonds bought by American states and counties, the cost of American forces in the region, and private American giving.
