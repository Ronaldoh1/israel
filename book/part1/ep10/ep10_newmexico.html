New Mexico: The Case Nobody Finished

The only active criminal inquiry on American soil is not federal. It is New Mexico's, and its story is a small version of the whole book. In 2019 the state's Attorney General opened a trafficking investigation into Epstein's Zorro Ranch south of Santa Fe and interviewed possible victims. In July 2019 it stopped, at the request of federal prosecutors in New York, and never restarted before he died. [cite:ep10_cbs_nm2026] [cite:ep10_kob_interim2026]

The released files reopened it. On 19 February 2026 Attorney General Raúl Torrez announced that "revelations outlined in the previously sealed FBI files warrant further examination". [cite:ep10_cbs_nm2026] The state legislature created a four-member truth commission with the power to subpoena, [cite:ep10_aj_commission2026] and on 9 March state investigators searched the ranch, with its new owners' cooperation; no findings have been announced. [cite:ep10_pbs_search2026] The state has also asked for an unredacted copy of a 2019 email in the files claiming that two girls were buried on the property. It is an unverified claim in a tip, and the book treats it as nothing more until the investigators say otherwise. [cite:ep10_cbs_nm2026]

The commission's interim report, on 5 August 2026, set out what it had found. At least five women and girls were sexually abused at the ranch between 1996 and 2012, and it estimated at least thirty more may have been abused in New Mexico. Epstein's aircraft made 227 flights to or from Albuquerque and Santa Fe between 1992 and 2019. "Federal and state authorities repeatedly failed to investigate", the report said: the federal prosecutors in New Mexico deferred to New York, which never made the ranch a priority; no one searched it until March 2026; and no FBI field office has opened a trafficking investigation focused on it. [cite:ep10_kob_interim2026] The commission found he likely received preferential treatment from state law enforcement, including avoiding sex-offender registration through a gap in state law. Its chair, Representative Andrea Romero, put it in one sentence: "The system was built for privacy and it worked for 26 years." [cite:ep10_abqj_interim2026]

The commission sent twenty subpoenas. The federal prosecutors' office in New York, the Federal Aviation Administration and JPMorgan refused or did not respond. [cite:ep10_kob_interim2026] Its final report is due on 31 December 2026. It cannot bring charges itself; it can only refer. [cite:ep10_abqj_interim2026]
