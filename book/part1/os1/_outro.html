Israel struck first on 5 June 1967 after Egypt closed the Straits of Tiran, and in six days took Sinai, Gaza, the West Bank, East Jerusalem and the Golan; whether its survival was at stake is disputed by Israeli historians; Resolution 242 called for withdrawal from "territories", a word read two ways, and only Sinai was returned.
