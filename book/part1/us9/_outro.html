Part Nine established that roughly a third of the people living under Israeli control are not Israeli citizens: about 3.43 million Palestinians in the West Bank and 2.13 million in Gaza at the end of 2025. [cite:us9_pcbs_2025] In the West Bank they live under military orders and military courts beside about 530,000 settlers who carry Israeli law with them through emergency regulations renewed every five years, most recently to February 2028. [cite:wb_acri_twosys] [cite:us9_jp_regs2023] [cite:wb_wiki_popstats] East Jerusalem's Palestinians are permanent residents who may vote for the city council but not the Knesset; 14,808 lost that residency between 1967 and 2022, and few of those who applied for citizenship received it. [cite:us9_aje_ejvote] [cite:us9_hamoked_revoc] [cite:us9_reuters_ejcit] In Gaza, Israel's control changed form in 2005 and again after 2023, and as of October 2026 it held about 64 per cent of the strip, while the Palestinian elections set for November were postponed to September 2027.
