Part Eighteen compares what each state does for families and for people who cannot earn enough. It begins with leave after a birth, the price of childcare and the money each state gives parents: a universal monthly allowance in Israel, a tax credit in the United States. It then sets Israel's abortion committees, which approve almost every request, beside the United States after the Supreme Court's 2022 Dobbs ruling, and compares fertility treatment and birth rates. The third section turns to old-age and disability pensions and to care in old age, including Israel's compulsory workplace pensions and the projected shortfall in America's Social Security trust funds. The last section sets the two countries' official poverty series side by side over time, explains why their headline rates are not measured the same way, and shows what transfers do to poverty in each.
