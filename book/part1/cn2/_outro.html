By about 200 the elements of the later system were in place, though none yet had the force of law. A rich man's closed list had forced the question of scripture, and the Roman church had shown it would refuse his money rather than his doctrine. [cite:cn_ce_marcionites] The collection of the Sunday assembly was held by its president. [cite:vt21_justin67] And Irenaeus had bound the four gospels to a line of bishops traced name by name in Rome, making the office the guarantee of the books. [cite:cn_iren_3_3] The teachers who appealed to other writings, Valentinus among them, were outside that line. Part Three shows what changed when an emperor decided which line to back.
