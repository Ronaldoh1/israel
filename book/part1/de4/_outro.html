Part Four found Nick Land to be a philosopher of runaway technological change who left Warwick in 1998, became the best-known theorist of a right-wing accelerationism that left accelerationists contested from 2013, and wrote "The Dark Enlightenment" from Shanghai between March and July 2012. [cite:de4_wiki_land] [cite:de4_accel_manifesto] [cite:de4_land_de] The serial credits the Cathedral and neocameralism to Moldbug, quotes Thiel once, prescribes exit, secession, hard money and an end to central banking, and expects nothing short of cataclysm to bring them about. [cite:de4_land_de] Its race passages make hereditarian claims in Land's own voice while rejecting white nationalism, the turn to antisemitism and Nazism as politics; in his examples, exit becomes sorting, from white flight to secession to genetic "speciation," and in a separate 2014 post to space colonisation as a "genetic filter." [cite:de4_land_de] [cite:de4_haider] Nothing in the text plans the capture of an American state. Part Five turns to the man whose program Land borrowed: Curtis Yarvin, from his childhood to the founding of Unqualified Reservations.
