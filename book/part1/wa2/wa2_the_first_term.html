The First Term, 2017–2021

The first term delivered a series of decisions Israeli governments had sought for decades. The American embassy opened in Jerusalem on 14 May 2018; in March 2019 Trump recognised Israeli sovereignty over the Golan Heights. [cite:jerusalem_embassy_2018] [cite:gi8_ucsb_procl9852] In January 2020 the administration released "Peace to Prosperity", a plan led by Jared Kushner that would have let Israel annex about 30 percent of the West Bank, including the Jordan Valley, and offered the Palestinians a demilitarised state on the rest; the Palestinian leadership, which had no part in drafting it, rejected it. [cite:wiki_jared_kushner] In 2020 Kushner brokered the Abraham Accords, under which the United Arab Emirates, Bahrain, Morocco and Sudan normalised relations with Israel without a Palestinian state. [cite:ny_npr_abraham] [cite:wiki_jared_kushner]

Who asked

Sheldon and Miriam Adelson had pressed successive presidents to move the embassy, supported recognition of the Golan, and gave $90 million to a pro-Trump super PAC in 2020; Miriam Adelson gave about $100 million in 2024. At the Knesset in October 2025 Trump praised her, saying she "loves Israel". [cite:aj_adelson_knesset] Evangelical leaders made the same demands, as The Theology Shield on this shelf records. [cite:trump_evangelical_embassy]

The record shows the donors asked, the donations were made and the policies followed. It does not show that any one decision was made because of a donation, and Trump's own statements present them as convictions. Supporters credit these steps with recognising realities and producing the first Arab–Israeli normalisation deals in decades; critics say they rewarded occupation and set the Palestinians aside.
