The parts of this book follow the record in the order it happened. This appendix reads the same record all at once, as a network, and asks the question the parts could not: what if a piece of it had been missing? Every person, company, bank, property, office and document the book names is a node on the companion network map, and every tie carries the sentence and the source that establish it. Take a node out, and the map shows how much of the operation would have been left to run.

The appendix explains how the map was built and how it weighs a removal, sets out its tests and how far they can be trusted, asks who else could have held Epstein's place, and traces the network back to its earliest documented points. The figures are not predictions. They are a disciplined way of asking what the operation depended on.
