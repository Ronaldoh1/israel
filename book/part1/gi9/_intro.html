Part Nine moves from the fronts of the past to the ground Israel's forces held in October 2026. Between the end of 2024 and the autumn of 2026 Israeli troops came to hold three belts of land beyond the lines they had held on 7 October 2023: a strip of south Lebanon behind what Israel calls its Forward Defense Line, the United Nations buffer zone in Syria together with the Syrian summit of Mount Hermon, and a growing share of the Gaza Strip behind the "Yellow Line." This part sets out each one in turn: when and how it was taken, how large it is according to each source that has measured it, what Israel's government and military have said it is for, and what the neighbouring government, the armed groups concerned and the United Nations have said about it. Then it asks the question this volume puts to every front. Is this the expansion the phrase "Greater Israel" describes, or a security belt of the kind Israel has held and given up before? Every status is given as of October 2026, a week before Israel's election of 27 October.
