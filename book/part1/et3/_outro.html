The church at Aksum claims the original Ark, kept in the Chapel of the Tablet, where "only the guardian monk may view" it; he is appointed for life by his predecessor and confined to the chapel. [cite:et_wiki_zion] Every church's tabot is concealed from all but priests, and women may not enter the Old Church. [cite:et_wh_solomonic] [cite:et_wiki_zion] The claim cannot be tested, and the record shows only the structure: the holiest object behind one door, under one man, who names his successor. Part Four turns to the books Ethiopia kept that the West let go.
