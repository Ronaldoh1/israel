The last part takes the volume's findings in three groups, as the Hamas and Hezbollah volumes did: what the record settles about Israel and Iran from the quiet recognition of 1950 to the war of 2026, what it leaves in dispute between documented positions, with the basis of each side, and what is still open as of October 2026. It then sets the three theses this volume was written to test against the record: that Netanyahu's warnings of an Iranian bomb were false alarms, that Iran's government does not want war with the United States, and that the United States was dragged into war by Israel. Each verdict separates what the documents support, what they do not, and what remains argument. It ends with the question of Iran's Jewish community, often put as a paradox, stated as the record has it.
