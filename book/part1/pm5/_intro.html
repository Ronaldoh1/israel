The largest sums in this volume were not given to anyone. They were spent, by super PACs, in primaries, to elect some candidates and defeat others, most often sitting members of Congress or candidates who had broken with the lobby's positions. Part Five follows those campaigns: the older defeats often cited as precedents, then the super PAC's three cycles, 2022, 2024 and 2026, race by race, with the amounts, the outcomes and the other forces in each race. It reports wins and losses alike, and it keeps the question of what the money decided separate from the record of what was spent.
