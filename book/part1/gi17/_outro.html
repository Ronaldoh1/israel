Part Sixteen established that the world's response since October 2023 has been broad in what it said and narrow in what it enforced. In the Security Council the United States vetoed six Gaza ceasefire drafts and Palestine's full membership, until the Council endorsed the American plan in Resolution 2803 in November 2025; the General Assembly voted by majorities of 124 to 149 states for an end to the occupation, a ceasefire and a two-state declaration, and the UN's commission of inquiry found genocide, which Israel called "distorted and false."
