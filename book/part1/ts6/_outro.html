Part Six found that between 1891 and 1953 Christian support for a Jewish return reached the highest offices in Washington and London, but by routes that do not fit the story of a single end-times theology driving policy. William Blackstone, a dispensationalist who also hoped for the conversion of Jews, put the case to President Harrison on 5 March 1891 with 413 or 431 elite signatures, and at Brandeis's request prepared a second memorial whose presentation to Wilson the sources describe differently and which reached him, on Moorhead's account, after the Balfour Declaration.
